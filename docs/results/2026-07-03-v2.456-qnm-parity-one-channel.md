# v2.456 - the parity is a one-channel object: closing the parity-observability question

The candidate's parity is the heterotic model-independent axion (v2.434), with a Planckian decay constant
f_a ~ M_Pl. That single fact suppresses *every* parity signal except the isotropic CMB birefringence angle.
Collecting the channels — including a new estimate for GW-propagation birefringence — closes the
parity-observability question (the parity-sector analog of v2.447's primordial closure).

```text
  OBSERVABLE:
    isotropic CMB birefringence   beta ~ alpha_EM ~ 0.03-0.3 deg  (matches measured 0.34)   v2.451
  PLANCK-SUPPRESSED (unobservable):
    primordial chiral GW          Pi ~ H_inf/M_CS ~ 1e-6                                     v2.444
    birefringence anisotropy      delta_beta/beta ~ H_inf/(2pi f_a) ~ 1e-6                   v2.455
    GW-propagation birefringence  ~ H/M_CS ~ 1e-6  (vs LISA/ET ~1e-2)                        this cycle
```

All three suppressed channels share one origin: the gravitational Chern–Simons / axion coupling is set by a
**Planckian scale** (M_CS ~ M_Pl, f_a ~ M_Pl), so every graviton-sector or fluctuation-sourced parity signal
carries a factor ~ H/M_Pl ~ 10⁻⁶ or smaller. The photon-sector isotropic birefringence evades this because it's a
frequency-independent, path-integrated *rotation* set by the dimensionless (c_γ α_EM)(Δθ/f_a) — no Planckian
suppression (v2.451). 5/5 checks pass.

## What this closes

The candidate's parity is, observationally, a **one-channel object**: the isotropic CMB birefringence angle — its
size (~α_EM) and its positive handedness — is the *sole* accessible handle, and the effort to test the parity
belongs entirely there, not on GW birefringence, primordial chirality, or anisotropy. This is the parity-sector
analog of v2.447 (which closed the primordial-inflation channel): together they map exactly where the candidate's
distinctive physics is and is not testable — the discrimination lives in the isotropic CMB birefringence (parity)
and the late-time inflation-line / dark-energy fronts, and **nowhere** in the primordial, anisotropic, or
gravitational-wave parity channels.

## Honest scope

Order-of-magnitude parametric estimates sharing the M_CS ~ M_Pl / f_a ~ M_Pl assumption (the model-independent
axion's Planckian scale — compactification-dependent; a sub-Planckian f_a would lift the anisotropy and GW
channels, exactly the discriminator of v2.455). The chiral-GW (v2.444) and anisotropy (v2.455) numbers are the
previously-computed ones; the **GW-propagation-birefringence estimate is the new, least-certain piece** — CS GW
birefringence accumulates along the path, so a precise amplitude needs the axion profile + source frequency/
distance, not computed here; the robust claim is only that the Planckian M_CS keeps it many orders below realistic
GW-polarization sensitivity. "One-channel object" is contingent on f_a ~ M_Pl (the natural model-independent-axion
case, not a theorem for every parity completion). The isotropic-β channel rests on the ~3.6σ hint + the O(1)
anomaly × misalignment inputs (v2.451 caveats carry).
