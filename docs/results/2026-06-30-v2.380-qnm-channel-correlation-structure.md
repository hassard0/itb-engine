# v2.380 - SWING (deflationary): the four channels are not four independent tests -- they probe ~2-3 observable directions

## Question

The cross-sector bridges (v2.357 parity<->screening, v2.379 parity->BH) hinted the four prediction channels
are correlated. Does the theory make four *independent* predictions, or does its observable content collapse?

## Result (effective dimension ~2.5, not 4)

The 4x4 correlation matrix of the channel observables across the feasible family:

```text
            parity  screening  ringdown   BH
  parity     1.00     0.46      0.07     0.50
  screening  0.46     1.00      0.12     0.88
  ringdown   0.07     0.12      1.00     0.12
  BH         0.50     0.88      0.12     1.00

  effective observable dimension (correlation participation ratio): 2.45 of 4
```

All 5/5 checks pass. **Screening and BH are near-identical** (0.88 -- both essentially linear in `g_R2`, so
one observable not two). **Ringdown is orthogonal** to the other three (max 0.12 -- driven by `g_R3`, a free
direction v2.372, uncorrelated with the `g_R2/g_4` scale). **Parity is partially correlated** with the `g_R2`
group (~0.5, through the anomaly link `g_R2_parity ~ sqrt(g_4 g_R2)`, but with independent variation inside the
birefringence window).

## What this establishes

The theory's genuinely independent observable content is about **two-and-a-half directions**, not four:
- (i) the leading matter/curvature **scale `g_R2`**, which sets screening, the black-hole entropy shift, *and*
  about half the parity signal at once;
- (ii) the **cubic curvature `g_R3`**, which sets ringdown alone.

This honestly deflates the "four channels" story: measuring the `g_R2`-scale (via any of screening, BH, or
partly birefringence) forecasts the others, so only **ringdown adds a fully orthogonal probe**. It also
explains *why* the cross-sector bridges exist (v2.357/379) -- they are the correlations within the `g_R2` group
made explicit. For testability: the theory is falsifiable in four channels but *independently* constrained in
only ~2-3 -- to pin the full 5-parameter theory (v2.372) one needs both the `g_R2`-scale channels *and*
ringdown, and neither substitutes for the other.

## Honest scope

The correlations are over a seeded random-walk sample, and each channel uses its toy map (`beta = 3.4
g_R2_parity`, screening over-cap, ringdown floor, `Delta S_ext` with toy CLR coefficients) -- so the precise
values and the 2.45 effective dimension are toy-basis. But the STRUCTURE is basis-robust and follows from which
couplings each channel depends on: screening and BH are both monotone in `g_R2` (near-identical whatever the
normalization); ringdown depends on `g_R3` (free of `g_R2/g_4`, hence orthogonal); parity depends on
`g_R2_parity` (anomaly-linked, hence partial). The screening=BH near-degeneracy is close to definitional (both
linear in `g_R2`) -- an honest deflation, not a coincidence. The effective-dimension via correlation
participation ratio is a standard but convention-laden measure. Robust content: the four channels reduce to
~2-3 independent observable directions, so "four channels" overstates independent testability. Toy numbers,
structural coupling-dependence.
