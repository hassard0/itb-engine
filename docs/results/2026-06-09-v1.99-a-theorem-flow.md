## Tying the RG flow to the a-anomaly

v1.89 treated the Wilson coefficients as running couplings flowing to a UV fixed point.
v1.70–72 identified the Euler central charge **a = g_R2** and the Weyl² central charge
**c = g_C**. v1.99 connects them through the **a-theorem** (Komargodski–Schwimmer 2011;
holographic Myers–Sinha): *a* is monotone along RG flows and **decreases from UV to IR**
(a_UV ≥ a_IR).

## The check

The v1.89 toy flow has a UV-attractive fixed point at **a* = g_star = 0.15**
(β_{g_R2} = −0.40(g_R2 − 0.15)). The a-theorem requires the UV fixed point to *dominate*
— a* ≥ a_IR for every IR theory — so any framework with **g_R2 > a*** has its central
charge *increasing* toward the IR, **violating** the a-theorem.

## Result — the toy flow violates the a-theorem (a diagnostic)

- **a(t) is monotone along the flow** for every framework (the exponential approach to
  the fixed point is monotone) — so far, consistent.
- **But a* = 0.15 lies below 9 of 13 frameworks' a = g_R2** (Hořava 0.45, parity 0.40,
  data-driven 0.33, LQG 0.30, GFT 0.28, CDT 0.22, high-g8, string 0.20, causal-set).
  For those, the flow has a **increasing toward the IR** — a **violation** of a_UV ≥ a_IR.
- Only the low-a theories (asymptotic safety 0.15, emergent 0.10, discovered-novel 0.14,
  pure GR 0) are a-theorem-consistent under this flow.

## The constraint it derives

> The a-theorem demands the UV fixed point **dominate**: **a\* ≥ max framework a = 0.45**.
> The v1.89 phylogeny's toy fixed point (a*=0.15) is *too low* to be a-theorem-consistent.
> Reconciling the engine's own RG flow (v1.89) with the a-theorem (v1.70/71) therefore
> **predicts a property of the asymptotic-safety UV fixed point**: it must carry a *large
> Euler central charge*, a* ≥ 0.45, dominating all the IR frameworks.

This is a genuine cross-cycle synthesis — a constraint the engine extracts by combining
two of its own results (the RG phylogeny and the a-anomaly). And consistent with Dr. M.:
the Weyl² charge **c = g_C is non-monotone** in 4d (no c-theorem; Cardy), so (c − a) need
not flow monotonically — only *a* is the monotone.
