# v2.284 - Anatomy of the binding bound: why the repulsive-force conjecture defines the feasible edge

## Question

v2.283 found that the canonical engine roster admits only pure_gr, and that string_tree_eft,
asymptotic_safety and cdt each miss by exactly ONE constraint -- `repulsive_force_conjecture`. WHY do
all three valid-UV-completion encodings land just over this one bound?

## Result

The Heidenreich-Reece-Rudelius repulsive-force conjecture is encoded as

```text
  margin = g_4 g_6  -  g_R2  -  gamma g_R2^2  >= 0      (gamma = 1)
           [matter]    [curvature cost: linear WGC term + quadratic RFC enhancement]
```

Decomposing per framework (verified to match the engine margin to 1e-9):

```text
  framework          matter   -linear  -quad     RFC margin   linear-WGC margin
  pure_gr            0.0000   0.0000   0.0000    +0.0000      +0.0000
  string_tree_eft    0.2000   0.2000   0.0400    -0.0400      +0.0000   <- on WGC line
  asymptotic_safety  0.1200   0.1500   0.0225    -0.0525      -0.0300   <- below WGC
  lqg_induced        0.2700   0.3000   0.0900    -0.1200      -0.0300   <- below WGC
  cdt                0.2200   0.2200   0.0484    -0.0484      +0.0000   <- on WGC line
```

The four higher-derivative frameworks split into two groups. **string_tree_eft and cdt sit EXACTLY on
the original-WGC boundary** (`g_4 g_6 = g_R2`, linear margin 0): they would be feasible under the weaker
original Weak Gravity Conjecture, and fail ONLY because of the quadratic `gamma g_R2^2` enhancement of
the STRONGER repulsive-force conjecture, by margins of just `-0.04` and `-0.048`. **asymptotic_safety
and lqg_induced fail even the linear WGC** (`g_4 g_6 < g_R2`): their matter sectors are genuinely too
weak to support their curvature, below the boundary rather than tipped over it. pure_gr trivially
saturates (all couplings zero). All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The binding edge of the engine's feasible region is a single interpretable inequality, and the v2.283
"string/AS/cdt are one constraint out" result resolves at the coupling-arithmetic level: the toy
frameworks are calibrated so their matter product lands right at the WGC line, which makes the
repulsive-force conjecture's quadratic term the discriminating constraint -- the engine's tightest
gravitational-universality bound, sitting a single prefactor-step from the encodings by design.

## Honest scope

Exact arithmetic on the engine's encoded `repulsive_force_conjecture` (verified to match the engine
margin to 1e-9). The split into linear-WGC-boundary (string, cdt) vs below-linear (asymptotic_safety,
lqg) is the engine's literal coupling values -- the repo's representative O(1) toy encodings -- so "on
the WGC boundary" is a statement about the ENCODING, not a claim that string theory saturates the
physical WGC (which it need not at tree level). `gamma = 1` is the engine's chosen RFC strength; a
different gamma shifts the boundary-group frameworks but not the below-linear ones (whose matter product
is below `g_R2` regardless of gamma). A constraint-anatomy / feasible-edge result, not a new constraint
or a claim about the physical frameworks.
