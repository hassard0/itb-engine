# v1.23 — Prefactor-realism audit: which headline findings survive the toy prefactors?

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core Xeon E5-2667 v4, 251 GB RAM, 4× Tesla V100). All
Monte-Carlo runs at 300 000 draws, 16 workers, ~25 s each.
**Research partner:** Dr. M. (Gemma-4-26B, local on Pluto `192.168.4.193:8080`).

---

## Context

Every prior ITB report carries the same caveat (README "Honest limitations",
`2026-05-08-honest-synthesis.md`): the 31 constraints are correct in *shape*
but use **O(1) placeholder prefactors** — "the right streets but the wrong
house numbers." Every headline finding (LQG fails, intersection non-empty,
experiment re-ranking) was computed at **one** point in prefactor space.

This audit asks the question that gates publication-grade conclusions:

> Treating the six most-uncertain prefactors as known only to within a factor
> of ~2, what fraction of the plausible prefactor space reproduces each
> headline finding — and which findings are artifacts of a single arbitrary
> number?

This is the first systematic robustness pass over the engine's own
conclusions. It does **not** add new physics; it tells us which existing
conclusions to trust.

## Method

New tooling (`experiments/`, parallel, runs on Vulcan):

- `stack.py` — single source of truth for the 31-constraint stack and the five
  frameworks, with the six knife-edge prefactors exposed as tunable knobs, plus
  a switchable BNOSSW mean-form and RFC structural form.
- `realism_mc.py` — Monte-Carlo over the 6-D prefactor box. Records, **per
  framework, the probability that each individual constraint excludes it**
  (every violated constraint, not just the single hardest-binding one — so a
  dominant excluder cannot mask the others). Plus an intersection-emptiness
  sub-sample (4 000 full Nelder-Mead searches per condition).

This generalizes the existing `prefactor_sensitivity` module, which swept one
prefactor against only ~4 constraints; here every prefactor is tested against
the **full** stack, jointly.

The six knobs, with canonical value and plausible (factor-of-~2) range — these
ranges are stated modeling assumptions, not published numbers:

| knob | canonical | range | constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bnossw_pref` | 1.0 | 0.5–2.0 | BNOSSW MMI harmonic-mean coefficient |
| `rfc_gamma` | 1.0 | 0.5–2.0 | repulsive force conjecture quadratic term |
| `cubic_kappa` | 1.0 | 0.5–2.0 | cubic graviton-matter bound g_R3 ≤ κ·g_4² |
| `complexity_cmax` | 1.5 | 1.0–2.5 | Susskind/Lloyd complexity cutoff |
| `scalar_wgc_beta` | 0.5 | 0.25–1.0 | Palti scalar-WGC |
| `cft_alpha` | 0.5 | 0.25–1.0 | CFT-to-flat-space mapping |

Five conditions:

| | stack | prior | BNOSSW form | RFC form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | full | uniform | harmonic | matter_product (orig) |
| **B** | full − RFC | uniform | harmonic | — |
| **C** | full | uniform | **geometric** | matter_product |
| **D** | full | **log-uniform** | harmonic | matter_product |
| **E** | full | uniform | harmonic | **convex_hull (corrected)** |

## Findings

### F1 — The "Repulsive Force Conjecture excludes everything" result is an encoding artifact.

At the canonical encoding (condition A) the RFC excludes **all four**
non-trivial frameworks in **100%** of the box, for any γ>0. Cause: the v1.x RFC
is encoded as `g_4·g_6 − g_R2 − γ·g_R2² ≥ 0`, multiplying *two matter-sector*
coefficients against a graviton coefficient. The encoded frameworks all have
`g_4·g_6 ≈ g_R2` by construction, so the margin is `≈ −γ·g_R2² < 0` for any γ>0
— an engineered knife-edge, not physics.

Dr. M. independently flagged this as "dimensionally suspect and structurally
incorrect": RFC/WGC convex-hull conditions relate the matter charge-to-mass
coupling (a *single* matter coefficient) to gravitational strength, not a
product of two matter coefficients.

**Demonstration (condition E):** re-cast as `g_4 − g_R2 − γ·g_R2² ≥ 0`
(convex-hull form, no spurious product), RFC excludes **none** of the
frameworks — condition E is numerically *identical* to condition B (RFC
removed). The 100% universal exclusion was entirely an artifact.

→ **Action taken:** `RepulsiveForceConjecture` now accepts
`form="convex_hull"` (corrected) alongside the default `"matter_product"`
(kept for backward compatibility). Any prior conclusion citing RFC as a
discriminator is void.

### F2 — "LQG is the most-excluded framework" is robust.

Once the RFC artifact is set aside (conditions B and E), the exclusion
fractions become genuinely discriminating: **LQG 91%**, string 66%, CDT 59%,
**AS 54%**. LQG is the most-excluded and asymptotic safety is the most robust
survivor. This ordering survives the prior change (condition D, log-uniform,
strengthens it) and is independent of the RFC fix.

### F3 — The real LQG discriminators are the complexity cutoff and cubic curvature, not holography.

Per-constraint exclusion of LQG vs the others (condition B):

| constraint | string | AS | **LQG** | CDT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `complexity_cutoff` | 9% | **0%** | **35%** | 6% |
| `cubic_graviton_matter_bound` | 7% | 8% | **22%** | <1% |

The complexity cutoff excludes LQG in 35% of the box but asymptotic safety in
**0%** — it is the single most LQG-specific constraint. This matches the
physical intuition that LQG's larger cubic-curvature (spin-foam vertex) and
its tension with computational-complexity bounds are its real liabilities.

### F4 — The famous "LQG fails BNOSSW monogamy" headline does NOT survive a change of proxy form.

MMI is an entropy-vector inequality; rendering it in a Wilson-coefficient basis
requires choosing a representative form. Under the harmonic-mean proxy, BNOSSW
excludes LQG in **44%** of the box; under the equally-defensible geometric-mean
proxy, only **5%**. The result is an artifact of the functional-form choice and
should be retired as a robust claim. (The v1.20 report's headline #2 —
"LQG fails the n=3 monogamy inequality specifically" — does not survive this
audit.)

### F5 — The non-empty-intersection result is reasonably robust.

The all-31-constraint intersection is non-empty in 65% (A), 74% (B/E), 78% (C)
of the box under a uniform prior, and 51% under a log-uniform prior. "There
exists a theory satisfying every constraint simultaneously" survives prefactor
uncertainty in roughly half to three-quarters of cases — a defensible
robustness statement, in contrast to the framework-exclusion headlines, which
are largely artifacts (F1, F4) or carried by just two constraints (F3).

## Dr. M.'s critique (verbatim points)

1. F1 is a methodological artifact — RFC is dimensionally/structurally miscast
   (independent confirmation).
2. The ignorance prior should be **log-uniform (Jeffreys)** for scale-dependent
   EFT coefficients, not uniform. (Tested as condition D; it strengthens F2/F3.)
3. To sharpen LQG vs AS at literature precision, encode the **forward-limit
   graviton dispersion positivity** (Im A(s,0) > 0 for 2→2 graviton scattering),
   which pins the sign and minimum magnitude of g_R2 — AS sits at a fixed-point
   value, LQG struggles to keep positivity without tuning area-gap corrections.
4. Add a **matter-sector s³ positivity** bound (g_6 vs g_4 from the
   dimension-8 matter coupling), genuinely independent of the graviton-sector
   conjectures.
