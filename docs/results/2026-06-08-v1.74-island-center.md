## The question

v1.73 measured the *shape* of the consistent-QG island. v1.74 finds its **center**
— the engine's affirmative answer to "what does the most robustly-consistent EFT
look like?" Concretely, the **Chebyshev / deepest-interior point**: the coefficient
vector that **maximizes the minimum signed-distance margin** to the consistency
walls — the point furthest inside every bound at once. Its achieved min-margin is
the island's **inradius** (how much slack the safest EFT has); the walls tight
there are the **active set** that pins it.

## A subtlety, handled honestly

A naive max-min over *all* 36 constraints is degenerate: `t_hooft_anomaly_matching`
is a narrow two-sided **band** (slack 0.02), so its signed-distance margin can never
exceed 0.02 — it would pin any center at inradius 0.02 and leave the location
undetermined. Anomaly *bands* are equality-like slabs the island must live **in**,
not walls to flee. So we **require the bands satisfied** (feasibility) but exclude
them from the max-min objective, which is then over the one-sided
positivity/causality/swampland **walls**. Metric: gradient-normalized
signed-distance margin (the defensible choice; raw margins would weight constraints
by arbitrary coefficient scale). The **active set is the robust content**; the
exact center moves with the normalization.
