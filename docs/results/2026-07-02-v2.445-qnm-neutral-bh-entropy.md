## Two independent principles agree on the Weyl² sign

- **Causality / conformal-collider (Hofman–Maldacena):** forces g_C ≥ 0 (verified in-engine).
- **Black-hole second law (Reall–Santos / Cheung–Liu–Remmen entropy positivity):** the neutral-BH entropy shift
  ΔS ~ +g_C, so a consistent EFT with ΔS ≥ 0 also wants g_C ≥ 0.

Both give the **same sign**, and the candidate sits on it (g_C > 0). So the candidate predicts a **positive
Schwarzschild entropy shift** — extending the extremal-charged WGC entropy positivity (v2.378) to the neutral
sector, and exhibiting a genuine **cross-consistency between two independent consistency principles** (causality
and the generalized second law) on one coupling. This adds a **thermodynamic pillar** to the rigorous backbone
(positivity, causality, bootstrap, swampland) the candidate already rests on — all mutually consistent on the same
point. The magnitude is Planck-suppressed (a structural statement, not an observable), but the sign agreement is a
non-trivial check the candidate passes.

## Honest scope

The g_C ≥ 0 positivity is a **rigorous** engine result (g_C < 0 violates the Hofman–Maldacena wedge, source-tagged
rigorous), genuinely independent of a=c (only the *value* uses a=c). But **"ΔS ~ +g_C" is sign-level, not a
computed coefficient**: it uses the standard result that on Ricci-flat Schwarzschild the leading entropy
correction is the Riemann²/Weyl² term with the causal/unitary sign giving ΔS > 0 — the exact Wald coefficient is
not recomputed, and identifying the engine's g_C with the standard Weyl² Wald coefficient (same sign convention)
is a defensible assumption. So the robust claim is the **sign convergence** (causality forces g_C ≥ 0; the
second-law positivity wants the same sign; the candidate has g_C > 0), not a computed entropy value. Magnitude is
Planck-suppressed (unobservable — a consistency statement like the WGC/extremal-decay results). Whether second-law
entropy positivity is a *strict* requirement of every consistent EFT (vs a strong conjecture) is itself debated.
