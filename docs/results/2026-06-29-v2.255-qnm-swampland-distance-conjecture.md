# v2.255 - The Swampland Distance Conjecture: the light tower, and why it predicts small r

## Question

The companion swampland pillar to the Weak Gravity Conjecture (v2.254), reconnecting to the engine's
`distance_conjecture`. The Distance Conjecture (Ooguri-Vafa) says a large field excursion brings down
an exponentially light tower of states, breaking the EFT. What is the observational consequence -- and
does it connect to the inflation tensor sector (v2.253)?

## Result

The SDC tower descends as `m(Delta phi) = M_0 exp(-alpha Delta phi/M_Pl)` (`alpha ~ O(1)`):

```text
  Delta phi / M_Pl    tower mass / M_Pl
  0.5                 6.1e-1
  1.0                 3.7e-1
  5.0                 6.7e-3
  10.0                4.5e-5      (by ~7 M_Pl it is below 1e-3 M_Pl, voiding the EFT)
```

The sharp consequence is in inflation. The Lyth bound ties the inflaton excursion to the
tensor-to-scalar ratio, `Delta phi/M_Pl ~ sqrt(r/0.01)`:

```text
  r       Delta phi (M_Pl)   regime            context
  0.036   1.90               trans-Planckian   current bound (BICEP/Keck) -- mild SDC tension
  0.010   1.00               threshold         Lyth threshold ~ M_Pl
  0.003   0.55               sub-Planckian      CMB-S4 target -- swampland-safe
  0.001   0.32               sub-Planckian      LiteBIRD reach
```

**The Distance Conjecture predicts small r.** A sizeable `r` requires a TRANS-Planckian inflaton
excursion -- exactly what the SDC disfavours, since the tower would descend during inflation and
spoil the EFT. So the current bound `r < 0.036` (implying `Delta phi ~ 1.9 M_Pl`) sits in the mildly
tense trans-Planckian regime, while a future bound `r < 0.002` would be sub-Planckian /
swampland-safe. The SDC is thus a FALSIFIABLE swampland statement about the v2.253 tensor sector -- it
bets on small-field inflation, testable by CMB-S4 / LiteBIRD -- and it is the physical-tower
counterpart of the engine's `distance_conjecture` aspect-ratio bound on the Wilson coefficients.
