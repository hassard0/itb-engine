# v2.239 - Kerr generalization: how spin splits and amplifies the strong-field observables

## Question

The v2.229-v2.238 sub-program was non-rotating (Schwarzschild). Rotation is the one parameter Nature
actually varies. How does black-hole spin `a` change the strong-field observables -- and does the
in-house machinery reproduce the famous Kerr amplification (the ~42% extremal accretion efficiency
that powers AGN/quasars)?

## Result

Kerr equatorial Bardeen-Press-Teukolsky formulas (M=1), prograde / retrograde branches:

```text
  a        ISCO (pro/retro)     efficiency (pro/retro)     photon (pro/retro)
  0.000     6.000 / 6.000         0.0572 / 0.0572           3.000 / 3.000
  0.500     4.233 / 7.555         0.0821 / 0.0451           2.347 / 3.532
  0.900     2.321 / 8.717         0.1558 / 0.0390           1.558 / 3.910
  0.990     1.455 / 8.972         0.2640 / 0.0379           1.168 / 3.991
  0.998     1.237 / 8.994         0.3210 / 0.0378           1.074 / 3.998   (Thorne limit)
  1.000*    1.000 / 9.000         0.4226 / 0.0377           1.000 / 4.000   (*analytic limits)
```

**Spin SPLITS the v2.238-locked observables into prograde/retrograde branches and AMPLIFIES the
prograde channel.** As `a: 0 -> 1` the prograde ISCO shrinks `6 -> 1` (frame-dragging) while the
retrograde ISCO grows `6 -> 9`; the equatorial photon orbit splits `3 -> 1` (pro) and `3 -> 4`
(retro); and the prograde accretion efficiency climbs from 5.72% to the extremal limit
`1 - 1/sqrt3 ~ 42.3%`, while retrograde drops to ~3.77%. Schwarzschild (`a=0`) is reproduced exactly.

The 42% prograde efficiency -- nearly an order of magnitude above Schwarzschild and ~75x the
rest-mass efficiency of stellar fusion (~0.7%) -- is the rotational energy extraction that makes
spinning black holes the engines of the most luminous AGN and quasars. It is the physical,
spin-driven generalization of the single-deformation fingerprint (v2.238).

## What this establishes

Extends the strong-field-test sub-program from the toy non-rotating deformation to the real rotating
(Kerr) black hole, reproducing the standard ISCO / efficiency / photon-orbit results exactly and
showing how spin breaks the Schwarzschild degeneracy (the v2.238 locking) into pro/retro branches.
It anchors the deformation-fingerprint program in the one black-hole parameter that is actually
observed to vary, and recovers the headline astrophysical fact (Kerr accretion efficiency up to
~42%).

## Honest scope

Equatorial, geodesic (test-particle) circular orbits of the EXACT Kerr metric, M=1 -- the
ISCO/efficiency/photon formulas are the standard Bardeen-Press-Teukolsky results (exact, not
deformation-perturbative; this is real Kerr, not a bump). The `a=1` EXTREMAL point has a coordinate

Thorne `a=0.998` value (radiation torque), included in the sequence. Efficiency assumes a standard
thin disk radiating the binding energy. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
