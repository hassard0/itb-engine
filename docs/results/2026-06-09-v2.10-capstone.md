## The question

Take the space of higher-derivative quantum-gravity EFTs. Impose **every consistency
condition we can encode** — amplitude positivity, causality, holographic-information
bounds, the swampland, anomaly flow, black-hole thermodynamics — then confront the
survivors with **real experimental data**, and ask: *what's left, and what would decide it?*

## The method

A theory is a point in **8 Wilson coefficients** (matter g_4,g_6,g_8; graviton g_R2=Euler,
g_C=Weyl², g_R3; parity g_R2_parity,g_R3_parity). **38 theoretical constraints** carve out
a consistent "island"; **9 observables** give each survivor a falsifiable fingerprint;
**4 ingested experiments** (sub-mm gravity, cosmic birefringence, GW speed & dispersion)
shrink the island toward what the data allow.

## The six most robust findings (v2.07 jackknife: median robustness 0.98)

1. **The consistent island is thin and low-dimensional** — ~0.6% by volume, **~3.4
   effective dimensions** (one stiff collective-scale mode, several sloppy ones).
2. **LQG, Hořava, and GFT are robustly excluded** — by *multiple* constraints at once
   (no single bound's correctness rescues them); the exclusion is localized to their
   *graviton* sector.
3. **The Euler-vs-Weyl² split is the engine's spine** — resolving a=g_R2 from c=g_C
   organizes the constraints (the Hofman–Maldacena wedge), the observables (η/s↔Euler
   vs complexity↔Weyl²), the island's fattest direction, and the whole (a,c) RG portrait.
4. **A swampland-dominated load-bearing core** — of 38 constraints, ~15 do the work and
   the distance conjecture + anomaly inflow dominate; 22 are redundant in this basis.
5. **The a/c↔η/s↔BH-entropy↔inflation↔complexity unifications** — single couplings play
   many roles (one R² operator is the inflaton, the dark-energy scalaron, *and* the
   holographic η/s); consistent gravity is strictly larger than gauge² (non-double-copy).
6. **The empirical-swampland data-driven EFT** — folding consistency + data points to a
   specific **screened-scalaron, positive-handed-parity EFT** that no textbook framework
   realizes.

## The one central contingency

The program's headline — *a parity-violating universe* — **lives or dies on the ~3.6σ
cosmic-birefringence hint** (β = 0.34°±0.09°). The data-driven EFT is decisively favored
only for **β ≳ 0.065** at LiteBIRD precision; under a null it ties the parity-even survivor
and loses by parsimony. It is effectively a **one-observable theory** — and **LiteBIRD /
CMB-S4 (~2030) will deliver a clean ~10σ verdict** either way.

## What to measure next

The least-constrained directions are the **Weyl² sector (g_C)** and the **g_8 / g_R3 blind
spots** — which have *no funded experiment*, so the program's Fisher rank stalls at 6/8.
The single sharpest near-term test is **next-gen CMB birefringence**; the missing legs are
a high-scattering-moment probe (g_8) and a cubic-graviton / GW-nonlinearity probe (g_R3).

## The honest limitations

Toy O(1) constraint prefactors (the realism program says which conclusions survive a
factor-of-2 — the *structure* does, the *precise numbers* don't); order-of-magnitude
cross-sector maps; cosmic birefringence is a **hint, not a discovery**; schematic RG /
spin-2 ghost. Several cycles are deliberately *negative* or *self-correcting* — that is the
point.

## Bottom line

Given amplitude/causality/holographic consistency **plus two ingested experiments**, the
engine points to a **thin, low-dimensional island of consistent quantum gravities** whose
data-favored member is a **screened-scalaron, parity-violating EFT** — pinned by **six
measurements**, gated by **two missing experiments**, and decided, at its heart, by **one
cosmic-birefringence measurement arriving ~2030**. The engine states that conclusion and
its single point of failure in the same breath.
