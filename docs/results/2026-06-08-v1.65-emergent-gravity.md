# v1.65 — Emergent gravity, and a third dimension of engine scope (fundamentality)

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan; consult to Dr. M. (Pluto).

---

## What this adds

A 12th candidate framework — **Verlinde entropic / emergent gravity** — chosen
because it probes the engine's scope in a *new* way, and a third scope flag to
capture it.

In emergent gravity, gravity is not a fundamental interaction but a
thermodynamic/entropic phenomenon; its physics is an infrared, large-scale
(MOND-like, dark-matter-substituting) modification, with no UV expansion of a
fundamental graviton field. The engine's amplitude-positivity bounds expand
exactly that fundamental graviton amplitude — so for emergent gravity they are
not the wrong-precision tool, they are the *wrong tool entirely*.

## The third scope dimension: `fundamental`

`Framework` now carries three scope flags:

| flag | assumption it guards | breakers |
|---|---|---|
| `lorentz_invariant` | analyticity/unitarity of the S-matrix | Hořava–Lifshitz |
| `local` | locality (swampland/positivity program) | Causal Sets, Emergent |
| **`fundamental`** (new) | gravity is a fundamental UV field with a Wilson-coefficient expansion | **Emergent gravity** |

Emergent gravity violates **two** (locality and fundamentality), so the scope
layer flags it doubly:

```
$ itb predict emergent_gravity
  scope.in_scope = False
  violations = ["locality ...", "a fundamental UV graviton field ..."]
  -> OUT OF SCOPE: the engine's feasibility verdict is NOT meaningful ...
  coefficients: g_4=0.3 g_6=0.2 g_8=0.15 g_R2=0.1 g_R3=0.05 parity=0  (near-GR UV)
  forward-positivity ratio 2.0;  Yukawa 63.7 um;  parity-conserving
```

Its near-GR UV coefficients land in the allowed region numerically — but the
double scope flag makes clear the engine declines to adjudicate it, and *why*.

## Why this matters

The engine's epistemic boundary is now genuinely three-dimensional. The realism
program established what the engine can conclude given (a) toy *numbers*
(v1.23–25) and (b) toy *framework coefficients* (v1.25); the scope program now
establishes what it can conclude given (c) the *assumptions* of its constraints —
Lorentz invariance, locality, and the existence of a fundamental UV graviton.
A candidate that violates any of these gets an explicit, reasoned refusal rather
than a misleading number. Of the 12 catalogued frameworks, **3 are now out of
scope** (Hořava–Lifshitz, Causal Sets, Emergent), each for a stated, distinct
reason — a map of not just *which theories survive* but *which theories the engine
is even entitled to judge*.

## Honest limitations

Emergent gravity's UV coefficients are representative (its real content is IR, not
in this basis); the mapping is loose by construction (that looseness IS the scope
point). The `fundamental` flag is a binary proxy for a subtle question (degrees of
emergence vary across proposals); here it cleanly separates fundamental-field QG
(string, AS, CDT, LQG, GFT, discovered) from emergent/thermodynamic gravity.
