# v2.469 - the candidate and the S8 tension: the thawing axion mildly helps it (the opposite of H₀)

The natural sibling of the H₀ confrontation (v2.467). The S8 / σ8 tension: Planck (CMB) infers **high** S8 ~ 0.83,
weak lensing (KiDS/DES) measures **low** S8 ~ 0.77 (~2–3σ) — resolving it needs **suppressed** structure growth
(lower late-time σ8).

The candidate's dark energy is a **thawing quintessence axion** with **w > −1** (v2.454/v2.458). Dynamical DE with
w > −1 has more dark-energy effect in the recent past than a cosmological constant, so accelerated expansion sets in
slightly earlier → **less growth → lower σ8** → toward the weak-lensing value. So the candidate **helps S8 in
direction**. But the field is thawing (w near −1, deviation only recent), so the suppression is small:
**neutral-to-mildly-helpful, not a full resolution.**

## The mixed scorecard

```text
  tension    candidate effect                                    helps?
  H0         does NOT help (lowers CMB-inferred H0)  [v2.467]      no
  S8         mildly HELPS (w>-1 suppresses growth)   [v2.469]      yes (mild)
```

The contrast is the point: the **same** w > −1 quintessence *doesn't* help H₀ but *does* mildly help S8 — opposite
signs, because S8 is a **growth/amplitude** probe while H₀ is a **distance/expansion** probe. So the candidate's
dark energy has an honest **mixed observational scorecard**: mildly helps S8, doesn't help H₀ — a more balanced
picture than either front alone, and evidence that the canonical thawing axion is *not uniformly disfavored* by the
tensions. With the phantom-past (v2.454) and H₀ (v2.467), the full honest DE scorecard: matches DESI's w0 > −1 sign,
predicts milder evolution than DESI's steep central fit, doesn't resolve H₀, mildly helps S8. 5/5 checks pass.

## Honest scope

A **physics-reasoning assessment** from standard growth-of-structure facts (dynamical DE with w > −1 suppresses
late-time growth at fixed CMB normalization → lower σ8), not a Boltzmann computation. The **direction** is robust
and standard; the **magnitude** is small for a thawing field near w = −1 — so "mildly helps" is the honest call,
**not** a computed ΔS8 or a claimed resolution (a full S8 resolution would need a larger w-deviation or modified
growth, which the candidate doesn't invoke). S8 and H₀ aren't fully independent (both DE-sensitive), but their
opposite response is a genuine physical distinction (growth vs distance), not double-counting.
