# v2.390 - SWING: the consistent theory has a definite absolute scale -- consistency alone pins the coupling strength to a factor ~3, no data

## Question

Matter dominance (v2.389) shows matter sets the gravitational scale. But is the theory *scale-invariant* --
can all couplings be uniformly rescaled `g -> lambda g` and stay consistent (region a cone, only ratios
matter) -- or is the absolute scale fixed?

## Result (a bounded scale window, not a cone)

Most bounds are scale-homogeneous (positivity; the degree-2 dispersion tower; degree-1 CEMZ) and do not bound
a uniform rescaling. But scaling the constructed point on the **theory-only** stack (no data):

```text
  feasible only for  lambda in [0.323, 1.069]   -- a bounded window, factor ~3.3, NOT a cone
  scale DOWN past 0.323  ->  cubic_graviton_matter_bound fails  (couplings too weak)
  scale UP   past 1.069  ->  anomaly_cancellation fails          (couplings too strong)
  constructed (lambda=1) sits at 91% up the window -- near the strong-coupling (anomaly) ceiling
```

All 5/5 checks pass. The consistency conditions alone -- with no data -- pin the theory's overall coupling
strength to a factor ~3.

## What this establishes

The theory is nearly **scale-rigid**: it cannot be made arbitrarily weakly or strongly coupled. A handful of
absolute-scale constraints -- a cubic graviton-matter bound from below, anomaly matching from above -- fix the
overall strength before any measurement. This is a predictivity statement complementary to v2.373 (the region
is small): not only are the coupling *ratios* carved (the ~5-parameter shape), the overall *scale* is nearly
carved too -- the theory has almost no free overall normalization. And the constructed max-margin point sits at
91% up the window, hard against the strong-coupling ceiling set by anomaly cancellation: the theory lives close
to the most strongly-coupled scale consistency permits, which is why the anomaly/parity sector is so tightly
determined (v2.371) -- anomaly matching is nearly saturated along the scale direction. It sharpens matter
dominance (v2.389): matter sets the gravitational scale, and consistency in turn nearly fixes the matter scale,
so the *whole* theory is scale-rigid, not just its gravitational sub-sector.

## Honest scope

This tests uniform rescaling along the RAY through the constructed point (all six couplings together);
non-uniform rescalings are not covered, so "factor ~3" is for the constructed direction (the cleanest notion of
overall scale). The binding constraints carry toy O(1) prefactors, so the exact edges (0.32, 1.07) and factor
3.3 are toy-basis; the ROBUST content is structural -- the theory-only region is a bounded scale window, not a
cone, and it is the absolute-scale constraints (a cubic graviton-matter floor, an anomaly ceiling), NOT the
homogeneous positivity/dispersion bounds, that break scale invariance. A different encoding of those two
constraints would move the edges but keep the bounded-window structure. Theory-only (no data), so the rigidity
is intrinsic to the consistency conditions; data narrows further. The "91% up" and "anomaly nearly saturated"
are for this ray and this point. Robust content: consistency alone fixes the overall coupling scale to an
order-few window (not a free normalization), broken by absolute-scale constraints, with the constructed point
near the strong-coupling ceiling. Toy edges, robust bounded-window structure.
