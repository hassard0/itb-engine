# v2.330 - Repairing lqg: the minimal modification that makes the data-favored framework consistent

## Question

lqg_induced is the program's most striking framework: DATA-favored (the only named parity-violating
framework, so it matches the cosmic-birefringence signal, v2.321/v2.322) yet THEORY-infeasible (its outlier
cubic curvature `g_R3 = 0.30`, twice any peer, v2.311). It has the right qualitative feature but the wrong
quantitative couplings. What is the MINIMAL modification to lqg that makes it both theory- and
data-consistent, and what does the repair change?

## Result (a near-miss: trim the cubic, keep the parity)

lqg lies only a modest coupling-space distance (~0.10) from the consistent+observed region, and the minimal
repair is interpretable:

```text
  coupling       lqg     repaired   delta
  g_4            0.600   0.600      +0.000
  g_6            0.450   0.438      -0.012
  g_8            0.400   0.400      +0.000
  g_R2           0.300   0.252      -0.048
  g_R3           0.300   0.209      -0.091   <- the biggest change (the outlier cubic, v2.311)
  g_R2_parity    0.080   0.078      -0.002   <- KEPT (in the cosmic-birefringence data window)
  g_R3_parity    0.040   0.038      -0.002   <- KEPT
```

The repair primarily TRIMS the outlier cubic curvature `g_R3` (the single biggest change -- exactly the
over-large coupling v2.311 identified) and the leading curvature `g_R2`, and -- crucially -- KEEPS the
parity violation inside the data window. All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

lqg's parity violation is its RIGHT feature (data-favored, preserved by the repair) and its cubic curvature
is its WRONG one (trimmed). 'Repaired lqg' lands in the constructed framework's family -- parity-violating,
trimmed curvature -- so lqg and the engine's constructed theory are the SAME KIND of theory, differing
essentially only in lqg's overshoot on the cubic. The leading parity-violating quantum-gravity framework
therefore points the right way: it is almost the consistent+observed theory the engine constructs, and the
one thing it gets wrong is a single over-large higher-curvature coupling. That reframes lqg's exclusion not
as a disqualification but as a near-miss with a clear, minimal fix, and it ties the program's data-favored
framework directly to its constructed theory.

## Honest scope

The repaired point and its distance are the engine's literal feasibility verdict on a minimal-displacement
search (random sampling near lqg + coordinate-descent refinement toward lqg) -- an APPROXIMATE projection,
not the exact closest point, so the ~0.10 distance and the exact deltas are upper bounds /
convention-dependent (the L2 metric mixes the dimensionful couplings equally, an arbitrary choice). The
robust, structural content: lqg is infeasible (exact), it is a modest distance from feasibility (the repair
exists and is small), the single biggest repair is trimming the outlier cubic `g_R3` (consistent with
v2.311), and the parity coupling is KEPT in the data window (lqg's parity is innocent of its infeasibility,
v2.310). The "same kind of theory as the constructed framework" reading is qualitative. The data window
carries the v2.321 ~3.6-sigma / order-of-magnitude caveats; under the null hypothesis (v2.329) the parity
is not required but still permitted. Toy basis, O(1) prefactors.
