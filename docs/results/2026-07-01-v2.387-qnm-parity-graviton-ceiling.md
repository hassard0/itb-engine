## What this establishes

Unlike `g_R2` -- which sits right at its screening cap (v2.354) -- the parity coupling sits at only ~1/4 of its
graviton-positivity ceiling: **parity is the one sector the theory is not pushed to the edge in**. This yields a
concrete, falsifiable target window for gravitational-wave birefringence: the theory permits a parity signal
anywhere from the CMB-implied value up to ~4x larger and stay graviton-consistent, so future GW-birefringence
measurements have room to find a *larger* chiral signal than the CMB implies without breaking the framework --
but a signal **above the ceiling would violate graviton positivity and falsify the parity-as-graviton
picture**. The graviton ceiling is a hard upper bound on how chiral gravity can be, turning the open-ended "how
big is the parity violation" into a bounded interval `[CMB value, ~4x CMB]`. It also refines the discrimination
story (v2.377): the anomaly variants (0.06-0.09) all sit deep inside the graviton-allowed region, so the
**anomaly determination, not graviton positivity, is what pins the parity value** -- graviton positivity only
rules out the far upper tail.

## Honest scope

The ceiling `sqrt(kappa g_4 g_6) - g_R2` uses the engine's `kappa=1` (v2.386 convention); it scales as
`sqrt(kappa)`, so the specific 0.267 is `kappa`-dependent and toy, but the headroom being order-few (>2) is
robust for any O(1) `kappa` (it would take `kappa ~ 1/16` to close the 4x gap). The CMB value 0.06 and 2-sigma
upper 0.153 are from the toy birefringence map (v2.329/370), so absolute numbers are toy-basis; the robust

from `g_R2_parity ~ 0.06` being much smaller than `sqrt(g_4 g_6) - g_R2 ~ 0.25`. The "GW-birefringence target
window" inherits v2.386's caveat: the bound is on the amplitude-level parity coupling, so mapping the ceiling
to a specific GW observable needs the dCS->waveform relation absent from the engine -- "falsifiable ceiling"
means the coupling ceiling. Sampled family. Robust content: the parity coupling sits at ~1/4 of its
graviton-positivity ceiling region-wide (order-few headroom), so graviton positivity is a loose upper bound and
the anomaly determination pins the value, with a hard consistency ceiling ~4x above the CMB. Toy magnitudes,
robust ordering, source-cited bound.
