# v1.95 — Genetic recombination: breeding the quantum-gravity frameworks for consistent hybrids and hybrid vigor

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan.

---

## A generative search by breeding

Earlier discovery cycles found new consistent theories by *optimization*. v1.95 tries
*breeding*. Each framework's coefficient vector is a genome with three sectors —
**matter** (g_4, g_6, g_8), **graviton** (g_R2, g_C, g_R3), **parity** (g_R2_parity,
g_R3_parity) — and we recombine them.

## Result 1 — many consistent hybrids, with hybrid vigor

Over all 13³ = 2197 ordered (matter, graviton, parity) triples:

- **300 hybrids (14%) are consistent** — new theories that *no catalogued framework
  realizes*, assembled from compatible sectors.
- **60 hybrids show "hybrid vigor"**: a larger interior margin (robustness) than *all
  three* of their parent frameworks. The champion is **string-EFT matter + causal-set
  graviton** (+ any parity), at robustness **0.039 vs 0.014** for its best parent —
  ~2.8× deeper inside the consistency walls. Mixing string's matter sector with the
  causal-set graviton sector yields a more robustly-consistent theory than either alone.

## Result 2 — dominant and incompatible sectors

- **Most dominant matter sector: string tree-EFT** — it forms consistent hybrids with
  the most graviton partners.
- **Most dominant graviton sector: pure GR** (trivially, zero curvature satisfies the
  graviton bounds with any matter/parity).
- **Incompatible graviton sectors: LQG-induced, Group Field Theory, Hořava–Lifshitz** —
  their curvature sectors (large g_R2/g_R3) form **no** consistent hybrid with *any*
  matter/parity partner. This is a clean structural restatement of *why* these
  frameworks are excluded: it's their **graviton sector**, irreducibly — swap in any
  matter and parity sector and it still fails (graviton positivity / EFThedron / the
  a/c wedge). The exclusion lives in the curvature couplings, not the matter content.

## Result 3 — the genetic algorithm

A sector-crossover GA (population 200, 30 generations, mutation 0.02, selection by
min-wall-margin) evolves a maximally-robust consistent hybrid to robustness **0.042** —
about 2–3× the best single parent (hybrid vigor again), but **below the v1.74 island
center (0.077)**. Honest: the recombination space (framework sectors + small mutation)
does not reach the *global* Chebyshev center found by free optimization — breeding
improves on the parents but the unconstrained optimum is deeper. The evolved hybrid is
a robust consistent theory sitting in the island interior.

## Honest scope

Hybrids are **toy recombinations**, not derived theories — there's no guarantee a given
(matter, graviton, parity) combination corresponds to a real UV completion. The robust
content is structural: **which sector combinations are mutually consistent**, that
**hybrid vigor exists**, and that the **excluded frameworks' incompatibility is
localized to their graviton sector**.

## Headline

Breeding the quantum-gravity frameworks by sector produces **300 consistent hybrid
theories** and **60 cases of hybrid vigor** (a hybrid deeper inside the consistency
walls than all its parents — champion: **string matter ⊕ causal-set graviton**). The
exclusion of LQG, GFT, and Hořava is **localized to their graviton sector** — no matter
or parity partner rescues it. A sector-crossover GA evolves robust hybrids but doesn't
reach the v1.74 free-optimization center.
