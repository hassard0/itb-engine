# v2.375 - SWING (all orders): the curvature sector is a log-convex moment tower -- an infinite string-like tower, not a finite-derivative theory

## Question (extend the moment tower to all orders)

The engine mandates one moment rung, `g_R3^2 <= g_R2 g_R4`. If the curvature couplings are moments of a
positive spectral density (the dispersive representation the equivalence-principle argument establishes,
v2.369), what does the *whole* sequence look like -- and does the higher-derivative expansion truncate?

## Result (an infinite, log-convex, convergent tower)

A moment sequence is **log-convex**: `g_{R,n}^2 <= g_{R,n-1} g_{R,n+1}` for all n. A log-convex sequence lies
above the log-linear interpolation through its first two points, so iterating from the constructed
`(g_R2, g_R3) = (0.193, 0.09)` gives a rigorous geometric **lower bound** on every higher coupling:

```text
  g_{R,n} >= g_R2 * (g_R3/g_R2)^(n-2),   r = g_R3/g_R2 = 0.466  (< 1)

  g_R2 >= 0.193   g_R3 >= 0.090   g_R4 >= 0.042   g_R5 >= 0.020
  g_R6 >= 0.0091  g_R7 >= 0.0043  g_R8 >= 0.0020  g_R9 >= 0.00093  ...
```

All 5/5 checks pass. Every floor is strictly positive, so the tower **never truncates**, and `r < 1` means the
higher-derivative expansion **converges**.

## What this establishes

The constructed theory has a nonzero curvature correction at **every order** (Ricci^2, Ricci^3, Riemann^4,
R^5, ...), each floored by the previous two -- an infinite, log-convex, geometrically-bounded tower. That is
the signature of a **string-like UV** (an infinite Regge tower generates infinitely many higher-derivative
operators) and the **opposite of a truncated f(R)-type or finite-derivative modified gravity** (which would
have `g_{R,n} = 0` above some order). The geometric ratio `r = 0.466 < 1` gives a convergent EFT with a finite
curvature cutoff, and `r` is a proxy for the ratio of the curvature scale to the tower/Regge scale.

This is the all-orders completion of the ringdown arc: v2.369 showed the leading quartic `g_R4` is strictly
above its floor (a nonzero minimum ringdown deviation); this shows the *entire* curvature tower is floored
away from zero order-by-order. The string-like character (v2.342 closest-to-string, v2.343 multi-state, v2.369
shared tower) is now visible directly in the coupling hierarchy.

dispersive/Stieltjes representation (v2.261) the equivalence-principle argument (v2.369) motivates; under that
