## Making v2.04's concern concrete

v2.04 found the data-driven EFT is a *one-observable theory* — its whole case rests on
cosmic birefringence β = 0.34° ± 0.09° (a ~3.6σ **hint**). v2.08 stress-tests that
directly: re-derive the engine's favored quantum gravity as a function of the **assumed**
β, in the cleanest contest — the **data-driven EFT** (consistent, g_R2_parity = β/3.4,
tracks the signal) vs the **consistent parity-even survivor** (string tree-EFT, β=0), with
parity-even winning a tie by parsimony. (The catalogued parity-violating frameworks are
separately excluded — infeasible or wrong-sign, v1.78 — so they aren't the relevant
competitor.)

## Result — a sharp pivot on the birefringence reading

| scenario | β (deg) | σ | P(data-driven) | winner | decisive (BF>10)? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** canonical (MK hint) | 0.34 | 0.09 | **0.999** | data-driven | ✓ |
| **B** null / parity-even | 0.00 | 0.09 | **0.500** | **string (parsimony)** | ✗ |
| **C** tightened (LiteBIRD) | 0.34 | 0.03 | 1.000 | data-driven | ✓ |
| **D** half-value | 0.17 | 0.09 | 0.856 | data-driven | **✗ (edge)** |
| **E** higher | 0.45 | 0.09 | 1.000 | data-driven | ✓ |

- **The favored quantum gravity pivots on the birefringence interpretation.** Under the
  **null** (β=0), the data-driven EFT merely *ties* the parity-even survivor (posterior
  0.5) — so by parsimony **string tree-EFT wins**: the data-driven EFT adds an unneeded
  parity coupling and **was a creature of the hint**.
- **The data-driven EFT is decisively favored (Bayes factor > 10) only for β ≳ 0.19** at
  the current hint precision (σ=0.09), tightening to **β ≳ 0.065** at LiteBIRD precision
  (σ=0.03). The canonical β=0.34 is well above both → decisively favored *today*.
- **The half-value scenario (β=0.17) is *not* decisive (0.86)** — right at the edge. So a
  partial systematic that halved the signal would already leave the data-driven EFT only
  *weakly* preferred.

## What it means

This is the honest, quantitative face of v2.04's "one-observable theory": the engine's
favored parity-violating EFT is **contingent on the birefringence reading**. It is
decisively favored at the measured β, *equally good as parity-even at the null* (and so
disfavored by parsimony there), and only marginally favored at half the signal. The whole
parity-violation conclusion lives or dies on whether the ~3.6σ hint survives — and
LiteBIRD/CMB-S4 (σ~0.03) will resolve it sharply: above β≈0.065 the parity-violating EFT is
decisive, below it parity-even wins. The engine states its central conclusion *and* its
single point of failure in the same breath.

## Honest scope

Toy Gaussian likelihoods + the β = 3.4·g_R2_parity map; the data-driven EFT tracks the
assumed β by construction, so this measures **contingency, not confirmation**. The robust
content is the **pivot structure** — parity-even at the null, parity-violating decisively
above β ≈ 0.19 (hint) / 0.065 (LiteBIRD).
