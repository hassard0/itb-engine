# v2.311 - What CP-even couplings bind lqg? An outlier cubic, and an unopenable curvature-matter box

## Question

v2.310 showed lqg's 6 failures are CP-even (parity-toggle-invariant) and not relieved by uniform
rescaling. Which couplings actually bind, failure by failure? This completes the diagnostic.

## Result (two clean mechanisms, both engine-defined)

**1. An outlier cubic curvature g_R3.** lqg's `g_R3 = 0.30` is 2x any other framework:

```text
  pure_gr 0.0   string 0.15   asymptotic_safety 0.10   lqg 0.30   cdt 0.15
```

The per-failure attribution (which coupling's reduction clears each failure) shows g_R3 is the SOLE cause
of `graviton_forward_positivity` and contributes to three more. Dialing it down to the peer value (0.15)
clears 4 of the 6 failures:

```text
  cleared by g_R3 -> peer:  cft_flat_space_bound, complexity_cutoff,
                            cross_sector_efthedron, graviton_forward_positivity
  residual:                 repulsive_force_conjecture, bnossw_monogamy
```

So lqg's leading problem is a cubic-curvature coupling sitting at twice the consistent-framework scale --
a concrete statement of where lqg overshoots.

**2. An unopenable (g_R2, matter) box.** The residual two failures are a structural box that NO CP-even
move on g_R2 can open. Scanning g_R2 (g_R3 at the peer) against the engine:

```text
  repulsive_force + monogamy satisfied only for   g_R2 <= 0.22
  anomaly_cancellation satisfied only for          g_R2 >= 0.2675
  allowed interval:  EMPTY  (gap 0.048)            lqg actual g_R2 = 0.30
```

lqg's leading curvature coupling cannot simultaneously satisfy the repulsive-force bound (which wants it
small relative to the matter product `g_4 g_6`) and anomaly cancellation (which wants it large enough to
match `g_4 g_6 - g_R2^2`). Lowering g_R2 to clear repulsive force breaks anomaly matching, and there is no
value in between -- the engine finds no feasible g_R2 at all. All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

lqg's consistency boundary is, exactly: an outlier cubic curvature `g_R3` (2x peers, the unique cause of
graviton forward positivity, 4 of 6 failures) PLUS an unopenable curvature-vs-matter box on `g_R2` (the
repulsive-force ceiling lies below the anomaly floor). A complete, engine-defined diagnosis of why this
framework sits at the boundary -- and it is squarely in the ordinary (CP-even) couplings, consistent with
v2.310's finding that parity is innocent.
