# v2.392 - SWING (honest negative): g_6 = g_8 is a Chebyshev-center artifact, not a consistency prediction -- the matter Regge edge is free

## Question

The constructed theory has `g_6 = g_8 = 0.4` exactly. In moment language (matter tower `m_0=g_4, m_1=g_6,
m_2=g_8`) that is `m_2 = m_1`, so the top matter moment ratio `g_8/g_6 = 1` -- which, read as a spectrum-edge
diagnostic, would place a matter state exactly at the Regge/spectrum edge. Is `g_6 = g_8` forced by consistency,
or a coincidence of the max-margin point?

## Result (a center artifact)

```text
  constructed g_8/g_6 = 1.000
  family g_8/g_6: mean 1.161, range [0.544, 3.219], std 0.296
  near-equal [0.9, 1.1]: 35%;   g_8 > g_6: 68%
```

All checks pass. The ratio ranges over a factor >5 with `1.0` well interior, so **`g_6 = g_8` is not forced** --
it is a numerical coincidence of the Chebyshev (max-margin) construction.

## What this establishes

The "matter state at the Regge edge" reading is **not robust**: the matter spectrum edge (max moment ratio)
varies widely across consistent theories. This dovetails exactly with `g_8` being the **dark parameter**
(v2.381): the top matter moment is both observationally invisible *and* dynamically free, so nothing -- neither
data nor consistency -- pins where the matter Regge tower ends. It also refines the honest tiering of the
candidate (v2.382): coincidental equalities among the constructed couplings (`g_6 = g_8`, which also drives
`r_matter = g_6/g_4`, v2.376) must be checked against the family before being read as structure -- here the
check fails, so `g_6 = g_8` belongs in the "toy-basis center coincidence" bin, not the "robust structure" bin.
The positive residue: the matter dispersion tower `g_6^2 <= g_4 g_8` leaves `g_8` real headroom (mean 1.66x its
floor), confirming the matter sector's top moment is the loose, free, dark corner -- consistent with matter
dominance setting the *scale* (v2.389) but not the internal matter spectrum *shape*.

## Honest scope

The `g_8/g_6` range is over a seeded random-walk sample, so the exact bounds and std are sampler/toy-basis
dependent -- but the REFUTATION is robust and not marginal: the ratio spans a factor >5 with 1.0 well interior,
so `g_6 = g_8` is not forced under any reasonable reading. The "spectrum-edge" interpretation of the moment
ratio is the standard moment-problem heuristic, used only to motivate why `g_8/g_6 = 1` looked meaningful; the
result stands regardless (it is simply that `g_8/g_6` is free). This is a negative result -- it removes a claim
rather than adding one -- so no toy-magnitude overclaim risk; the only caveat is the sampled-family scope.
Robust content: `g_6 = g_8` is a max-margin coincidence, the top matter moment ratio ranges freely (~0.5-3),
the matter Regge edge is unconstrained -- consistent with `g_8` dark (v2.381).
