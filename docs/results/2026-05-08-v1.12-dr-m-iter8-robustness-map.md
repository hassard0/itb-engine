## Three genuinely new findings from this iteration

### 1. LQG-induced is on TWO knife-edges, not just one

Beyond the persistent BNOSSW MMI failure (which is robust at +20%), LQG
now shows knife-edge sensitivity to two more constraints:
- Cubic graviton-matter bound: ±10%
- Complexity cutoff: +7%

Combined: **LQG-induced's failure mode is structurally unstable to prefactor
choices.** If the literal Caron-Huot 2024 cubic graviton-matter prefactor
turns out to be 0.95 instead of 1.0, LQG also fails *that* constraint,
giving it 4 independent failures (BNOSSW MMI + Cubic-GM + Complexity +
strict-anomaly from earlier scenarios). If the literal Susskind/Lloyd
complexity-cutoff scale ends up at 1.6 in our normalized basis, LQG passes
complexity. The engine cannot tell us which of these will hold without
publication-grade prefactor input.

### 2. CDT/MMI is the ONLY 0%-margin knife-edge

CDT sits exactly at MMI's canonical boundary. **A publication-grade BNOSSW
MMI form for n=3 holographic regions would settle CDT's status definitively
in one direction or the other** — currently the engine has no way to
determine which side of the boundary CDT falls on.

### 3. The Weak Gravity Conjecture isn't biting

All five frameworks pass WGC by 60-70% margin. WGC is currently *not*
an active constraint on any candidate UV completion at toy values. Either
- (a) our WGC encoding is too loose (likely), or
- (b) the relevant frameworks all genuinely satisfy WGC by a wide margin.

This points to publication-grade WGC encoding being a possible source of
new constraints — if a tighter prefactor (or proper extremal-mass-ratio
treatment) reduces the bound, frameworks might transition in.

## Methodological observation

Across iterations 1-7, Dr. M.'s constraint additions were producing
diminishing returns — null results, no new framework discrimination.
**Iteration 8's robustness map is the first iteration in this sequence
that produced new framework-status findings without adding any new
constraints to the engine's registry.** The information content was
already there in the existing constraints; we just hadn't asked the
right question.

This is a useful pattern: **before adding more constraints, exhaust the
analytic questions you can ask of the existing ones.** Sensitivity sweeps,
intersection searches, fragility maps, fingerprint analysis — these
amplify the signal in the engine's existing structure rather than adding
to it.
