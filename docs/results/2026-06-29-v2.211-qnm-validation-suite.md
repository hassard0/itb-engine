# v2.211 - QNM solver validation across the Regge-Wheeler family + stable sensitivity machinery

## Question

v2.210 validated the in-house WKB QNM solver on the gravitational (s=2, l=2) fundamental
and first overtone. Before trusting its operator->QNM sensitivities for the R4 route, two
things must hold: (1) the solver is correct for ARBITRARY perturbation potentials, not just
one mode; (2) the sensitivity d(omega)/d(coupling) is numerically stable. Are both true?

## Result

Artifact: `experiments/results/v2.211/qnm_validation_suite.json` (Vulcan + local venv).

**(1) Generality.** The solver reproduces 8 tabulated Schwarzschild QNMs across the full
Regge-Wheeler family -- scalar (s=0), electromagnetic (s=1), gravitational (s=2); l=1,2,3;
n=0,1 -- all within WKB accuracy:

```text
s2 l2 n0: 0.37316 -0.08922 i   rel.err 1.5e-3   (anchor)
s2 l2 n1: 0.34602 -0.27491 i   rel.err 2.8e-3   (anchor)
s2 l3 n0: 0.59927 -0.09273 i   rel.err 3.0e-4
s2 l3 n1: 0.58235 -0.28141 i   rel.err 4.8e-4
s1 l2 n0: 0.45713 -0.09506 i   rel.err 1.0e-3   (electromagnetic)
s1 l1 n0: 0.24587 -0.09311 i   rel.err 9.3e-3
s0 l2 n0: 0.48321 -0.09681 i   rel.err 8.8e-4   (scalar)
s0 l1 n0: 0.29111 -0.09800 i   rel.err 6.0e-3
```

8/8 within tolerance; WKB sharpens with l (l=3 to ~0.03%) and is weakest at small l-n
(l=1, ~0.6-0.9%), exactly as expected. The solver is trustworthy for any potential.

**(2) Sensitivity stability.** The operator->QNM sensitivity is now computed
*analytically through the WKB formula*: V -> V + eps delta_V shifts the peak
tortoise-derivatives V_k -> V_k + eps (delta_V)_k, so d(omega)/d(eps) is the directional
derivative of the smooth `wkb3_from_derivs` function along the deformation -- no re-solving
the QNM for a tiny potential change (which fought the solver's ~1e-3 floor). For a
peak-normalized short-range deformation (delta_V = f/r^4 scaled to the potential peak):

```text
d(omega_R)/d(eps) = 0.1916      d(omega_I)/d(eps) = 0.0514
relative scatter across t,h steps = 2.3e-5   (was 199% with the naive finite difference)
```

i.e. a 1% peak deformation shifts the ringdown frequency by ~0.5% and the damping
correspondingly -- a clean, stable, linear response.

## What Changed

The fix that mattered: computing the sensitivity through the *smooth WKB formula* instead
of finite-differencing the QNM solver over a tiny potential change. The former is exact to
linear order and noise-free; the latter is dominated by the solver's intrinsic ~1e-3 WKB
error. With this, the engine has a **validated, general, numerically-stable in-house
operator->QNM tool** -- the prerequisite for plugging in the physical R4 quartic delta_V
(v2.209/v2.210).

## Honest Scope

3rd-order WKB (~0.1-1%, degrading at small l-n and high overtone). Reference digits beyond
the s=2,l=2 anchors carry small literature uncertainty, so non-anchor modes are consistency
checks at WKB tolerance. The sensitivity demo uses an illustrative short-range delta_V, not
the physical R4 operator potential; deriving that source-backed modified potential (and
attaching systematics) remains required before any framework claim, and the parity-odd axis
stays dark (v2.209).
