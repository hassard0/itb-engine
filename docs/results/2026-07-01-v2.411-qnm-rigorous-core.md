## What this establishes

The engine's real content is now explicit and separable: **(1)** LQG-induced gravity is excluded by
amplitude/causality with no toy input; **(2)** the candidate's amplitude *structure* — matter positivity, the
`g_6² ≤ g_4 g_8` dispersion tower, the Hofman-Maldacena a/c wedge, the parity-decomposed L/R graviton bounds —
is rigorous. What remains toy is confined to the region *size* (swampland proxies do most of the local carving,
hence the 46× gap) and the observable *magnitudes* (data maps) — and both layers are now **tagged**, so any
downstream result can state its rigor tier instead of inheriting a blanket "toy" caveat. So the engine is not a
toy: it is a rigorous amplitude/causality carver whose swampland and observable layers wear toy prefactors, now
made first-class. The concrete de-toying path follows directly: replace each SOURCED_PROXY form with its
literature-exact inequality (the WGC convex-hull bound, a field-range SDC, the Dvali species count) and each
DATA map with a derived observable relation — moving constraints from proxy/data into the rigorous tier one at a
time, each a checkable improvement.

## Honest scope

The rigor classification is a defensible **judgement** keyed to each constraint's published citation, not a
theorem; "rigorous" means the inequality *structure* is the source result, while overall units/prefactors may
still be simplified (Λ=1) — "source-exact in form", not "zero approximation". A few borderline calls
(cft_flat_space, cross_sector_efthedron use simplified EFThedron slices) could be argued into sourced_proxy; the
LQG-exclusion is robust to those because LQG also fails `graviton_forward_positivity`, the most solid bound. The
LQG exclusion rests on the LQG-induced **framework encoding** (a model of its low-energy Wilson coefficients,
uncertain) — so it is "LQG as encoded here violates source-exact positivity", not a theorem about all of loop
quantum gravity. Local fractions are box-sample estimates. This cycle is a genuine engine change (new capability
in stack.py) plus the real result it enables; it does not itself make the toy prefactors real — it makes the
toy-vs-real boundary explicit, shows the rigorous side is already productive, and lays out the de-toying path.
