# v1.75 — Error bars on the central prediction: does the most-robust EFT survive prefactor uncertainty?

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core; 300 island-center optimizations in 77 s wall).

---

## The test

v1.74 named a specific most-robustly-consistent EFT and its central prediction
(sub-mm Yukawa 93 µm, a/c ≈ 0.92, η/s ≈ 0.81, nearest framework = string tree-EFT)
— all computed with the **CANONICAL** O(1) constraint prefactors. The realism
program's core discipline is to ask *which conclusions survive when we admit we
know those prefactors only to within a factor of ~2*. v1.75 applies that
discipline to the affirmative result: we draw **300 prefactor vectors** from the
stated `PLAUSIBLE_RANGES` (11 knobs: BNOSSW, RFC γ, cubic κ, complexity, scalar-WGC
β, CFT α, graviton forward c, matter s³, anomaly ρ, CEMZ κ, EFThedron α), rebuild
the stack for each, and **recompute the island center** with the *same* optimizer
(`island_center.find_center`, refactored to a single shared function). 300 valid
interior centers; we report the distribution of each prediction.

## What survives (robust)

| prediction | median | [p16, p84] | robustness verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a/c inside HM wedge** | — | — | **100% of draws** ✓ robust |
| **η/s (KSS units)** | 0.86 | [0.83, 0.88] | tight — robustly violates KSS by ~14% |
| **sub-mm Yukawa (µm)** | 80 | [73, 89] | robustly sub-mm, 60–110 µm |
| inradius (interior slack) | 0.056 | [0.045, 0.069] | always > 0 — robustly interior |
| g_R2 (center) | 0.16 | [0.13, 0.20] | stable |

- **The a/c prediction is fully robust:** every single draw places the center
  inside the Hofman–Maldacena wedge [1/3, 31/18]. The *value* spans 0.41–1.52
  (median 0.66, softer than the canonical 0.92), but consistency with the
  conformal-collider bound is universal.
- **η/s is the tightest prediction:** the center always sits at η/s ≈ 0.86 KSS
  units — a robust ~14% KSS violation, essentially independent of the prefactors.
- **The sub-mm Yukawa survives as a scale prediction:** it stays in 60–110 µm
  across all draws (78.7% within ±20% of 93 µm). The canonical 93 µm sits inside
  the p84; the distribution centers slightly lower (80 µm). So **"a sub-mm
  fifth-force Yukawa at the dark-energy scale (~tens of µm)" is robust**; the exact
  93 µm was the canonical-prefactor value and carries factor-ish uncertainty. The
  independent v1.44 dark-energy axion scale remains well within the spread.

## What's softer (honest)

**The "string tree-EFT is nearest" claim is a plurality, not a robust majority.**
Under prefactor variation the nearest framework is:

| framework | fraction of draws nearest |
|---|---|
| **string_tree_eft** | **0.37** |
| causal_set | 0.25 |
| discovered_high_g8 | 0.20 |
| asymptotic_safety | 0.12 |
| cdt | 0.04 |
| (others) | < 0.01 |

String tree-EFT is the **single most-often-nearest** framework (37%, the mode), so
the v1.74 statement is the *typical* outcome — but the center's neighborhood is
really a **cluster** of low-g_R2 survivors {string, causal sets, high-g8,
asymptotic safety}, and which one is closest flips with the prefactors. The honest
reading: the most-robust EFT lives among the string/causal-set/asymptotic-safety
cluster, with string tree-EFT most often the closest single member — not a unique,
prefactor-independent "winner."

## Reading

This is the realism program working as intended on an *affirmative* result, not
just an exclusion. The **structural** predictions — interior with positive slack,
a/c inside the conformal-collider wedge, a ~14% KSS-violating η/s, a sub-mm Yukawa
at the dark-energy scale — survive the toy-prefactor uncertainty cleanly. The
**precise** quantities (exact a/c, exact Yukawa, the single nearest framework)
carry the expected factor-ish spread and should be quoted as distributions, not
points. The central prediction is real but should be stated with its error bars:

> The most robustly-consistent EFT predicts a **sub-mm fifth-force Yukawa of
> 80 µm (60–110 µm)** at the dark-energy scale, a central-charge ratio **a/c
> inside the Hofman–Maldacena wedge** (median 0.66), and a holographic
> **η/s ≈ 0.86 ± 0.03** in KSS units — and sits nearest the string-EFT /
> causal-set / asymptotic-safety cluster.
