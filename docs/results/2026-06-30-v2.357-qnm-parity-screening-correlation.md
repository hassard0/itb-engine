# v2.357 - The parity and screening channels are correlated: stronger birefringence forces a deeper screening mandate

## Question

v2.356 mapped three observationally independent falsification channels and flagged that the underlying
couplings are correlated through the constraints. Can the parity<->screening correlation be made explicit and
quantitative -- are two of the three channels actually physically linked?

## Result (yes -- positively correlated, with quadratic amplification)

The link is the anomaly-inflow budget, which ties the parity coupling to `g_R2` -- and `g_R2` is exactly what
the unscreened Eot-Wash bound caps:

```text
  g_R2_parity^2 <= rho g_4 g_R2   =>   g_R2 >= g_R2_parity^2/(rho g_4)
  screening over-cap factor f(g_R2_parity) = [g_R2_parity^2/(rho g_4)] / g_R2_max  ~  g_R2_parity^2

  g_R2_parity = 0.0471 (CMB floor)  -> anomaly-min g_R2 = 0.070  -> over-cap 1.12
  g_R2_parity = 0.0600 (constructed) -> anomaly-min g_R2 = 0.113  -> over-cap 1.81
  g_R2_parity = 0.0780 (anomaly edge) -> anomaly-min g_R2 = 0.192  -> over-cap 3.06

  scaling floor->edge: observed 2.743  ==  quadratic (0.078/0.0471)^2 = 2.743
  family corr(g_R2, g_R2_parity) = +0.45   (n=2137);  every member respects the anomaly floor
```

All 5/5 checks pass. The over-cap factor exceeds 1 **even at the birefringence floor**, so the screening
mandate is locked in by the parity data itself (at the constructed g_4): the minimum parity the data allows
already forces `g_R2` above the unscreened cap.

## What this establishes

The parity and screening channels are **one correlated prediction wearing two observational hats**, not
independent. Stronger cosmic birefringence forces a *quadratically* deeper screening mandate. This
mechanistically explains v2.355 (the screening mandate's universality is data-linked -- it is the *same*
anomaly inflow + birefringence floor that pushes `g_R2` past the cap) and refines v2.356's three-channel map:
the channels are observationally distinct but parity and screening are physically coupled through `g_R2`. So
a future tightening of the birefringence signal (raising the parity floor) would *deepen* the screening
mandate, while a downward revision would relax both together. The engine confirms the linkage: over the
feasible family `g_R2` and `g_R2_parity` are positively correlated (+0.45) and every member respects the
anomaly floor.

## Honest scope

The correlation is **exact algebra at fixed g_4** (the anomaly floor on `g_R2` chained with the screening
cap), and the family positive-correlation is a genuine engine cross-check. But the over-cap NUMBERS (1.12 ->
3.06) are at the constructed `g_4 = 0.529` and scale as `1/g_4`, so a larger `g_4` weakens the amplification
(the family correlation is +0.45, below 1, precisely because `g_4` also varies). Both inputs are toy-basis:
the anomaly prefactor `rho` (v2.344, load-bearing) and the order-of-magnitude Eot-Wash cap (v2.354). The
whole linkage rests on the birefringence detection being real (v2.329) -- it is the parity DATA that drives
`g_R2` up; without it the correlation is vacuous. "Mandate active at the parity floor" uses the 2-sigma
birefringence lower edge and the default `rho`; a smaller `rho` or larger `g_4` could drop the floor factor
below 1, reopening a thin unscreened window at the parity floor (consistent with v2.355's small-coupling
branch). Robust content: parity and screening are physically coupled through `g_R2` via the anomaly, with
`f ~ g_R2_parity^2`. Toy basis, O(1) prefactors.
