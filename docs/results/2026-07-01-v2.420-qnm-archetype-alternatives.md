# v2.420 - the alternatives map: which consistent-QG archetypes rival the candidate, and exactly what rejects each

Pivoting from "is the candidate rigorous" (the v2.411–419 arc) to the complementary physical question: what
*other* archetypes of consistent QG EFT does the engine permit, and by what — rigorous positivity, real data, or
toy prefactor — is each separated from the candidate?

All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The space of consistent QG the engine permits has a clean structure. The **"too much gravity" (D)** and **"too
little matter" (E)** archetypes are **rigorously excluded** — source-exact amplitude positivity forbids them with
zero toy input, a clean statement that consistency bounds the gravity/matter balance from *both* sides. The
**curvature-minimal (C)** archetype survives the rigorous core but is rejected with help from toy constraints
(anomaly, aspect-ratio SDC) plus data — *not* a clean exclusion. And the headline: the **parity-conserving (B)**
archetype — the candidate with its parity coupling switched off — is **rigorously consistent** and is rejected by
exactly **one** thing, the cosmic-birefringence datum. So **B is the candidate's single live rival**: a perfectly
consistent parity-conserving quantum gravity that only the (contingent, ~3.6σ) birefringence measurement
separates from the candidate, and that returns as viable if the birefringence hint fades — matching the Z₂
handedness mirror (v2.406) and the load-bearing-datum finding (v2.408). Forward-looking net: **the
candidate-versus-alternative question reduces to "is cosmic birefringence real?"** — every other archetype is
settled independent of that measurement (D, E rigorously excluded; C structurally/toy-disfavored).

## Honest scope

The five archetypes are representative single points probing distinct qualitative features, not an exhaustive
clustering — they show these archetype *classes* are separated by the stated causes. D and E are somewhat
extreme, so "rigorously excluded" means these representative points are (establishing the class is
positivity-bounded), not a precise boundary. B's rejection "by cosmic_birefringence_data only" is the exact
full-stack violation list for that point, contingent on the birefringence hint being real (~3.6σ, v2.329). C's
rejection mixes toy (anomaly, SDC) and data, so C is not a clean rigorous exclusion — flagged.
