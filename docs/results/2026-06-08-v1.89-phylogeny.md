## A new way to look at the frameworks

The 13 frameworks have always been treated as **isolated points** in
Wilson-coefficient space. v1.89 asks a question that turns them into a *structure*:
treat the couplings as **running**, and ask whether any frameworks lie on a **common
RG trajectory** — is one the UV (or IR) image of another? A "tree of life" for
quantum gravities.

## The toy flow (Dr. M.-confirmed structure)

Schematic one-loop higher-derivative-gravity beta functions reflecting the known
qualitative structure:

- **Weyl² coupling g_C is asymptotically free**: β_C = −a g_C² → g_C → 0 in the UV
  (Fradkin–Tseytlin 1981; Avramidi–Barvinsky).
- a **UV-attractive non-Gaussian fixed point** (asymptotic safety; Reuter–Saueressig):
  the remaining couplings flow toward g* = (g_4,g_6,g_8,g_R2 = 0.30,0.20,0.20,0.15;
  g_R3,g_C,parity = 0).
- matter moments & cubic curvature **irrelevant** → flow to g*; **parity-odd**
  couplings → 0 in the UV (parity restoration).

We integrate dg/dt = β(g) for each framework toward the UV and IR.

### The striking part — a connected clade of discrete-spacetime approaches

The **direct ancestor→descendant edges** (one framework lying on another's
trajectory, closer to the fixed point) form a connected clade:

```
   lqg_induced  ──▶ group_field_theory        (GFT as the UV completion of LQG!)
   lqg_induced  ──▶ causal_set
   string_tree_eft ▶ causal_set
   cdt          ──▶ causal_set
   group_field_theory ▶ causal_set
```

- **`lqg_induced → group_field_theory`** is the headline: GFT is *literally the
  proposed UV completion of loop quantum gravity / spin foams* — and the toy RG flow
  independently places GFT as the UV image of LQG. The engine recovers a real
  physical relationship from a schematic flow.
- **`causal_set` is the common UV descendant hub** of string-EFT, LQG, CDT, and GFT —
  the discrete/combinatorial approaches all flow *through* a causal-set-like point on
  their way to the fixed point.

The other frameworks — **asymptotic safety, emergent gravity, Hořava, pure GR, and
the discovered branches** — are **RG-isolated**: they reach the same UV fixed point
by their own trajectories, not lying on a common path with the others. So the
phylogeny is: *one root (the NGFP), one connected clade of discrete-spacetime
theories, and several lineages that descend to the root independently.*

## Honest scope

The beta functions are **schematic toy forms** chosen to reflect the established
qualitative structure (Weyl² asymptotic freedom + an asymptotic-safety NGFP). Real
higher-derivative-gravity betas are **scheme-dependent**, and the theory has the
**massive spin-2 ghost** — so (Dr. M.) the flow describes the *structure* of the RG
(which frameworks are connected, whether a fixed point organizes them), **not** unique
physical trajectories. Disconnection in a fuller treatment could arise from multiple
fixed points with disjoint basins. The robust content here: a single NGFP makes the
landscape a **rooted tree**, and the discrete-spacetime approaches form a genuine
RG-connected clade with the LQG→GFT relation recovered.
