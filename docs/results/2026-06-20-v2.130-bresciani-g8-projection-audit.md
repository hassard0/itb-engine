## Method

The audit compares two contracts:

```text
engine g_8:
  sector = matter_forward_limit
  meaning = forward-limit scattering positivity moment
  amplitude power = s^4

Bresciani v2 gravity basis:
  sector = four_graviton_eight_derivative_gravity
  spin S = 2
  coefficients = c_1^(2), c_2^(2), c_3^(2)
  combinations = c_plus, c_minus
  unitarity bound = s^4/(2*pi) * (abs(c_plus) + 1.4*abs(c_minus)) <= 1
```

The important point is that both involve `s^4`, but they are not the same axis.
The source basis is a four-graviton Riemann^4-type gravity sector; engine `g_8`
is a matter forward-limit moment joined to `g_4` and `g_6`.

## Interpretation

Bresciani v2 is useful, but not as a G8 sidecar. It supplies a source-backed
partial-wave/unitarity formalism for eight-derivative four-graviton operators.
The correct treatment is to preserve it as a possible gravity Riemann^4/R4 axis
extension, not force it into the existing matter `g_8` coordinate.

The original G8 sidecar remains unsatisfied.
