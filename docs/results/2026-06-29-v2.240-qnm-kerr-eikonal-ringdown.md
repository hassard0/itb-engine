# v2.240 - The eikonal Kerr ringdown from the photon orbit: how spin sets the QNM frequency

## Question

The Kerr strong-field thread (v2.239) gave the spin-dependent photon orbits. How does that feed the
RINGDOWN -- the spin dependence of the QNM frequency that LIGO/Virgo actually fits to measure the
final black hole's spin? Connect v2.239's photon orbits back to the ringdown, in the eikonal limit.

## Result

In the eikonal (large-`l`) limit a Kerr QNM is governed by the corresponding photon orbit (Yang et
al. 2012, the Kerr generalization of v2.229). For the dominant co-rotating `l=m` modes the relevant
orbit is the equatorial photon orbit, and `omega_R ~ (l+1/2) Omega_ph(a)` with
`Omega_ph(a) = 1/(r_ph(a)^{3/2} +/- a)` (M=1):

```text
  a        Omega_ph (pro/retro)     eikonal omega_R, l=2 (pro/retro)
  0.000     0.19245 / 0.19245        0.4811 / 0.4811
  0.300     0.21905 / 0.17320        0.5476 / 0.4330
  0.500     0.24412 / 0.16292        0.6103 / 0.4073
  0.700     0.28115 / 0.15407        0.7029 / 0.3852
  0.900     0.35157 / 0.14636        0.8789 / 0.3659
  0.998     0.47373 / 0.14293        1.1843 / 0.3573
```

**The ringdown frequency tracks the equatorial photon-orbit frequency.** It reproduces Schwarzschild
at `a=0` (`Omega_ph = 1/(3 sqrt3)`) and SPLITS under spin: the prograde branch RISES monotonically to
the extremal `Omega_ph = 0.5` (the per-`(l+1/2)` ringdown frequency ~2.6x the Schwarzschild value),
while the retrograde branch FALLS to ~0.143. **So a higher-spin (prograde) remnant rings at a HIGHER
frequency** -- this is the spin dependence LIGO/Virgo fits to measure the final black hole's spin from
the dominant ringdown mode, and it is the photon-orbit origin (v2.239) of that measurement. The
single Schwarzschild shadow<->ringdown locking (v2.238) is now two distinct prograde/retrograde
frequencies.

## What this establishes

Closes the loop from the Kerr geodesic observables (v2.239) back to the ringdown thread: the
spin-dependent ringdown frequency -- the LIGO spin-measurement observable -- emerges in closed form
from the equatorial photon orbit, reproducing Schwarzschild and the correct monotonic spin trend
WITHOUT needing the deferred Teukolsky/Leaver solver. It unifies the geodesic and ringdown halves of
the strong-field program under the photon orbit.

## Honest scope

Eikonal (large-`l`), equatorial co-rotating (`l=m`) modes, exact Kerr photon orbits. The eikonal
OVERESTIMATES the `l=2` frequency by the usual `O(1/l)` (~22% at `a=0`, v2.229) -- the value is the
large-`l` LIMIT and the SPIN TREND, not a precise `omega_220(a)` (that needs the Teukolsky/Leaver
solver, the deferred effort). The damping follows the same orbit's Lyapunov rate (computed for
Schwarzschild in v2.229; the Kerr equatorial Lyapunov exponent is the natural extension, not done
here). This connects v2.239's photon orbits to the ringdown frequency; it is not a new bound.
Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
