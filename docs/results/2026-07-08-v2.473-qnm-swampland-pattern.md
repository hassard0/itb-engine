# v2.473 - the swampland pattern: the candidate's aggressive-conjecture violations ARE its observable predictions

v2.472 found the candidate violates the TCC (r ≈ 0.0037 is ~28 orders above its ceiling). This cycle adds a
**second tension** and the **pattern** behind both.

## Second tension — the axion-WGC

The candidate's dark-energy axion has f_a ~ M_Pl (v2.461) and a dark-energy-scale potential V ~ (meV)⁴, requiring an
instanton action S ~ 4·ln(M_Pl/meV) ~ **276** (V ~ M_Pl⁴ e⁻ˢ). But the axion Weak Gravity Conjecture wants a light
instanton, S·f_a ≲ M_Pl → **S ≲ 1** for f_a ~ M_Pl. So the DE axion needs S ~ 276 where the axion-WGC wants S ≲ 1 —
a **~276× violation** (the known quintessence-axion vs axion-WGC tension).

## The pattern

```text
  SATISFIES structural:  refined-dS, AdS-distance, ESC, WGC-for-states (BH decay), species-scale
  VIOLATES aggressive:   TCC  <-> inflation r ~ 0.0037 (LiteBIRD)
                         axion-WGC <-> dynamical dark energy, w > -1 (DESI)
```

The two conjectures the candidate **violates** are exactly the ones that would **forbid its two observable
predictions**: the TCC forbids observable inflationary tensors, the axion-WGC forbids an ultralight
super-Planckian-action axion. So **the candidate's observable predictions ARE its swampland-aggressive-bound
violations** — confirming them (a LiteBIRD r-detection, a DESI dynamical-w detection) would **disfavor the
aggressive conjectures, not the candidate**. The candidate makes a coherent bet — the aggressive bounds are too
strong — and its two near-term observables are exactly the arbiters. This turns the "non-uniform
swampland-consistency" of v2.472 from a blemish into a sharp, testable stance: **observable quantum-gravity
predictions live precisely at the aggressive-swampland edge** — which is *why* they're observable at all (an
unobservable-QG world is the one where the TCC and axion-WGC both hold). 5/5 checks pass.

## Honest scope

A second tension from standard estimates plus an interpretive **pattern**, not a theorem. Both tensions are
**class-level** (any observable-r inflation violates the TCC; any ultralight f ~ M_Pl quintessence axion violates
the axion-WGC — inherited, not uniquely generated). The aggressive conjectures are **conjectural and contested**
(argued to be too strong), so "violates" is tension-with-a-conjecture, not a proof the candidate is wrong — a
coherent bet they're too strong. The axion-WGC form S·f ≲ M_Pl has O(1) variants; S ~ 276 is the standard DE-scale
estimate. The pattern is a genuine clean observation but an **interpretation**: it holds because both aggressive
conjectures are, by construction, bounds forbidding the observable regime — so any candidate *with* observable QG
predictions shows the same alignment, which is itself the point.
