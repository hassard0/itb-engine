# v2.243 - Black-hole superradiance: observed spins as an ultralight-boson detector

## Question

A fresh BSM-relevant thread, reconnecting to the repo's new-physics-constraint purpose. A massive
bosonic field around a SPINNING black hole is amplified (superradiance) when `omega < m Omega_H`,
and the amplified cloud extracts the hole's spin. So an observed, rapidly-spinning black hole that
has NOT been spun down EXCLUDES the ultralight bosons that would have grown a cloud. What boson-mass
windows do observed spins probe?

## Result

Kerr horizon angular velocity (`G=c=1`, `a* = a/M`), `Omega_H = a*/(2(1+sqrt(1-a*^2)))`:

```text
  a*       Omega_H     alpha_max(m=1) = M*mu_max
  0.000    0.00000      0.00000
  0.500    0.13397      0.13397
  0.900    0.31339      0.31339
  0.998    0.46933      0.46933
  1.000    0.50000      0.50000   <- extremal limit Omega_H = 1/2
```

The superradiance condition `mu < m Omega_H / M` gives the max superradiant boson as the
gravitational fine-structure constant `alpha_max = m Omega_H`. Converting to physical mass
(`mu = alpha * hbar c / (G M / c^2)`), a fast-spinning hole (`a* = 0.9`, `alpha_max ~ 0.31`)
excludes:

```text
  system                        mu_max (eV)
  stellar-mass (10 Msun)        4.2e-12
  GW150914 remnant (~62 Msun)   6.8e-13
  SMBH 1e6 Msun                 4.2e-17
  M87* (6.5e9 Msun)             6.4e-21
```

**Black-hole spin is an ultralight-boson detector.** Observed rapidly-spinning holes across the mass
spectrum exclude bosons in windows no laboratory reaches -- from `~1e-12 eV` (stellar) down to
`~1e-21 eV` (supermassive) -- spanning the QCD axion, fuzzy-dark-matter (`~1e-22 eV`), and
dark-photon ranges. A measured high spin where superradiance WOULD have spun the hole down excludes
that boson mass: the "Regge-plane" gap.

## What this establishes

A self-contained, verifiable reconstruction of a genuine BSM/quantum-gravity probe -- black-hole
superradiance turning spin measurements into ultralight-boson constraints -- reconnecting the
strong-field black-hole work to the repo's new-physics-constraint purpose. It derives the exact Kerr
superradiance condition and the mass windows from first principles (the `Omega_H -> 1/2` extremal
limit verified), and identifies the boson-mass ranges (`1e-12` to `1e-21 eV`) where observed spins
do the constraining.

## Honest scope

The superradiance CONDITION (`mu < m Omega_H/M`) and `Omega_H(a*)` are EXACT Kerr results (verified).
The actual EXCLUSION additionally requires the instability GROWTH TIME to beat the hole's
spin-down/age timescale -- the Detweiler rate scales as `alpha^{4l+5}` (`~alpha^9` for the dominant
`l=m=1` mode) and peaks near `alpha ~ 0.4`, so the real bound is a window in `(mass, spin)`, not the
full condition; that timescale calculation (and the cloud's continuous-GW signature) is the detailed
step, not done here. The mass estimates use a representative `a*=0.9`; published bounds fold in the
measured spin posteriors and ages. This is an order-of-magnitude reconstruction of a real probe, not
a new bound. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
