# v1.62 — The realism corrections didn't tweak the picture; they created it

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan.

---

## What this checks

How much did the v1.23–25 corrections (convex-hull RFC, geometric BNOSSW) change
each framework's verdict? We compute prefactor robustness for all 11 frameworks
on the **original** stack (miscast `matter_product` RFC + harmonic BNOSSW) vs the
**corrected** stack, and flag out-of-scope frameworks via `scope.py`.

## The headline

**On the original stack, every framework reads 0%.** The miscast RFC
(g_4·g_6 − g_R2 − γ·g_R2²) excludes essentially everything for any γ>0 (the v1.23
F1 artifact) — a uniform, useless false verdict. (Pure GR is the lone exception:
trivially feasible at the origin, where all couplings vanish and RFC is inert.)

**The corrected stack produces the entire discriminating picture** — the spread
from discovered_novel (77%) and AS (46%) down through CDT (33%), string (18%),
the parity branch (9%), GFT (1%), and LQG (0%).

So the realism corrections were not a refinement of an already-working engine —
**they were the difference between an engine that excludes everything
indiscriminately and one that discriminates frameworks at all.** The whole
framework-discrimination program (which theories survive, the LQG verdict, the
discovered branches, the 2030 forecast) rests entirely on the v1.23 RFC fix.
This retroactively validates the realism audit as load-bearing, not cosmetic.

## Cross-checks and caveats

- **LQG: 0% → 0%.** Robustly excluded either way — by the artifact RFC originally,
  by the real constraints (forward positivity, parity, complexity) in the
  corrected stack. Same verdict, legitimate reason only in the corrected version.
- **Hořava–Lifshitz, Causal Set:** flagged OUT OF SCOPE (Lorentz-violating /
  non-local, v1.59); their numbers (0% and 42%) are not meaningful regardless of
  stack — included only for completeness, with the flag.
- Robustness here uses the full corrected knob set (now 9 prefactors incl. the
  v1.61 CEMZ/EFThedron) sampled over their plausible boxes; the original-stack
  run just swaps the RFC form and BNOSSW mean. The dramatic 0%→spread shift is
  driven by the RFC form (the dominant artifact).
