# v1.88 — The minimum decisive experiment set: what is the smallest experiment program that solves quantum gravity?

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan.

---

## The question, made rigorous

Second of the unique meta-experiments. The engine has *observables* with Jacobians
— each says how a coefficient combination would show up in a measurement. So we can
ask the mission question quantitatively: **what is the smallest set of measurements
whose joint Fisher information pins all 8 Wilson coefficients to a unique point?** A
D-optimal experimental design over the engine's own observables.

## Method

At a representative consistent point (the data-driven EFT), each observable o gives
a Jacobian J_o = d(observable)/d(coefficients); its Fisher contribution is
F_o = J_oᵀ J_o / σ_o² (toy σ's, stated). Greedily add the measurement that most
increases log det(F + εI) until the Fisher matrix is **full rank (8 = all directions
constrained)** and the worst parameter uncertainty falls below a toy precision.

## Honest blind spots (and the probes that fix them)

With the engine's *current* observables, **g_8 (the s⁴ matter moment) and g_R3 (cubic
curvature) are touched by NO observable** — the EFT is *unpinnable*. We add two
minimal **design-probe** observables that would measure them:
- `HighScatteringMoment` (~ g_6 s⁴ + g_8 s⁶) — a higher-energy / higher-partial-wave
  scattering measurement, for **g_8**;
- `CubicGravitonAmplitude` (~ g_R3) — a graviton 3-point / GW-nonlinearity probe, for
  **g_R3**.

And `StarobinskyInflation` has a **zero Jacobian** (n_s, r are set by the e-fold
number N, not the couplings) — so the CMB inflation *detection* contributes **nothing**
to pinning the theory.

## Result — 6 measurements solve it

| step | experiment added | Fisher rank | worst σ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | matter scattering (high moment) | 2 | unconstrained |
| 2 | GW/CMB birefringence | 4 | unconstrained |
| 3 | matter scattering (low-s) | 5 | unconstrained |
| 4 | BH extremal entropy | 6 | unconstrained |
| 5 | holographic η/s | 7 | unconstrained |
| 6 | cubic-graviton amplitude | **8** | **0.2 < 0.5** |

**The minimum decisive experiment program is 6 measurements:**

> matter scattering at two energies (pins g_4, g_6, g_8) + GW/CMB birefringence (the
> two parity couplings) + BH extremal entropy (g_C) + holographic η/s (g_R2) + a
> cubic-graviton amplitude (g_R3).

Rank climbs 2→4→5→6→7→8; the parameter volume stays unbounded in the unconstrained
directions until the *6th* experiment closes the last one, at which point the worst
uncertainty collapses below precision. The information-accrual curve and the
observable×coefficient coverage matrix are in `min_experiment_set.png`.

## What's surprising (and honest)

- **The famous detections are not in the minimal set.** Inflation contributes zero
  (degenerate, zero Jacobian). And **sub-mm gravity and gravitational-entanglement
  (GIE) are *redundant*** — they measure g_R2, which η/s already pins, so the greedy
  drops them. (Caveat: η/s is a *holographic-dual* statement, not a lab measurement;
  the *practical* minimal set would use sub-mm for g_R2. The information-theoretic and
  the practical "minimum" can differ — worth stating.)
- **The sloppiest direction is a matter-sector combination** (≈ −0.63 g_4 + 0.62 g_6
  − 0.28 g_8) — the same collective matter degeneracy v1.73's census found as the
  stiff/sloppy axis. It is the last to be pinned and needs *two* scattering energies
  to break.
- **Two coefficients have no current probe** (g_8, g_R3): without a high scattering
  moment and a cubic-graviton/GW-nonlinearity measurement, the consistent EFT cannot
  be uniquely determined — a genuine experimental blind spot the program should flag.

## Interpretation

The answer to "what is the minimum experiment program that solves quantum gravity"
(within this toy precision and observable set): **six measurements**, spanning the
matter (×2), parity, black-hole, holographic, and cubic-curvature sectors — and
*not* including the headline inflation/sub-mm detections in the information-minimal
set. The deepest practical message is the **two blind spots**: the cubic-curvature
coupling g_R3 and the s⁴ matter moment g_8 currently have no observable, so closing
the program requires inventing those two probes.

## Headline

**Six measurements pin the consistent quantum-gravity EFT** — two scattering
energies, GW/CMB birefringence, BH entropy, η/s, and a cubic-graviton amplitude.
Inflation (a real detection) contributes *nothing*; sub-mm is *redundant* for
pinning; and g_8 and g_R3 are blind spots with no current probe. The minimum
experiment program is small, but two of its six legs don't exist yet.
