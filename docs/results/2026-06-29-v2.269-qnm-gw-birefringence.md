## Result

Parity-violating (gravitational Chern-Simons / Pontryagin) gravity treats the two graviton helicities
differently as they propagate. The right/left circular polarizations
`e_{R,L} = (e_plus +/- i e_cross)/sqrt(2)` are exact **helicity +/-2 eigenstates** (verified: a
rotation by `psi` about the line of sight multiplies them by `exp(-/+ 2 i psi)`):

```text
  helicity eigenvalue R = +0.7385 - 0.6743 i   (= exp(-2 i * 0.37), matched to 1e-12)
  e_R, e_L orthonormal:  <e_R,e_L> = 0,  <e_R,e_R> = 2
```

Amplitude birefringence amplifies one and damps the other, `h_{R,L} = h^GR exp(+/- zeta)` with
`zeta ~ kappa k D` (`k = 2 pi f / c`) growing linearly with frequency and distance. The clean
signature is a propagation-induced net circular polarization on an intrinsically **linear** (edge-on,
`Stokes V = 0` in GR) source:

```text
  V = tanh(2 zeta) ,   chromatic (zeta ~ f):
    f =  35 Hz   zeta = 9.1e-9    induced V = 1.8e-8
    f = 100 Hz   zeta = 2.6e-8    induced V = 5.2e-8
    f = 250 Hz   zeta = 6.5e-8    induced V = 1.3e-7
  GR (parity-conserving) limit: induced V = 0 exactly
```

The induced circular polarization is **nonzero only if gravity violates parity** and is **chromatic**
(grows with frequency) -- which distinguishes it from an achromatic source effect or an
inclination-driven intrinsic circular polarization. All 5/5 consistency checks pass.

## Honest scope

The helicity-eigenstate result and the `V = tanh(2 zeta)` formula are EXACT for the model. The
amplitude-birefringence form `zeta ~ kappa k D` is the standard LEADING Chern-Simons parametrization
(Alexander-Yunes); the actual `zeta` is an integral of the CS scalar's evolution (the time-derivative
of the coupling) along the line of sight, so `kappa` here is a representative effective coupling in
illustrative units, NOT a calibrated physical value -- the representative `kappa` bound is an
illustration of how a `V < 0.1` measurement maps to a coupling bound, not a re-derivation of the
published LVC limit. Real sources have an intrinsic inclination-dependent circular polarization that
must be modelled to extract the propagation part (the chromaticity is the discriminant). A
GW-propagation / parity-test result, not an engine constraint refit.
