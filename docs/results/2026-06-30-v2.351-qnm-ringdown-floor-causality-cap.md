# v2.351 - A CP-even cross-sector cap: causality bounds the ringdown floor by the leading matter coupling

## Question

The CP-even companion to v2.350 (which routed a parity observable to the parity-even sector). Can two exact
curvature-sector constraints chain into a corollary that bounds the ringdown floor -- and does it, with
v2.349, fully bracket the guaranteed ringdown deviation?

## Result (yes -- causality caps the floor at kappa^2 * g_4)

```text
  moment tower:      g_R3^2 <= g_R2 * g_R4    =>   g_R4 >= g_R3^2 / g_R2     (the floor)
  CEMZ causality:    |g_R3| <= kappa*sqrt(g_4*g_R2)  =>  g_R3^2 <= kappa^2 g_4 g_R2
  divide by g_R2:    g_R4_floor = g_R3^2/g_R2  <=  kappa^2 * g_4   (kappa = 0.8, kappa^2 = 0.64)

  constructed floor  0.042  <= cap 0.339   (saturation 12%)
  family floor min/max:  0.000 / 0.165     max saturation of cap: 0.45
```

Dividing the CEMZ bound by `g_R2` caps the moment-tower floor itself: `g_R4_floor <= kappa^2 * g_4`. So
**causality limits how large the guaranteed-minimum ringdown deviation can be**, tying its maximum to the
leading matter coupling `g_4`. The engine confirms the analytic cap across the whole consistent+observed
family (every member's floor stays below `kappa^2 g_4`), reaching at most ~45% of the cap where CEMZ is most
nearly saturated. All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

This **completes the ringdown-floor picture**. Combining with v2.349 (the floor collapses to 0 where
`g_R3 -> 0`), the guaranteed ringdown floor is fully bracketed:

```text
  0  <=  g_R4_floor  <=  kappa^2 * g_4
```

-- vanishing on the small-`g_R3` edge and capped by causality x matter on the other. The ringdown floor is
therefore **not a free magnitude**: it is pinned between zero and a causality-set, matter-scaled ceiling.
For the constructed theory the floor (0.042) sits at just 12% of its causality cap (0.339), so the center is
far from the causality boundary in the ringdown sector -- consistent with the v2.339 causality headroom. This
is the CP-even, data-independent companion to v2.350's parity-routed cross-sector bound: both are exact
chains of two engine constraints that bridge sectors (there, parity data -> parity-even matter x curvature;
here, causality -> curvature ringdown floor <- matter coupling).

## Honest scope

The cap is **exact algebra** (dividing CEMZ by `g_R2`, no approximation), and the feasible family respecting
it is genuine confirmation -- the cap cannot be violated by any feasible point since it follows analytically
from CEMZ. But the inputs are toy-basis encodings: the CEMZ form + its prefactor `kappa = cemz_kappa`
(default 0.8, a v2.345-*slack* prefactor) and the moment-tower form (no prefactor). So the cap coefficient
(`kappa^2 = 0.64`) scales as `kappa^2` -- an O(1) change in the causality prefactor moves it -- though kappa
was found slack for feasibility (v2.345). The `g_R4 -> ringdown-shift` map is rank-1 schematic (v2.336), so
"ringdown floor" is the `g_R4` lower bound, not a sourced frequency shift. This is a CP-even,
data-independent statement (causality + the curvature moment tower only, no parity data). The family
saturation maximum is sampler-dependent, but the cap itself is analytic. Toy basis, O(1) prefactors.
