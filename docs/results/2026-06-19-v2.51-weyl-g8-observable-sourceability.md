# v2.51 - Weyl/g8 observable sourceability: source-rich, measurement-blocked

## Question

v2.50 confirmed a robust non-tower frontier: `g_C` / Weyl^2 is the fattest raw island direction and `g_8` is the next single-coefficient direction. The open question is whether either axis has enough source-backed observable structure to promote an internal cut into a real framework discriminator.

This audit asks, for each candidate route:

- Is the theory basis source-backed?
- Does an implemented observable or constraint actually touch the target axis?
- Is there an external numerical measurement or measurement program ready to apply as a cut?
- Is the route claim-ready?

## Interpretation

The `g_C/g_8` frontier is not empty rhetoric. Both axes have source-backed theoretical structure. But the audit finds zero routes that currently provide an external numerical measurement that can be applied as a framework-level cut.

The split is useful:

- `g_C` is source-rich but mostly structural or holographic-proxy based. The conformal-collider wedge is already in the stack as a consistency bound, and complexity / black-hole entropy are not direct measurements of a framework's `g_C`.
- `g_8` has the clearest measurement concept through high-moment or partial-wave information, but the engine route remains an internal design probe without a sourced measurement program.

This means v2.50 remains an experiment-design frontier, not a solution to quantum gravity.
