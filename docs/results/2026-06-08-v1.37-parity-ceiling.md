## Context

(v1.35: pinned & observationally stiff). So this cycle pins its ceiling
precisely: for each cubic coupling g_R3 on a fine grid, maximize
|g_R2_parity| + |g_R3_parity| over the corrected stack with **30 restarts/point**
(vs v1.31's 6), and identify the binding constraint at each maximum.

Three precise facts (binding-constraint histogram across the ceilings:
anomaly_inflow 36, dispersion_tower 6, scalar_convexity 5, complexity 3):

1. **The parity ceiling is ≈ 0.14, and roughly FLAT** across the window
   g_R3 ∈ [0.04, 0.22] — **not** monotone-increasing as g_R3 → 0. This refines
   v1.31: consistent parity violation does **not** require *strong* cubic
   suppression — only g_R3 ≲ 0.22. (LQG's g_R3 = 0.30 sits just *outside* the
   window — the real reason LQG can't carry its parity consistently.)

2. **Anomaly inflow sets the ceiling.** It is the binding constraint at most of
   the maxima. The bound g_R2_parity² + 2·g_R3_parity² ≤ ρ·g_4·g_R2 directly caps
   parity content, so the ceiling is ≈ √(ρ·g_4·g_R2) ≈ √(0.06·0.65·0.40) ≈ 0.12 —
   consistent with the observed ≈ 0.14.

3. **The parity window closes at both ends.** Below g_R3 ≈ 0.04 the parity sector
   collapses to zero (the cubic parity coupling g_R3_parity is tied to the cubic
   sector by 't Hooft anomaly matching, so a vanishing cubic starves it), and
   above g_R3 ≈ 0.22 no parity-violating consistent theory exists. Parity
   violation lives in a bounded cubic window, peaked near g_R3 ≈ 0.08–0.16.

## Consequence — a parity measurement would constrain the anomaly coefficient ρ

Because the ceiling is √(ρ·g_4·g_R2)-limited, the maximum gravitational parity
violation the consistent region permits is set by the **anomaly-inflow
coefficient ρ** (toy value 0.06). This closes a loop with the realism program: a
measured gravitational parity violation would not just flag new physics, it would
**pin ρ** — turning the engine's most uncertain swampland coefficient into an
experimental observable. Conversely, a null parity result at sensitivity ≪ 0.14
would push ρ down and shrink the parity window toward the catalogued
(parity-conserving) frameworks.

## Honest limitations

- The ceiling value (≈0.14) scales with √ρ and √(g_4·g_R2); all three are toy. The
  robust, structure-level content is: (a) anomaly inflow is the ceiling-setting
  constraint; (b) the parity window is bounded in g_R3 (roughly [0.04, 0.22]) with
  LQG's cubic coupling just outside it; (c) the ceiling is flat, not peaked at
  g_R3 → 0 (correcting v1.31's impression).
- Optimizer noise remains at the per-point level (±0.01); the window edges
  (0.04, 0.22) are approximate.
