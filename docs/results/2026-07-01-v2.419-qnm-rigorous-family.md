## What this establishes

Rigor determines a **family**, not the candidate. The rigorous-core feasible set is ~5–6× looser per dimension
than the full-stack island, and within it the candidate's defining features are **not forced** — g_R2 can be ~0
(no curvature correction), parity can be 0 (parity-conserving), g_8 can be large. The full stack — adding the
swampland/observable tiers and the cosmic-birefringence datum — collapses this to the tight island that pins the
candidate. So the honest scope of "what rigor establishes about quantum gravity" is bounded and explicit: the
source-exact core delivers the framework **exclusions** (LQG) and the amplitude **structure** (positivity
relations, the matter × cubic-curvature forcing of v2.417 *when those sectors are present*), but leaves a broad
family and does **not** select the specific candidate. The candidate's coordinates are picked out by the **data**
plus the swampland/observable model layers. **Rigor determines the rules and the boundaries; data determines the
point.** This is the correct framing of the whole program — the single-candidate headline (v2.406) is a
rigor+data+model result, exactly as the rigor ledger (v2.415) tiered it, now shown at the level of the feasible
set's geometry.

## Honest scope

Ranges are from a random-walk exploration (seeded at the candidate), so they're lower bounds on the true feasible
extent — the rigorous family is *at least* this broad. The "g_R2 can be ~0 / parity can be 0" statements are the
robust qualitative content; exact maxima (e.g. g_8 to ~9) are walk-dependent and not claimed precisely. This is
the honest *complement* to the de-toying arc, **not a weakening**: the arc's rigorous results (LQG excluded,
matter dominance's ceiling, the forcing lattice) all still hold — they are statements about *exclusions* and
*conditional* structure, which is exactly what a family-carving core provides; they were never claims that rigor
uniquely picks the candidate.
