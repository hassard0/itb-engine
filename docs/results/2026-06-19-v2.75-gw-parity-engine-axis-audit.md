## Required Adapter Contract

Promotion would require all of:

- source-backed operator identity from `beta_1_0` to an engine axis;
- dimensionless normalization from `kappa_Gpc_inv` to a Wilson coordinate;
- canonical helicity sign that reconciles Ng and engine conventions;
- frequency-distance transfer from `D_C_Gpc*f/100Hz` to the engine omega basis;
- framework-exclusion math in the mapped engine basis.

## Interpretation

The engine has parity coordinates, but they are not source-normalized coordinates
for the reproduced Ng likelihood. The internal sign convention is useful for
toy constraints; it is not enough to claim a source-backed map from Ng
`kappa` or Jenks `beta_1_0`.

This closes the current direct-promotion attempt. The Ng packet remains valuable
as source-native likelihood material, but a real operator-normalization source is
required before it can affect framework verdicts.
