# v2.247 - Black-hole echoes: the ringdown delay that probes horizon-scale new physics

## Question

A fresh QG-at-the-horizon thread. If the event horizon is replaced by a quantum-corrected REFLECTIVE
surface at `r = 2M(1+epsilon)` -- a firewall, fuzzball, gravastar, or any exotic compact object (ECO)
motivated by the information paradox -- the ringdown is followed by a train of ECHOES. What is the
echo delay, and how sensitively does it depend on where the surface sits?

## Result

Waves bounce between the surface and the photon-sphere barrier; the echo delay is the round-trip
light-crossing time in the tortoise coordinate `r*(r) = r + 2M ln(r/2M - 1)`:

```text
Delta t_echo = 2 [ r*(3M) - r*(2M(1+epsilon)) ] ~ -4M ln(epsilon)   (leading)

  epsilon    echo delay (M)   -4 ln(eps)
  1e-04        36.07            36.84
  1e-10        91.33            92.10
  1e-20       183.43           184.21
  1e-40       367.64           368.41
```

Because `r*(surface) = 2M(1+epsilon) + 2M ln(epsilon)` diverges only LOGARITHMICALLY as the surface
approaches the horizon, the delay is only weakly sensitive to `epsilon`: 36 orders of magnitude
(`1e-4` to `1e-40`) change it by ~10x. A Planck-scale surface (`epsilon ~ 1e-40`) gives `Delta t ~
368 M`:

```text
  30 Msun stellar BH        54 ms
  GW150914 remnant          112 ms
  Sgr A* (4.3e6 Msun)       ~2.2 hours
```

**The echo delay is a clean, weakly-model-dependent probe of horizon-scale new physics.** Detecting
(or bounding) post-ringdown echoes at this delay tests whether the remnant has a true horizon or a
quantum-corrected surface -- the information-paradox question made observational, with the
~tens-of-ms delay (for stellar holes) being exactly what the LIGO/Virgo echo searches target. The
logarithmic sensitivity means even Planck-scale structure does not push the echoes out of reach.

## What this establishes

A self-contained, verifiable reconstruction of the black-hole echo delay -- the leading observable in
horizon-scale tests of quantum gravity -- from the repo's tortoise-coordinate machinery (v2.210),
with the key logarithmic sensitivity made explicit and the physical delays mapped onto the LIGO/LISA
regimes. It extends the "is it really a black hole?" suite (the tidal Love number v2.235, the no-hair
test, the shadow) with the horizon-structure channel.

## Honest scope

This is the geometric-optics ECHO DELAY (the leading round-trip light-crossing time), exact for the
Schwarzschild tortoise coordinate. The full echo WAVEFORM -- amplitudes, the surface reflectivity,
the damping/frequency content of successive echoes, and the spinning (Kerr) case -- requires solving
the wave equation with the modified inner boundary condition (not done here). The `epsilon <->
Planck-scale` relation is convention-dependent (coordinate vs proper distance differ by O(1) in the
log), so the physical delays are order-of-magnitude. Echoes remain a debated/unconfirmed signal in
the data. Reconstruction of a real QG-at-the-horizon observable, not a detection claim. Parity-odd
`g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
