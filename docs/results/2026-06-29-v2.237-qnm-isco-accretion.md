# v2.237 - The ISCO and accretion efficiency: the matter-orbit companion to the photon sphere

## Question

Where the photon sphere (`r = 3M`) governs light (ringdown, shadow), the innermost stable circular
orbit (ISCO, `r = 6M`) governs MATTER: it is the inner edge of a thin accretion disk and the binding
energy released down to it sets the radiative efficiency. Can the repo's geodesic machinery
reproduce the ISCO observables from first principles, and how does a deformation shift this
multi-messenger probe?

## Result

Timelike circular geodesics have energy per unit mass `E(r) = sqrt(2 f^2 / (2f - r f'))`; the ISCO is
the marginally stable orbit (the MINIMUM of `E(r)`), the efficiency is `eta = 1 - E_ISCO`, and the
orbital frequency is `Omega = sqrt(f'/(2r))`. Schwarzschild, reproduced exactly:

```text
  r_ISCO   = 6.00000   (= 6M)
  E_ISCO   = 0.942809  (= sqrt(8/9))
  eta      = 0.05719   (= 1 - sqrt(8/9) ~ 5.72% radiative efficiency)
  Omega    = 0.068041  (= 1/(6 sqrt6 M))
```

Deformation sensitivities (`f = 1 - 2/r + eps/r^k`):

```text
  k     dr_ISCO/d eps     d eta/d eps     d Omega/d eps
  3       -0.5273          +0.00436         +0.00755
  4       -0.1528          +0.00109         +0.00228
```

A near-horizon / higher-curvature deformation SHRINKS the ISCO, RAISING both the accretion
efficiency and the orbital frequency; a more localized (`k=4`) bump does the same but weaker.
Because the ISCO is the inner accretion-disk edge (EM: X-ray continuum fitting / EHT) AND the
inspiral-to-merger transition frequency (GW), this is a **multi-messenger probe** -- the matter-orbit
companion to the photon-sphere<->shadow link (v2.230): a deformation moves the disk efficiency and
the merger frequency coherently.

## What this establishes

A clean, self-contained reproduction of the ISCO observables (the famous 5.72% Schwarzschild
radiative efficiency, the merger-onset frequency) from the geodesic effective potential, plus the
deformation sensitivities -- adding the matter-orbit channel to the strong-field-test sub-program
(photon sphere / shadow / ringdown / tidal). It rounds out the geodesic "anatomy" of the black hole:
horizon (`2M`), photon sphere (`3M`, light), ISCO (`6M`, matter), each an observable whose shift
under a deformation is a test.

## Honest scope

Schwarzschild baseline (exact) + linear deformation response, equatorial circular timelike geodesics
of a static spherical metric in the `g_tt g_rr = -1` gauge. KERR (spin) shifts the ISCO strongly
(prograde down to `r = M`, efficiency up to ~42%) and breaks sphericity -- not included. The
deformation profile (`eps/r^k`) is illustrative, not a derived QG metric; the efficiency assumes a
standard thin disk radiating the binding energy. This frames a multi-messenger test (a deformation
correlates disk efficiency and merger frequency), not a coupling bound. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays
dark (v2.209).
