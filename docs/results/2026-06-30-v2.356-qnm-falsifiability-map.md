independent channels. What is the complete refutation map -- the single most decision-relevant artifact for a
new theory -- and does each falsification threshold hold up when re-computed from the engine?

## What this establishes

The theory **cannot be dismissed or confirmed in one stroke**: the three channels probe different physics
(CMB/GW parity, black-hole ringdown, laboratory fifth-force) with different data-dependence. The
decision-relevant bottom line: the most *testable* prediction (parity) is fully data-contingent and already
under near-term pressure, while the two *data-independent-core* predictions (ringdown, screening) are robust
but harder to reach. So a skeptic's strongest line is the parity channel's data-dependence (v2.329), and the
theory's strongest claim is the screening channel's data-independent core (v2.355). This complements the
v2.323 robustness-tier ledger with the prediction/refutation view, synthesizing the session's arc.

## Honest scope

This is a **synthesis**, but every threshold is re-computed from the engine here (the parity falsifier by an
infeasibility check, the ringdown bracket by exact arithmetic, the screening factor against the Eot-Wash
cap), not asserted from prior cycles. Each channel carries its own caveats verbatim: parity rests on the
birefringence detection being real (v2.329) and on toy birefringence maps; the ringdown floor magnitude is
rank-1 schematic (v2.336, the deep-research-flagged limitation) and the cap coefficient scales as
`cemz_kappa^2`; the screening cap is an order-of-magnitude Eot-Wash reading and its universality is
data-linked (v2.354/355). "Three independent channels" refers to the OBSERVABLES being distinct, not to
statistical independence of the couplings (they are correlated through the constraints). The map
characterizes the constructed center; family-level statements are in the per-channel cycles. Toy basis, O(1)
prefactors.
