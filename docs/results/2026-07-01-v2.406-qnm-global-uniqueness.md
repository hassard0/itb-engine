strongest form of the program's headline: the swampland-complete carving leaves *essentially one region*, so

conditions, and the data's role is sharp -- it selects one of the two handedness mirror-islands the theory
alone permits. The 38/40 funnel-convergence also explains *why* the constructed max-margin point is well-defined
and stable (v2.361): the basin has a single interior center every descent finds.

## Honest scope

Multi-start greedy descent is a HEURISTIC global search -- strong evidence for a single island (40 independent
random starts all funnel to one cluster, none to a distinct far region), but not a proof: a tiny/narrow-basin
island elsewhere could be missed. So "globally unique" means "no second island found by a broad multi-start
over the O(1) box". The search is confined to the a-priori box (v2.373); islands outside it (larger couplings)
aren't searched -- but that is the physical O(1) range and larger couplings are excluded by scale-rigidity
(v2.390). Couplings are clipped >= 0 (positivity forbids most negatives anyway). The single-handedness result
is the birefringence data breaking the theory-only Z2 (v2.364) -- theory-only would show two mirror islands, so
"single island" is a with-data statement. The 6-coupling base is used (moduli at defaults), so moduli
directions are not part of the island count. Adds no new physical datum; upgrades the uniqueness claim from
framework-exclusion to global-search connectivity. Robust content: a broad multi-start over the O(1) box finds
a single connected feasible island of one handedness with no disconnected alternative -- the candidate is
globally unique (up to moduli) to the strength of a 40-start search.
