# v2.355 - Is the screening mandate data-independent? Partly: the constructed SECTOR mandates it; the DATA selects the sector

## Question

v2.354 showed the consistent+observed (birefringence-on) region is unscreened-EMPTY, so the theory mandates
a screened R^2 scalaron. Its own scope flagged the question: is that robust, or contingent on the
cosmic-birefringence detection like the parity headline?

## Result (a clean split -- partly robust)

```text
(A) constructed sector (g_4,g_6,g_8,g_R3), parity = 0, birefringence OFF, small g_R2:
      g_R2=0.0616: [graviton_forward_positivity, cross_sector_efthedron, anomaly_cancellation]
      g_R2=0.05  : [graviton_forward_positivity, cross_sector_efthedron, anomaly_cancellation]
      g_R2=0.04  : [graviton_forward_positivity, cross_sector_efthedron, anomaly_cancellation]
      g_R2=0.03  : [graviton_forward_positivity, cross_sector_efthedron, anomaly_cancellation]
      -> infeasible via three CP-EVEN, data-independent constraints

(B) release the sector, drop birefringence:
      unscreened feasible point FOUND: g_4~0.42, g_6~0.22, g_8~0.27, g_R2~0.03, g_R3~0.02, g_R2_parity~0.03
      with birefringence ON: unscreened region still EMPTY
```

All 5/5 checks pass. Two separable facts:

- **(A) data-independent core.** Holding the constructed matter+curvature couplings and setting parity = 0
  with birefringence OFF, small `g_R2` (<= the unscreened cap) is still infeasible at every tested value --
  and the cause is exactly three **CP-even** constraints (graviton forward positivity, cross-sector
  EFThedron, anomaly cancellation), no parity or data constraints. So **given the constructed sector**,
  screening is forced with no appeal to the birefringence detection.
- **(B) data-linked universality.** If the matter sector is released and birefringence dropped, an unscreened
  feasible theory **does** exist -- at *smaller* couplings (a genuinely different point) -- whereas with
  birefringence ON the unscreened region stays empty. So the cosmic-birefringence data is what excludes the
  small-coupling unscreened alternative and pins the theory to the screening-mandated constructed sector.

## What this establishes

The screening mandate is **stronger than the parity headline but not absolute**: its *core* is
data-independent (the constructed sector mandates screening by CP-even consistency alone), while its
*universality* (no unscreened theory anywhere) rests on the same birefringence data as everything else. This
is the most robust prediction the program has -- the first with a genuinely data-independent component --
and the honest refinement of v2.354.

## Honest scope

Both branch-existence statements are **empirical** searches: "an unscreened branch exists without
birefringence" is established by exhibiting one feasible point (robust -- a witness), while "with
birefringence the unscreened region is empty" is a not-found result over a modest sample here (8000; v2.354
used 30000) corroborated by the analytic `g_R2` lower bound -- strong evidence, not proof. Part (A) is
near-deterministic (a grid over `g_R2` at the fixed constructed sector) and its data-independence is genuine
(the three CP-even constraints carry no parity/data input). The `g_R2_max ~ 0.063` cap is an
order-of-magnitude Eot-Wash DATA reading (v2.354 scope), and the CP-even constraints carry their own O(1)
prefactors (`graviton_fwd_c`, `efthedron_alpha` -- both v2.345-slack, but they DO shift the exact sector
boundary). So "the constructed sector mandates screening" is robust in structure; the exact couplings where
the CP-even wall sits are prefactor-dependent. Toy basis, O(1) prefactors.
