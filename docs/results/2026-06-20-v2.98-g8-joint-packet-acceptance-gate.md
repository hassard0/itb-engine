# v2.98 - g8 joint-packet acceptance gate: executable route, no real packet

## Question

v2.97 defined joint `g_8 + secondary_axis` targets for the tightest eligible
near-degeneracy. Can the engine now validate a future real joint packet instead
of only describing the target?

## Gate Behavior

The gate accepts correctly shaped future packets for:

- `g_8 + g_C`
- `g_8 + g_R2`

The accepted synthetic fixtures satisfy the v2.97 numeric targets:

| packet | `g_8` total sigma / target | secondary total sigma / target | claim-ready |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `synthetic_ready_joint_g8_g_c_packet` | `0.001118 / <0.002` | `0.031623 / <0.050837` | false |
| `synthetic_ready_joint_g8_g_r2_packet` | `0.001118 / <0.002` | `0.038079 / <0.0629` | false |

They remain non-promoting because they are synthetic fixtures.

The negative controls reject:

| packet | rejection |
|---|---|
| `incomplete_joint_packet` | Missing framework pair, central values, uncertainties, normalizations, projections, likelihood, systematics, shared EFT domain, and discriminator math. |
| `imprecise_joint_packet` | `g_8` and secondary-axis uncertainties both exceed the v2.97 targets. |

## Interpretation

The joint route now has an executable acceptance path. A real external packet
can be evaluated directly against the `g_8 + g_C` or `g_8 + g_R2` requirements,
including:

- source-backed engine-axis normalizations
- joint likelihood or covariance
- closed systematics
- shared EFT-domain bounds
- pair-excluding discriminator math

This still does not solve the physics problem. It removes another in-repo
ambiguity: the next object must be a real joint packet, not another synthetic
fixture or precision-incomplete design seed.
