## Claim Boundary

All six active frontier routes now have executable diagnostic infrastructure or
packet contracts. This is a better research posture, but it is still not a
solution or a framework exclusion. Every route depends on missing real external
evidence, source-owned adapters, or source-backed scale policies.

The top-priority route remains future public R4 shape likelihood ingestion,
because it has the most mature in-repo scoring adapter and only lacks a real
public likelihood/covariance packet.
