# v2.272 - The stochastic gravitational-wave background: a spectral zoo and the inflation gap

## Question

A fresh GW-cosmology probe (opening after the graviton-observable arc v2.266-v2.271). The
superposition of all unresolved GW sources is a stochastic background. How is its origin diagnosed,
what does the NANOGrav nHz detection mean, and can it show us inflation directly?

## Result

The background is characterized by its energy density per log frequency,
`Omega_GW(f) = (2 pi^2 / 3)(f^2 / H_0^2) h_c(f)^2`, and the SPECTRAL INDEX is the discriminant. The
arithmetic linking the characteristic-strain slope `alpha`, the energy-density slope `2 alpha + 2`, and
the pulsar-timing index `gamma = 3 - 2 alpha` is exact (verified), with the invariant
`gamma + Omega_slope = 5`:

```text
  source                       alpha    Omega_GW ~ f^(2a+2)   timing gamma
  SMBH binaries                -0.667   +0.667               4.333  (= 13/3)
  inflation (scale-invariant)  -1.000    0.000 (flat)        5.000
  cosmic strings (plateau)     -1.000    0.000 (flat)        5.000
  phase transition             peaked (not a power law)
```

The NANOGrav 15yr nHz signal (`A ~ 2.4e-15` at `1/yr`) converts to `Omega_GW(1/yr) ~ 8e-9` and sits at
the SMBH-binary prediction (its measured `gamma ~ 3.2` is mildly shallower than the nominal `13/3`, a
live question). The decisive QG point is the **gap**: the primordial inflationary background, fixed by
the tensor-to-scalar ratio via `Omega_GW ~ (1/24) Omega_r r A_s`, is at most `~2.8e-16` (at the current
`r < 0.036`) -- below the PTA, LISA and LIGO sensitivities (`~1e-9` to `1e-13`) by many orders of
magnitude. All 6/6 consistency checks pass.

## What this establishes

A stochastic GW detection is diagnosed by its spectral slope (binaries `f^2/3`, inflation/strings flat,
phase transitions peaked), and -- crucially -- a detection is astrophysical or exotic, **NOT a direct
image of inflation**: the inflationary graviton background is too faint for current and near-future
broadband detectors, so seeing it needs a dedicated future mission. That gap is itself the quantitative
statement of how faint the quantum-gravitational vacuum signal is, tying the SGWB back to the v2.253
inflation/`r` thread and the v2.271 graviton-observable arc.

## Honest scope

The `h_c <-> Omega_GW <-> gamma` conversions are EXACT (standard definitions). The NANOGrav amplitude
(`A ~ 2.4e-15`) and the SMBHB slope are source-backed (NANOGrav 15yr); the measured `gamma ~ 3.2` is
mildly shallower than the nominal `13/3`, which the collaboration flags -- reported honestly, not
tuned. The primordial estimate `Omega_GW ~ (1/24) Omega_r r A_s` is the standard ORDER-OF-MAGNITUDE
result; it omits the detailed transfer function, reheating history and spectral tilt `n_T` (which can
mildly blue-tilt the spectrum), and `Omega_r`, `H_0` are fiducial -- so the `~1e-16` ceiling is
order-of-magnitude, but the conclusion (primordial background far below current detectors) is robust.
Detector sensitivities are representative peak values, not full power-law-integrated curves. A
GW-cosmology / spectral-classification result, not an engine constraint refit.
