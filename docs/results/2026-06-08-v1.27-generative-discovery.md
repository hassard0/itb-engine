# v1.27 — Generative mode: theories not yet on the map, and the experiments that would find them

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core, 4× V100). 500 multistart restarts × 2 searches.
**Builds on:** the corrected v1.26 stack.

---

## Context

Every prior cycle used the engine *defensively*: which catalogued frameworks
(string, AS, LQG, CDT) survive? This cycle inverts it — used *generatively*:
search the feasible region of the corrected constraint stack for consistent
theories that occupy parts of Wilson-coefficient space **no catalogued framework
predicts**, and for each, invent the single measurement that would reveal it.

Method (`experiments/discover.py`): scipy Nelder-Mead multistart that maximizes
either (a) distance from all five known frameworks ("novelty") or (b)
gravitational parity content, subject to an interior feasibility margin
(worst-case margin ≥ 0.01 — so solutions are robustly inside the allowed region,
not boundary-hugging). Each discovery is then stress-tested against
constraint-prefactor uncertainty (feasible fraction over the plausible box), and
its "invented experiment" is the Wilson coefficient whose measurement most
separates it from its nearest known framework.

Honest framing: these are *points in the toy 7-coefficient EFT space* that are
(a) consistent with all encoded constraints and (b) unoccupied by any catalogued
QG program — candidate targets, not finished theories. Their value is as
concrete, falsifiable signposts: "look here; no current framework does, and this
measurement would confirm or kill it."

## Discovery 1 — a robust novel theory: weakly-coupled, suppressed g_8 and cubic curvature

```
g_4 = 0.340   g_6 = 0.117   g_8 = 0.045
g_R2 = 0.136  g_R3 = 0.017  g_R2_parity = 0.021  g_R3_parity = 0.017
worst-case margin +0.0016   distance to nearest framework (AS) 0.331
prefactor robustness: feasible in 76% of the plausible prefactor box
```

This sits a full 0.33 away from every catalogued framework and stays feasible in
**76%** of prefactor space — as robust as the known frameworks. Its character:
a **weakly-coupled** completion with an **anomalously small dimension-8 matter
coupling g_8 (0.045)** and a **near-vanishing cubic curvature g_R3 (0.017)**.
No catalogued framework predicts this corner: string/AS/CDT all carry g_8 ~
0.3–0.4 and g_R3 ~ 0.10–0.15.

**Invented experiment:** a precision measurement of the **dimension-8 matter
coefficient g_8**. It differs by 0.255 from asymptotic safety (the nearest
framework) — the largest separation of any observable. A measurement finding
g_8 ≪ 0.3 with the other couplings moderate would point at this theory and away
from every catalogued framework.

## Discovery 2 — a consistent parity-violating QG exists, but is fragile and must suppress the cubic

```
g_4 = 0.692  g_6 = 0.484  g_8 = 0.345  g_R2 = 0.386  g_R3 = 0.124
g_R2_parity = 0.092   g_R3_parity = -0.036   (parity content 0.128)
worst-case margin +0.0050   prefactor robustness: feasible in 18% of box
```

Every *feasible* catalogued framework is parity-conserving; LQG, the only
parity-violator, is excluded (v1.26, ~2% viable). So: **can a consistent
parity-violating quantum gravity exist at all?** The generative search says
**yes — but conditionally.** The maximally parity-violating feasible theory
carries g_R2_parity = 0.092 (comparable to LQG's 0.08) yet remains consistent —
because it **suppresses the cubic curvature**: g_R2/g_R3 = 3.1, versus LQG's
1.0. This is the structural lesson of v1.24–25 turned into a prediction:

> A consistent parity-violating gravity must keep its cubic curvature coupling
> well below its quadratic one (g_R2/g_R3 ≳ 3). LQG fails precisely because its
> spin-foam dynamics inflate the cubic coupling to g_R3 ≈ g_R2.

But this theory is **fragile** — feasible in only **18%** of prefactor space,
versus 76% for the parity-conserving novel theory and 44% for asymptotic safety.
So parity violation is *allowed* but *disfavoured*: it survives only in a thin
slice of the consistency landscape.

**Invented experiment:** measure the **cubic gravitational parity-violating
coupling g_R3_parity** at precision better than ~0.13 (e.g. frequency-dependent
graviton birefringence in the cubic-curvature sector / high-order GW dispersion).
A non-zero g_R3_parity *with* a suppressed parity-conserving cubic g_R3 would be
the smoking gun for this consistent-parity-violating branch; a null at that
precision closes it.

## Discovery 3 — the allowed region is fragmented into many islands

> **⚠️ Corrected by v1.28.** The "~31 islands" below is an artifact of greedy
> clustering of boundary-hugging optimizer points. Proper diverse-seed +
> segment-connectivity analysis (v1.28) shows the feasible region is essentially
> **one connected sliver** (≤4 straight-line lobes, plausibly 1). See
> `2026-06-08-v1.28-theory-space-phases.md`.

94 robustly-feasible novelty solutions cluster into **~31 islands** at a
coefficient-distance threshold of 0.15. Even allowing for boundary effects
inflating the count, the allowed region is clearly **not a single convex blob**
but a fragmented set of disconnected pockets — consistent with the long-standing
"theory-space phase transitions" idea (notes 2026-05-08). Each island is a
candidate framework class with its own coefficient signature; the catalogued
frameworks occupy only a handful of them.

## Why this matters

The realism program (v1.23–26) was subtractive — it removed false claims and
showed the engine robustly disfavours only LQG. This cycle shows the engine can
also be *additive*: it proposes **specific, robust, currently-unoccupied
theories** and the **specific measurements** that would discriminate them. Two
concrete experimental targets fall out that no framework-comparison study would
have surfaced:

1. **Precision g_8 (dimension-8 matter coupling)** — separates a robust
   weakly-coupled novel branch from all catalogued frameworks.
2. **Cubic gravitational parity violation g_R3_parity** — tests whether the
   thin consistent-parity-violating branch is realized in nature.

## Honest limitations

- Discoveries live in the toy 7-coefficient EFT with placeholder prefactors; the
  *coordinates* of each theory will move once constraints are encoded at
  literature precision. What is robust is the *qualitative* existence of (a) a
  robust novel branch with suppressed g_8/g_R3 and (b) a fragile but allowed
  parity-violating branch with suppressed cubic curvature.
- "Island" counts are sensitive to the clustering threshold and to boundary
  hugging; treat ~31 as "many, not one", not a precise count.
