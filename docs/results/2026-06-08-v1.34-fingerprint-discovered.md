## Context

The engine-discovered theories are now first-class encoders (`DiscoveredNovel`,
`DiscoveredParityViolating`, and new this cycle `DiscoveredHighG8`). This cycle
places all eight theories — 5 catalogued + 3 discovered — in observable space
(sector forward amplitudes: matter, graviton, parity) and asks where the
discovered ones sit relative to known physics.

## Result 1 — the discovered theories' nearest catalogued neighbours

| discovered theory | nearest catalogued framework (observable space) | obs-distance |
|---|---|---|
| `discovered_high_g8` | string tree-EFT | 0.211 |
| `discovered_parity_violating` | **LQG-induced** | 0.352 |
| `discovered_novel` | asymptotic safety | 0.696 |

The most striking entry: **`discovered_parity_violating` is the observational
neighbour of LQG** — both parity-violating, in the same region of observable
space — yet it *satisfies* the full constraint stack that excludes LQG (v1.26),
because it suppresses its cubic curvature (g_R2/g_R3 ≈ 8 vs LQG's 1). In other
words, the engine generatively reconstructed **the consistent theory LQG "should
have been"**: keep LQG's parity violation, drop its cubic-coupling inflation.
That is a concrete, physically-meaningful new candidate — a parity-violating
gravity occupying LQG's observational niche but on the right side of the
consistency constraints.

`discovered_novel` (nearest neighbour AS, but a full 0.70 away) is the genuinely
isolated one — far from every catalogued framework in observable space too, not
just in coefficient space.

## Result 2 — degeneracies and separations

| | pair | obs-distance | discriminating sector |
|---|---|---|---|
| hardest to tell apart | string ↔ CDT | **0.041** | graviton |
| | string ↔ discovered_high_g8 | 0.211 | graviton |
| easiest | Pure GR vs discovered_parity_violating | 2.301 | matter |

The string/CDT near-degeneracy (0.041) reappears at the observable level — the
third independent confirmation (v1.26 survival, v1.30 S/N, here) that these two
cannot be distinguished at toy precision. `discovered_high_g8` is observationally
close to string/CDT in the graviton sector and is separated from them only in the
**matter sector (g_8)** — consistent with it being the high-g8 end of the loose
matter direction (v1.33).

## Takeaway

The three discovered theories are not numerical curiosities: each has a clear
observational identity — a consistent LQG-like parity-violating theory, a
genuinely isolated weakly-coupled theory, and a high-g8 string-like theory — and
each is separated from its catalogued neighbour by a *named sector* (parity,
matter, matter respectively). This is the generative output in its most usable
form: new candidate theories with the measurement that would confirm each.
