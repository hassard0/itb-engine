# v1.77 — The engine ingests real data: the sub-mm bound as the first experimental constraint, and the island self-corrects

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core). Methodology gut-checked with Dr. M. (Pluto).

## A first for the program

Every one of the engine's ~36 constraints has been a **theoretical axiom** —
positivity, causality, holographic entropy, swampland. v1.76 found that the
engine's own central prediction (a dark-energy-scale f(R) scalaron, α = 1/3 at
λ ≈ 80 µm) is **excluded** by Eöt-Wash sub-mm gravity. v1.77 closes that loop: we
turn the experiment into the engine's **first data-sourced constraint** and let the
consistent island self-correct.

`SubmmGravityYukawaBound` (`src/itb/constraints/submm_gravity.py`) encodes the
Eöt-Wash 95% CL exclusion as the single-coefficient cut **g_R2 ≤ 0.063** (the
λ = 50 µm crossing where the predicted α = 1/3 meets the experimental curve), under
the explicit **unscreened** assumption. It is wired into `build_stack` behind an
`include_data` flag (default **False**, so every prior theoretical result and all
prior tests are untouched — suite stays **446 green**); `submm_screened=True` makes
it vacuous.

## The island self-corrects

| | before data (axioms only) | after ingesting sub-mm data |
|---|---|---|
| island center g_R2 | 0.213 | **0.046** |
| scalaron λ | 93 µm (**EXCLUDED**) | **43 µm (allowed)** |
| center inradius | 0.077 | 0.017 (tighter) |
| island volume fraction | 0.61% | **0.012%** |
| fraction of island surviving | — | **1.9%** |

- **The center moves below the exclusion threshold.** The most-robustly-consistent
  EFT jumps from g_R2 = 0.213 (λ = 93 µm, ruled out) to g_R2 = 0.046 (λ = 43 µm,
  *allowed*) — a heavier scalaron whose fifth force is below current sensitivity.
  Full data-center: g_4 = 0.25, g_6 = 0.08, g_8 = 0.28, g_R2 = 0.046, g_R3 = 0.017,
  g_C = 0.08.
- **The island collapses to ~2% of its theoretical volume** (0.61% → 0.012%, over
  200 000 samples). A single sub-mm experiment removes ~98% of the consistent
  region. Dr. M.: not suspicious — a light dark-energy-scale scalar gives a
  long-range force that is "scalpel-sharp" prey for Eöt-Wash unless screened.

## Every toy framework's scalaron is excluded (unscreened)

Under the unscreened assumption, **all** previously-feasible frameworks fall
outside, because each has g_R2 ~ 0.1–0.4 → λ > 50 µm:

| framework | g_R2 | λ (µm) |
|---|---|---|
| emergent_gravity | 0.10 | 64 |
| discovered_novel | 0.136 | 74 |
| asymptotic_safety | 0.15 | 78 |
| string_tree_eft / causal_set | 0.20 | 90 |
| discovered_high_g8 | 0.21 | 92 |
| cdt | 0.22 | 95 |
| discovered_parity_violating | 0.395 | 127 |

This is the honest physical content: **no toy QG framework, if its R² scalaron sits
at the dark-energy scale and is unscreened, survives sub-mm gravity** — they all
need a heavier scalaron, a higher cutoff, or screening. The data-corrected center
(g_R2 = 0.046) sits in a *new*, smaller-g_R2 region none of the toy frameworks
occupy (they were all built with larger g_R2).

## Honest methodology (Dr. M.)

- **Ontology.** Theoretical axioms answer "can a consistent UV completion *exist*?";
  experimental bounds answer "is the vacuum *this* one?". Intersecting them is the
  standard way to map the **"Empirical Swampland"** — legitimate, provided the two
  kinds of constraint are not conflated. We keep them separable (the `include_data`
  flag) precisely so the theoretical island remains a distinct object.
- **Screening is not really binary.** The `screened` flag is a deliberate
  simplification. Dr. M.'s caveat: chameleon/Vainshtein screening is a *non-linear,
  density-dependent* mapping between the coefficient and the observable force, not an
  on/off switch. If an EFT relies on screening to survive, the result is sensitive
  to the local density of the Eöt-Wash apparatus. So "screened → vacuous" is the
  optimistic bracket; a full treatment would make the bound a density-dependent
  function. Stated openly, not hidden.
- **Reading uncertainty.** The exclusion curve points are order-of-magnitude
  readings of the published figure; the ~98% reduction is robust to that (v1.76
  missed the bound by ~an order of magnitude).
- **Framework:** the **Bayesian Swampland program** (theoretical consistency as a
  prior, data as a likelihood) is the formal home for this; our hard-cut version is
  its sharp-threshold limit.
