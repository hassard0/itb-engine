## Route Blockers

### Tower

The tower route has math-excluding candidates, but zero promotable native adapters. The blocker is not algebra; it is ownership and adapter exposure:

- missing framework-owned endpoint;
- missing framework-owned displacement;
- not exposed by registered framework adapter;
- positive-control and finite-range scope blockers.

Next artifact: a registered in-scope framework-owned `TowerEvidence` adapter with endpoint, displacement, source, normalization, uncertainty, and excluding tower math.

### Birefringence

The birefringence route remains empirically alive: v2.49 has five positive-sign rows and a naive WMAP/Planck+ACT pair at 4.53 sigma. It is still blocked by:

- no 5 sigma single-dataset detection;
- instrument-angle miscalibration degeneracy;
- foreground/systematics not closed;
- one-observable dependence;
- no source-backed engine-axis mapping.

Next artifact: a systematics-closed external beta measurement plus a source-backed mapping from beta to the engine parity axis.

### Weyl `g_C`

The `g_C` route is source-rich but structural:

- conformal-collider `a/c` is already a consistency bound;
- holographic complexity and black-hole entropy are proxies;
- zero external numerical `g_C` measurement routes are ready.

Next artifact: a direct external numerical Weyl-sector measurement or validated framework adapter.

### Matter `g_8`

The `g_8` route is the most actionable next artifact:

- v2.50 confirms it as the second frontier direction;
- v2.51 finds a plausible high-moment/partial-wave concept;
- v2.52 blocks it because the measurement route is still an internal design probe.

Next artifact: a sourced spin-4, detector, or high-moment measurement program with direct mapping to `g_8` and closed systematics.
