## Context

The generative cycles found novel theories and a parity-violating frontier. This
cycle synthesizes them into one structural map: across the feasible region of
the corrected stack, **which coefficient directions does consistency leave free
(new-physics frontier), and which does it pin (robust predictions)?** Estimated
from a 128-point boundary cloud (random-direction extreme points + the v1.32
catalog).

## The freedom map — per-coefficient feasible extent

| coefficient | feasible range | extent | reading |

| **g_R3_parity** (cubic parity) | [−0.06, +0.06] | **0.117** | tightest — robust prediction |

Two clean structural facts:

1. **The matter sector is loose; the graviton-parity sector is pinned.** The
   dimension-8 matter coupling g_8 is the *least-constrained* direction by a wide
   margin — consistent theories span almost its whole allowed range. This is the
   structural reason the generative search kept finding novel theories spread
   along g_8 (low-g8 at 0.045, high-g8 at 0.55, both robust) and why **precision
   g_8 is the engine's top invented experiment** — it is the direction where the
   constraints say the least.

2. **The robust predictions of consistent quantum gravity live in the parity
   sector.** g_R3_parity is pinned to |g_R3_parity| ≲ 0.06 and g_R2_parity to
   ≲ 0.10 — the cubic gravitational parity coupling is the *most constrained*
   quantity in the whole EFT. A measurement finding cubic gravitational parity
   violation above ~0.06 would falsify the entire consistent region, not just
   one framework.

PC1–2 confirm the freedom is overwhelmingly in the matter sector. **PC3 is the
v1.31 physics, emergent from the data**: the cubic curvature coupling and the
parity content vary against each other — the geometric statement of "consistent
parity violation requires cubic suppression."

## Closing the generative arc (v1.27–33)

Putting the generative cycles together, the structure of consistent quantum
gravity (at toy precision, corrected stack) is:

- a **single connected sliver** (v1.28), whose
- **interior** holds the catalogued frameworks (parity-conserving, mid-g8), while its
- **frontier is parity-violating** (v1.32), bounded by a **cubic-suppression
  trade-off** (v1.31, PC3), and
- whose **one loose direction is the dimension-8 matter coupling g_8** (this
  cycle) — the others, especially cubic parity, are pinned.

The two experiments the program invented map onto this exactly: **precision g_8**
probes the loose frontier (is Nature in the low-g8 or high-g8 novel branch?), and
**cubic gravitational parity g_R3_parity** probes the most robust prediction (the
parity sector is pinned; a detection there would be revolutionary).

## Honest limitations

- Extents are empirical (128-point cloud); true feasible ranges are at least this
  wide (clouds under-cover), so g_8 being loosest and cubic-parity tightest is
  robust as an *ordering*, even if the exact numbers are lower bounds.
- Toy-precision throughout.
