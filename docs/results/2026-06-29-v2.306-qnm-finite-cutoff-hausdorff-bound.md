# v2.306 - The finite-cutoff moment bound: Hausdorff beats Stieltjes

## Question

A fresh swing in a new sector. Every positivity bound the engine uses is a STIELTJES moment condition --
the Wilson couplings are moments of a spectral density on the whole half-line. But a real EFT has a FINITE
cutoff, so its UV-completing density has BOUNDED support. Does demanding finite support give a stronger
bound than positivity?

## Result (a ceiling positivity misses)

Yes. A moment sequence of a measure on a bounded interval `[0, S]` must satisfy the HAUSDORFF conditions,
strictly stronger than Stieltjes. Normalizing `m_0 = g_4` (so `u = g_6/g_4`, `v = g_8/g_4`), a measure on
`[0, S]` reproducing `(1, u, v)` exists iff:

```text
  v >= u^2        Stieltjes / Cauchy-Schwarz   (g_6^2 <= g_4 g_8)   -- what positivity already gives
  v <= u * S      the NEW finite-support CEILING (x^2 <= S x on [0,S]) ->  g_8 <= S * g_6
```

The minimal support consistent with the couplings is `S_min = v/u = g_8/g_6`. Verified constructively:
the two-atom measure with atoms at `{0, g_8/g_6}` and weights `{1 - g_6^2/(g_4 g_8), g_6^2/(g_4 g_8)}`
reproduces `(g_4, g_6, g_8)` exactly, sits inside `[0, g_8/g_6]`, and has non-negative weights precisely
when Stieltjes holds:

```text
  framework          g_4   g_6   g_8    S_min = g_8/g_6   two-atom measure
  string_tree_eft    0.50  0.40  0.40   1.000            valid (reproduces moments, weights >= 0)
  asymptotic_safety  0.40  0.30  0.30   1.000            valid
  lqg_induced        0.60  0.45  0.40   0.889            valid
  cdt                0.55  0.40  0.35   0.875            valid
```

The bound has teeth Stieltjes lacks: the point `(g_4, g_6, g_8) = (1, 0.5, 1.0)` satisfies positivity
(`0.25 <= 1.0`) yet is EXCLUDED by any cutoff `S < 2.0` -- a finite cutoff forbids couplings that
infinite-support positivity allows. All 6/6 checks pass.

## What this establishes

"The EFT has a cutoff" is not a slogan but a quantitative UPPER bound on the Wilson tower: `g_8 <= S g_6`,
with threshold `S_min = g_8/g_6`. Pure (infinite-support) positivity gives only the lower Cauchy-Schwarz
wall `g_6^2 <= g_4 g_8`; the finite cutoff sandwiches the tower from above as well. The engine frameworks
all pass, sitting at `S_min` between 0.875 (cdt) and 1.0 (string, AS) -- i.e. at the edge of the
finite-support window, where weakly-coupled descending towers should sit. This is a new, constructive,
exactly-verified consistency condition in a sector (finite-support moment problem) the engine had not
engaged.

## Honest scope

The Hausdorff conditions and the two-atom construction are EXACT mathematics (the truncated Hausdorff
moment problem); the verification reproduces moments to 1e-12 and the weight-positivity <-> Stieltjes
equivalence is exact. The PHYSICAL identification of the moment variable with a literal cutoff scale is
schematic: in the toy basis the couplings are dimensionless O(1) numbers, so `S_min = g_8/g_6` is a
statement in normalized moment units, not a GeV cutoff -- the robust content is the STRUCTURE (finite
support adds `g_8 <= S g_6`, which positivity misses, with threshold `g_8/g_6`), not the numeric scale.
Derived for the 3-term matter tower `(g_4, g_6, g_8)`; longer towers add more Hausdorff determinant
conditions (future work). Implemented as a standalone experiment, NOT wired into the 38-constraint stack
(to avoid a feasibility cascade); promotion to an opt-in constraint is a candidate next step. Toy basis,
O(1) prefactors. A fresh-sector new-theory result.
