## What this adds

Two genuinely new consistency constraints, each from a *different* physical
principle than what the engine already encoded:

1. **Cross-sector dimension-8 EFThedron bound** (Dr. M.'s recommendation — the
   non-redundant pick): the first **matter↔graviton** positivity, from analyticity
   of the matter+graviton → matter+graviton amplitude:

   **g_8 · g_R2 ≥ α · g_6 · g_R3**   (α ≈ 1.1)

   All existing bounds are intra-sector (matter convexity/dispersion tower, or
   graviton positivity); this is the first to link the s⁴ matter moment (g_8) to
   the graviton curvature (g_R2) through the next-order moments. s-power
   bookkeeping is consistent (g_8·g_R2 ~ s⁵ ~ g_6·g_R3).

2. **CEMZ graviton-causality bound**: a cubic curvature coupling induces a
   graviton *time advance*; causality requires
   **|g_R3| ≤ κ·√(g_4·g_R2)** (κ ≈ 0.8). This rests on **causality**, distinct
   from the analyticity/unitarity behind the dispersive bounds.

Both implemented as `Constraint` subclasses with tests; full suite **398 green**.

  they **corroborate** the anti-large-cubic conclusion from two independent
  principles (causality, cross-sector positivity). Convergent evidence is exactly
  what one wants: the verdict is not an artifact of one bound.

## A subtle, honest observation

The parity-conserving survivors (string, AS, CDT) pass the cross-sector EFThedron
only **narrowly** (margins ~0.01). So this bound is *nearly binding* for everyone
— a tight, informative constraint. If the literal EFThedron coefficient α is even
slightly above 1.1, it would start excluding the survivors too. That makes α a
new load-bearing prefactor worth pinning, and flags the cross-sector bound as one
to encode at literature precision next.

## Honest limitations

α ≈ 1.1 and κ ≈ 0.8 are motivated prefactors (the literal coefficients need the
explicit cross-amplitude Hankel kernel / the CEMZ higher-spin scale). The robust,
genuinely-new content: a cross-sector Hankel positivity coupling g_8 g_R2 to
g_6 g_R3 exists, it catches the spin-foam family, and it nearly binds the
survivors. CEMZ is, as Dr. M. flagged, partly parallel to forward positivity
(both bound the cubic) but from a distinct principle.
