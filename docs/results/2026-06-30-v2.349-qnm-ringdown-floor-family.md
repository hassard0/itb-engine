# v2.349 - Is the ringdown floor guaranteed across the whole consistent+observed family, or only at the center?

## Question

A fresh sector after the parity arc: the OTHER prediction channel, ringdown. v2.336/v2.337 established the
constructed *center's* ringdown floor -- the moment-tower mandate `g_R4 >= g_R3^2/g_R2 = 0.042`, a guaranteed
minimum quartic-curvature deviation. But that floor depends on the other curvature couplings and collapses
wherever `g_R3 -> 0`. So: does a pure-GR ringdown refute the ENTIRE theory family, or only its `g_R3 != 0`
members?

## Result (only the center -- the floor collapses on the small-g_R3 edge)

Sampling the consistent+observed family (2137 accepted points) and computing the moment-tower floor at each:

```text
  constructed-center floor:        0.042
  family floor   min/median/max:   0.000 / 0.030 / 0.165
  family g_R3     min/max:         0.000 / 0.199
  floor guaranteed family-wide?    No        g_R3 can vanish?   Yes
```

`g_R3` (the parity-even cubic) reaches ~0 within the feasible region, so the floor **collapses toward zero**
there. The firm floor (0.042) is a property of the constructed **center** and of members with sizeable
`g_R3`, not of every consistent+observed theory. All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The ringdown floor is a **center property, not a family-wide guarantee**. A pure-GR ringdown (below the
floor) would refute the `g_R3 != 0` members -- including the constructed theory -- but NOT the small-`g_R3`
sub-family, whose ringdown is indistinguishable from GR at this order. So the honest testability shape of the
ringdown channel depends on *where in the family the true theory sits*: maximal (a guaranteed deviation) at
the constructed center, vanishing on the small-`g_R3` edge. This extends v2.336/337 from the center to the
whole family -- the curvature-sector parallel of v2.341's center->family extension for the consistency
trilogy -- and it sharpens the asymmetry already noted in v2.337: the ringdown is not just looser in
magnitude than the parity channel, its very *floor* is conditional on the curvature sector being non-trivial,
because no current data constraint reads `g_R3` from below.

## Honest scope

The family is a seeded random-walk sample (Metropolis-like, near-uniform, step 0.03), so the exact minimum
floor and minimum `g_R3` are sampler-dependent -- a finite walk may not reach the true extremal `g_R3`. The
robust content is the **qualitative verdict** (g_R3 reaches near zero feasibly, so the floor is not
family-guaranteed), not the precise 3-decimal extremum. The moment-tower floor `g_R3^2/g_R2` is exact
arithmetic, but `g_R3, g_R2` are toy-basis couplings and the `g_R4 -> ringdown-shift` map is rank-1
schematic (v2.336, the deep-research-flagged limitation), so "ringdown floor" means the `g_R4` lower bound,
not a sourced frequency shift. This is a CP-even, curvature-sector property, independent of the
cosmic-birefringence data (unlike the parity channel). Toy basis, O(1) prefactors.
