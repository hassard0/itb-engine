## Context

v1.23–24 perturbed the **constraint prefactors**. But the engine's **framework
coefficients** are equally toy — "representative, not derived" (2026-05-08
honest synthesis). This cycle audits that other half: it perturbs each
framework's Wilson coefficients over literature-motivated ranges and asks
whether the v1.24 verdicts survive.

Dr. M. (Pluto consult) supplied the physics that makes this principled:

- **Spin-foam g_R3.** EPRL/FK vertex asymptotics in the large-spin limit give
  an effective action whose cubic curvature term R³ is the *next* order after
  R², suppressed by ≥1 power of the spin j. So LQG's ratio g_R3/g_R2 is **not**
  ≈1 (the engine's value) but **∈ [0.1, 0.7]** — "a ratio of 1.0 is pessimistic
  for LQG survival." This directly undercuts v1.24's mechanism.
- **Pinned vs representative coefficients** (used to set the MC spread):
  string {g_4,g_6,g_8,g_R2,g_R3} pinned; AS {g_4,g_R2} pinned, {g_6,g_8,g_R3}
  representative; LQG {g_4} pinned, rest representative; CDT {g_4} pinned, rest
  representative. Pinned → ±10%, representative → ±40%; g_R3 modelled physically
  as ratio·g_R2 over Dr. M.'s ranges.

## Self-correction of v1.24: forward positivity does NOT robustly exclude LQG

The decisive scan — vary LQG's g_R3, hold the rest at nominal:

| LQG g_R3 | g_R2/g_R3 | forward-pos margin | satisfied? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.30 (engine value) | 1.01 | −0.056 | ✗ |
| 0.255 (threshold) | 1.18 | ≈ 0 | boundary |
| 0.21 (Dr. M. upper) | 1.43 | +0.054 | ✓ |
| 0.10 | 3.10 | +0.184 | ✓ |
| 0.03 (Dr. M. lower) | 10.0 | +0.264 | ✓ |

Across **Dr. M.'s entire physical range** (g_R3 ≤ 0.21), LQG **satisfies**
forward positivity. v1.24's headline — "forward-limit graviton positivity
excludes LQG" — was itself an artifact, this time of the engine's **inflated
g_R3 = 0.30**. The realism program caught its own overreach one cycle later.
v1.24's constraint is still correct physics; it simply does not bind LQG once
LQG's cubic coupling is given its physically-motivated (small) value.

LQG is **full-stack infeasible across the entire g_R3 scan** — even at g_R3=0.03
where forward positivity passes by +0.26, other constraints still exclude it.

Even with the **form-robust** geometric BNOSSW (discounting the fragile harmonic

the most-excluded framework": it no longer depends on RFC (v1.23), BNOSSW
(v1.23/F4), or forward positivity (v1.25). It rests on LQG's parity violation
and computational-complexity tension.

## Three honest findings about the engine itself

### G1 — Framework-coefficient uncertainty is as consequential as prefactor uncertainty.

Admitting it (as the engine's own docs say one must), *every* framework gains
substantial exclusion probability. The binding-constraint story reshuffles
completely between the prefactor-only view (v1.24) and the coefficient-aware
view (v1.25). At toy precision the engine cannot robustly attribute a *specific*
binding constraint to a framework — only coarse orderings survive.

### G2 — "Asymptotic safety is the robust survivor" weakens; string tree-EFT is the most robust.

The robust ordering is **LQG (98%) > CDT (70%) > AS (41%) > string (17%)**.
String — not AS — is the most robust survivor, because its coefficients are the
best-*determined* (all pinned), so they don't wander into violation. Honest
subtlety: robustness-to-coefficient-uncertainty conflates "physically favoured"
with "well-measured." AS's apparent robustness in v1.24 was an artifact of
holding its representative g_6/g_8 fixed; let them vary by ±40% and the
dispersion tower (g_6² ≤ g_4·g_8) excludes AS in 32% of draws.

### G3 — The matter s³ positivity is a near-null, as predicted.

`MatterS3Positivity` (g_4 ≥ c_m·g_6, Dr. M.'s matter-sector suggestion) is
non-binding at nominal and acquires only minor power under coefficient
uncertainty (AS 9%). Consistent with the v1.20 observation that Class-A matter
constraints do little local pruning. Encoded for completeness; not a
discriminator.

## The meta-conclusion of the realism program so far

Across v1.23–25, two independent sources of toy-ness — constraint prefactors and
framework coefficients — each dominate the engine's framework-discrimination
output. The conclusions that survive **both** audits:

1. **A fully consistent theory exists** (intersection non-empty in 51–78% of
   prefactor space) — robust.
2. **LQG is the most-excluded framework** (~98%), driven by its parity violation
   (anomaly inflow) and complexity-bound tension — robust, after three different
   proposed mechanisms (RFC, BNOSSW, forward-positivity) were each shown to be
   non-robust.
3. Everything sharper than that — *which* constraint binds, whether AS or string
   is "the" survivor, the experiment re-ranking — is **not** robust at toy
   precision.

The path to a real result is now sharply defined: the highest-value targets are
the coefficients that are **both decisive and representative** — LQG's g_R3 and
parity content, AS's g_6/g_8 — pinned against published spin-foam vertex
asymptotics and FRG truncations, respectively. Until those are pinned, the
engine's discrimination is modeling-uncertainty-limited, not physics-limited.
