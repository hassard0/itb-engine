# v2.281 - Cross-validating the engine's GW + swampland constraints against the reconstructed bounds

## Question

A deliberate RECONNECTION to the engine after a long classical-GR / GW-phenomenology arc
(v2.251-v2.280) that independently reconstructed source-backed GW and swampland bounds from scratch.
The engine already encodes the same physics as live constraints -- do the two strands agree, and what
does running the engine on the frameworks say?

## Result

The cross-validation table (reconstructed bound vs engine constraint) holds:

```text
  physics                  reconstructed                       engine constraint            agree
  species scale            v2.264 Lambda = M_Pl/sqrt(N)        species_scale_bound          IDENTICAL form
  GW speed                 v2.251/270 |dv/c| < 5e-16           gw_speed_bound (5e-16)       same source value
  GW dispersion            v2.251 LVK dispersion               gw_dispersion_bound          same physics
  graviton mass            v2.266 m_g < 1.2e-22 eV (GW150914)  ligo_graviton_mass (1.27e-23 Will'18)  documented source-choice diff
  parity / birefringence   v2.252/269 cosmic+GW birefringence  ligo_birefringence / parity  same coupling
```

Running the engine (`itb.engine.check`) on all five frameworks against the full stack plus the GW
speed/dispersion bounds, **four frameworks satisfy the entire 11-constraint GW + swampland +
graviton-positivity sector** (pure_gr, string_tree_eft, asymptotic_safety, cdt), and the lone failure
is **lqg_induced, which violates `graviton_forward_positivity` (margin -0.06)** -- the forward-limit
graviton dispersion positivity, which is *exactly* the moment-tower / Hankel-positivity physics the
v2.261/v2.262 cycles built. So the engine's graviton-positivity constraint INDEPENDENTLY reproduces
the v2.262 result that lqg is the marginal/anomalous framework (its moment ratio
`x = g_R3/g_R2 = 1` sits on the positivity boundary). All 5/5 consistency checks pass.

## What this establishes

Two independent strands -- the from-scratch positivity theory (v2.261/v2.262) and the engine's encoded
constraints -- agree both on the GW/swampland bounds AND on which framework fails them. The engine's
`species_scale_bound` is literally the v2.264 derivation; its `gw_speed_bound` is the v2.251/v2.270
value; and its `graviton_forward_positivity` flags lqg exactly where the v2.262 moment tower found it
marginal. This reconnects the long phenomenology arc to the engine's actual `check()`/`frameworks()`
machinery and cross-validates the v2.262 lqg flag from a completely different direction.

## Honest scope

A cross-validation / consistency cycle: it verifies that the engine's encoded GW + swampland +
graviton-positivity constraints agree with the independently reconstructed source-backed bounds
(v2.251-v2.270) and that the engine's framework-level verdict on lqg matches the v2.262 moment-tower
diagnostic -- it does NOT add a new constraint or change any coupling. The species-scale form match is
exact; the numeric matches are at the source-value level; the graviton-mass case is a real, documented
difference in which published bound is encoded (engine: the tighter Will 2018 solar-system-combined
1.27e-23 eV; reconstruction: the pure-GW GW150914 1.2e-22 eV), preserved honestly. The lqg failure is
the engine's OWN verdict (margin -0.06 on `graviton_forward_positivity`), reported and connected to
v2.262, not re-derived here. Frameworks' couplings and the engine's O(1) prefactors carry their usual
representative-value caveat.
