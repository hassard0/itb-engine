# v2.276 - The Hawking-Page transition: black holes have a phase diagram (and it is holographic)

## Question

A fresh QG thermodynamics probe completing the black-hole-entropy sub-arc (v2.257/v2.273/v2.274/v2.275).
An asymptotically-flat black hole has negative specific heat and cannot sit in stable equilibrium. What
changes in anti-de Sitter space?

## Result

A Schwarzschild-AdS_4 black hole (`f = 1 - 2M/r + r^2/L^2`) has a genuine phase diagram:

```text
  T(r_+) = (1/4pi)(1/r_+ + 3 r_+/L^2),  M = (r_+/2)(1+r_+^2/L^2),  S = pi r_+^2,  F = M - TS = (r_+/4)(1 - r_+^2/L^2)

  r_+      T        F          C         phase
  0.400    0.2944   +0.0840     -2.86     small branch (unstable, C<0)
  0.500    0.2785   +0.0937    -11.00     small branch (unstable, C<0)
  0.800    0.2905   +0.0720    +12.76     large branch, thermal AdS dominates (F>0)
  1.000    0.3183    0.0000    +12.57     <- Hawking-Page point (F=0)
  1.500    0.4112   -0.4688    +19.05     large branch, black hole dominates (F<0)
  2.500    0.6287   -3.2813    +43.69     large branch, black hole dominates (F<0)

  T_min = sqrt3/(2pi L) = 0.2757  at  r_+ = L/sqrt3 = 0.5774   (no black hole below T_min)
  T_HP  = 1/(pi L)      = 0.3183  at  r_+ = L                  (Hawking-Page transition)
```

Three facts make the diagram, all verified: (1) `T(r_+)` has a MINIMUM at `r_+ = L/sqrt3`, so below
`T_min = sqrt3/(2 pi L)` no black hole exists; (2) the specific heat `dM/dT` changes sign there -- the
small branch is unstable (`C<0`), the large branch stable (`C>0`); (3) the free energy
`F = (r_+/4)(1 - r_+^2/L^2)` (verified against `M - TS`) changes sign at the HAWKING-PAGE point
`r_+ = L`, `T_HP = 1/(pi L)`: below it thermal AdS dominates (`F>0`, black hole metastable), above it
the large black hole dominates (`F<0`). All 5/5 consistency checks pass.

## What this establishes

There is a first-order phase transition between empty thermal AdS and a large black hole -- and Witten
showed it is holographic: the gravity dual of the CONFINEMENT-DECONFINEMENT transition of the boundary
gauge theory (thermal AdS = confined phase, black hole = deconfined plasma). This completes the
BH-thermodynamics sub-arc: the v2.257 entropy and v2.275 Page curve followed a single evaporating hole;
here the hole has a genuine equilibrium phase structure with a sharp, holographically meaningful
transition, tying the entropy thread to AdS/CFT.

## Honest scope
