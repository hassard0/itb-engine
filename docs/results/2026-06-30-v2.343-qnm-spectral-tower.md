# v2.343 - The matter sector requires a multi-state tower, not a single resonance: a third line for the string-like UV

## Question

The trilogy (v2.338 unitarity, v2.339 causality) concluded the constructed theory's UV is a higher-spin
tower (string-like) -- but qualitatively. Is there a RIGOROUS, independent line for this from the matter
sector alone?

## Result (yes -- the spectral density has >= 2 states)

The matter dispersion bound `g_6^2 <= g_4 g_8` is a Cauchy-Schwarz inequality on the moments of the matter
spectral density: it is SATURATED (equality) iff a SINGLE state dominates, and STRICT iff the spectrum has
TWO OR MORE states. So the strict-vs-saturated status reads off, rigorously, single resonance vs tower:

```text
  theory               g_6^2/(g_4 g_8)
  asymptotic_safety    0.750
  engine_constructed   0.756   <- strictly < 1  =>  >= 2 states (a tower)
  string_tree_eft      0.800
  cdt                  0.831
  lqg_induced          0.844
```

The constructed theory has ratio 0.756 -- strictly below 1 -- so its UV contains at least two states: a
multi-state tower, not a single resonance. And it is among the MOST spread (tied lowest with
asymptotic_safety), so its tower is broader / lower-lying than string's (0.80) or lqg's (0.84). All 4/4
checks pass.

## What this establishes

This is a THIRD independent line for a higher-spin (string-like) tower UV -- and it comes purely from the
spectral structure encoded in the matter Wilson coefficients, independent of the unitarity (no-ghost) and
causality (CEMZ) arguments. So three separate routes now point to a tower: positivity/unitarity (v2.338),
causality (v2.339), and the spectral multi-state requirement (here). This sharpens the v2.342 "string-like
in two senses" identity: not only is the matter sector closest to string in coupling space and the UV
string-like by unitarity+causality, but the matter spectral density independently DEMANDS the multi-state
tower that string theory supplies -- the new theory genuinely wants a Regge-like UV, established three
independent ways.

## Honest scope

The Cauchy-Schwarz / single-state interpretation is rigorous and standard: for `g_4, g_6, g_8` understood
as moments of a positive spectral density (the dispersive / forward-limit representation), `g_6^2 = g_4 g_8`
holds iff the measure is a single atom and `g_6^2 < g_4 g_8` iff it has >= 2 atoms -- so "strict ratio < 1
=> at least two states" is a theorem, not a heuristic. The caveat: the engine's `g_4, g_6, g_8` are the TOY
moment encodings of the frameworks, so "at least two states" is a statement about the toy spectral
representation; the exact ratio (0.756) is the toy value and the "broader/lower-lying tower" comparison is
relative among the toy encodings. The identification of the multi-state tower with a HIGHER-SPIN / Regge /
string tower is the qualitative pairing with the trilogy (v2.339's CEMZ requirement), not a computation of
the tower's spins or scale -- the spectral argument shows ">= 2 states", the higher-spin character comes
from causality. This is a CP-even, data-independent property. Toy basis, O(1) prefactors.
