# v2.229 - The eikonal QNM <-> photon-sphere correspondence: ringdown from null geodesics

## Question

A fresh, fully self-contained thread (pivoting out of the EFT-ringdown vein after v2.215-v2.228).
Black-hole ringdown has a geodesic origin: in the eikonal (large-`l`) limit the quasinormal modes
are governed by the UNSTABLE circular null geodesic at the photon sphere (Cardoso-Miranda-Berti-
Witek-Zanchin, PRD 79 (2009) 064016):

```text
omega_lmn  ->  Omega_c (l + 1/2)  -  i (n + 1/2) |lambda|
```

with `Omega_c` the photon-sphere orbital frequency and `lambda` the Lyapunov exponent
(instability rate) of that orbit. Can the repo's own validated WKB solver verify this end to end?

## Result

The Schwarzschild (M=1) photon-sphere quantities, derived from first principles (`r_ph = 3`,
`f = 1 - 2/r`):

```text
Omega_c = sqrt(f(r_ph))/r_ph                              = 1/(3 sqrt3) = 0.192450
lambda  = sqrt( f(2f - r^2 f'')/(2 r^2) )|_{r_ph}         = 1/(3 sqrt3) = 0.192450
```

(For Schwarzschild `Omega_c = lambda`; Kerr splits them.) The WKB QNMs (`n=0`) converge to these
monotonically as `l` grows:

```text
  l    omega_R/(l+1/2)   err vs Omega_c    -omega_I/(n+1/2)   err vs lambda
  2       0.14926           22.4%             0.17843            7.3%
  3       0.17122           11.0%             0.18546            3.6%
  4       0.17980            6.6%             0.18834            2.1%
  6       0.18646            3.1%             0.19053            1.0%
  8       0.18896            1.8%             0.19134            0.6%
 10       0.19017            1.2%             0.19173            0.4%
 12       0.19084            0.8%             0.19194            0.3%
```

**The correspondence is verified end to end.** `omega_R/(l+1/2) -> Omega_c` (to 0.8% at `l=12`)
and `-omega_I/(n+1/2) -> lambda` (to 0.3%), monotonically. Black-hole ringdown is, in this limit,
the light-crossing "ring" of the photon sphere damped at the rate unstable null rays peel away --
a purely geodesic picture, independent of any EFT content.

## What this establishes

A clean, self-contained verification of a foundational result in black-hole physics using the
repo's own solver -- and the geometric-optics FOUNDATION that all the EFT ringdown corrections
(v2.215-v2.228) perturb. It demonstrates the solver's correctness from an entirely independent
(geodesic) direction, and reconnects the ringdown program to its physical origin in unstable null
orbits. The first-principles photon-sphere quantities (`Omega_c`, `lambda`) match their closed
forms to machine precision, and the QNM convergence is the `O(1/l)` eikonal expansion made
explicit.

## Honest scope

The eikonal correspondence is an ASYMPTOTIC (large-`l`) relation; the finite-`l` QNMs differ from
the geodesic limit by `O(1/l)` corrections (22% at `l=2` for `omega_R`, shrinking to 0.8% at
`l=12`) -- this is physics, not solver error (the WKB solver is most accurate at large `l`, v2.218).
Schwarzschild only (`Omega_c = lambda` is special to Schwarzschild; Kerr splits them). This is a
geodesic-limit result; it does not by itself constrain quantum gravity, but it is the
geometric-optics foundation the EFT ringdown corrections perturb. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark
(v2.209).
