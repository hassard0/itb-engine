# v2.07 — The robustness jackknife: which of the program's headline findings survive perturbation?

**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core).

---

## The realism program, applied to the whole arc at once

v1.80 stress-tested *one* finding (the birefringence tension) against the toy-prefactor
uncertainty. v2.07 does it for **all the headline findings at once**: encode ~14 as
machine-checkable predicates, then re-evaluate each under **29 perturbations** — every one
of the 11 realism prefactors pushed to its plausible-range extreme (low/high, 22), plus
dropping each of the v1.93 top-6 load-bearing constraints (6) — all on a fixed sample set
so only the constraint change matters. The **robustness score** = fraction of perturbations
under which the finding still holds.

**Median robustness 0.983.** The program's **qualitative structure is perturbation-stable**:
the framework verdicts (LQG/Hořava excluded), the **swampland-dominated load-bearing core**
(distance conjecture top gatekeeper 0.97, swampland dominance 0.93), the thin **non-empty,
~3–4-dimensional island**, and the Euler-vs-Weyl² structural facts (blind spots, η/s↔sub-mm,
double-copy) all survive pushing every prefactor to its extreme and dropping any single
load-bearing constraint.

## The honest fragilities

Three findings score below 0.8 — and naming them is the point of the exercise:

- **Exact island fraction (0.76)** — the *size* of the island moves out of the narrow
  [10⁻⁴, 10⁻³] band under some prefactor extremes, even though *non-emptiness* (1.00) and
  *low-dimensionality* (0.83) hold. The island is robustly **thin and low-dimensional**;
  its **precise volume is not** a robust number. (Honest, expected: it's a quantitative
  threshold, not a qualitative claim.)
- **String tree-EFT feasibility (0.76)** — string-EFT sits *near a boundary* of the
  consistent region (also seen in v2.01, where its exact point was borderline on the full
  38-stack), so it flips feasible↔infeasible under ~24% of prefactor extremes. A genuine,
  honest caveat: string-EFT's consistency is **marginal**, not deep.
- **"a/c wedge in top-4 sole-gatekeepers" (0.10)** — this predicate is **mis-specified**, and
  the jackknife caught it: the Hofman–Maldacena wedge *is* a dominant constraint by
  **irreplaceability** (v1.71 "single dominant"; v1.93 +36% island growth, rank 7), but it
  is rarely a *sole* gatekeeper, because (v1.97) most constraints never act alone. The low
  score reflects a **bad metric choice** (dominance ≠ sole-gatekeeping), not a fragile
  physics claim. We flag it rather than hide it.

## What it means

The credibility of the program rests on its headline findings being robust to the toy
prefactors — and they are: **8 findings are rock-solid (≥0.95), the rest cluster at
0.76–0.93, and the only true outlier is a self-caught predicate-design error.** The
distinction is exactly the one the engine has emphasized throughout: the **qualitative
structure** (thin island, swampland core, framework exclusions, Euler-vs-Weyl² unifications,
blind spots) is robust; the **precise numbers** (island fraction, the marginal feasibility
of a near-boundary framework) are not — and we say which is which.

## Honest scope

The predicate thresholds are my choices; the robust content is the **ranking** of which
findings are perturbation-stable. Structural findings (config-independent) are scored 1.0 by
construction — they don't depend on the toy prefactors. This complements, rather than
replaces, the per-cycle realism checks.
