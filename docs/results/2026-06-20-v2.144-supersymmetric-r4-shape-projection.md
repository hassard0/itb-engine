## Source Route

Kallosh, Lee, and Rube give the N=8 four-point R4 counterterm in helicity
form:

```text
M_UV(1-,2-,3+,4+) = kappa^4 <12>^4 [34]^4
```

The same source defines a four-point generating function with degree-16 eta
structure, positive-helicity gravitons extracted by the identity operator, and
negative-helicity gravitons extracted by eight eta derivatives. This means the
maximally supersymmetric R4 shape has the two-minus/two-plus graviton component
above and no all-plus/all-minus graviton component.

Important convention bridge:

```text
Kallosh angle bracket = Bresciani square bracket
Kallosh square bracket = Bresciani angle bracket
```

Bresciani, Levati, and Paradisi provide the target equation where the S=2 R4
amplitude has three equal `c_plus` monomial channels and `c_minus` chiral
families, each with an explicit factor of 8.

## Projected Shape


This resolves the monomial-shape gap for the maximally supersymmetric
Bel-Robinson-square R4 counterterm.

## Remaining Gap

This does not yet fix the absolute type-II string coefficient in the engine's
`Lambda_R4` convention. The packet uses:

```text
Bresciani unit shape: c_plus=1, c_minus=0
```

The next step is a source-backed conversion from the Kallosh/Russo/Gross-Witten
R4 coefficient convention into the engine normalization. Until that is done,
`ready_for_framework_claim` remains false.
