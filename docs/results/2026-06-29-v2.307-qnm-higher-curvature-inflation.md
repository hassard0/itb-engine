# v2.307 - Higher-curvature corrections to Starobinsky inflation: n_s is the CMB handle on the cubic

## Question

A fresh swing into COSMOLOGY. Starobinsky inflation IS the R^2 (g_R2) corner of the engine's curvature

## Result (a sharp asymmetry: n_s sensitive, r not)

Using standard f(R) = R + a R^2 + b R^3 inflation (Einstein-frame potential, slow-roll, M_pl=1), the b=0
case is a strict self-check and reproduces Starobinsky exactly:

```text
  n_s = 0.9650   vs analytic 1 - 2/N = 0.9636   (N=55)
  r   = 0.00350  vs analytic 12/N^2 = 0.00397
```

Both within 0.004 -- the numerics are validated. Turning on the cubic reveals a sharp ASYMMETRY between
the two observables over the scanned window `b in [-0.0015, 0.0015]`:

```text
  observable   swing over the cubic window        sensitivity
  r            [0.00040, 0.00530]                 0.14 of the BK18 limit  -> nearly INSENSITIVE
  n_s          [0.9045, 0.9799]                   17.9 Planck sigma       -> HIGHLY sensitive
```

So n_s -- not r -- is the CMB handle on the cubic curvature coupling. The Planck 3-sigma band on n_s
([0.9523, 0.9775]) carves a SUB-WINDOW `b in [0.0, 0.00019]`, far tighter than the positivity scan, and
even slightly favours a positive cubic: parts of the positivity-allowed cubic range are CMB-EXCLUDED
because they push n_s off the observed value while leaving r untouched. n_s shifts monotonically with b.
All 6/6 checks pass.

## What this establishes

For the cubic curvature operator, CMB data is MORE constraining than positivity. A measurement of the
scalar tilt n_s pins the sign and magnitude of g_R3 in a way no ringdown or amplitude bound does -- the
tensor ratio r is an independent and currently slack cross-check (it stays far below 0.036 throughout).
This is a genuine cosmological handle on the engine's higher-curvature sector: the abstract carved cubic
coupling becomes a falsifiable shift of the Starobinsky point along the n_s axis, with the cubic's sign
setting the direction.
