# v2.68 - GW parity Callister sample density adapter: source-native histograms ready

## Question

v2.67 parsed the Callister variable-evolution HDF file, but it was sample-based
rather than a fixed posterior grid. This iteration asks whether those samples
can be converted into normalized source-native density estimates without
interpreting them as PPV or engine evidence.

## Real Release Density Diagnostic

densities:

| density | samples | bins | norm | peak |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `kappa_Dc` 1D | `4500` | `80` | `1.0` | `0.0050000000` |
| `kappa_z` 1D | `4500` | `80` | `1.0` | `-0.0187500000` |
| joint `kappa_Dc,kappa_z` | `4500` | `[80, 80]` | `1.0` | `(-0.0350000000, -0.1937500000)` |

The artifact also records `fixed_rate_delayedSFR.hdf` peak locations as a
diagnostic reference:

| fixed-rate reference | peak |
|---|---:|
| `kappa_D` 1D | `0.1027568922` |
| `kappa_z` 1D | `-0.0591478697` |
| joint `kappa_D,kappa_z` | `(0.1065162907, -0.0100502513)` |

This is not a model-selection result. The fixed-rate and variable-evolution
products have different population assumptions and posterior representations.

## Interpretation

The route has moved from file parsing to controlled posterior comparison. That
is progress, but it is not a quantum-gravity discriminator. No Callister
posterior has been promoted to `g_R2_parity`, `g_R3_parity`, or a final PPV
`beta_1`.
