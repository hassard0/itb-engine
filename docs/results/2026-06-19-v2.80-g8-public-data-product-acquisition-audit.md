## Interpretation

The current public source landscape is better than a blank slate: there are
citable energy-correlator data products, open-data methods, and theory bridges
that speak the right partial-wave or detector language. But none supplies the
hard object required by the engine:

- source-backed normalization to engine `g_8`
- public covariance or likelihood containing `g_8`
- bounded low-energy QG EFT applicability domain
- closed component-level systematics budget
- registered-framework exclusion math

The next artifact must either derive an adapter from a public energy-correlator
dataset into engine `g_8` with covariance, or identify a new measurement
published directly in the spin-4/detector low-energy QG EFT basis.
