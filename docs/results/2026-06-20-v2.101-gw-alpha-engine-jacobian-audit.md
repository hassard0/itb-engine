## Audit Outcome

The source parameters are real and numeric:

| parameter | central | lower 90 | upper 90 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `alpha_bar_1` | 0.87 | -0.16 | 2.82 |
| `alpha_bar_2` | -0.35 | -3.27 | 3.77 |

But direct promotion into existing secondary axes is rejected:

| target axis | decision | key reason |
|---|---|---|
| `g_C` | reject direct Jacobian | cubic source parameter cannot be treated as a quadratic Weyl / central-charge axis without a source-backed Jacobian. |
| `g_R2` | reject direct Jacobian | cubic source parameter cannot be treated as a quadratic scalar / Euler-style axis without a source-backed Jacobian. |
| `g_R3` | non-promoting extension candidate | curvature order and family are closer, but the source-native axis is not registered for the v2.98 gate and still lacks normalization, likelihood export, bounded shared EFT domain, and framework projection. |

## Interpretation

This is not a hard stop. It prevents a wrong shortcut. The GW170608 result should
be preserved in its source-native cubic basis first, then tested for a defensible
`g_R3`-family projection or a new joint gate. Forcing `alpha_bar` into `g_C` or
`g_R2` would make the platform look more complete while weakening the physics.
