## Interpretation

Direct native tower source promotion is retired, so the next live route is the
source-backed `g_8` adapter derivation. This does not make `g_8` claim-ready. It
means the next artifact should decide whether a source-backed operator identity
exists from detector/energy-correlator observables into engine `g_8`, or whether
that retained route must also be retired.
