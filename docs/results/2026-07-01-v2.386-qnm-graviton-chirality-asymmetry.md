# v2.386 - SWING (native parity channel): the theory predicts an order-2 left/right graviton asymmetry, locked to the CMB handedness

## Question

The engine maps `g_R2_parity` to CMB *photon* birefringence as an order-of-magnitude proxy. But `g_R2_parity`
is the gravitational Chern-Simons / Pontryagin coupling -- its native home is the graviton sector. What does it
predict there?

## Result (a large, robust left/right graviton asymmetry)

The engine's polarization-decomposed positivity (Caron-Huot-de Rham-Tolley-Zhou 2024) gives *independent*
bounds on the two circular graviton polarizations:

```text
  (g_R2 + g_R2_parity)^2 <= kappa g_4 g_6   (left)      (g_R2 - g_R2_parity)^2 <= kappa g_4 g_6   (right)

  constructed:  L = g_R2 + g_R2_parity = 0.253   R = g_R2 - g_R2_parity = 0.133   ->  L/R = 1.9
                margins:  left 0.148  <  right 0.194   (left-handed sector closer to its bound)
  family:       asymmetry mean 2.09, range [1.5, 3.9];  left tighter in 100% of theories
```

All 5/5 checks pass. The two graviton polarizations have different effective quartic self-couplings
`g_R2 +/- g_R2_parity` -- the amplitude face of gravitational-wave birefringence -- and the asymmetry is
**order-2** across the whole feasible region, with the left-handed sector always tighter.

## What this establishes

A large, native, cross-messenger chirality prediction. The asymmetry ratio
`(g_R2+g_R2_parity)/(g_R2-g_R2_parity)` is **independent of the bound normalization** `kappa`, so the order-2
value is robust; only the margins depend on `kappa`. The handedness -- **left stronger than right** -- is locked
to the sign of `g_R2_parity`, which the CMB birefringence `beta > 0` selects (v2.364): flip the measured CMB
rotation and the graviton asymmetry flips with it. So the **same parity violation seen in the CMB makes
left-handed gravitational waves order-2 more strongly self-interacting than right-handed** -- order-unity,
unlike the ~1e-19 GW propagation-speed birefringence (v2.358). This reframes the parity test: the coupling's
cleanest home is the graviton sector's left/right asymmetry, with the CMB as the toy-mapped low-energy shadow.
It also sharpens the observability picture (v2.380/381): parity is not one channel but a chirality *structure*,
whose gravitational face carries an order-unity signal the CMB proxy hides.

## Honest scope

The polarization-decomposed positivity is source-cited (Caron-Huot et al. 2024) and the `g_R2 +/- g_R2_parity`
decomposition is exact. The asymmetry RATIO is `kappa`-independent, so the order-2 result is robust; only the
margins use `kappa=1`. CAVEATS: the asymmetry is the AMPLITUDE-level (quartic-coupling) chirality -- the same
parity structure as GW propagation birefringence but NOT identical to the kinematic speed birefringence (which
is separately tiny, v2.358); "GW birefringence" here means the coupling chirality, honestly distinct from the
speed effect, and a fully physical GW-birefringence waveform amplitude would need the dCS->waveform map, absent
from the engine. The magnitude of `g_R2_parity` is toy and birefringence-contingent (v2.329), but the asymmetry
ratio ~2 follows from `g_R2_parity/g_R2 ~ 0.3` (fixed by the birefringence band + positivity), robust across
the region while the absolute couplings are toy-basis. Handedness-to-`beta` uses the engine sign convention
(v2.364). Sampled family. Robust content: an order-2 left/right graviton coupling asymmetry, left always
tighter, locked to the CMB handedness -- a native, cross-messenger, order-unity chirality prediction. Toy
magnitudes, robust ratio, source-cited bound.
