# v2.214 - Operator->QNM sensitivity via the published parametrized-ringdown basis (the right route)

## Question

v2.212/v2.213 proved an in-house machine-precision QNM solver is required to RECOMPUTE the
operator->QNM sensitivity (the WKB-peak shortcut is ~150% off; a few-% re-solve is erratic).
But is recomputing the sensitivity even the right goal? McManus, Berti, Macedo, Kimura,
Maselli, Cardoso (PRD 99 (2019) 104077) already PUBLISHED the linear QNM-shift coefficients
e_j -- peer-reviewed, high-precision. Can the engine just USE them?

## Result

Artifact: `experiments/results/v2.214/qnm_parametrized_basis.json`.

Yes -- and this reframes the whole route. The published basis is

```text
delta_V = (1/r_H^2) sum_j alpha_j (r_H/r)^j,   omega = omega_0 + sum_j alpha_j e_j,  r_H = 2M.
```

So the engine's job is linear algebra, not QNM solving:
1. take a modified Regge-Wheeler potential `delta_V(r)`,
2. **DECOMPOSE** it into the `(r_H/r)^j` basis -> the `alpha_j` (a power expansion in u=r_H/r:
   `alpha_j` are the Taylor coefficients of `h(u)=r_H^2 delta_V(r_H/u)` at u=0),
3. **CONTRACT** with the published `e_j` -> `delta_omega = sum_j alpha_j e_j`.

The decomposition + contraction machinery is **exact and validated to machine precision**:

```text
basis_j0: alpha_k recovered to 3e-15,  shift matches published e_0 to 2e-15
basis_j1: alpha_k recovered to 2e-16,  shift matches published e_1 to 2e-16
basis_j2: alpha_k recovered to 2e-16,  shift matches published e_2 to 7e-17
linearity (1*dV0 + 2*dV1 - 0.5*dV2): rel.err 2e-16
polynomial-in-(1/r) decomposition: alpha_j recovered exactly
```

So a single basis deformation recovers its published e_k, and arbitrary combinations sum
correctly. The `e_j` are the **claim-grade transfer function**; the engine supplies the
(source-backed) `delta_V` and does the exact linear algebra.

## What this resolves

The v2.212/v2.213 impasse was self-inflicted: we tried to RECOMPUTE the e_j in-house and
hit the precision wall (a machine-precision QNM solver is hard, and my Frobenius ansatz
gives an awkward 5-term recurrence rather than the canonical 3-term Leaver one). The correct
route is to **stand on the peer-reviewed e_j** -- exactly the discipline the repo applies
elsewhere (use source-backed inputs, don't re-derive what the literature has validated). The
engine's value-add is the operator-specific `delta_V` and its decomposition, which IS the
part the engine can compute (cf the v2.210/v2.211 WKB potential machinery).

## Honest Scope

- The e_j used here are j=0,1,2 (McManus Table 1, odd-parity gravitational l=2 n=0). A real
  short-range R4 `delta_V` peaks near the photon sphere (u ~ 0.6) and needs the EXTENDED e_j

  The parity-odd axis g_R4_c3 stays dark (v2.209). Claim gate closed.
