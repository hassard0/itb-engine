# v2.278 - The Hulse-Taylor binary pulsar: the quadrupole formula and the first evidence for GWs

## Question

A fresh sector stepping off the black-hole cycles: the cleanest classical confirmation that
gravitational waves are real. A binary radiates energy as GWs at the quadrupole rate, so its orbit
decays. Does the quadrupole formula reproduce the famous Hulse-Taylor measurement?

## Result

For PSR B1913+16 (two `~1.4 Msun` neutron stars, `P = 7.75 hr`, `e = 0.617`) the Peters formula

```text
  dP/dt = -(192 pi/5)(G^{5/3}/c^5)(m1 m2/M^{1/3})(2 pi/P)^{5/3} f(e),
  f(e)  = (1 + 73/24 e^2 + 37/96 e^4)/(1-e^2)^{7/2}

  f(e=0.617)          = 11.853      (circular f(0)=1 -> eccentricity boosts emission ~12x)
  predicted dP/dt     = -2.4026e-12
  observed  dP/dt     = -2.3980e-12   (Weisberg-Huang 2016, intrinsic)
  predicted/observed  =  1.0019        (agreement to 0.19%)
  merger time         ~ 306 Myr
```

The prediction matches the measurement to **0.19%** -- all 6/6 consistency checks pass. The high
eccentricity boosts the GW luminosity `~12x` over a circular orbit (most radiation comes from the fast
periastron passage, where the quadrupole moment changes fastest), and the orbit will spiral in and
merge in `~300 Myr`.

## What this establishes

This is the 1993 Nobel-winning indirect detection of gravitational waves: the binary is demonstrably
losing exactly the energy the quadrupole formula says it radiates as GWs, two decades before LIGO saw
them directly. It anchors the whole GW program (v2.266-v2.272): the same quadrupole emission whose
stochastic background, polarizations and propagation those cycles probed is here measured -- classically
and unambiguously -- in a single binary.

## Honest scope

The Peters quadrupole formula and `f(e)` are exact leading-order results, and the predicted `dP/dt`
uses the source-backed PSR B1913+16 parameters (Weisberg-Huang 2016); the `~0.1%` agreement is the
published result (after the small kinematic / galactic-acceleration correction to the OBSERVED value,
which this experiment does not re-derive -- it compares to the already-corrected intrinsic value). The
merger time uses the CIRCULAR-orbit Peters formula with a leading `(1-e^2)^{7/2}` eccentricity factor;
the exact inspiral time needs the coupled `da/dt, de/dt` integral (the orbit circularizes as it
shrinks), so `~300 Myr` is order-correct, not exact. Point-mass, leading post-Newtonian quadrupole
order. A classical-GR / GW-confirmation result, not an engine constraint refit.
