# v2.273 - Black-hole greybody factors: how the potential barrier shapes the Hawking spectrum

## Question

A fresh QG thread pivoting off the GW arc, REUSING the validated v2.210 WKB / Regge-Wheeler machinery.
Hawking radiation is famously "thermal" -- but is it a pure blackbody? A quantum emitted near the

## Result

The emitted flux is the Planck spectrum WEIGHTED by the barrier transmission -- the greybody factor:
`dN/dt domega = (1/2pi) Gamma_l(omega) / (exp(omega/T_H) - 1)`. The leading WKB (Schutz-Will)
transmission through the Regge-Wheeler peak,
`Gamma_l = 1/(1 + exp(2 pi (V_max - omega^2)/sqrt(-2 V'')))`, reuses the SAME `V_max` and tortoise
curvature `V''` as the QNM formula. For the `l=2` gravitational barrier (`V_max = 0.1513`,
`omega_c = sqrt(V_max) = 0.389`, `T_H = 1/8pi = 0.0398`):

```text
  omega     Gamma(omega)
  0.05      1.3e-3      (barrier reflects soft quanta)
  0.19      6.3e-3
  0.389     0.5000      (omega^2 = V_max, exactly 1/2)
  0.58      0.9998      (above the barrier -> transmits)
  1.00      1.0000

  eikonal: omega_c * b_c / (L+1/2) -> 1   (L=2: 0.808, L=10: 0.990, L=40: 0.9993)
  high-freq capture sigma = pi b_c^2 = 27 pi = 84.82  (b_c = 3 sqrt3, the v2.230 shadow)
  low-freq area theorem  sigma -> A_H = 16 pi = 50.27
```

`Gamma` rises monotonically from ~0 (low frequency) through exactly `1/2` at `omega^2 = V_max` to ~1
above the barrier (verified). At HIGH frequency the barrier peak becomes the photon-sphere orbit
(`omega_c b_c/(L+1/2) -> 1` as `L` grows, verified to <0.1% by `L=40`), so the capture cross-section is
the geometric `27 pi M^2`. The greybody therefore SUPPRESSES the low-frequency Hawking flux below a
pure blackbody (verified). All 5/5 consistency checks pass.

## What this establishes

The same Regge-Wheeler barrier governs both how a black hole RINGS (QNMs, v2.210) and how it GLOWS
(Hawking, v2.257): the black hole is a leaky, frequency-filtered thermal source, not an ideal
blackbody. The high-frequency limit recovers the v2.230 photon-sphere capture cross-section and the
low-frequency limit the horizon-area theorem -- so this single object ties the ringdown, the shadow and
the Hawking temperature together, all from the one potential the engine already computes.

## Honest scope

The Schutz-Will WKB greybody is the LEADING barrier approximation: accurate near and above the peak
(correct `1/2`-point, correct geometric high-frequency cross-section), but NOT accurate at low
frequency -- the true low-`omega` behaviour is the analytic area theorem (`sigma -> A_H`), quoted here
as the complementary limit, not reproduced by the WKB formula (which underestimates the soft tail). So
the low-frequency Hawking SUPPRESSION is qualitatively right but quantitatively WKB-limited. `M=1`
units, Schwarzschild, massless fields; the bosonic flux is shown (fermionic flips the `-1` to `+1`).
The eikonal peak->photon-sphere identification is exact in the large-`L` limit and verified
numerically. A BH-physics result reusing the validated QNM potential, not an engine constraint refit.
