# v2.302 - The curvature coupling bracketed by four QG principles, three regimes

## Question

A fresh swing completing the information-theoretic sub-arc (v2.300 entanglement-vs-positivity, v2.301
entanglement-amplified ringdown). Beyond entanglement and positivity, the engine encodes a thermodynamic
(GSL) and a null-energy (quantum focusing) bound on the curvature coupling. Taken together, how do the
disparate consistency principles of quantum gravity jointly constrain `g_R2`?

## Result (one coupling, four principles, three regimes)

The leading curvature coupling is bracketed by FOUR independent QG principles:

```text
  LOWER (thermodynamic):  Generalized Second Law       g_R2 >= -c_GSL = -0.5
  UPPER (three forms):
     null energy:         Quantum Focusing Conjecture   g_R2 <= g_4/alpha = 2 g_4      (LINEAR in g_4)
     entanglement:        BNOSSW monogamy               g_R2 <= 3 g_4 g_6/(g_4+g_6)    (HARMONIC mean)
     unitarity:           forward positivity            g_R2 <= sqrt(g_4 g_6)          (GEOMETRIC mean)
```

Because the QFC bound is linear in `g_4` alone, it breaks the `g_4 <-> g_6` symmetry of the two means,
so the tightest upper bound PARTITIONS the matter plane into three regimes by `x = g_6/g_4` (verified at
a test point in each):

```text
  regime              x = g_6/g_4   binding principle
  g_4 >> g_6          0.10          ENTANGLEMENT (monogamy)
  balanced            1.00          UNITARITY (positivity)
  g_6 >> g_4          8.00          NULL ENERGY (quantum focusing)
  boundaries:  x = 0.146 (entanglement<->unitarity),  x = 4 (unitarity<->null energy)

  engine frameworks (all balanced, x = g_6/g_4 ~ 0.73-0.80):
    string_tree_eft   x=0.80  unitarity(positivity) 0.447   g_R2=0.20  inside bracket
    asymptotic_safety x=0.75  unitarity(positivity) 0.346   g_R2=0.15  inside
    lqg_induced       x=0.75  unitarity(positivity) 0.520   g_R2=0.30  inside
    cdt               x=0.73  unitarity(positivity) 0.469   g_R2=0.22  inside
```

The engine's frameworks all live in the balanced, unitarity-dominated band, so positivity is their
active constraint -- but a UV completion in either asymmetric regime would have its curvature coupling
fixed instead by entanglement or by quantum focusing. All 6/6 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The curvature sector is not bounded by any single principle: thermodynamics (GSL), entanglement
(monogamy), unitarity (positivity), and the null energy condition (quantum focusing) are four
complementary windows on the SAME coupling, each decisive in its own corner of the matter plane -- a
unified, partition-clean picture of how the disparate consistency conditions of quantum gravity jointly
carve the gravitational EFT. It completes the information-theoretic sub-arc (v2.300-v2.302) by placing
the entanglement bound in the company of its thermodynamic and null-energy siblings.
