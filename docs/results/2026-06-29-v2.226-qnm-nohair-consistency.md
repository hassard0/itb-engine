# v2.226 - Black-hole-spectroscopy no-hair consistency test, and how R4 violates it

## Question

The isospectrality sub-arc closed at v2.225. Open a fresh single-event thread: the no-hair
theorem makes a Schwarzschild black hole's entire QNM spectrum a function of ONE parameter (the
mass M), so the dimensionless frequency RATIOS `omega_lmn / omega_220` are universal GR constants
-- the no-hair fingerprint. Black-hole spectroscopy's consistency test: every measured mode must
point to the same M; a beyond-GR operator shifts modes differently and breaks that single-mass
consistency. Does R4 break it, and how detectably?

## Result

**GR no-hair fingerprint (validated vs Berti-Cardoso-Will):**

```text
  omega_221 / omega_220 :  solver 0.9273   Berti 0.9278   (0.06%)
  omega_330 / omega_220 :  solver 1.6059   Berti 1.6042   (0.11%)
  omega_440 / omega_220 :  solver 2.1682   Berti 2.1655   (0.13%)
```

The in-house solver reproduces the GR no-hair ratios to <0.2% -- the spectroscopy consistency
fingerprint is claim-grade.

**R4 violation of the 220/221 no-hair consistency (source-backed qEFT, v2.217):**

```text
  GR frequency ratio  wR_221/wR_220 = 0.928   ->  R4 shift  -1.31  per gamma
  GR damping  ratio   wI_221/wI_220 = 3.079   ->  R4 shift  -172   per gamma

  critical SNR (1 sigma):  frequency channel  2.90 / gamma
                           damping  channel   0.019 / gamma   (~131x more sensitive)
```

R4 violates the 2-mode (fundamental + first overtone) no-hair consistency -- the standard test
(Isi et al. used exactly 220+221 on GW150914). The **damping no-hair channel is ~131x more
sensitive** than the frequency channel: it inherits the overtone damping coefficient
`dtq_1 = 171.35`, so the no-hair test, like the R4 reach (v2.217-v2.220), is overtone-damping
dominated. The R4 violation is computed from PUBLISHED qEFT coefficients, not the unreliable
cross-multipole WKB sensitivity.

## What this establishes

A fresh, self-contained black-hole-spectroscopy result: the GR no-hair frequency fingerprint
(claim-grade, validated) and a source-backed demonstration that R4 violates the 220/221 no-hair
consistency, with the violation -- like every R4 ringdown observable in this program -- dominated
by the overtone damping channel. It connects the repo's R4 work to the headline LIGO/Virgo
no-hair test framework.

## Honest scope

The GR no-hair RATIOS are claim-grade (solver reproduces Berti to <0.2%). The R4 VIOLATION of the
220/221 pair is source-backed (published qEFT `dwq`/`dtq`, v2.217). The cross-multipole no-hair
violation (330/440 vs 220) is NOT computed: only the `n=0,1` `l=2` qEFT coefficients are
published, and the in-house cross-`l` WKB sensitivity to the `(r_g/r)^10` R4 deformation is
unreliable (the v2.212 overshoot does NOT cancel across different multipoles -- unlike the same-`l`
parity ratio of v2.224). The detectability uses ISOLATED resolvability (v2.221 covariance would
inflate `rho_crit` a few-fold). The `dtq_1` perturbative-delicacy caveat carries (v2.217, valid for
`gamma << 6e-3`). Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
