# v1.38 — The map in one picture: catalogued frameworks on the parity floor, the frontier fanning out

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan.
**Builds on:** the whole generative + observability arc (v1.27–37).

---

## Context

Two axes emerged as the program's most important: **g_8** (the loosest direction
of consistent theory space, v1.33) and **|parity|** (the top testable new-physics
signal, v1.35). This cycle projects everything onto that (g_8, |parity|) plane —
the 5 catalogued frameworks, the 3 engine-discovered theories, and the 79 catalog
extreme points (v1.32) — to show the structure in a single picture.

## The projection

```
  (g_8, |parity|)   F = catalogued framework   D = discovered   . = catalog-extreme
  |parity|
  0.16 +--------------------------------------------------------
       |                         ... D                          
       |                     .        .F   <- LQG (|parity|=0.12, EXCLUDED)
       |                   .      .   ..   .  .                 
       |                    .    . .   .  .. . . .              
       |              .      .  .     ...       ..              
       |           . . .   .  .    ..   ..  ..  .               
       |            .     .  . . .  . .    .  ..   . .          
       |                    ...     .    .  .   . .D            
       |        .     .          ....          ..               
       |   D                                                    
       |F                      F   F   F                        
  0.00 +--------------------------------------------------------
       0.0                                                  g_8 0.7
```

- **The catalogued frameworks lie on the parity = 0 floor** (Pure GR, string, AS,
  CDT), spanning only mid-g_8 (0.0–0.40). The single framework off the floor is
  **LQG (|parity| = 0.12) — and it is excluded** (v1.26, ~2% viable).
- **The consistent frontier fans out above and across**: all 79 catalog extreme
  points are parity-violating, spanning g_8 ∈ [0.10, 0.58], and the three
  discovered theories anchor the corners — `discovered_novel` (low-g_8, lower
  left), `discovered_high_g8` (high-g_8, right), `discovered_parity_violating`
  (top, the consistent LQG-cousin).

In one image: **the theories physics has catalogued occupy a thin parity-
conserving strip at moderate g_8; the consistent region the engine maps extends
far beyond them — across the whole g_8 range and up into parity violation —
and is almost entirely unexplored.** The two invented experiments are the two
axes of this plot: a spin-4 matter partial wave moves you along g_8 (v1.36); a
gravitational-parity measurement moves you up in |parity| (v1.35/37).
