**Date:** 2026-06-19

## Question

v2.41 added a promotion guard. v2.42 asks:

> Is the guard-blocked state wired into the same frontier classifier that controls
> claim readiness?

Yes. The frontier classifier now has a distinct `tower_promotion_guard_blocked` branch
between mathematical tower exclusion and `tower_discriminator_claim_ready`.
