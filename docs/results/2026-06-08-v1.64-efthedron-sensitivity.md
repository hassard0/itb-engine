## The sweep

The cross-sector EFThedron bound g_8·g_R2 ≥ α·g_6·g_R3 (v1.61) has an O(1)
prefactor α. We sweep α over [0.8, 1.5] and find, per in-scope framework, the
critical α at which its margin hits zero (α_crit = g_8·g_R2 / g_6·g_R3).

## What it means

- **At the canonical α = 1.1, only LQG and GFT are excluded** by the EFThedron —
  exactly the spin-foam-family result of v1.61.
- **But the catalogued survivors sit just above:** raising α from 1.1 toward 1.5
  progressively excludes CDT (1.28), then string (1.33), then asymptotic safety
  (1.50). So the cross-sector EFThedron is genuinely **load-bearing** — its O(1)
  coefficient, between 1.1 and 1.5, reshapes which catalogued frameworks survive.
  α is now a priority coefficient to encode at literature precision.
- **The engine-discovered branches are robust** (α_crit 3–15) — their suppressed
  cubic curvature (low g_R3, the v1.31 lesson) keeps g_6·g_R3 small, so the
  cross-sector bound never bites them in the plausible range. The α_crit ordering
  is, once again, the cubic-suppression hierarchy: the more a theory suppresses
  its cubic coupling, the safer it is from the cross-sector bound.

## A clean structural takeaway

Across three independent constraints now — forward positivity (v1.24), the cubic
graviton-matter bound, and the cross-sector EFThedron (v1.61) — the *same*
quantity orders the frameworks: **cubic-curvature suppression** (high g_R2/g_R3,
low g_6·g_R3). Theories that suppress the cubic (string, AS, CDT, and especially
the discovered branches) survive; those that inflate it (LQG, GFT) are excluded,
and the survivors' margins are set by how much they suppress it. This convergence
across distinct principles is strong evidence the cubic-suppression verdict is
physics, not an artifact of any one bound — while v1.64 shows the precise
*boundary* still depends on the load-bearing α.

## Honest limitations

α_crit is exact given the representative framework coefficients; both are toy. The
robust content: at canonical α only the large-cubic frameworks fail, the survivors
fail at α ≈ 1.3–1.5, and the cubic-suppression ordering is constraint-independent.
The precise survivor set at α > 1.1 hinges on the literal EFThedron coefficient.
