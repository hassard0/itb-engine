# v2.282 - Decomposing the lqg framework anomaly: which couplings source each constraint failure

## Question

Deepens v2.281, which found lqg_induced the lone framework failing the GW + swampland sector. Running
the full engine, lqg violates SIX constraints across three classes. Are these one anomaly or several --
and do they trace to lqg's curvature couplings (the moment-tower boundary, v2.262) or its matter
couplings?

## Result

Scaling lqg's curvature sector (`g_R2, g_R3, g_R2_parity, g_R3_parity`) and matter sector
(`g_4, g_6, g_8`) toward zero independently and reading the engine margins:

```text
  constraint                    base    curv_off  matter_off  heal(curv)  classification
  bnossw_monogamy              -0.043   +0.257    -0.300      0.85        curvature-driven
  cft_flat_space_bound         -0.075   +0.525    -0.600      0.85        curvature-driven
  complexity_cutoff            -0.025   +0.255    +1.220      0.95        either-sector (mixed)
  cross_sector_efthedron       -0.029   +0.000    +0.000      0.0         either-sector (mixed)
  graviton_forward_positivity  -0.060   +0.000    -0.060      0.0         curvature-driven
  repulsive_force_conjecture   -0.120   +0.270    -0.390      0.7         curvature-driven
```

**All six failures heal when the curvature sector is turned off** (every `curv_off` margin is `>= 0`),
so lqg's anomaly is fundamentally a CURVATURE-sector problem -- consistent with its couplings sitting
on the moment-tower positivity boundary (`x = g_R3/g_R2 = 1`, v2.262). In particular
`graviton_forward_positivity` -- the forward-limit positivity that IS the v2.261/v2.262 moment tower --
is **purely** curvature-driven: scaling the matter sector to zero leaves it at `-0.060` (no help at
all), while scaling the curvature sector heals it. All 5/5 consistency checks pass.

## What this establishes

lqg is not failing for six unrelated reasons. Its single over-large, boundary-saturating curvature
sector trips the amplitude-bootstrap positivity bounds (the moment tower), the gravitational-
universality bounds (repulsive force, complexity) and the entanglement-monogamy bound together --
turning the curvature couplings off heals all of them. The from-scratch moment-tower diagnosis (v2.262)
and the engine's full constraint suite now agree not just on the diagnosis (lqg is anomalous) but on
the MECHANISM (the curvature couplings are the disease). This is a genuine engine-driven result, using
the real `check()`/`Theory` API.

## Honest scope

An engine-driven sensitivity analysis: it scales lqg's encoded couplings and reads the engine's margins
-- it does not re-derive any constraint or change the engine. "Curvature-driven" means the failure
heals when the curvature couplings are scaled to zero (matter held); the heal scales come from a
`0.05`-step scan, so they are resolved to `+/-0.05`. The classification reflects which SECTOR sources
each failure (the curvature sector is scaled as a block, not per-coupling). lqg's couplings are the
engine's encoded representative values (the O(1)-prefactor caveat applies), so this diagnoses the
engine's lqg ENCODING -- the same object v2.262 and v2.281 analyzed, consistent across all three. A
consistency / mechanism result, not a new constraint or a claim about physical loop quantum gravity.
