## Question

v2.14 and v2.15 showed that the four v2.13 parity lobes are split by the hard
nonzero-threshold encoding of `swampland_distance_conjecture`. The open question was
whether that diagnosis only held for one convenient control.

This cycle tested four hierarchy-prior families:

- hard active-coefficient thresholds,
- smooth floors using `sqrt(g^2 + floor^2)`,
- soft-active log spread, where exact zeros smoothly deactivate,
- sector norms over matter, curvature, `g_C`, and parity sectors.

None of these is asserted to be the correct Swampland Distance Conjecture. The literature
guardrail matters: Ooguri and Vafa frame the Distance Conjecture as a statement about
infinite distance in moduli space and towers of light states, and Palti's review presents
the Swampland program as a UV-completion discriminator, not a Wilson-coefficient
max/min rule. A recent "beyond moduli" discussion also emphasizes that extending the
distance idea beyond moduli requires additional structure, not a naive coefficient prior.

Primary sources:

- Ooguri and Vafa, *On the Geometry of the String Landscape and the Swampland*:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0605264
- Palti, *The Swampland: Introduction and Review*:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.06239
- Debusschere, Tonioni, and Van Riet, *A distance conjecture beyond moduli?*:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2407.03715v3

## Result

| variant family | parity-zero detour connected? | first connecting parameter |
|---|---:|---:|
| default stack | no | n/a |
| no distance prior | yes | n/a |
| hard active threshold | yes | `0.03` |
| smooth floor | yes | `0.03` |
| soft-active log spread | yes | `0.01` |
| sector-norm threshold | yes | `0.04` |

Under the default stack, every lobe-to-parity-zero descent fails first on:

```json
{"swampland_distance_conjecture": 4}
```

At connecting parameter values, the lobe-to-zero paths become feasible and the worst
remaining lobe margin moves to `generalized_anomaly_inflow`, with a positive minimum
margin of about `0.003845`. Direct centroid-to-centroid lines still fail for two pairs
on `t_hooft_anomaly_matching` by margins around `-4.4e-4` and `-3.1e-4`, but the
multi-segment parity-zero detour is feasible.

## Interpretation

The v2.13 four-lobe topology is not robust under a family of plausible coefficient-space
hierarchy diagnostics. The exact threshold values are not physical constants; they are
artifact meters. They show how much smoothing, symmetry deactivation, or sector grouping
is needed before the lobes reconnect through parity zero.

The defensible claim is narrow:

> Under the current ITB toy stack, parity-lobe disconnection is controlled by the
> functional form of the distance hierarchy prior. It is not evidence for four
> disconnected physical quantum-gravity phases.

The next load-bearing task is stronger than more sampling: replace the coefficient
max/min prior with a model that carries an explicit moduli/tower surrogate, then rerun
Jenga, adversarial audit, island census, and topology. Until then, results gated by
`swampland_distance_conjecture` should be labeled as results under the present hierarchy
prior.

## Bottom line

v2.16 strengthens the negative result from v2.14/v2.15. The current topology signal is
not robust enough to support a quantum-gravity phase claim. The engine's next bottleneck
is a physically motivated Distance Conjecture encoding with explicit moduli/tower
content.
