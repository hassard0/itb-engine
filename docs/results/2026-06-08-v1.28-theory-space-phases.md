# v1.28 — How many consistent-theory classes? Theory-space connectivity (corrects v1.27)

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core, 4× V100).
**Builds on / corrects:** v1.27 Discovery 3 ("~31 islands").

---

## Context

v1.27 suggested the feasible region fragments into ~31 islands. That count came
from greedy clustering of boundary-hugging optimizer solutions — a method prone
to over-counting. This cycle tests it rigorously.

## Method evolution (two honest failures, then the right tool)

1. **Uniform rejection sampling** of the 7-D box found **2 feasible points in
   ~480 000 draws** — the feasible region is a thin sliver (hit rate ~10⁻⁶).
   Rejection sampling cannot map it.
2. **Margin-maximizing seeds + feasible random walk**: all 26 seeds collapsed to
   the *same* deep-interior basin (the Chebyshev centre near CDT, g_4≈0.56,
   g_R2≈0.28, g_R3≈0.11), and 0.03-step walks almost never stayed feasible. One
   connected blob — but the seeds weren't diverse, so this can't see other
   phases.
3. **Diverse novelty seeds + straight-segment feasibility** (the right tool):
   multistart that *maximizes distance from known frameworks* subject to an
   interior margin gives feasible seeds spread across the region; two seeds are
   linked if the straight segment between them is entirely feasible (a *lower
   bound* on connectivity — curved feasible paths can only merge components
   further).

## Result

50 diverse feasible seeds → **4 straight-line-connected components**:

| component | seeds | span | nearest framework | character |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 18 | 0.45 | CDT (0.15) | parity-violating, g_R3≈0.10 |
| 2 | 14 | 0.46 | string (0.12) | parity-violating, g_R3≈0.09 |
| 3 | 10 | 0.40 | CDT (0.18) | parity-violating, g_R3≈0.11 |
| 4 | 8 | 0.36 | CDT (0.17) | parity-violating, g_R3≈0.09 |

Two conclusions:

1. **v1.27's "~31 islands" is wrong — an artifact of clustering boundary points.**
   Properly sampled, the feasible region is essentially **one connected sliver**.
   Its deep interior is a single basin near CDT (method 2); its novelty-frontier
   resolves into at most **4 straight-line lobes**, and since straight-line
   connectivity is only a *lower* bound, the true number of phases is **≤ 4 and
   plausibly 1** (curved feasible paths likely merge the lobes). There is **no
   evidence for many disconnected theory-space phases** at this precision.

2. **The novelty-frontier is entirely parity-violating with suppressed cubic
   curvature.** Every diverse "most-unlike-known-frameworks" seed turned on
   parity (because the catalogued feasible frameworks are all parity-conserving)
   and kept g_R3 ≈ 0.09–0.11 — span/centroids reinforce v1.27's prediction that
   a consistent parity-violating gravity must suppress its cubic coupling
   (g_R2/g_R3 ≈ 3). The "new physics" frontier of the allowed region is the
   parity-violating-but-cubic-suppressed branch.

## Honest limitations

- Straight-segment feasibility under-counts connectivity for non-convex regions:
  it can report "disconnected" when a curved feasible path exists. So 4 is an
  upper bound on the number of phases on the sampled frontier; the interior is a
  single basin. A definitive count needs a feasible-path solver (the engine's
  `path_distance.py`) between component representatives — queued.
- All of this is at toy-prefactor precision; the geometry will shift under
  literature-grade encodings.

## Corrected takeaway

The consistent region of theory space is, to the precision the engine can
resolve, a **single thin connected sliver**, not a fragmented archipelago. Its
deep interior sits near CDT; its outer frontier is a parity-violating,
cubic-suppressed branch that no catalogued framework occupies. The interesting
"undiscovered theory" structure is on that frontier (v1.27), not in a
multiplicity of disconnected phases (v1.27 Discovery 3, hereby corrected).
