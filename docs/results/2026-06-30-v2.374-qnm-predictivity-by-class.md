# v2.374 - SWING: which physics carves the EFT? Predictivity by constraint class -- swampland + positivity dominate, holography barely

## Question

v2.373 found the swampland-complete carving is ~10^-5 predictive and consistency-driven. WHICH physics does
that carving -- positivity/unitarity, holographic/entropy bounds, or swampland/anomaly conditions?

## Result (universality and amplitude dominate; information barely carves)

Dropping each of the engine's three constraint classes from the theory-only stack and measuring how much the
feasible region opens:

```text
  baseline (all consistency):                 2.5e-5   (1 feasible / 40k)
  drop A_AMPLITUDE   (20 positivity bounds):   9.3e-4   opens ~37x   (37 feasible)
  drop B_INFORMATION ( 7 holographic bounds):  1.3e-4   opens  ~5x   ( 5 feasible)
  drop C_UNIVERSALITY(11 swampland+anomaly):   1.0e-3   opens ~42x   (42 feasible)

  ranking (biggest -> smallest carver):  C_UNIVERSALITY > A_AMPLITUDE >> B_INFORMATION
```

All 5/5 checks pass. Removing **C_UNIVERSALITY** (WGC, distance conjecture, anomaly inflow, 't Hooft matching,
BNOSSW) opens the region the most (~42x), removing **A_AMPLITUDE** (scalar/graviton positivities, dispersion
towers, forward bounds) nearly as much (~37x), and removing **B_INFORMATION** (Bekenstein, subadditivity, GSL,
quantum focusing) barely at all (~5x). Notably C does this with only 11 constraints vs A's 20 -- so
per-constraint, universality carves even more efficiently.

## What this establishes

In physics terms: quantum gravity's low-energy EFT is pinned mainly by **UNITARITY (amplitude positivity) and
SWAMPLAND/ANOMALY consistency, NOT by entropy/holographic bounds** -- a substantive statement about where the
predictive content of the swampland program actually lives. It reproduces at the VOLUME level what v2.325
found for the BINDING (the anomaly/universality family and the leading positivities dominate; the holographic
bounds are largely slack). The holographic/information bounds, while individually motivated, are for these
theories largely *implied* by positivity + universality -- they add little independent constraint.

## Honest scope

The RANKING (C >= A >> B) is the robust content, read from the dropped-class feasible COUNTS (tens of feasible
per class -- enough to rank), NOT from the ratios to the baseline (a rare event, few feasible per n, large
error) -- so the opening-FACTORS (~37-42x) are order-of-magnitude, but the ORDER of the classes is reliable.
The grouping is the engine's own ConstraintClass taxonomy (A/B/C) -- a reasonable but not unique partition
(e.g. CEMZ causality sits in A here). "B barely carves" is FOR REALISTIC POSITIVE-COUPLING EFTs in this box;
the holographic bounds would bite harder for exotic couplings (negative, or large parity), so their redundancy
is regime-dependent (echoing the v2.325 always-slack set). Toy-basis engine encoding, box-dependent as in
v2.373. Robust content: universality and amplitude do the bulk of the volume carving, holography little, for
realistic higher-derivative gravity.
