# v2.222 - The overtone covariance wall: ringdown spectroscopy self-limits

## Question

v2.221 showed the 2-mode (220+221) joint fit inflates each mode's resolvability ~4-5.5x vs the
isolated case, yet the overtone R4 advantage survived (140x). Ringdown SPECTROSCOPY proposes to
fit MANY overtones (220+221+222+...) to test the Kerr spectrum. **Does the covariance penalty
COMPOUND as overtones stack -- and how fast?** Add the n=2 second overtone to the joint Fisher
and measure it, first principles, self-contained.

## Result

```text
                       R_tau (first overtone, n=1)        Fisher condition number
 isolated (1-mode)              2.53                              --
 2-mode (220+221)              13.97   (5.5x)                   1.02e+06
 3-mode (220+221+222)         494      (35x further, 195x)     3.03e+09

 overtone R4-reach advantage:  140x (2-mode)  ->  10.2x (3-mode)
```

**Covariance compounds super-linearly.** Adding the n=2 overtone inflates the first-overtone
resolvability `R_tau1` by a *further* ~35x (to ~195x vs the isolated mode), and the Fisher
condition number jumps ~3000x (1e6 -> 3e9). The heavily-damped high overtones (`Q_2 ~ 0.31`) are
broad and nearly degenerate in resolvability space, so stacking them collapses each mode's
measurability. **This is the first-principles mechanism behind the published overtone-overfitting
concerns (Baibhav-Berti et al.):** the ringdown Fisher matrix goes near-singular.

## Consequence for the R4 program

The v2.220/v2.221 overtone advantage is contingent on a LOW-ORDER ringdown model. It holds
strongly at 2 modes (140x) but erodes to ~10x if the fit is pushed to 3 modes. So the **optimal
R4 strategy is the 2-mode (220+221) model** -- enough to exploit the overtone's huge R4
sensitivity (v2.217), few enough to stay off the covariance wall. More overtones do not help and
actively hurt.

## What this establishes

A clean, self-contained demonstration that overtone ringdown spectroscopy is self-limiting via a
covariance wall, derived from first principles (the Fisher condition number explosion), connecting
the repo's R4 program to the active overtone-overfitting debate in the literature. It both CAPS
the ringdown arc honestly (the overtone lever has a ceiling) and gives an actionable modeling
prescription (use 2 modes).

## Honest scope

Equal mode self-SNR; white-noise / `t=0`-start Fisher (v2.219 idealizations); the n=2 qEFT
deformation coefficient is NOT published (only n=0,1 are), so this measures the n=2 mode's
*effect* on the n=0/n=1 resolvability, not the n=2 R4 reach itself. The 3-mode condition number
~3e9 is still safely invertible in double precision; a 4th mode would approach the precision limit
(itself the point -- the wall). This REFINES v2.220/v2.221 (the overtone advantage needs a
low-order model) without overturning the 2-mode result. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209);
the full isospectrality splitting still needs the un-sourceable polar correction (v2.218); the
`dtq_1` perturbative-delicacy caveat (v2.217) carries.
