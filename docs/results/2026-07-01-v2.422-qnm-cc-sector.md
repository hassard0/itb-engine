# v2.422 - CORE EXTENSION CC1: the cosmological-constant / dark-energy sector

The engine had no vacuum-energy coupling — the one big quantum-gravity problem it never touched. This is the
**first slice of a new core-engine sector** (user-chosen direction). It adds a dimensionless vacuum-energy
parameter `g_Lambda` (dark-energy density in cutoff-scale units: >0 de Sitter, <0 anti-de Sitter, 0 Minkowski)
and its first swampland constraint — the **refined de Sitter conjecture** (Ooguri-Palti-Shiu-Vafa 2018) — wired
**opt-in** (`build_stack(include_cc_sector=True)`) so the entire existing stack, all frameworks, and all goldens
are untouched (the g_R4-tower precedent). The new constraint is tagged **`sourced_proxy`** in the rigor registry
— the honest tiering from the de-toying arc automatically files this conjectural swampland statement in the
proxy layer, not the source-exact core.

## Physics + result

A positive vacuum energy at a potential extremum satisfies the refined dS conjecture only if the potential is
sufficiently **concave** (the tachyonic second condition `M_Pl² min(V'')/V ≤ −c'`). The candidate's dark energy
rides the Starobinsky R² scalaron (`g_R2` is the R² inflaton, v1.86), whose plateau is concave, so the condition
maps to `g_Lambda ≤ g_R2 / c_dS`.

All 6/6 checks pass. The candidate **admits a positive cosmological constant**, capped at `g_Lambda ≤ g_R2`;
modest AdS and Minkowski are consistent. So the **same g_R2 that drives inflation (v1.86) and anchors the theory
as its keystone (v2.396) also caps the dark energy the candidate can carry** — a genuine new cross-link between
the curvature sector and the cosmological constant, consistent with the refined dS swampland conjecture.

## Honest scope

A **first-proxy encoding of a conjectural statement**, tagged as such. The refined dS conjecture is itself
unproven/debated, and the map from its abstract `V''/V` condition to the engine's dimensionless `g_R2` is
order-of-magnitude — the robust content is "a positive Λ is bounded by the scalaron curvature"; the exact `c_dS`
is O(1) and the linear `g_Lambda ≤ g_R2` form is the simplest proxy, not a derived relation. `g_Lambda` is
dimensionless (vacuum energy in cutoff units); the engine still has no absolute scale, so this does **not**
address the CC *magnitude* problem (why Λ ~ 10⁻¹²⁰ M_Pl⁴) — it addresses the *structural* question of whether a
positive Λ is admissible and what bounds it. The sector is opt-in, so nothing about prior candidate results
changes. "Accommodates dark energy" means a positive `g_Lambda` is feasible up to the curvature bound, not that
the observed value is predicted.
