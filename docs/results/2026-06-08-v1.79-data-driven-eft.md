## What we set out to do, and what we found

The plan was to construct the single best EFT consistent with **theory + sub-mm
gravity + cosmic birefringence** — the affirmative payoff of the data arc. Trying
to do so revealed something sharper than a fitted point: **the combined region is
empty-or-knife-edge**, and the reason is a clean, quantitative tension the engine
can state as a prediction.

## The four-way pinch — anomaly inflow is the hinge

Direct feasibility probing (not optimization) pinned the binding constraint
exactly: **generalized anomaly inflow**,

        g_R2_parity² + 2·g_R3_parity² ≤ ρ · g_4 · g_R2 ,   ρ = 0.06 .

This caps the parity coupling by the matter×curvature product. Combined with:

- **sub-mm gravity** (v1.77): g_R2 ≤ 0.063 (unscreened scalaron), and
- **helicity positivity**: |g_R2_parity| ≤ g_R2,

the largest parity coupling a consistent, sub-mm-safe EFT can carry is
g_R2_parity ≤ √(ρ·g_4·g_R2), which with g_R2 ≤ 0.063 is small — and hence so is the
predictable cosmic-birefringence angle β = κ_β·g_R2_parity.

## The result: a ceiling on birefringence, and a 2.8σ tension

Maximizing the achievable β over the feasible region under each scenario:

| scenario | β_max | g_R2_parity,max | vs measured 0.34° ± 0.09° |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UNSCREENED** (theory + sub-mm) | **0.09°** | 0.025 | **2.8σ below — TENSION** |
| **SCREENED** (sub-mm vacuous, g_R2 free) | **0.32°** | 0.094 | **0.24σ — accommodated** |

**The engine cannot produce the measured cosmic birefringence with an unscreened
dark-energy-scale scalaron.** Anomaly inflow + sub-mm gravity together cap it at
β ≤ 0.09°, ~2.8σ below Minami–Komatsu's 0.34°. Relax the sub-mm bound (screening),
and g_R2 can be ~0.33, lifting the ceiling to 0.32° — consistent.

So the v1.79 synthesis is a **falsifiable either/or** linking two experiments
through a consistency axiom:

> If the cosmic-birefringence signal is real, then a consistent EFT can match it
> **only if the scalaron is screened** (chameleon/Vainshtein/dark coupling).
> Unscreened dark-energy-scale modified gravity is in ~2.8σ tension with the
> birefringence — the two cannot both hold at face value.

This dovetails with v1.77 (unscreened sub-mm gravity already excludes the
dark-energy scalaron) and v1.78 (birefringence wants a sizeable parity coupling):
the only way to keep both experiments is screening. The engine reaches this from
its *own* anomaly-inflow axiom, with no input from the screening literature.

## Honesty

- This is a **target/tension under stated assumptions**, not a claim about nature:
  κ_β = 3.4 and ρ_inflow = 0.06 are order-of-magnitude; β is a **3.6σ hint**, not a
  discovery; the unscreened/screened dichotomy is itself a simplification of
  density-dependent screening (v1.77).
- The robust, assumption-light content is the **structure**: anomaly inflow ties
  the parity coupling to the matter/curvature sector, so a sub-mm-bounded
  (unscreened) curvature sector *forces* a small birefringence — a real,
  qualitative ceiling, not an artifact of the exact numbers.
