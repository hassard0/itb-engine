- **ET and SKA open the graviton sector.** Einstein Telescope/CE (GW birefringence)

## The physics that sets the ranking (Dr. M.)

- **Blue tilt.** GW velocity birefringence accumulates as a phase ∝ **f² · distance /
  M_CS**. The fractional effect grows with frequency, so for the *graviton* coupling
  **LIGO/Virgo ≫ PTA** in intrinsic sensitivity, and **Einstein Telescope/Cosmic
  Explorer** give the sharpest future graviton-sector test. PTA (nHz) is f²-suppressed
  for propagation birefringence (its chirality comes more from source than propagation).
- **The crucial caveat — two couplings, not one.** CMB cosmic birefringence is the
  axion–**photon** coupling (φ F F̃); GW/PTA birefringence is the axion–**graviton**
  coupling (φ R R̃). These are **independent** in a general EFT. Fixing the graviton
  coupling from CMB assumes a single axion with **g_γγ ~ g_RR** — a toy assumption.
  So the multi-messenger comparison does more than check consistency: **it tests the
  single-axion hypothesis.** A future ET/SKA graviton coupling inconsistent with the
  CMB-implied one would *break* the single-field picture — itself a discovery.
