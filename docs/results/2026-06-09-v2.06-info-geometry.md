## EFT space as a Riemannian manifold

Treat the space of EFTs as a manifold with the **Fisher information metric**
`g_ij = Σ_obs (∂O_a/∂θ_i)(∂O_a/∂θ_j)/σ²` (σ=1 toy), built from the observable Jacobians.
In Ruppeiner thermodynamic geometry (Dr. M.-confirmed): the **scalar curvature |R|
diverges at critical points and at the manifold boundary**, and is ~0 for a flat
(non-interacting) interior; R>0 is repulsive, R<0 attractive (Ruppeiner, RMP 67, 1995).
We map R over the **(a,c) = (g_R2, g_C)** slice — the conformal-collider plane (v2.03) —
through the island center, computing the Gaussian/scalar curvature via the Brioschi
formula on the finite-differenced metric.

(The first attempt used a (g_C, g_8) slice; that metric is *intrinsically flat* —
`E(g_C)dg_C² + const·dg_8²` has zero curvature — so we use (a,c), where the **a/c =
g_R2/g_C** observable couples both axes and produces real curvature.)

## Result — a curvature divergence at the edge, a flat interior

| region | Fisher scalar curvature |
|---|---|
| **small-g_C edge** (a/c → ∞) | **|R| ~ 2.2×10⁴** (diverges) |
| island interior (median) | |R| ~ 0.12 |
| at the island center | R ≈ **−0.25** (mildly negative) |
| divergence ratio | **~87,000×** |

- **The Fisher curvature *diverges* (|R| ~ 10⁴) toward small g_C** — the a/c = g_R2/g_C
  observable blows up and the metric degenerates (det g → 0) at the **Weyl² → 0 / a/c → ∞**
  manifold edge. This is exactly the Ruppeiner-style **criticality at the manifold
  boundary** Dr. M. predicted: where the description becomes singular, |R| → ∞.
- **The island interior is flat and statistically stable.** |R| ~ 0.12, with a mildly
  **negative** R ≈ −0.25 at the center (an "attractive"/clustering geometry in the
  Ruppeiner convention) — the sloppy interior is *not* critical. The **data-driven EFT and
  the v1.74 center sit deep in this smooth region**, far from any curvature ridge.
- **The consistency boundary is reached *before* the curvature singularity.** The
  build_stack boundary (cyan in the figure) encloses a region that does *not* extend to
  the small-g_C divergence — the singularity lies *outside* the island, at even smaller
  g_C. So the engine's consistent theories live in the **smooth, stable interior**, away
  from the information-geometric criticality: consistency fails (the constraints bite)
  before the *statistical* description breaks down.

## What it means

The conformal-collider information manifold is **negatively curved** (R<0, "clustering")
in its consistent interior and **singular at the a/c → ∞ edge** (Weyl² → 0). The favored
physics — the data-driven EFT, the island center — sits in the benign, low-curvature bulk.
A clean, honest reading: the engine's consistent theories are *statistically
well-conditioned* (the observables remain independent, the metric non-degenerate), and the
only criticality is the geometric edge where the central-charge ratio diverges, which the
hard consistency constraints rule out first.

## Honest scope

Toy σ=1, a 2D slice, and finite-difference curvature are numerically delicate; the robust
content is the **qualitative structure** — a curvature *divergence* at the a/c → ∞ /
metric-degeneracy edge and a *flat, mildly-negative interior* where the favored theories
live — not the precise |R| values. Sign convention (Ruppeiner): R>0 repulsive, R<0
attractive.
