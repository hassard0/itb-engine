## Method

The harness loads the v2.115 marginal likelihood JSON, then computes:

- likelihood weights from the network marginal grid
- weighted alpha means
- weighted 5% and 95% source-parameter intervals
- a source-parameter covariance matrix
- a v2.102-shaped packet with `reproduced_source_native_likelihood`
