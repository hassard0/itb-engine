# v2.249 - Horizon tidal heating: the absorption channel of the 'is it a black hole?' test

## Question

A black hole exchanges energy with an inspiralling companion through its HORIZON (tidal heating). A
horizonless exotic compact object (ECO) cannot. Does this give a third horizon-test channel
(complementing the static tidal Love number, v2.235/v2.236, and the ringdown echoes,
v2.247/v2.248), and how does it relate to superradiance?

## Result

The horizon flux is proportional to `(Omega_orbit - Omega_H)`, so its SIGN is the SAME superradiance
condition as v2.243-v2.246. The flux flips at `r_crit = (1/Omega_H - a)^{2/3}` where
`Omega_orbit = Omega_H` (prograde Kerr, `Omega_orbit = 1/(r^{3/2}+a)`):

```text
  a*     Omega_H   r_crit    r_ISCO    Omega_orbit(ISCO)    regime at ISCO
  0.10   0.0251    11.66     5.67      0.0735               absorbing (orbit -> hole)
  0.30   0.0768     5.45     4.98      0.0877               absorbing
  0.50   0.1340     3.65     4.23      0.1086               superradiant (hole -> orbit)
  0.70   0.2042     2.60     3.39      0.1439               superradiant
  0.90   0.3134     1.74     2.32      0.2254               superradiant
  0.99   0.4338     1.20     1.45      0.3644               superradiant
```

For SLOWLY-spinning holes the inspiral is ABSORBING at the ISCO (the standard tidal heating that
drains orbital energy into the hole). For FAST-spinning holes (`a* >~ 0.4`) `r_crit` drops below the
ISCO, so the ENTIRE stable inspiral is **superradiant** -- the hole feeds energy to the orbit and
spins down (e.g. `a*=0.9`: `r_crit=1.74 < ISCO=2.32`, `Omega_orbit=0.225 < Omega_H=0.313`). The
regime crosses over near `a* ~ 0.4-0.5`.

**A horizonless ECO has NO horizon flux of either sign**, so the inspiral gravitational-wave phase
differs. Horizon tidal heating is the ABSORPTION channel of the "is it a black hole?" test -- and its
sign is governed by the same `Omega_H` that drives superradiance, tying the horizon-test and
superradiance suites together.

## What this establishes

A third, distinct horizon-test channel (absorption) alongside the static response (Love number) and
the reflection (echoes), with the clean, verifiable result that its sign is the superradiance
condition -- so a prograde inspiral around a fast hole is superradiant throughout the stable orbit. It
unifies the session's two strong-field sub-programs (horizon tests + superradiance) through the shared
horizon angular velocity.

## Honest scope

The flux SIGN and the critical radius (`Omega_orbit` vs `Omega_H`) are exact-Kerr geodesic results
(verified). The flux MAGNITUDE and the actual GW PHASE shift (the observable that distinguishes BH
from ECO) require the full Teukolsky horizon flux and its post-Newtonian expansion -- horizon
absorption enters the phase at 2.5PN for Schwarzschild and 1.5PN (spin-enhanced) for Kerr -- not
computed here. Equatorial, adiabatic circular inspiral; `l=m=2` dominant tidal coupling. ECO
absorption is set to zero (a perfectly reflecting surface); partial reflectivity interpolates.
Reconstruction of a real horizon observable, not a detection forecast. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays
dark (v2.209).
