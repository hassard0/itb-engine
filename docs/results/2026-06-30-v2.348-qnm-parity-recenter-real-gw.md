# v2.348 - Does the theory survive re-centering under the REAL GW-birefringence bound, against the full stack?

## Question

v2.347 found the constructed parity value 0.06 in tension with the engine's own estimate of the real LIGO O3
GW-birefringence strength (`|g_R2_parity| <~ 0.05`, vs the toy-loosened 0.1) -- but it only examined the
two-constraint parity subspace. Does the construction survive against the FULL stack with the real GW bound,
or does tightening it empty the consistent+observed region?

## Result (it survives -- the real GW bound sharpens the parity prediction, it doesn't kill the theory)

Rebuilding the consistent+observed stack with `LIGOBirefringenceBound` tightened 0.1 -> 0.05:

```text
  original   g_R2_parity = 0.06    under standard stack:  FEASIBLE
  original   g_R2_parity = 0.06    under real-GW stack:   INFEASIBLE  (only: ligo_birefringence_bound)
  recentered g_R2_parity = 0.0486  under real-GW stack:   FEASIBLE
  changed couplings: ['g_R2_parity']   (matter + curvature untouched)
  parity window: [0.0471, 0.0783] (w 0.0312)  ->  [0.0471, 0.05] (w 0.0029)   ~11x tighter
```

1. The original construction (0.06) is feasible under the standard stack but goes infeasible under the real
   GW bound -- and the constraint that kills it is **exactly** `ligo_birefringence_bound`, nothing else. The
   tension is real and localized to the parity sector.
2. Re-centering the parity coupling to the middle of the surviving window `[0.0471, 0.05]`,
   `g_R2_parity = 0.0486`, is **feasible against the full tightened stack** (zero violations) -- the theory
   is restored by moving one coupling.
3. Only the parity coupling changes; matter and curvature are untouched (parity is the stiff, decoupled
   direction, v2.333), so every non-parity prediction is unaffected.

All 6/6 checks pass.

## What this establishes

Taking the real GW bound seriously is **not a falsification but a sharpening**: the parity prediction window
narrows from `[0.0471, 0.0783]` (width 0.031) to `[0.0471, 0.05]` (width 0.003), a ~11x tighter pin centered
at ~0.0486 instead of 0.06. The program is self-correcting: v2.347 surfaced the tension, v2.348 resolves it
and verifies the resolution **globally** (against the whole stack, not just the parity subspace). The
re-centered theory is a parity-violating construction whose coupling is now pinned to a narrow window
straddled by the CMB floor below and the real GW bound above -- exactly the near-term falsification straddle
v2.347 identified, now confirmed to leave a non-empty feasible region.

## Honest scope

The `0.05` real-O3 GW figure is the engine's own docstring estimate (order-of-magnitude, with cutoff-scale +
propagation-distance uncertainty), so the re-centered value `0.0486` and the ~11x sharpening illustrate the
**structure** (a narrow CMB-floor-to-real-GW window), not precise numbers -- an O(1) shift in either
birefringence map moves both edges. The feasibility checks are exact against the toy-basis stack with the 4
data constraints, but the stack and maps are toy encodings. Re-centering changes only `g_R2_parity` because
parity is nearly decoupled in the engine's encoding (v2.333); a real UV theory could correlate parity with
the matter sector (anomaly matching hints at this), coupling the re-centering to other couplings -- not
captured here. Everything rests on the CMB hint being real (v2.329); if it is a systematic there is no parity
requirement and no window to re-center. Robust content: under the real GW bound the full region is non-empty
(the theory survives), the tension is localized to the GW constraint, and the parity prediction sharpens
rather than dies. Toy basis, order-of-magnitude prefactors.
