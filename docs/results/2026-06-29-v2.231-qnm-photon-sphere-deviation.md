# v2.231 - Cross-channel sensitivity of a photon-sphere deviation: a ringdown<->shadow null test

## Question

v2.230 showed ringdown and the black-hole shadow share the photon sphere. Turn that into a
QUANTITATIVE cross-channel test: deform the metric with a one-parameter non-Kerr "bumpiness"
`eps` (lapse `f = 1 - 2/r + eps/r^k`, M=1) and compute how `eps` shifts the shadow radius `b_c`,
the eikonal ringdown frequency (`Omega_c = 1/b_c`), and the ringdown damping (Lyapunov rate
`lambda`). What is locked, and what is independent?

## Result

Fractional sensitivities `d ln(.)/d eps` for three deformation profiles:

```text
  k    d ln b_c    d ln Omega_c    d ln lambda    locking residual
  2    -0.16667     +0.16667        +0.05556         +1.5e-10
  3    -0.05556     +0.05556        -0.03704         +1.1e-11
  4    -0.01852     +0.01852        -0.03704         +4.0e-13
```

Two structural results, both verified:

**1. Locking identity (parametrization-independent).** Because `Omega_c * b_c = 1` holds
*identically* for any static metric, `d ln(Omega_c) = -d ln(b_c)` for EVERY deformation (residual
< 1e-10). The eikonal ringdown frequency and the shadow radius shift by **equal and opposite
fractional amounts**. So a measured shadow size predicts the ringdown frequency and vice versa --
a **GW/EM null test of the photon-sphere hypothesis**: a violation means the ringdown is not the
light-ring mode, or the object is not a static photon-sphere body.

**2. Independent damping.** The Lyapunov rate `lambda` depends on the curvature `f''(r_ph)`, NOT on
`b_c`, so `d ln(lambda)` is not locked to `-d ln(b_c)` (e.g. `k=3`: `d ln lambda = -0.037` vs
`d ln Omega_c = +0.056` -- different sign and magnitude). The damping carries independent
information, so the joint (shadow + ringdown frequency + ringdown damping) **over-determines** a
one-parameter deformation -- a consistency over-test no single channel provides. The
(frequency, damping) pair even distinguishes deformation profiles (`k=2` gives `(+0.167, +0.056)`,
`k=3` gives `(+0.056, -0.037)`).

## What this establishes

A rigorous, self-contained upgrade of the v2.230 consistency into quantitative cross-channel
sensitivities, with a clean metric-independent identity (the ringdown<->shadow locking) and a
demonstrated independent third observable (the damping). It frames a concrete falsifiable test
structure for the photon-sphere/Kerr hypothesis combining gravitational-wave and electromagnetic
data.

## Honest scope

Eikonal (large-`l`) and static-spherical only: the locking identity is exact in the eikonal limit
(finite-`l` `O(1/l)` corrections, v2.229/v2.230); Kerr/rotation breaks the spherical shadow and
splits `Omega_c != lambda`. The deformation `f = 1 - 2/r + eps/r^k` is illustrative (a generic
photon-sphere bump), not a specific QG metric -- the LOCKING identity is metric-independent, but
the per-profile coefficients are illustrative. Turning this into a real bound needs measured
shadow + ringdown data and a chosen metric ansatz (not done here). This frames a QG null test; it
does not itself constrain a coupling. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
