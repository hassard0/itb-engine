## What this establishes

The honest answer to "how much does rigor force": **a lot of the parity-even structure.** Source-exact bounds
**box g_4, g_6, and g_R2 on both sides** to windows a factor ~1.5–3 wide around the candidate, and **cap g_R3 and
the parity coupling from above.** The irreducible residual freedom is exactly three understood things:
1. **g_8's upper scale** is unbounded by rigor — the observationally-*dark* matter direction (v2.381);
2. **the lower ends of g_R3 and parity** are free — a parity-conserving, cubic-curvature-minimal theory is
   rigorously fine, and *data* (cosmic birefringence) selects the nonzero values (this is the v2.420 rival, and
   the fundamental reason rigor cannot force the *full* candidate);
3. **the overall coupling scale**, where rigor gives only lower/ratio bounds, is closed from above by
   EFT-validity and data.

So the ceiling of option #2 is quantified: **rigor forces the candidate's parity-even *shape* into a tight cage;
data forces the *scale* (g_8, the upper cuts) and the *parity*.** This is stronger and more precise than "rigor
gives a loose family" (v2.419, which used the *bare* source-exact core): once the rigorous *consequences* are
included, the parity-even sector is caged, and what remains open is precisely the physically-understood dark
scale and data-selected parity. Rigor gets much of the way to forcing the candidate — and the gap is exactly the
part physics tells us needs measurement.

## Honest scope

A 1-D scan of each coupling with the *others held at candidate values*, so the edges are conditional slice bounds,
not the full 6-D rigorous region (looser — couplings co-vary); the robust content is *which edges are rigorously
bound vs free*, not the exact widths. "Effective rigorous core" = source-exact bounds plus the constraints they
imply (v2.412); edges are reported as bound by a source-exact ("rigorous"-tier) constraint. Rigorous bounds carry
the v2.411 "source-exact in form" caveat (prefactors O(1)-simplified; shown headline-robust in v2.427). g_8's
"unbounded" is within the scan cap (2.0). The parity floor being free is the v2.420 result.
