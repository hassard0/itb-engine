## The linchpin question

The whole discriminating program (v1.40–43) rests on one assumption: the
**gravitational** EFT cutoff Λ sits near the dark-energy scale (~2.4 meV). Is
that arbitrary, or is it picked out? This cycle confronts the assumption with
three independent facts at once:

1. the **observed** dark-energy density ρ_Λ = (2.4 meV)⁴;
2. the naive EFT vacuum energy ~ Λ⁴;
3. the engine's **EFT-validity bound** |g| ≲ 2 and the R²-scalar Yukawa
   λ_Y = √(6 g_R2)·ℏc/Λ.

**Planckian cutoff** → the cosmological-constant problem in full: vacuum energy
is 10¹²² × observed (the famous fine-tuning). The "fix" of degravitating it with
a light R²-scalar (Compton wavelength ~ Hubble) needs **g_R2 ~ 10¹²¹**, which
**violates the engine's EFT-validity box by ~121 orders of magnitude**. So the
engine's consistency bounds make the standard CC "fixes" manifestly unnatural —
the 10¹²⁰ problem reappears as a 10¹²⁰ box violation.

**Dark-energy-scale gravitational cutoff** → all three tensions vanish at once:
- vacuum energy ~ Λ⁴ ~ ρ_Λ automatically: **tuning factor 1.0, no CC problem**;
- g_R2 stays **O(1)** — inside the engine's validity box;
- the R²-Yukawa lands at **90 µm — sub-mm, testable** (v1.40–43).

## The crucial caveat (honestly flagged, and it is the testable part)

A meV **gravitational** cutoff sounds radical, but it is **not excluded**: gravity
is only probed down to ~50 µm, so the gravitational sector can have new physics
at meV while the Standard-Model cutoff is independent and far higher. This is the
standing rationale of short-range-gravity experiments. The hypothesis is
therefore **falsifiable exactly where v1.41 said to look**: a 1/r deviation at
70–130 µm confirms a sub-mm gravitational cutoff (and with it this resolution of
the CC naturalness puzzle); its absence pushes Λ up and re-opens the CC problem.

## Honest status

This does not *prove* the cutoff is at the dark-energy scale — it shows that
scale is the consistency-favoured one and that the alternative is ~121 orders
unnatural by the engine's own bound. The proposition is a hypothesis with a
sharp, near-term experimental test (sub-mm gravity at 70–130 µm). What the engine
adds is the quantitative three-way link: **CC naturalness ⇔ O(1) Wilson
coefficients ⇔ sub-mm gravitational signal.**
