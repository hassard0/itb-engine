## What this cycle does

The catalogue had 5 named frameworks (+3 engine-discovered). This adds three more
— **Hořava–Lifshitz** (Lorentz-violating UV completion), **Causal Set theory**
(discrete, non-local), and **Group Field Theory** (second-quantized spin foams) —
as encoders, and runs them through the corrected stack to test whether the
engine's "LQG robustly disfavoured" verdict generalises.

Taken at face value: Causal Sets join the survivors (43%, comparable to AS 54%,
string 66%); GFT and HL are disfavoured like LQG — so the anti-LQG verdict appears
to **extend to the spin-foam family and to Lorentz-violating gravity**, driven (as
always) by the cubic-curvature/parity structure (g_R3 ~ g_R2 and/or parity).

## The deeper finding (Dr. M.): the engine's verdicts are only valid in scope

Dr. M.'s cross-check surfaced something more important than the numbers — a
**scope limitation of the whole engine**:

- The amplitude-positivity bounds are *derived from* **analyticity, unitarity, and
  Lorentz invariance** of the S-matrix. **Hořava–Lifshitz explicitly breaks
  Lorentz invariance**, so those bounds do not apply in their standard form — the
  engine's exclusion of HL is **not reliable** (HL may evade the constraints
  rather than fail them).
- The swampland/positivity program assumes **locality**. **Causal sets are
  fundamentally non-local**, so the engine's "feasible" verdict for CS is equally
  **not meaningful** — CS merely lands in the allowed region of constraints that
  may not govern it.

> **The engine validly tests only LOCAL, LORENTZ-INVARIANT frameworks.** Within
> that scope (string, asymptotic safety, CDT, LQG, GFT, and the discovered
> branches) its verdicts stand. Outside it (Hořava–Lifshitz, causal sets) the
> feasibility result is an artifact of applying inapplicable constraints. This is
> a fundamental honesty boundary the new frameworks made explicit — and it is
> arguably the most important output of this cycle.

## A genuine coefficient uncertainty: GFT

The engine (with LQG-like values, g_R3 ≈ g_R2, ratio 1.0) excludes GFT. Dr. M.
expects the GFT **condensate phase to recover a cleaner Lorentz-invariant GR
limit** with the cubic coupling suppressed (ratio ≈ 1.5), which would **pass**
forward positivity. So GFT's verdict hinges on whether its effective cubic
curvature inherits LQG's spin-foam enhancement (fails) or is condensate-suppressed
(passes) — a real, unresolved physics question, and exactly the kind of
coefficient the realism program flags as load-bearing.

## Net

- **Catalogue expanded** to 8 named + 3 discovered frameworks (`horava_lifshitz`,
  `causal_set`, `group_field_theory` encoders + tests; 384 suite green).
- **Within-scope verdict generalises:** local Lorentz-invariant approaches with
  cubic ~ leading and/or parity violation (LQG, GFT-if-LQG-like) are disfavoured;
  those with suppressed cubic and parity conservation (string, AS, CDT, causal-set
  values) survive.
- **Scope boundary made explicit:** the engine cannot validly adjudicate
  Lorentz-violating (HL) or non-local (CS) theories — a real limitation, honestly
  recorded.
- **GFT is the interesting borderline case**, hanging on a single
  condensate-cubic coefficient.

## Honest limitations

All three frameworks' coefficients are representative (like the original five);
Dr. M. flagged which are motivated vs guesses. The scope finding (HL/CS outside
the constraints' assumptions) is robust and qualitative; the GFT pass/fail is
genuinely coefficient-dependent.
