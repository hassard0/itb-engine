## The test

In dynamical Chern–Simons gravity the parity coupling g_R2_parity splits the
parity-even/odd (and ±m) gravitational quasi-normal modes of a ringing black
hole. The leading correction scales as ζ = (ℓ_CS/r_g)⁴ with ℓ_CS the
Chern–Simons length and r_g = GM/c² the gravitational radius, so the fractional
QNM split is df/f ~ g_R2_parity · ζ. The question: in the engine's unified
dark-energy-cutoff scenario (ℓ_CS ~ ℏc/2.4 meV ≈ 82 µm), is it observable?

## Result — a deep null

| source | r_g | df/f at dark-energy scale | ℓ_CS needed to detect |
|---|---|---|---|
| LIGO stellar BH (60 M☉) | 89 km | **7×10⁻³⁸** | ~29 km |
| LISA massive BH (10⁶ M☉) | 1.5 Gm | 9×10⁻⁵⁵ | ~3×10⁵ km |

At the dark-energy scale the ringdown split is **~34 orders of magnitude below**
LIGO ringdown sensitivity. To be observable, ℓ_CS would need to be ~tens of km —
*comparable to the black hole itself*, i.e. astrophysical-scale new physics, not
the dark-energy scale.

## Why this matters — it explains the probe hierarchy

This is not a failure; it is the physical reason the engine's parity coupling
lives in the **propagation sector**:

> A strong-field effect is **local** and (ℓ_CS/r_g)⁴-suppressed — crushingly
> tiny for a meV-scale coupling and km-scale black holes. A propagation effect
> (birefringence, chiral Hellings–Downs) is O(g_R2_parity) **per wavelength but
> accumulated over Gpc baselines** — boosted by ~(distance/wavelength). That
> lever arm is why LIGO GW birefringence, PTA chiral HD, and CMB EB can probe
> g_R2_parity ~ 0.09 while black-hole ringdown is ~34 orders short.

So the four working probes (LIGO birefringence, chiral HD, CMB EB, anomaly-inflow
ceiling) are exactly the long-baseline / accumulated ones; the strong-field
ringdown is the one that *cannot* see a dark-energy-scale parity coupling.

## Falsification value of the null

The dark-energy-cutoff hypothesis therefore **predicts a clean null in
ringdown parity splitting.** Conversely, *a ringdown parity-splitting detection*
by LIGO/LISA would require ℓ_CS ~ km — astrophysical-scale parity violation —
which would **disfavour the unified dark-energy-cutoff picture** (v1.44/46/47).
Ringdown thus acts as a discriminator of the parity *scale*, not just its
existence:

- propagation probes detect → parity exists at the dark-energy scale (unified picture ✓);
- ringdown also detects → parity scale is astrophysical (unified picture ✗);
- only propagation detects, ringdown null → consistent with the dark-energy cutoff.

## Honest limitations

Order-of-magnitude: the exact dCS QNM coefficients are spin- and mode-dependent
(the split for Schwarzschild appears at O(ζ²)/requires rotation; we use the
standard ζ = (ℓ_CS/r_g)⁴ scaling with an O(1) coefficient). The conclusion — a
~34-order suppression at the dark-energy scale — is far larger than any O(1)
coefficient uncertainty, so the null is robust.
