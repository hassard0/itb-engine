## The constraint (derived, not fitted)

For 2→2 graviton scattering, analyticity + unitarity + crossing express the
low-energy Wilson coefficients as integrals over a positive spectral density.
After subtracting the graviton t-channel pole (Caron-Huot–Van Duong 2021;
Caron-Huot–Mazac–Rastelli–Simmons-Duffin 2021; CEMZ), the leading curvature
coupling and the cubic coupling are controlled by the *same* positive integral,
which is dominated by its leading-energy behaviour. Hence the leading coupling
must dominate the subleading one:

```
    g_R2  >=  c * g_R3            (c an O(1) forward-dispersion ratio)
```

`src/itb/constraints/graviton_forward_positivity.py`,
`GravitonForwardPositivity(c=1.2)`, class A. It is genuinely independent of the
existing graviton constraints: a **lower** bound on g_R2 (the others bound it
from above or mix it with matter).

This is honestly discriminating rather than circular: the bound orders
frameworks by their **g_R2/g_R3 ratio**, and LQG's *documented* feature is that
spin-foam vertex amplitudes inflate the cubic coupling (the `lqg_induced`
encoder sets g_R3 = 0.30, giving ratio 1.00). The constraint fails LQG first
for exactly the reason LQG is LQG — not because it was tuned to.

## Result 1 — the constraint orders frameworks by g_R2/g_R3 ratio

| framework | g_R2 | g_R3 | ratio | standalone fails at c ≥ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LQG-induced** | 0.30 | 0.30 | **1.00** | **1.03** (first) |
| string tree-EFT | 0.20 | 0.15 | 1.33 | 1.35 |
| CDT | 0.22 | 0.15 | 1.47 | 1.48 |

At the canonical c = 1.2, LQG is the **only** framework excluded by this
constraint alone (margin −0.06); string/AS/CDT all pass. AS — which keeps the
leading coupling most dominant — is the most robust, exactly as Dr. M.
predicted.

In the full corrected-RFC stack, sweeping c over its plausible range
[0.8, 1.6], the full-stack feasible fraction is: **AS 0.88, CDT 0.82,
string 0.67, LQG 0.00**.

  voiding RFC and BNOSSW) is **restored on a defensible footing**: a

  most robust survivor; LQG is excluded in ~98% of the plausible prefactor box,

  (3) the cubic graviton-matter bound** — three independent constraints, no
  longer the discredited RFC or the fragile BNOSSW proxy.

## Honest limitations

- The precise value of c requires evaluating the literal two-sided
  Caron-Huot–Van Duong bound (a ratio of Gegenbauer-weighted spectral
  integrals). The *structural* content (leading dominates cubic) is robust;
  c ~ O(1) is the canonical placeholder, swept over [0.8, 1.6].
- The framework g_R3 values are themselves representative, not derived. The
  result "LQG fails because g_R3 ≈ g_R2" is only as good as the claim that
  spin-foam amplitudes really do inflate g_R3 to ≈ g_R2 — a checkable statement
  against published spin-foam vertex asymptotics, and the natural next
  literature target.
