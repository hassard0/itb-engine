# v2.262 - Forced per-framework higher-curvature towers from Hankel positivity (lqg is marginal)

## Question

v2.261 established that a consistent UV completion's curvature couplings form a Stieltjes moment
sequence (all Hankel matrices PSD), with v2.234's `g_R4 >= g_R3^2/g_R2` as the leading 2x2 minor.
What does this hierarchy say about the engine's ACTUAL framework couplings -- the forced higher
operators (`g_R5`, `g_R6`, ...) per framework, and is there a per-framework consistency diagnostic?

## Result

Given a framework's first two curvature moments (`m_0 = g_R2 > 0`, `m_1 = g_R3`), the MINIMAL
Hankel-saturating tower -- the lower envelope of the positivity-allowed region -- is a
single-effective-state geometric sequence

```text
  g_R(k+2) = g_R2 * x^k ,   x = g_R3 / g_R2   (one spectral state at mass^2 = x, weight g_R2)
```

so `g_R4_min = g_R3^2/g_R2` (exactly v2.234), `g_R5_min = g_R3^3/g_R2^2`, etc. -- and the 2x2 Hankel
det vanishes (sits on the boundary). Per engine framework:

```text
  framework            x=g_R3/g_R2   g_R4,g_R5,g_R6           regime
  pure_gr              (no curvature)
  string_tree_eft      0.750         0.1125,0.0844,0.0633     converging (operators decouple)
  asymptotic_safety    0.667         0.0667,0.0444,0.0296     converging (operators decouple)
  lqg_induced          1.000         0.3000,0.3000,0.3000     MARGINAL (operators do NOT decouple)
  cdt                  0.682         0.1023,0.0697,0.0475     converging (operators decouple)
```

The ratio `x = g_R3/g_R2` is the effective mass^2 of the lightest curvature state and a new
per-framework diagnostic: `x < 1` means the higher-curvature operators DECOUPLE (a converging tower,
a healthy EFT), while `x = 1` means the tower is MARGINAL -- the operators do not decouple.
**`lqg_induced` sits exactly at `x = 1` (g_R2 = g_R3 = 0.3), a marginal/non-converging tower**, while
string / asymptotic_safety / cdt all have `x < 1` converging towers.

## What this establishes

A concrete per-framework prediction extending v2.234/v2.261 from abstract structure to the engine's
real couplings: each consistent framework's minimal higher-curvature tower is geometric with ratio
`x = g_R3/g_R2`, and that ratio is an independent consistency diagnostic. `lqg_induced` falls at the
marginal boundary `x = 1` -- consistent with its already-anomalous status (it fails 4/38 engine
constraints, and v2.234 showed it needs the largest forced `g_R4`). The moment tower thus flags lqg
a second, independent way.

## Honest scope

The minimal (single-effective-state) tower is the FLOOR of the Hankel-allowed region -- a framework's
true higher couplings could be larger (more spectral states); the geometric tower is the lower
envelope, not a unique prediction. The `x = g_R3/g_R2` diagnostic inherits the v2.261
representative-prefactor caveat: the operator<->moment indexing is the representative structural form,
so "marginal" is a representative statement about the engine's toy couplings, not an operator-exact
decoupling claim. The framework couplings are the engine's encoded (toy) values. A theory-structure
diagnostic, not a new bound on a measured coupling. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
