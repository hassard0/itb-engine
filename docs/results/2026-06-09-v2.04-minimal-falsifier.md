# v2.04 — The minimal falsifier: the single cheapest measurement that would confirm or kill the data-driven EFT

**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Compute:** Vulcan.

---

## Falsifiability, ranked

The data-driven EFT (v1.79) makes a 9-observable fingerprint. v2.04 asks the
Popperian question: **which single measurement, soonest, would most decisively confirm
or kill it?** For each observable we compute the **falsification power** = |prediction −
null| / σ_projected — how many sigma a measurement would separate the EFT from its null
(parity-even β=0, KSS η/s, Lloyd-saturated dC/dt, unscreened sub-mm, r=0) — using the
v1.92 roadmap precisions and years.

## Result — a one-observable theory

| observable | prediction | null | σ (year) | power | experiment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CMB birefringence β** | 0.32° | 0° | 0.03° (2030) | **10.6σ** | LiteBIRD / CMB-S4 |
| inflation r | 0.004 | 0 | 0.001 (2030) | 4.0σ | CMB-S4 / Simons |
| sub-mm screening | screened | unscreened | (2028) | ~2.9σ | next-gen sub-mm |
| PTA chirality Π_V | 2% | 0 | 1.2% (2032) | 1.7σ | SKA-PTA |
| GW birefringence | 0.094 | 0 | 0.08 (2035) | 1.2σ | ET / CE |

- **The minimal falsifier is cosmic birefringence (LiteBIRD/CMB-S4, ~2030) at 10.6σ** —
  it is both the *highest-power* test and the *first* to cross the 5σ kill line. No
  earlier/cheaper measurement (the 2028 sub-mm screening test is only ~2.9σ) reaches
  decisive separation first.
- **The EFT's falsifiability is concentrated in a single observable.** Birefringence
  (~10σ) dwarfs the next-best (inflation r, ~4σ). The data-driven EFT is effectively a
  **one-observable theory**: it lives or dies by the cosmic-birefringence measurement.
- **The most dangerous observation** is a tightened β *consistent with zero*: the EFT's
  entire identity rests on β = 0.32°, so a LiteBIRD/CMB-S4 null there collapses it.

## What this says about the program

This sharpens v2.01 (the Bayesian comparison favored the data-driven EFT at 99.9%, also
**birefringence-driven**) and v1.92 (the forecast): the engine's *favored* theory and its
*entire experimental case* both rest on one ~3.6σ hint. That is a **feature for
falsifiability** (one clean, soon, high-power test settles it) and a **caveat for
confidence** (a single observable, currently a hint, carries the whole conclusion). The
honest reading: by ~2030 LiteBIRD/CMB-S4 will largely confirm or kill the engine's
data-driven EFT — a rare, crisp, near-term verdict for a quantum-gravity-flavored model.

## Honest scope

Projected σ's are roadmap estimates; the β = 0.32° vs 0 separation rests on the
β = 3.4·g_R2_parity map and the ~3.6σ birefringence *hint*. The robust content is the
**ranking** (birefringence first and highest) and the **single-observable concentration**,
not the precise 10.6σ.
