## Interpretation

The one-axis `g_8` target is not the only route. A future joint packet with
engine-normalized `g_8` plus a source-backed secondary axis can break the
tightest eligible pair with a much wider second-axis tolerance. Numerically,
`g_R2` is the widest target. The `g_C` option is slightly tighter but aligns
with the existing Weyl/`g_8` frontier.
