# v2.81 - g8 route decision: direct public-data promotion retired

## Question

v2.80 found public energy-correlator data products and useful theory bridges, but
none satisfied the v2.79 `g_8` adapter gate. This iteration turns that into an
explicit route decision so current QCD data products cannot be accidentally
promoted into quantum-gravity framework verdicts.

## Decisions

| route | decision |
|---|---|
| `cms_energy_correlator_direct_g8_promotion` | Retired for the current engine. The public CMS product targets QCD jet structure and `alpha_s`, not engine `g_8`. |
| `heavy_ion_eec_direct_g8_promotion` | Retired for the current engine. The measurement targets QCD medium response, not low-energy QG EFT `g_8`. |

## Interpretation

The `g_8` branch is now cleaner: direct promotion of current public
energy-correlator products is retired, but the route itself is not dead. Two
honest paths remain:

- publish or find a source-backed adapter from an energy-correlator or detector
  observable into engine `g_8` with covariance
- publish or find a new spin-4/detector measurement directly in the low-energy
  QG EFT basis

Until one of those exists, the `g_8` route remains non-claimable.
