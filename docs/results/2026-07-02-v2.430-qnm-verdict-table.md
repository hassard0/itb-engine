## What this establishes

Because the candidate *requires* the correlated triple, it is confirmed by **exactly one** of the eight coarse
outcome patterns and not confirmed by the other seven — a **maximally falsifiable** joint prediction. The two
nearest alternatives are cleanly separated: β=0 with the rest present → the **parity-conserving rival**; an
all-null result → **standard cosmology**. Crucially the *correlation* makes partial patterns decisive: a parity
detection **without** the matter tension breaks the keystone correlation and **kills** the candidate — a
discrimination independent single-probe tests could not make. So the candidate's ~2030 empirical status is a
clean, decision-ready verdict — the concrete answer to "how will we know," and the furthest an EFT-carving
program can push toward settling the theory short of the data itself.

## Honest scope

The verdict *logic* follows from two engine-verified feasibility facts (the candidate requires the correlated
triple, v2.429; β=0 selects the rival, v2.420) plus a binary discretization of three continuous measurements — so
it's a decision-support map, **not** a likelihood/Bayes computation (a full posterior needs the error models and
the O(1)-toy map magnitudes). The three outcomes are coarse binaries; boundary cases (marginal β, marginal w)
aren't resolved. The "matter tension" and "w band" outcomes rest on the toy coupling→observable maps (sign/pattern
robust, thresholds not). The parity axis is contingent on the ~3.6σ birefringence framing. "1/8 confirms" counts
discrete patterns, not probability mass.
