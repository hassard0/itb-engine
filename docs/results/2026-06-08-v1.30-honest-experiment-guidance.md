# v1.30 — Honest experiment guidance: which observable separates the survivors

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Builds on / fixes:** v1.29 (saturated priority metric).

---

## Context

v1.29 showed the exclusion-count priority ranking saturates (forecast-central-
value artifact). The engine's `first_disagreement` machinery gives the
non-saturating alternative: for each pair of *surviving* frameworks (string, AS,
CDT — LQG is excluded, v1.26), which physical observable separates them most?
This does not depend on an arbitrary forecast central value — only on the
frameworks' predicted observables.

Observables: forward 2→2 amplitudes per sector over an energy grid —
matter (g_4 s² + g_6 s³ + g_8 s⁴), graviton (g_R2 s² + g_R3 s³), parity
(g_R2_parity s² + g_R3_parity s³). Reference precision σ = 0.02.

## Result — measure high-energy matter forward scattering

| pair | best observable | signal | S/N |
|---|---|---|---|
| asymptotic safety vs string | **matter forward amplitude** | 0.300 | 15.0 |
| asymptotic safety vs CDT | **matter forward amplitude** | 0.300 | 15.0 |
| string vs CDT | graviton forward amplitude | 0.020 | **1.0** |

(reference) LQG vs AS: matter amplitude, S/N 22.5 — the excluded framework is
most cleanly separated from AS by the same matter observable.

## Actionable conclusions

1. **The single most informative measurement is the high-energy matter-sector
   forward amplitude** (2→2 matter scattering, sensitive to g_4/g_6/g_8). It
   separates asymptotic safety from both string and CDT at S/N 15 — because AS
   carries systematically smaller matter couplings (g_4 = 0.40 vs 0.50–0.55).
2. **String and CDT are nearly degenerate** (S/N 1.0) — the engine cannot
   distinguish them with any of these observables at σ = 0.02. This is the
   observable-level confirmation of v1.26's finding that the surviving
   frameworks cannot be ranked at toy precision; string and CDT in particular
   sit almost on top of each other in observable space.
3. This *replaces* the v1.29 / v1.19 exclusion-count ranking as the engine's
   honest experimental output: not "experiment X excludes N% of a plane" (an
   artifact), but "to distinguish theory A from B, measure observable O at S/N k"
   — a statement an experimentalist can act on.

## Honest limitations

- Signals are in toy-coefficient units; the S/N values scale with the (toy)
  σ = 0.02 and with the representative framework coefficients. What is robust is
  the *ordering*: matter-sector forward scattering ≫ graviton ≫ parity for
  separating the survivors, and the string/CDT near-degeneracy.
- A full treatment would use the Fisher metric (`fisher.py`) with realistic
  per-observable noise models and marginalize over the coefficient uncertainty
  of v1.25 — the natural v1.31 step.
