# v2.49 - Birefringence evidence freshness: alive, but not claimable

## Question

The tower/native route is now blocked on missing framework-owned spectra rather than engine plumbing. The best non-tower route is the data-driven EFT, but that route is dominated by one observable: isotropic cosmic birefringence. This audit asks whether the latest primary-source measurements still support the engine's baseline beta assumption strongly enough to justify a discriminator claim.

## Result

Artifact: `experiments/results/v2.49/birefringence_evidence_freshness.json`

The audit ingests five primary-source rows:

| row | beta | total sigma | zero exclusion | status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| engine_baseline_wmap_planck | 0.342 deg | 0.094 deg | 3.64 sigma | published hint |
| act_dr6 | 0.215 deg | 0.074 deg | 2.91 sigma | published hint, systematics unresolved |

| planck_pr4_map_space_commander | 0.48 deg | 0.283 deg | 1.70 sigma | systematic dominated |
| hybrid_ilc_planck_pr4 | 0.32 deg | 0.12 deg | 2.67 sigma | methodological cross-check |

All five rows preserve a positive sign for beta. A naive fixed-effect combination of the two most independent instrument families used here, WMAP+Planck and ACT DR6, gives beta = 0.264 deg with sigma = 0.058 deg, or 4.53 sigma from zero. This is useful as a pressure test, but it is not a discovery claim because it is below 5 sigma and does not close shared modelling, calibration, or foreground concerns.

The route status is therefore:

```text
alive_but_not_claimable
```

## Interpretation

This is the first loop after the tower-route ownership closure that makes a non-tower path concrete again. It does not solve quantum gravity. It says the one-observable parity route is still empirically alive and currently stronger than the native tower route, but the evidence remains too systematics-limited and too narrow to promote into a framework-level discriminator.

The next high-value loop is either:

- a non-circularity audit of the data-driven EFT construction, checking whether the engine's favored EFT can survive without reusing birefringence as both input and validation; or
- a `weyl_g8_discriminator_frontier` audit that asks whether the Weyl^2 / g_8 plane can separate frameworks without relying on a single parity observable.
