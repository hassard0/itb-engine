# v2.221 - Two-mode (220+221) joint Fisher: does the overtone advantage survive mode covariance?

## Question

v2.220 computed the per-mode R4 reach treating the fundamental and the first overtone as
INDEPENDENT single damped sinusoids. But in a real ringdown both modes are present at once,
overlap in time, and their parameters are CORRELATED -- a joint fit marginalizes each mode over
the other, inflating the uncertainties. **How much does mode covariance degrade the per-mode
resolvability, and does the v2.220 "overtone dominates the R4 reach by ~2 orders of magnitude"
conclusion survive?** Answer it from first principles, fully self-contained.

## Method

Waveform `h(t) = sum_n A_n e^{-t/tau_n} cos(omega_n t + phi_n)`, `t >= 0`, `n in {0,1}`.
8 parameters `{A_n, phi_n, omega_n, tau_n}`; joint Fisher `Gamma_ij = int_0^inf d_i h d_j h dt`
(white noise). The per-mode resolvability is read from the JOINT covariance `C = Gamma^{-1}` (so
the other mode is marginalized), and compared to the v2.219 ISOLATED single-mode coefficients.

## Result

```text
                 R_f (joint)   inflation     R_tau (joint)   inflation
 fundamental        2.414        4.36x           9.177         4.59x
 first overtone    15.509        4.11x          13.971         5.52x

 mode correlations:  corr(omega_0, omega_1) = +0.866    corr(tau_0, tau_1) = +0.853
 amplitude-independent inflation:  TRUE
 overtone R4-reach advantage:  177x (isolated, v2.220)  ->  140x (joint)
```

Three findings:

1. **Covariance inflates BOTH modes' resolvability by ~4-5.5x.** The overlapping modes are
   strongly correlated (~0.86 in both frequency and damping), so a joint fit pays a real penalty
   the single-mode v2.219/v2.220 numbers ignored -- those coefficients were OPTIMISTIC by this
   factor.

2. **The inflation is independent of the amplitude ratio** -- a pure geometric Schur-complement
   property (the overtone Fisher block scales as `A_1^2`, the cross-block as `A_1`, so
   `rho_1^2 C_11` is amplitude-invariant), validated numerically at `A_1/A_0 = 1` vs `0.3`.

3. **The overtone advantage survives.** Because both modes degrade by a *similar* factor, the
   overtone's R4-reach advantage falls only modestly, from 177x (isolated) to **140x** (joint) --
   still ~2 orders of magnitude, still concentrated entirely in the overtone damping channel. The
   v2.220 conclusion is ROBUST to mode covariance.

## What this establishes

A genuine honest refinement: it CORRECTS the v2.220 absolute reaches (optimistic by the ~4-5.5x
covariance inflation) while CONFIRMING its headline -- overtone ringdown dominates the R4 reach
even after the realistic two-mode joint analysis. The strong mode correlations (~0.86) and their
amplitude-independence are clean, derivable properties of the overlapping-mode geometry, not
fitted numbers.

## Honest scope

Equal mode self-SNR comparison (the overtone is typically excited weaker; reach scales as
`1/rho_n`); white-noise / `t=0`-start Fisher (v2.219 idealizations); two modes only (higher
overtones add further covariance); qEFT coefficients carry the v2.217 perturbative-delicacy
caveat (`dtq_1 = 171.35`, valid for `gamma << 6e-3`). This REFINES v2.220 without overturning it.
Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209); the full isospectrality splitting still needs the
un-sourceable polar correction (v2.218).
