## Claim Boundary

This contract makes the Weyl/G8 route executable without treating internal PCA
geometry as evidence. A real packet still needs source-backed engine-normalized
`g_C` and `g_8` packets, joint covariance or likelihood, bounded cross-axis
correlation, framework projection math, an exclusion statistic, scan policy,
reproducible analysis material, and external adversarial review.

Current public/source candidates remain useful but incomplete. They contain
pieces such as GW public data, higher-curvature analyses, Weyl constraints, and
partial-wave formalism, but none provides the full joint engine-normalized
`g_C+g_8` likelihood packet.
