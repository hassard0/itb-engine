## The question worth answering

The deepest near-term question is not *which* UV completion, but whether
**gravity is quantum at all**. There is now a concrete experiment that answers
it: **gravitationally-induced entanglement** (Bose, Mazumdar, Morley, Ulbricht,
Toroš, Paternostro, Geraci, Barker, Kim, Milburn 2017; Marletto–Vedral 2017). If
two mesoscopic masses, each placed in a spatial superposition, become **entangled
purely through their mutual gravitational attraction**, then — by the theorem
that local operations and classical communication cannot create entanglement —
the gravitational field must carry **non-classical (quantum) degrees of
freedom**. A positive result is, effectively, a direct detection of the quantum
nature of gravity. The hardware (levitated nanodiamonds, matter-wave
interferometry) is under active development.

## 1. The foundational test is within near-future reach

For the canonical parallel setup — two masses m in superpositions of size Δx,
centres separated by L, interacting for time τ — the entangling phase is
φ ≈ (G m² τ / ℏ)(Δx²/L³). With near-future-but-serious parameters:

| m | Δx | L | τ | entangling phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10⁻¹⁴ kg | 250 µm | 450 µm | 2 s | **≈ 0.09 rad** |

≈0.09 rad is **witnessable** with squeezing / repetition — i.e. this is an
experiment of the next decade, not the next century.

**What it means for the engine:** every theory in the engine's feasible region
is a theory of *quantized* gravity. So a positive GIE result is consistent with
the entire program; a robust **null** result would falsify the whole feasible
region at once (it would mean the higher-derivative-EFT-of-a-quantum-graviton
picture is wrong). GIE is therefore the single cleanest yes/no test of the
program's foundational assumption.

## 2. The engine's g_R2 is already a short-range-gravity observable

This is the part the ITB engine is uniquely positioned to supply. In quadratic
(Stelle 1977) gravity, the R² term — the engine's coefficient **g_R2** — adds a
massive scalar mode, giving a **Yukawa correction** to the Newtonian potential:

    V(r) = −(G m / r) [ 1 − (1/3) e^(−r/λ_Y) + (4/3) e^(−r/λ_2) ],
    λ_Y = √(g_R2) / Λ

with Λ the scale of new gravitational physics (the EFT cutoff — **not
necessarily Planckian**). This Yukawa is exactly what torsion-balance
short-range-gravity experiments (Eöt-Wash, current reach ≈ 50 µm — already a
constraint in the engine) measure, and it shifts the GIE phase when the
superposition probes r ~ λ_Y.

**If the cutoff sits at the scale current experiments already probe (Λ⁻¹ ≈ 50 µm),
the candidate theories make distinct, testable predictions:**

## The honest hinge (a real prediction, not a hedge)

Everything in §2–3 hinges on **where the gravitational EFT cutoff Λ is**. If new
gravitational physics enters near the scales short-range experiments already
reach (sub-mm), the engine's g_R2 differences are **measurable now**. If Λ is
Planckian, the Yukawa is sub-µm and only GIE's foundational yes/no survives. That

the place to look, and a detection there would simultaneously reveal the cutoff
scale and discriminate the candidate completions.** The engine has turned an
abstract coefficient into a target with a length in microns.
