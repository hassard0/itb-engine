# v2.283 - The edge of the feasible region: how each framework sits against the full engine

## Question

Extends v2.281/v2.282. The intended question was "how much headroom do the feasible frameworks have"
-- but running the canonical 38-constraint engine roster revealed a sharper, more honest picture, so
this cycle reports the engine's actual verdict on the feasible region's edge (and corrects the premise).

## Result

```text
  framework          feasible   # violated   what it fails
  pure_gr            YES        0            (strictly inside)
  string_tree_eft    no         1            repulsive_force_conjecture
  asymptotic_safety  no         1            repulsive_force_conjecture
  cdt                no         1            repulsive_force_conjecture
  lqg_induced        no         6            repulsive_force + graviton_forward_positivity,
                                             cross_sector_efthedron, cft_flat_space_bound,
                                             complexity_cutoff, bnossw_monogamy
```

Three facts, all from the engine's literal `check()` verdict: (1) **only pure_gr is strictly feasible**;
(2) string/AS/cdt each miss by EXACTLY ONE constraint -- `repulsive_force_conjecture`, a magnitude-driven
gravitational-universality bound -- a single marginal near-miss within the engine's representative-O(1)
prefactor band; (3) **lqg is the deep outlier**, failing five MORE constraints, the curvature
moment-tower positivity family (`graviton_forward_positivity, cross_sector_efthedron, cft_flat_space`)
plus complexity and entanglement-monogamy -- exactly the v2.282 pathology its `x = g_R3/g_R2 = 1`
boundary saturation trips. And lqg is uniquely **unrepairable by curvature scaling**: turning the
curvature down to relieve positivity makes it fail `anomaly_cancellation` (which NEEDS the curvature
terms, verified -- curvature off fails `['anomaly_cancellation']`), so it is caught in an
anomaly-vs-positivity tension no single curvature scale resolves. All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The engine's feasible region is sharply structured: pure GR strictly inside; the string /
asymptotic-safety / CDT encodings one marginal magnitude-bound step outside (a single shared
near-miss); and the lqg encoding deep outside with a genuine internal tension. This is the cleanest map
yet of the frameworks' standing, and it ties together v2.262 (lqg's `x = 1` boundary), v2.281 (lqg the
lone GW/swampland failure) and v2.282 (lqg's curvature-driven anomaly) into one feasibility-geometry
picture -- while honestly correcting this cycle's own initial "headroom" premise once the engine showed
all higher-derivative frameworks marginally infeasible.

## Honest scope

The engine's literal verdict from `check()` on the canonical `build_stack`; the failure counts and
binding constraints are exact. The "one marginal constraint" standing of string/AS/cdt and the "six
failures" of lqg are the engine's TOY encodings with representative O(1) curvature prefactors -- this is
NOT a claim that string theory is in the swampland, but that the engine's representative encoding of it
sits one prefactor-sized step over a single bound (the repo's "honest by construction" design). The lqg
anomaly-vs-positivity tension is read from the failing-constraint sets at curvature scale 0 vs 1
(resolved at the engine level, not a continuum proof). A consistency / feasible-region geometry result,
not a new constraint or a claim about the physical frameworks; this writeup corrects the cycle's initial
"headroom" framing with the engine's literal verdict.
