# v1.96 — The species scale: tying the EFT cutoff to a tower of states (a new swampland constraint)

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core; 1M-sample island measurement).

---

## A genuinely new constraint

Dvali's **species scale**: with N light species below the cutoff, gravity becomes
strongly coupled at Λ_species ~ M_Pl / √N (d=4), so the EFT cutoff cannot exceed it.
Large higher-derivative couplings source light states (the R² scalaron mass m ~
cutoff/√g_R2; van de Heisteeg–Vafa–Wiesner), so a large **curvature** sector → many
species → a low gravity cutoff → a bound on the coefficients. We encode

        N(g) = 1 + ν·(|g_R2| + |g_C| + |g_R3|) ≤ N_max ,

as a new Class-C constraint `SpeciesScaleBound` (ν=2, N_max=3), wired into the stack.

## Result — it is redundant in this basis

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| island volume *with* species bound | 234 / 1M |
| island volume *without* it | **234 / 1M** |
| **irreplaceability (island growth on removal)** | **0.0%** |
| frameworks excluded by it alone | **none** (max N = 2.7, Hořava) |
| species-failures that also fail the distance conjecture | **63%** |
| species-failures that also fail the complexity cutoff | 44% |

The species-scale bound carries **no independent information** in this toy basis: the
island is identical with or without it, no framework is excluded by it alone, and its
exclusions are already covered by the other swampland constraints — *especially the
distance conjecture* (63% overlap) and the complexity cutoff (44%). Two physically
*distinct* swampland principles (the species scale and the distance/complexity bounds)
**collapse to overlapping coefficient bounds** in this 8-coefficient basis.

This joins a recurring, honest pattern: like the **a-theorem** (v1.70) and the
**Wald-entropy/WGC** condition (v1.82), the species scale is a constraint of genuinely
independent *origin* that is *implied* by the existing axioms here. Dr. M.'s structural
point is borne out — it has the *same form* (an aggregate-coupling bound) as the
complexity cutoff, and in this basis that form-overlap makes it redundant.

## What would make it bite

In a fuller treatment with an explicit **tower spectrum** (the species counted from
actual light states, not a proxy for the curvature-coupling magnitude), the species
scale would constrain a direction the aggregate bounds don't — the *number* and
*spacing* of tower states, tied to the distance-conjecture geodesic. The toy basis,
lacking a resolved tower, cannot see that independent content — exactly the
basis-resolution lesson of v1.70→v1.71 (the a-theorem became load-bearing only once the
basis split Euler from Weyl²).

## Honest scope

Toy species-counting map (N = 1 + 2·Σ|curvature couplings|, N_max = 3); the robust
content is the **structure** (large curvature couplings → light tower → low species
cutoff → a coefficient bound) and the empirical finding that, *in this basis*, that
bound is redundant with the distance/complexity sector.

## Headline

The Dvali **species-scale bound** — a genuinely new swampland constraint tying the EFT
cutoff to a tower of light states — is **redundant in this toy basis**: it grows the
consistent island by 0% when removed and excludes no framework, because its
curvature-coupling form overlaps the distance conjecture (63%) and complexity cutoff
(44%). Like the a-theorem and the WGC/BH-entropy condition, it is a constraint of
independent origin awaiting a resolved tower-spectrum basis to carry independent
information.
