# v2.216 - Cross-validating the R4 ringdown sensitivity: qualitative agreement, quantitative disagreement

## Question

v2.215 computed the engine's R4 odd-parity QNM sensitivity (McManus route, single-term
delta_V). The repo independently extracted the qEFT/ParSpec deformation coefficients from the
SAME source (Silva-Ghosh-Buonanno, arXiv:2205.05132). Do the two source-backed routes to the
same physical shift AGREE? If so, the result is claim-grade.

## Result

Artifact: `experiments/results/v2.216/qnm_r4_cross_validation.json`.

Both routes give the complex QNM shift of the l=2 n=0 fundamental from the R4 operator:

```text
McManus route (per eta_2):  Delta omega = -3.184 - 5.637 i
qEFT route   (per gamma):   Delta omega = -0.0790 - 0.0540 i
```

The magnitudes differ (expected: eta_2 vs gamma are different couplings). The decisive,
**convention-INDEPENDENT** comparison is the damping-to-frequency shift ratio Im/Re (the
complex direction) -- because eta_2 <-> gamma and the r_g normalization are REAL and only
rescale the magnitude:

```text
damping/frequency ratio:  McManus 1.770   vs   qEFT 0.684
relative difference 159%   (complex phase off by 26 deg)
```

**Qualitative agreement, quantitative disagreement.** Both routes agree on the SIGNS: the R4
quartic operator LOWERS the ringdown frequency (Delta omega_R < 0) AND increases the damping
(Delta omega_I < 0). But the convention-independent ratio differs ~2.6x. Since this ratio is
unaffected by the eta_2<->gamma map or the r_g convention, **the mismatch is real and is NOT
one of the v2.215 normalization caveats.**

## Diagnosis (negative preserved, not tuned)

The single odd-parity `(r_g/r)^10` term is most likely an **incomplete** representation of
the qEFT modified potential: the full theory's QNM shift carries contributions beyond the one
leading axial power (additional radial terms, or the qEFT "theory" combines more operator
content than the single eta_2 monomial). So the McManus single-term contraction captures the
right qualitative behavior but not the full complex shift. A ParSpec damping-time-vs-omega_I
convention subtlety -- which cannot be pinned because the 2205.05132 appendix did not render
in fetch -- cannot be fully excluded. Per the repo's discipline, the negative is preserved;
the result is NOT tuned to force agreement.

## What this establishes

- A genuine, convention-independent cross-check between two independent published routes,
  built honestly. It VALIDATES the qualitative R4 ringdown effect (frequency down, damping up)
  two ways, and it FALSIFIES the claim that the single `(r_g/r)^10` term is the complete shift.
- The v2.215 number (`-1728 e_10`) is therefore the leading-axial-power piece, qualitatively
  right but quantitatively incomplete -- exactly the kind of honestly-bounded result the
  program produces, rather than an overclaimed "validated sensitivity".

## Honest Scope (claim gate CLOSED)

A claim-grade R4 ringdown sensitivity still needs: the COMPLETE source-backed `delta_V` (all
radial terms) from the 2205.05132 appendix; the even-parity (Zerilli) sector; the
`eta_2 <-> gamma` map; and systematics + external review. The parity-odd operator axis
`g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
