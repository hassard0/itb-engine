# v2.213 - Independent Riccati QNM solver + the precision requirement for claim-grade sensitivities

## Question

v2.212 showed the WKB-at-peak operator->QNM sensitivity is stable but inaccurate (misses
the published e_j by ~150%, because it is a peak quantity not a global one). The obvious
fix is to compute the sensitivity by a FULL re-solve of V + alpha*delta_V (which captures
the global physics). Does a full re-solve work, and what solver precision does claim-grade
require? To find out we built a second, independent QNM solver.

## Result

Artifact: `experiments/results/v2.213/qnm_riccati_solver.json`.

**(A) An independent QNM method (Riccati / log-derivative).** Integrating the bounded
log-derivative `phi = psi'/psi` (`phi' = -(phi^2 + omega^2 - V)`) from both ends and
matching avoids the amplitude blow-up that capped the naive shooting. It lands omega_220
within **~4%** of the exact value -- a genuinely different method from the WKB solver
(v2.210), confirming the frequency is in the right place:

```text
l=2 n=0: Riccati rel.err 3.8e-2   WKB rel.err 1.5e-3   (two methods agree to ~4%)
l=2 n=1: Riccati rel.err 1.8e-1   WKB rel.err 2.8e-3
```

The Riccati is BC-limited (the finite-r outgoing asymptotic) and does NOT improve with
domain size, so a few-% is its floor without a higher-order asymptotic series -- a ballpark
cross-check, not a precision tool.

**(B) The precision requirement (the real finding).** Computing e_j by a full Riccati
re-solve of `V + alpha*delta_V_j` and finite-differencing does NOT reproduce the published
coefficients -- it is erratic:

```text
j=0: e_resolve rel.err 3.46
j=1: e_resolve rel.err 2.34   (even the real-part sign flips)
j=2: e_resolve rel.err 0.54
```

The ~4% omega error does not cancel in the difference (the BC error differs between V and
V+delta_V), so finite-differencing two few-%-accurate omegas for the small e_j (~0.05-0.12)
is meaningless.

## What this scopes

Putting v2.212 and v2.213 together:

```text
method                         e_j accuracy      verdict
WKB-at-peak shortcut (v2.212)  ~150% off         peak-only, misses the global overlap
few-% direct re-solve (v2.213) erratic (0.5-3.5) frequency precision too low
```

**A claim-grade in-house operator->QNM sensitivity requires a QNM solver accurate to
<< e_j*alpha ~ 1e-3** -- i.e. a high-precision **pseudospectral (Chebyshev hyperboloidal)
or Leaver continued-fraction** solver, not a peak shortcut and not a few-% direct re-solve.
This is a concrete, quantitative requirement, established by ruling out the cheap methods.
The claim gate stays closed.

## Honest Scope

The QNM *frequencies* remain validated (WKB ~0.2%, cross-checked by Riccati to ~4%). What
is shown is that the *sensitivity* -- the quantity the R4 ringdown discriminator needs --
demands ~1e-3 frequency precision that only a spectral/continued-fraction solver provides.
No framework claim is made; the parity-odd axis stays dark (v2.209).
