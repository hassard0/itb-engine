# v2.05 — The observable redundancy map: how many independent things can we actually measure?

**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core; 1.5M-sample island).

---

## Nine observables, but how many independent?

The engine has 9 observables but only 8 coefficients on a ~3.4-dimensional island
(v1.73/v2.02) — so the fingerprint *must* be redundant. v2.05 measures by how much:
sample the island, evaluate all 9 observables at each survivor, and compute their
correlation structure across the island.

## Result — ~3 independent axes, with the Euler-vs-Weyl² split as the block structure

Over 402 island survivors, the **effective number of independent observables is 3.01**
(participation ratio of the 8×8 correlation eigenvalues; 9th observable is constant). The
9-observable fingerprint has only **~3 independent axes** — most observables are redundant.
The correlation blocks are strikingly clean:

| block | observables | correlation | driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Euler / R²** | sub-mm Yukawa ↔ **η/s** | **0.99** | g_R2 |
| **Weyl²** | BH entropy ↔ **complexity dC/dt** | **0.96** | g_C |
| parity | cosmic birefringence, PTA chirality | (one cluster) | g_R2_parity |
| matter | matter scattering | — | g_4, g_6 |
| **dead** | **inflation r** | **zero variance** | none (g_R2-independent) |

- **The Euler-vs-Weyl² split (v1.71) is visible in the *observables*.** Sub-mm gravity and
  η/s — both Euler/R²-driven — are **99% correlated** (essentially the same measurement).
  BH entropy and complexity — both Weyl²-driven (Euler being topological, v1.82/v1.98) —
  are **96% correlated**. The two holographic blocks are *distinct*: η/s probes one axis,
  complexity the orthogonal one. The basis split that organizes the constraints (v1.71),
  the observables (v1.98), and the island (v2.02) also organizes the **fingerprint
  correlation matrix**.
- **Inflation carries zero discriminating information**: the Starobinsky (n_s, r)
  prediction is coefficient-independent (zero Jacobian) and so has *zero variance* across
  the island — confirming v1.88, where it pinned nothing in the Fisher design. A beautiful
  measurement, but it cannot *discriminate* among consistent EFTs.

## What it means

The engine's apparent richness (9 observables) reduces to **~3 genuinely independent
probes** — a *matter* axis (g_4/g_6), an *Euler/R² holographic* axis (η/s ≈ sub-mm), and a
*Weyl² holographic* axis (complexity ≈ BH entropy) — plus the parity sector (the only
*measured* signal). This **echoes the ~3.4-dimensional island** (v1.73) from the *prediction*
side: the theory space is ~3.4-d, and the observables that probe it span ~3 independent
directions. Measuring more observables in the same blocks adds little; the leverage is in
covering *different* blocks — exactly the v1.92/v2.02 message that the under-probed
directions (g_C/Weyl², g_8) are where new measurements pay off.

## Honest scope

Toy observable maps; the robust content is the **block structure** (which observables are
redundant) and the **effective number of independent probes** (~3), not the precise
correlation values. The η/s↔a/c correlation is only moderate here because a/c = g_R2/g_C
mixes both blocks, whereas sub-mm and η/s are *pure* g_R2.
