canonical swampland-strength constraint stack.

## What this means honestly

This is the engine's first *prescriptive* output — not "pass/fail" or
"how robust", but a specific direction in coefficient space that all
candidate UV completions converge to. Three readings:

**(a) The toy values were systematically too large in g_R².** Most
likely. When I (and Dr. M. in earlier iterations) chose toy framework
values, we picked O(1)-normalized numbers without literature precision.
The engine is now telling us those choices over-emphasize the graviton
sector relative to matter.

**(b) The swampland constraints are too tight at canonical prefactors.**
Possible. If the literal RFC and Scalar WGC bounds are looser than
γ=1.0 and β=1.0, the projection shifts would shrink.

**(c) Real published Wilson coefficients for string-EFT, AS, CDT, LQG
also need this correction.** The publication-actionable question.

The third reading is the one a literature-aware research group could
test. **Specifically: do the published α'-corrected tree-level string
Wilson coefficients have g_R²/g_4 ratio closer to 0.33 (string toy) or
closer to 0.31 (engine-projected) ?** A 5% answer settles whether the
toy was off or the engine is producing a real correction.
