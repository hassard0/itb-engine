## Interpretation

Retiring direct `g_8` public-data promotion leaves no solved discriminator. The
next highest-value route is native tower evidence because it does not require a
new experiment and already has adapter and guard infrastructure. The missing
object is specific: framework-owned native tower spectra with asymptotic regime
ownership, passed through the existing acceptance harness.

The `g_8` route remains alive, but only through a source-backed derivation or a
new spin-4/detector measurement. Current public QCD energy-correlator products
stay retired as direct framework-discriminator inputs.
