## What this does

v1.46/47 predicted cosmic birefringence β ≈ 0.34° from the dark-energy axion.
This turns that into the actual observable: the **CMB EB power spectrum**.
Birefringence rotates the polarization, mixing E and B and generating a
parity-odd EB correlation that is **exactly zero in standard ΛCDM**:

    C_ℓ^{EB,obs} = ½ sin(4β) [C_ℓ^EE − C_ℓ^BB]

We computed the real ΛCDM C_ℓ^EE and C_ℓ^BB with CAMB (Planck cosmology:
H₀=67.4, Ω_b h²=0.0224, Ω_c h²=0.120, n_s=0.965, τ=0.054, lensed) and the
predicted EB for β = 0.34°.

## Result — an acoustic-peak-shaped EB signal at ~1% of EE

β = 0.34° ⇒ sin(4β) = 0.0237. Predicted spectrum (D_ℓ = ℓ(ℓ+1)C_ℓ/2π, µK²):

| ℓ | D_ℓ^EE | D_ℓ^BB | **D_ℓ^EB (predicted)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 0.78 | 0.003 | 0.009 |
| 300 | 9.10 | 0.031 | 0.108 |
| 500 | 8.32 | 0.062 | 0.098 |
| 700 | 37.4 | 0.083 | 0.442 |
| **1000** | **42.1** | 0.097 | **0.499** |
| 1500 | 12.7 | 0.083 | 0.150 |
| 2000 | 8.65 | 0.052 | 0.102 |

- The EB spectrum **inherits the E-mode acoustic peaks** (since EB ∝ EE − BB ≈ EE),
  peaking at **ℓ ≈ 990 with D_ℓ^EB ≈ 0.50 µK² (1.18 % of EE there)**.
- This acoustic-peak-shaped, ~1%-of-EE EB correlation is **precisely the signal
  Minami–Komatsu fit in the Planck data** (~3σ), and a clean target for **LiteBIRD
  and CMB-S4**, which will measure β to a few ×0.01°.

The prediction is now a concrete curve, not a number: see

## The multi-messenger close

The same dark-energy axion that produces this EB spectrum predicts (v1.46) a
**correlated gravitational-wave birefringence** at |g_R2_parity| ~ 0.09 (LIGO O5),
and (v1.47) sits on the **DESI** evolving-dark-energy hint with w₀ ≈ −0.84. So the
EB spectrum here is one of five linked signatures of a single field:

> CMB EB (this cycle, Planck/LiteBIRD/CMB-S4) ⟷ GW birefringence (LIGO O5) ⟷
> DESI w(z) ⟷ sub-mm gravity (v1.41) ⟷ the cosmological-constant scale (v1.44).

Measuring the EB amplitude and the GW-birefringence partner pins the same axion
two independent ways — confirming or killing the picture.

## Honest limitations

- The EB *shape* (acoustic peaks ∝ EE) and the *β→EB* relation are exact given
  birefringence; the *amplitude* rests on β = 0.34° (a ~3σ hint with calibration
  systematics). A miscalibrated polarization angle mimics a constant β — the
  reason LiteBIRD/CMB-S4 (with better angle calibration) are decisive.
- ΛCDM cosmology fixed at Planck best-fit; no tensor modes (r=0). Including r>0
  raises BB slightly and marginally lowers EB at low ℓ.
