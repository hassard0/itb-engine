- claimable tower exclusion: `phi_mean - 2*sigma > critical_phi`
- claimable tower allowance: `phi_mean + 2*sigma <= critical_phi`

## Interpretation

v2.25 gives the future tower-adapter work a concrete target. A sourced spectrum does
not merely need to provide a central value; its uncertainty interval must sit entirely
on one side of the tower threshold.

The defensible claim is:

> The engine now knows exactly what a future `TowerSpectrum` must prove, but no current
> framework has such a spectrum. There are still no framework-level tower exclusions.
