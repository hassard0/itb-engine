# v2.260 - Leaver continued-fraction machinery (validated) + an honest blocker diagnosis

## Question

A focused, serious attempt at the high-accuracy Leaver QNM solver -- the infrastructure that would
close the recurring v2.212 cross-multipole WKB-overshoot gap and unblock the 330/440 no-hair
violation (blocked since v2.226). Can it be delivered, and if not, exactly what is in the way?

## Outcome: solver NOT delivered, blocker precisely diagnosed, machinery shipped

Leaver's method needs the 3-term recurrence
`alpha_n a_{n+1} + beta_n a_n + gamma_n a_{n-1} = 0` for the series coefficients of `u` in
`psi = e^{iwr}(r-1)^{-iw} r^{2iw} u(r)`, `u = sum a_n ((r-1)/r)^n`. Deriving the coefficients
SYMBOLICALLY fails two ways:

- **v2.228** (symbolic-exponent shortcut, the power exponents kept as symbols): tractable but WRONG
  -- it silently drops the `e^{iwr} = e^{iw/(1-x)}` essential-singularity contributions (whose
  derivatives bring down `1/(1-x)^2` factors), giving a spurious 5-term recurrence with no `a_{n+1}`
  coupling.
- **v2.260** (essential singularity kept explicit, `sympy.simplify(L/prefactor)`): correct in
  principle but INTRACTABLE -- `sympy.simplify` of `exp` of a rational argument `1/(1-x)` times the
  operator times out (exit 124 at 220 s, even for `l=2` alone, on Vulcan).

So the Leaver coefficients require **analytic hand-derivation** (compute the `e^{iwr}` derivative
contributions by hand and match the 3-term recurrence), the published **Leaver-1985 c-coefficients**,
or a **different high-accuracy method** (high-precision direct integration with asymptotic-SERIES
boundary conditions -- the series are regular Frobenius at both ends, NOT essential-singularity-
blocked). This is a dedicated multi-step effort, not an autonomous-tick task -- recorded, not forced.
No guessed/unvalidated solver is shipped (claim-gating discipline).

**What IS delivered:** the reusable, VALIDATED continued-fraction machinery, parameterized by the
`(alpha, beta, gamma)` coefficient functions:

```text
CF evaluator: numeric 4.561553 == analytic 4.561553   (constant-coefficient tail, match to 1e-9)
root-finder:  recovered omega* = 0.7 as 0.700000000   (constructed known root, to 1e-9)
machinery_works = True
```

so a future dedicated effort just supplies the hand-derived coefficients and the QNM solver is
complete.

## What this establishes

An honest negative result with reusable infrastructure: the precise diagnosis of why the Leaver
solver is hard (the essential singularity defeats symbolic derivation), the concrete resolution paths,
and the validated continued-fraction evaluator + root-finder ready for the coefficients. This is the
claim-gated outcome -- a tested framework and a clear next step, not an overclaimed or unvalidated
solver.

## Honest scope

A NEGATIVE-result + infrastructure cycle: the QNM solver itself is NOT delivered (the Schwarzschild
recurrence coefficients are the missing piece). The continued-fraction evaluator and root-finder are
exact and tested on analytic cases, but they have NOT been run on the physical Leaver recurrence
(which does not yet exist in a validated form). No QNM value is claimed here; the validated v2.210
WKB solver remains the in-house QNM tool. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
