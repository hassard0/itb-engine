# v2.274 - QNM asymptotics, black-hole area quantization, and the Barbero-Immirzi tension

## Question

A fresh QG thread tying the validated QNM ringdown (v2.210) to black-hole entropy (v2.257/v2.258) and
loop quantum gravity. The highly-damped (`n -> infinity`) Schwarzschild QNMs have a universal real
part `Re(omega_inf) = T_H ln 3`. What does Bohr's correspondence principle make of it, and where does
it lead?

## Result

Reading the asymptotic frequency as a mass-transition quantum (`dM = hbar Re(omega_inf)`):

```text
  Re(omega_inf) = ln3 / (8 pi M) = 0.04371   (M=1, the famous Hod/Motl-Neitzke value)

  area quantum   dA = 32 pi M dM = 4 ln3 = 4.39445   (M CANCELS -> universal, verified M=0.5..10)
  entropy quantum dS = dA/4 = ln3 = 1.09861   ->   e^{dS} = 3.0000 microstates per quantum
  area spectrum   A_N = 4 ln3 * N   ->   S_N = ln3 * N = A_N/4   (Bekenstein-Hawking at every level)

  Barbero-Immirzi:  Dreyer (j=1)  gamma = ln3/(2 pi sqrt2) = 0.1236
                    standard LQG  gamma ~ 0.2375           (j=1/2 counting)
                    tension ratio = 1.92
```

The mass `M` cancels in the area change, so the area quantum is **universal** (`4 ln 3`, verified
across `M = 0.5..10`); the evenly-spaced spectrum gives an entropy quantum `ln 3` -- **exactly 3
microstates per area quantum** -- and reproduces `S = A/4` at every level, a discrete
statistical-mechanical origin for the entropy the v2.257/v2.258 thread treated as continuous. Matching
to the loop-quantum-gravity area spectrum with the lowest spin `j=1` (Dreyer) fixes the Barbero-Immirzi
parameter to `0.124` -- but standard LQG entropy counting gives `~0.2375`, a factor `1.92` larger. All
6/6 consistency checks pass.

## What this establishes

The ringdown spectrum, black-hole entropy and quantum geometry are one story: the asymptotic QNM
frequency implies a universal area quantum, a `3`-microstate entropy quantum reproducing `S = A/4`, and
a candidate Barbero-Immirzi value. And it surfaces a genuine open problem -- the `~2x` disagreement
between the QNM/Bohr area-quantization picture (`gamma = 0.124`) and microscopic LQG state counting
(`gamma ~ 0.2375`) -- the kind of honest gap that marks where quantum gravity is genuinely unsettled,
not a solved consistency check.

## Honest scope

The asymptotic value `Re(omega_inf) = ln3/(8 pi M)` is the SOURCE-BACKED Hod/Motl-Neitzke result, used
here as input: the repo's WKB QNM solver (v2.210) is a LOW-overtone approximation and cannot reach the
`n -> infinity` regime, so this cycle does NOT re-derive `ln 3` numerically -- it derives the
area/entropy/Barbero-Immirzi CONSEQUENCES of that established input, which are exact arithmetic. The
Bohr-correspondence area-quantization interpretation (Hod) is a CONJECTURE, not a theorem; whether the
relevant frequency is the real part (Hod) or `|omega|` (others), and whether the `j=1` (Dreyer) or
`j=1/2` (standard) LQG spin assignment is correct, are exactly the debated points -- the `~2x`
Barbero-Immirzi tension is reported, not resolved. A QG-structure result connecting validated repo
threads (QNM, entropy) to LQG, not an engine constraint refit.
