# v2.393 - SWING: matter sources the leading gravitational correction -- the anomaly forbids matter without curvature (converse of matter dominance)

## Question

Matter dominance (v2.389/391) bounds the gravitational sector from *above*. Is there a bound from *below* --
does consistency *force* higher-curvature gravity to exist?

## Result (matter without curvature is forbidden)

Direct feasibility test (theory-only stack):

```text
  pure GR (all couplings zero):                        feasible
  matter sector, ZERO higher-curvature gravity:        INFEASIBLE -> violates anomaly_cancellation
  matter + leading curvature g_R2 = 0.193:             feasible

  anomaly slab |g_4 g_6 - g_R2^2| <= 0.2, so given g_4 g_6 = 0.212 it forces
      g_R2 in [sqrt(0.212-0.2), sqrt(0.212+0.2)] = [0.108, 0.642]     -- lower edge > 0
  with only g_R2 on, other constraints cap it: feasible g_R2 in [0.108, 0.226] -- nonzero at both ends
```

All 5/5 checks pass. Pure GR (nothing) is fine, but **matter without a nonzero leading gravitational correction
is inconsistent** -- `g_R2 = 0` is forbidden once matter is present.

## What this establishes

Matter **sources** the leading gravitational correction -- the converse of matter dominance. The anomaly locks
`g_R2` to matter: given the matter product `g_4 g_6`, it forces `g_R2` into a nonzero band around
`sqrt(g_4 g_6)`. Combined with matter dominance (which caps `g_R2` from above), the leading curvature coupling
is pinned from **both sides** -- the anomaly floors it, the WGC/screening cap it. So the higher-curvature
gravitational sector is neither absent nor free: it is **sourced into existence by matter** (from below, via the
anomaly) and **bounded in size by matter** (from above, via positivity/WGC). This completes the matter-gravity
locking picture -- (i) matter forces the leading correction to exist (here), (ii) matter sets its scale and
caps it at <=40% of matter's (v2.389/391), (iii) the moment tower organizes the higher curvature couplings
among themselves (v2.375). It re-reads "quantum gravity is not general relativity" concretely: GR *with matter*
is inconsistent in this engine; consistency demands the higher-curvature completion, and demands it be
matter-sized. Pure GR survives only in the empty (no-matter) limit.

## Honest scope

The 4D gravitational-anomaly-cancellation constraint is the engine's TOY simplification (Alvarez-Gaume-Witten
representative, `c_anom=1`, `tolerance=0.2`) -- and physically 4D gravity has no perturbative gravitational
anomaly for the graviton alone (the AGW anomaly is for chiral matter in 4k+2 dimensions), so "matter sources
`g_R2`" is a statement *within this toy encoding* of anomaly matching, not a first-principles 4D theorem. The
robust structural content is that an anomaly-MATCHING (equality/slab) condition between the matter product and
the leading curvature coupling forbids the `g_R2 = 0` corner -- the generic consequence of any matching
condition of this shape. The band edges scale with the toy tolerance/`c_anom` -- toy-basis numbers; the robust
facts are qualitative: pure GR feasible, matter-only forbidden, `g_R2=0` forbidden given matter, `g_R2` forced
into a nonzero band. Theory-only (no data); data narrows further. The wide tolerance (0.2 vs `g_4 g_6 = 0.212`)
means the anomaly is a loose band, not a sharp locking -- "matter determines `g_R2`" overstates it; "matter
forces `g_R2` nonzero and into a band" is exact. Robust content: consistency (anomaly matching) forbids matter
without a nonzero leading gravitational correction -- matter sources gravity from below, complementing matter
dominance from above. Toy anomaly encoding, robust forbid-the-zero-corner structure.
