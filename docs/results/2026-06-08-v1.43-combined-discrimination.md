## Context

With matter amplitude, graviton amplitude, sub-mm Yukawa, and gravitational
birefringence all first-class observables, this computes the **total
discriminating power of the full experimental program**: for each candidate-
theory pair, the separation in measurement-noise units (S/N) summed in
quadrature over all four experiments, and which experiment dominates.

Assumed precisions: amplitudes σ=0.02; sub-mm gravity σ=0.01 (1% force, current
torsion balance); birefringence σ=0.01. A pair is "resolved" at total S/N > 3.

## Interpretation — a clean division of experimental labour

The full program partitions cleanly by what each experiment is *for*:

1. **Matter forward scattering** carries the bulk of the discrimination (15/21
   pairs) — the matter-coupling differences (g_4/g_6/g_8) are the broadest
   fingerprint, *provided* the angular/partial-wave information of v1.36 is used
   to lift the within-sector degeneracy.
2. **Gravitational birefringence** is the dedicated parity probe: it is the
   decisive experiment for exactly the four pairs involving a parity-violating
   theory (LQG, discovered_parity_violating). Nothing else cleanly separates
   them — birefringence is irreplaceable for the parity sector.
3. **Sub-mm gravity** is the tiebreaker for the one pair nothing else resolves:
   string vs CDT, whose g_R2 differ only 0.20 vs 0.22 → λ_Y 90 vs 95 µm. At 1%
   force precision their S/N is 2.6 (just shy of resolved); **sub-percent
   sub-mm gravity is the only route to telling them apart.**

## The string ≈ CDT degeneracy, confirmed a fourth time

string tree-EFT and CDT have now been found near-indistinguishable by four
independent measures: joint survival (v1.26), first-disagreement S/N (v1.30),
observable-space distance (v1.34), and now the full combined program (v1.43,
S/N 2.6). This is the engine's most robust *negative* discrimination result — and
v1.43 gives its one escape: push short-range gravity below 1% force precision.

## Where the program stands

The engine now produces, end-to-end and from first-class observables, a complete
experimental discrimination map: **which experiment resolves which pair of
candidate quantum gravities, at what precision.** A realistic near-future program
— matter scattering with partial-wave resolution, sub-mm gravity at ~93 µm to
≲1%, and GW birefringence ~10× past LIGO O3 — distinguishes every catalogued and
engine-discovered theory except the string/CDT pair, which needs sub-percent
short-range gravity.
