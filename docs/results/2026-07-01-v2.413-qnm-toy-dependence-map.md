## What this establishes

A doubly constructive de-toying step. The two toy pieces one would *most* worry about turn out **harmless**: the
`complexity_cutoff` (an explicit research-grade conjecture with an arbitrary weighted-L2 form and `C_max=1.5`)
and the `swampland_distance_conjecture` (the very aspect-ratio proxy v2.410 showed misses the real field-space
physics) each open the candidate region only ~1.11×, and dropping them leaves the scale window *identical* — the
upper edge is set by the anomaly, not complexity. So **neither the v2.410 field-space gap nor the speculative
complexity bound actually threatens the candidate** — two worries retired. And it **pinpoints** the single
remaining toy family that does shape the result: the **anomaly sector** (`anomaly_cancellation` 1.36× +
`generalized_anomaly_inflow` 2.43×). Cosmic birefringence's 7.36× is real *data* leverage (v2.408), separated
out here. The engine now tags `HARMLESS_SPECULATIVE` and `LOAD_BEARING_TOY` in `stack.py`, so the next de-toying
cycle has exactly one target: derive/source the anomaly-sector inequality, or establish that its specific form
(matter × curvature ~ curvature²) is genuinely unsourceable in this gravity+matter basis and quantify precisely
what rides on it.

## Honest scope

Leverage is the opening of the local feasible fraction (constructed ± 0.15) when a constraint is dropped — a
local measure; a constraint could bind harder elsewhere, but "nearly redundant locally" is exactly the statement
that the candidate (which lives here) doesn't depend on it. "Harmless" means low leverage **and** unchanged scale
window **and** the constructed point still feasible — not that the constraint is meaningful in general (the SDC
and complexity bound remain speculative in *form*; the point is the candidate is *robust* to them). Cosmic
birefringence's 7.36× is real data leverage, listed only to separate it from the toy cuts. This is an engine
change (the two tags in stack.py) plus the leverage map that justifies it; it does not de-toy the anomaly — it
proves the candidate is robust to the two most speculative toys and isolates the anomaly as the one that
matters.
