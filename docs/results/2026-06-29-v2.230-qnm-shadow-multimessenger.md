# v2.230 - The photon sphere as the common origin of ringdown and the black-hole shadow

## Question

v2.229 showed the eikonal ringdown is governed by the photon sphere. The SAME unstable photon
sphere sets the black-hole SHADOW that the Event Horizon Telescope images. Can the in-house solver
verify the multi-messenger consistency -- that a gravitational-wave ringdown and an electromagnetic
shadow probe the same geometric quantity -- and what does that buy as a test?

## Result

First-principles Schwarzschild (M=1) photon sphere and shadow:

```text
b_c (shadow radius / critical impact parameter) = r_ph / sqrt(f(r_ph)) = 3 sqrt3 = 5.19615 M
Omega_c * b_c = 1   (exactly)   ->   Omega_c = 1 / b_c
```

So the eikonal ringdown real frequency is `omega_R -> (l+1/2)/b_c`. Two verifications:

**1. Ringdown <-> shadow consistency** (`omega_R * b_c / (l+1/2) -> 1`):

```text
  l=2    0.776       l=6    0.969       l=12   0.992
  l=4    0.934       l=8    0.982
```

**2. Overtone damping ladder** -- the imaginary parts are EQUALLY spaced in `n` with spacing
`-> -lambda = -1/b_c`:

```text
  l=10:  omega_I(n=0,1,2) = -0.0959, -0.2879, -0.4809   spacings -0.1921, -0.1930  (-> -0.19245, 0.3%)
```

**The photon sphere is the common origin of both.** The GW ringdown frequency, in units of the
shadow light-crossing rate `1/b_c`, approaches `l+1/2`; and the shadow scale `1/b_c` also sets the
overtone damping ladder. A LIGO/Virgo ringdown and an EHT shadow therefore probe the SAME geometric
quantity `Omega_c = 1/b_c`: **a non-Kerr or quantum-corrected metric that shifts the photon sphere
moves BOTH observables coherently -- a genuine gravitational-wave / electromagnetic cross-channel
test of the Kerr hypothesis.**

## What this establishes

A clean, self-contained multi-messenger result: the geometric link between two of the flagship
black-hole observables (LIGO ringdown, EHT shadow), verified with the repo's own solver and the
first-principles photon-sphere quantities (`b_c = 3 sqrt3`, `Omega_c b_c = 1` exact). It frames a
concrete quantum-gravity-adjacent test -- photon-sphere deviations from exotic or quantum-corrected
compact objects appear in both channels -- and extends the v2.229 geodesic foundation to the
electromagnetic sector.

## Honest scope

Eikonal (large-`l`) and Schwarzschild-only: the consistency ratio approaches 1 with `O(1/l)`
corrections (22% at `l=2` -> 0.8% at `l=12`), and the overtone ladder uses 3rd-order WKB (good for
these low `n` at `l=10`). The cross-channel TEST is the physical claim; turning it into a
quantitative joint bound needs a specific non-Kerr metric and real EHT + ringdown data (not done
here). Kerr would split `Omega_c != lambda` and make the shadow non-circular. This is a
geodesic/geometric-optics result; it frames a QG test but does not itself constrain a coupling.
Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
