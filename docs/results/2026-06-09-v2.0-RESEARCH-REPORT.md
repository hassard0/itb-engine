**An information-theoretic bootstrap for quantum gravity: constraining the higher-derivative EFT, confronting it with data, and auditing the engine itself.**

*Ian Hassard, with the ITB engine. 2026-06-09. v2.0 — 527 tests, 38 theoretical + 4 data constraints, 14 frameworks, 9 observables, 109 dated notes.*

> **Honest frame (read first).** This is a *toy* research platform. Most constraint prefactors and cross-sector mappings are O(1) / order-of-magnitude placeholders — "the right streets, the wrong house numbers." A dedicated *realism program* re-checks every headline against a factor-of-~2 uncertainty, so the repo distinguishes robust conclusions (signs, orderings, structure, which-experiment-constrains-what) from artifacts (precise coordinates). Several results below are deliberately *negative* or *self-correcting*; that is the point.

## Abstract

We represent a 4d higher-derivative gravitational effective field theory by **8 dimensionless Wilson coefficients** (matter `g_4,g_6,g_8`; graviton `g_R2`=Euler/R², `g_C`=Weyl², `g_R3`=R³; parity `g_R2_parity,g_R3_parity`) and intersect **~38 theoretical consistency conditions** — amplitude positivity, causality, holographic-information bounds, the swampland, anomaly flow, black-hole thermodynamics. The consistent region is a thin (~0.6%), low-dimensional (~3.4-effective-d) "island." We then **ingest four real experiments** (sub-mm gravity, cosmic birefringence, GW speed, GW dispersion) as additional constraints and find the data point to a specific **parity-violating, screened-scalaron EFT** — which sits in a **~2.8σ tension** between cosmic birefringence and unscreened sub-mm gravity, resolved only by screening. A suite of **meta-experiments** audits the engine: the axioms are internally consistent (Gödel), the load-bearing core is the swampland (Jenga), the exclusions are robust (thick shell), consistent gravity is strictly larger than gauge² (double copy), and the experimental program **stalls at rank 6/8** because two coefficient directions have no funded probe. We catalogue the honest limitations throughout.

## 1. The program

**What the engine is.** A constraint-satisfaction and forward-prediction machine over the EFT coefficient space. Each theory is a point `g ∈ ℝ⁸`; each consistency condition is a constraint `c.evaluate(g) → (satisfied, margin)` with a gradient; the assembled stack (`experiments/stack.py::build_stack`) defines a feasible region. Each consistent theory carries a falsifiable **observable fingerprint** (`itb predict`).

**The three questions, in order:**
1. *Which theories are even consistent?* — intersect the 38 theoretical constraints → the island.
2. *What do consistent theories predict?* — 9 observables (sub-mm Yukawa, η/s, a/c, cosmic/GW birefringence, PTA chirality, BH entropy, Starobinsky inflation, holographic complexity).
3. *What does the data say?* — ingest real measurements as constraints and watch the island shrink, sometimes to a tension.

**The constraint stack** (3 classes; full list in [CONSTRAINTS.md](../CONSTRAINTS.md)): **A — amplitude bootstrap** (20: forward positivity, dispersion towers, the EFThedron, Hofman–Maldacena, CEMZ causality); **B — information-theoretic** (7: Bekenstein, subadditivity, quantum focusing, the generalized second law); **C — gravitational universality** (10: swampland distance conjecture, WGC, anomaly inflow, 't Hooft matching, complexity, species scale). Plus **4 opt-in data constraints**.

## 2. The consistent island and the sector unifications

**The island is rare and low-dimensional.** Over a comparable-magnitude box, only **~0.6%** of EFTs satisfy the full stack; the survivors span **~3.4 effective dimensions** (PCA) — one stiff *collective-scale* mode (matter+graviton couplings moving together) and several sloppy directions. This is the swampland's "thin landscape," made quantitative, and the Sethna sloppy-model signature of intersecting positivity half-spaces. [v1.73]

**The center.** The island's Chebyshev center (deepest interior) is `g_4≈0.52, g_6≈0.38, g_8≈0.44, g_R2≈0.21, g_C≈0.23, g_R3≈0.08`; its central predictions (`a/c≈0.92`, `η/s≈0.81`, a **93 µm** sub-mm Yukawa) independently reproduce the dark-energy axion scale, and its nearest catalogued theory is **string tree-EFT**. Under realism uncertainty the *structural* predictions survive (a/c in the wedge 100% of draws; η/s = 0.86±0.03; sub-mm 60–110 µm). [v1.74, v1.75]

**One operator, many faces.** A central thread is that single couplings play many roles:
- **Resolving Euler (`g_R2`) from Weyl² (`g_C`)** activates the **Hofman–Maldacena wedge** `1/3 ≤ a/c ≤ 31/18`, which becomes the single dominant constraint. [v1.71]
- **η/s and a/c are the same Gauss–Bonnet coupling:** `1 − 4π(η/s) = (c−a)/c`; bulk causality sits strictly inside the wedge. [v1.72]
- **Black-hole entropy fixes a sign:** ΔS_ext > 0 ⟺ WGC, driven by Weyl² and matter (Euler is topological in 4d). [v1.82]
- **Holographic complexity super-saturates the Lloyd bound** (dC/dt > 1), driven by Weyl² — *orthogonal* to η/s (driven by Euler): the basis split shows up in the observables. [v1.98]
- **R² is the inflaton:** the same `g_R2`, at a high cutoff, is the **Starobinsky** inflaton (n_s≈0.964, r≈0.004) — dead-center in the Planck+BICEP sweet spot. One operator spans inflation (high cutoff) and the dark-energy scalaron (meV cutoff). [v1.86]

## 3. Ingesting data — the empirical swampland and the data-driven EFT

The engine is one of few QG-phenomenology tools that **folds real experiments into the consistency analysis**:

- **Sub-mm gravity (Eöt-Wash)** excludes the naive unscreened dark-energy scalaron (α=1/3, λ≈80 µm) by ~7×, collapsing the island to ~2% of its theoretical volume. [v1.76, v1.77]
- **Cosmic birefringence (Minami–Komatsu, β=0.34°±0.09°)** makes the engine *prefer a nonzero, positive-handed parity coupling* (excludes parity-even at 3.78σ) — the first data-driven preference for a parity-violating universe. The "respectable" frameworks predict zero. [v1.78]

**The data-driven EFT and the central tension.** Anomaly inflow + unscreened sub-mm gravity *cap* cosmic birefringence at β_max ≈ 0.09° — **~2.8σ below** the measured 0.34°. **Screening** lifts it to 0.32° (consistent). So a real birefringence signal **prefers a screened scalaron**. The β-matching screened EFT is registered as `discovered_data_driven` (a/c=0.93, β=0.32°, λ=115 µm). The tension is robust: it exceeds 1σ across 99% of the toy-mapping box (2σ across 73%), with the mechanism a Green–Schwarz bound of the parity coupling by the matter–curvature sector. [v1.79, v1.80]

**Multi-messenger.** The single parity coupling `g_R2_parity≈0.094` predicts CMB birefringence (detected), plus LIGO GW birefringence and PTA chirality (both just below current reach) — multi-messenger *consistent*; next tests LiteBIRD/CMB-S4 → ET → SKA. Caveat: CMB probes the axion–photon coupling, GW/PTA the axion–graviton coupling (linked only under a single-axion assumption). [v1.81]

**Which observable, which experiment.** A recurring lesson: *which observable, at what energy and precision* decides reach, not which sector or how "strong" a field looks.
- **GW170817's speed test is blind** to higher-derivative gravity (frequency-suppressed). [v1.84]
- **…but the GW dispersion test reaches the meV cutoff** via a ~10²⁰ cumulative-phase lever arm. [v1.85]
- **Neutron-star tides are the *weakest* curvature probe** — strong *gravity* (compactness ~0.2) is not high *curvature-energy* (ℏc/R_NS ~ 10⁻¹¹ eV ≪ meV), so δΛ/Λ ~ 10⁻¹⁷; blinder than the GW speed test. [v1.91]
- **Is gravity classical?** The parameter-free Diósi–Penrose collapse model is **excluded by ~14 orders of magnitude** by the 2021 underground X-ray experiment — weakly favoring gravity being quantum, the engine's premise. [v1.90]

## 4. Auditing the engine itself (the meta-experiments)

The program's credibility rests on turning the engine on *itself*:

- **Gödel internal-consistency.** The ~37 theoretical axioms are internally consistent (no contradiction among the principles); every minimal inconsistent core contains a *data* constraint — the tensions are empirical, not logical. [v1.87]
- **Constraint Jenga (load-bearing core).** Removing each axiom shows a **~15-constraint load-bearing core dominated by the swampland** (distance conjecture +664% island growth if removed, anomaly inflow +317%); **22 constraints are redundant** in this basis (the whole scalar-positivity tower, the BH-entropy/WGC condition); and **no single constraint is decisive for any framework's fate** (robust exclusions). [v1.93]
- **The adversarial self-audit (thick shell).** Most excluded theories fail ~9 of 38 constraints *at once*; only 0.3% are near-misses. The sole-gatekeeper volume is *provably* the Jenga irreplaceability (`V_without_c = V0 + gatekeeper(c)`); the theory that most nearly fools the stack (string-like, deep inside 37/38 bounds) is killed only by the distance conjecture. [v1.97]
- **The gravitational double-copy test.** The consistent graviton island is **strictly larger than gauge²**: the symmetric double copy (N=8-SUGRA-like, a=c) is only the a/c=1 diagonal; **~38% of the island is non-double-copy**. The catalogued frameworks are *almost* double copies (a/c≈0.7–0.86), none symmetric. [v1.94]
- **Genetic recombination (hybrid vigor).** Breeding the frameworks by sector yields **300 consistent hybrids** and **60 cases of hybrid vigor** (deeper than all parents); the exclusion of LQG/GFT/Hořava is **localized to their graviton sector**. [v1.95]
- **The RG phylogeny + the a-theorem.** Treated as running couplings, the frameworks form a rooted tree with **LQG → Group Field Theory** in the UV [v1.89]; and the **a-theorem** is violated by the toy flow for 9/13 frameworks unless the asymptotic-safety fixed point carries a *large* Euler central charge `a* ≥ 0.45` — a constraint the engine derives by fusing its own RG flow with the a-anomaly. [v1.99]
- **The minimum decisive experiment set.** A D-optimal Fisher design shows **6 measurements** pin all 8 coefficients; the headline inflation detection pins *nothing* (zero Jacobian); **g_8 and g_R3 are blind spots**. [v1.88]

## 5. The convergence forecast — a timeline with a gap

Turning the minimum experiment set into a timeline: accumulating Fisher information year by year, the funded roadmap (sub-mm 2028, LiteBIRD 2030, SKA 2032, ET 2035) pins the EFT to **rank 6/8 by ~2030 and then STALLS forever**. The consistent EFT is **never fully pinned** on the funded roadmap because **g_8 (s⁴ matter moment) and g_R3 (cubic curvature) have no funded experiment**. *Solving quantum gravity, on this measure, is gated not by precision but by two missing experiments* — a high-scattering-moment probe and a cubic-graviton / GW-nonlinearity probe. ## 6. Honest limitations

- **Toy prefactors.** Most constraint coefficients are O(1) placeholders; the realism program tells you which conclusions are robust, but exact coordinates move with the numbers. *Redundancy is basis-dependent*: the a-theorem (v1.70), Wald entropy (v1.82), and the species scale (v1.96) are constraints of independent *origin* carrying no independent *information* in this basis — exactly what the v1.71 Euler/Weyl² split fixed for the a-theorem.
- **Order-of-magnitude mappings.** Cross-sector maps (κ_β for birefringence, the η/s and BH-entropy normalizations, the GW-dispersion and species-counting forms) are schematic; the robust content is signs, orderings, and scalings.
- **Hints, not discoveries.** Cosmic birefringence is a ~3.6σ *hint*; screening is a binary flag (really density-dependent); the CMB β is the axion–photon coupling while GW/PTA are axion–graviton (single-axion assumption).
- **Schematic RG / ghost.** The phylogeny and a-theorem-flow use toy beta functions; higher-derivative gravity is scheme-dependent and has a massive spin-2 ghost — the flow is *structure*, not trajectories.
- **The engine is honest about all of this on purpose.** Several cycles are negative or self-correcting (the a-theorem redundancy; GW170817 blindness; the corrected RFC/BNOSSW artifacts; Dr. M.'s arithmetic caught and fixed against known limits).

## 7. Bottom line

Given amplitude/causality/holographic consistency **plus two ingested experiments**, the engine points to a **parity-violating, screened-scalaron EFT**: it matches cosmic birefringence, requires screening to survive sub-mm gravity, and predicts GW/PTA parity signals just below current reach. The consistent island is thin (~0.6%) and ~3.4-dimensional, rests on a swampland-dominated load-bearing core, is strictly larger than gauge², and would be pinned by **6 measurements** — except **two of its directions have no funded experiment**, so the program stalls at rank 6/8. Its sharpest tension is the birefringence/sub-mm-gravity clash; its sharpest near-future test is next-gen CMB birefringence (LiteBIRD/CMB-S4). Every claim here carries its caveats explicitly — the engine is built to be honest about the difference between what it has shown and what it has merely sketched.
