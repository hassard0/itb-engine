## Question

v2.28 showed how to convert an SDC moduli distance into a `TowerSpectrum`. v2.29 asks:

> Can a sourced Kaluza-Klein radius ratio be converted directly into the same tower-verdict
> machinery?

Yes. The new `kk_radius_tower_spectrum()` utility uses:

```text
m_KK / m0 = R0 / R
phi_tower = log(R / R0)
```

with one-sigma uncertainty supplied as `log_radius_sigma`.

## Interpretation

closer to a compactification observable than the abstract `Delta_moduli` target, but it
is still conditional on a sourced framework-specific radius ratio.
