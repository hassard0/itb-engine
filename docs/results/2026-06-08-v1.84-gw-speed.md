## The setup — and a hypothesis that turned out wrong

Sub-mm gravity (v1.77) squeezed the *matter* sector; cosmic birefringence (v1.78)
the *parity* sector. The plan for v1.84 was a third experiment on the
*tensor-propagation* sector — the famous GW170817 + GRB170817A speed bound
|c_GW − c|/c < 5×10⁻¹⁶ — with the expectation that it would *also* squeeze the
dark-energy cutoff, "triangulating" the v1.77 conclusion from an independent sector.

**It doesn't.** And the reason is physics worth stating plainly.

## Why GW170817 is blind to higher-derivative gravity (Dr. M.-confirmed)

- GW170817 killed quartic/quintic Galileons and much of Horndeski because those give
  an **O(1), frequency-independent** speed shift — a background modification of the
  tensor kinetic term, c_T² = 1 + O(1).
- The engine's couplings are **higher-derivative** (R², Weyl², R³). They modify the
  graviton **dispersion**, ω² = c²k²(1 + a(k/M)² + …), so the speed deviation is
  **frequency-suppressed**:

        δc_GW/c ~ κ_c · (|g_R2| + |g_C|) · (E_GW / E_cutoff)² .

- LIGO GWs are very low energy (E_GW ~ 4×10⁻¹³ eV at 100 Hz). So even at the
  **dark-energy cutoff** (2.4 meV), (E_GW/E_cutoff)² ~ 10⁻¹⁹ and **δc_GW ~ 10⁻²⁰** —
  five orders of magnitude below the 5×10⁻¹⁶ bound.

## The honest synthesis — the three experiments do NOT triangulate

This is the genuine, more interesting finding (and a correction to the cycle's own
hypothesis): **different experiments probe the cutoff in fundamentally different
ways.**

- **Sub-mm gravity** (v1.77) probes the **mass/range** of light new modes — the R²
  scalaron's Yukawa fifth force is a *low-energy, long-range, frequency-independent*
  effect, directly visible at sub-mm distances. It **does** squeeze the dark-energy
  cutoff (and excludes the unscreened scalaron).
- **GW170817 speed** (this cycle) probes the tensor **propagation speed** — a
  *high-derivative, frequency-suppressed* (k/M)² dispersion that is invisible at LIGO
  energies. It **does not** squeeze the dark-energy cutoff.

So the low-cutoff pressure in the engine comes **specifically from light long-range
modes** (the scalaron), not from the high-derivative graviton sector. The honest
"three-sector triangulation" is *false* — and recognizing that is the result.

**The relevant tensor-sector probe** (Dr. M.): not the GW170817 arrival-time *speed*
test, but LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA's **intra-messenger dispersion tests** — searches for a
frequency-dependent phase Ψ(f) *within* the waveform — which *are* sensitive to
(k/M)² terms. That is the experiment to confront the engine's graviton dispersion
with; a natural future cycle.
