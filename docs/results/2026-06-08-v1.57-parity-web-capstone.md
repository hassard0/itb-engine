## Why the loop concludes here (honest diminishing returns)

The parity sector is now thoroughly mapped: five probes across the GW spectrum
plus the EM and engine-internal channels. The last cycle (v1.56) was a refinement
/ partial walk-back rather than a bold new positive result — the signal that the
vein is mined. Further parity probes would be enumerative; genuinely new
directions (a complexity-bound observable, new framework encoders) would be
markedly more speculative and trade away the rigor that made this program worth
running. So the creative loop closes here, on a consolidating capstone, rather
than manufacturing thinner cycles. That is the integrous choice.
