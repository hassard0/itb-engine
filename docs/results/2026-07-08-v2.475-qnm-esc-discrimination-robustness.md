# v2.475 - robustness check tempers v2.474: the ESC discrimination separates the branches but doesn't robustly pick one

v2.474 claimed the candidate's low double-ratio (1.32) favors the heterotic-string (Regge) branch (1.23) over KK
(1.05). That used **flat residues**. Testing robustness by varying the residue power-law r_n ~ n^p:

```text
  Regge (m^2~n): a_k = zeta(k-p)   KK (m~n): a_k = zeta(2k-p)
   p     Regge   KK    sep   candidate (1.32) favors
  -1.0   1.06   1.02  0.04   Regge
  -0.5   1.11   1.03  0.08   Regge
   0.0   1.23   1.05  0.18   Regge   (v2.474 flat case)
   0.5   1.64   1.08  0.55   KK      (Regge climbs ABOVE the candidate)
```

**Two findings:**
1. The **separation** Regge > KK is **robust** for every p — because m²~n vs m~n put different ζ-arguments in the
   moments, a genuine spectral distinction. So the fingerprint *does* distinguish the tower types **in principle**.
2. The candidate's **preference** is **not** robust — it favors Regge only for flat/decreasing residues (p ≲ 0) and
   **flips toward KK** once residues grow (p > 0), because the Regge double-ratio climbs above the candidate's 1.32.
   And growing residues (p > 0) are the **physical** case for a real string Regge tower (level degeneracies grow
   with mass, ~exp(√n)).

So v2.474's headline "the candidate favors the heterotic-string branch" **overstated**. Honestly: the fingerprint
separates Regge from KK robustly, but *which* the candidate prefers is residue-model-dependent — suggestive of the
string branch for flat residues, **not** a robust conclusion once realistic growing residues are allowed. An honest
self-correction; the FINDINGS and Report II claims v2.474 seeded are tempered accordingly. The heterotic
identification still rests on its other supports (the rigorously-required nonzero R², the parity/Green–Schwarz
argument, the string-like tower *shape*) — just not on a robust fingerprint *discrimination* of Regge over KK.
5/5 checks pass.

## Honest scope

A robustness self-correction using the same power-law-residue toy family (still not the exact heterotic/KK
degeneracies). **Robust and surviving**: the separation (Regge double-ratio > KK) for every p. **Corrected**: the
candidate's preference for Regge is a flat-residue artifact; growing residues flip it. The exact flip point (~p ≈
0.2–0.3) and the p > 0 values carry the usual toy caveats (the 1.32 partly reflects the g_6 = g_8 Chebyshev
artifact; low rung only). The corrected claim is deliberately weaker than v2.474.
