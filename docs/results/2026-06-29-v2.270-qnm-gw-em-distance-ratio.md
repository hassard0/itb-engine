# v2.270 - The GW vs EM luminosity-distance ratio: a standard-siren test of modified GW propagation

## Question

A fresh GW-propagation probe (continuing v2.266-v2.269) and the achromatic-AMPLITUDE partner of the
v2.269 chromatic-phase birefringence. In modified gravity the GW amplitude need not redshift like GR
demands, so the distance inferred from a GW amplitude (`d_L^GW`) can differ from the true EM luminosity
distance (`d_L^EM`). A 'standard siren' with both signals measures the ratio directly -- what does
GW170817 say?

## Result

Two standard parametrizations, both exactly 1 in GR:

```text
  running Planck mass (Belgacem-Maggiore):  d_GW/d_EM = Xi(z) = Xi_0 + (1 - Xi_0)/(1+z)^n      [GR: Xi_0=1]
  extra dimensions   (Pardo et al.):        d_GW/d_EM = [1 + (d_EM/R_c)^n]^((D-4)/(2n))         [GR: D=4]
```

Both reduce to ratio 1 to 1e-15 in the GR limit (verified). Extra dimensions with `D > 4` let
gravitons leak off the brane, suppressing the GW amplitude so `d_L^GW > d_L^EM`, monotonically in `D`:

```text
  extra-dimension scan at GW170817 (40 Mpc, Rc = 100 Mpc, n = 2):
    D = 4.0   d_GW/d_EM = 1.0000
    D = 4.5   d_GW/d_EM = 1.0187
    D = 5.0   d_GW/d_EM = 1.0378
    D = 6.0   d_GW/d_EM = 1.0770
    D = 7.0   d_GW/d_EM = 1.1177
```

GW170817's measured agreement `d_L^GW/d_L^EM ~ 1` (to ~20%) therefore pins the number of spacetime
dimensions to **D = 4.02 +/- 0.07** (Pardo, Fishbach, Holz, Spergel 2018) and bounds any Planck-mass
running. All 6/6 consistency checks pass (including the leading-order expansion
`ratio ~ 1 + (D-4)/(2n)(d/R_c)^n`).

## What this establishes

A direct multimessenger test of whether gravity *propagates* as GR says. Crucially this is an
ACHROMATIC effect (distance/redshift-dependent, frequency-independent) -- the opposite of the v2.269
birefringence, whose chromaticity was its discriminant. The two propagation tests are complementary,
splitting modified-gravity GW propagation into a parity-odd chromatic phase channel (v2.269) and a
parity-even achromatic amplitude channel (here). It also ties the GW sector back to the extra-dimension
physics of the v2.264 species scale. Future high-redshift sirens (LISA, Einstein Telescope) sharpen
the `Xi(z)` running bound where the `(1+z)^{-n}` term bites.

## Honest scope

The GR limits and the monotonic / expansion behaviour are EXACT properties of the two standard
parametrizations (Belgacem-Maggiore; Pardo et al.). The GW170817 numbers (`z = 0.0098`, `d_EM ~ 40
Mpc`, ratio consistent with 1 at the ~20% level) and the resulting `D = 4.02 +/- 0.07` are the
source-backed published values; this experiment reproduces the STRUCTURE and the qualitative bound, not
the full Bayesian standard-siren likelihood (which folds in the inclination-distance degeneracy and the
GW amplitude calibration). `R_c`, `n` and `Xi_0` are model parameters, not measured here; the dimension
scan uses a representative `R_c = 100 Mpc` to illustrate the `D`-dependence (the actual bound depends on
the assumed crossover scale, as Pardo et al. discuss). A GW-propagation / standard-siren result, not an
engine constraint refit.
