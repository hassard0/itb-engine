# v2.289 - Relativistic stellar structure: the TOV equation, the Buchdahl bound, and compactness

## Question

A fresh sector (relativistic astrophysics) bridging the v2.273-v2.277 black-hole thread and the
v2.266-v2.280 gravitational-wave program. How compact can matter be before general relativity forbids a
static star -- and where do neutron stars sit relative to that limit and the black hole?

## Result

The Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff equation -- relativistic hydrostatic equilibrium, in which pressure both
responds to AND sources gravity -- makes the central pressure of a uniform-density (Schwarzschild
interior) star

```text
  P_c / rho = (1 - sqrt(1-beta)) / (3 sqrt(1-beta) - 1),   beta = 2GM/Rc^2 (compactness)
```

DIVERGE at `beta = 8/9`, the **Buchdahl bound**: no static star can be more compact without infinite
central pressure (verified). A first-principles RK4 integration of the TOV equation reproduces the
analytic interior profile:

```text
  TOV check (beta = 0.4): P_surface/P_c = 0,  m(R)/M = 1.000000,
                          P_mid numeric / analytic = 6.044639e-3 / 6.044639e-3  (match to 1e-4)

  compactness hierarchy:
    Sun                        beta = 4.2e-6     no photon sphere
    white dwarf                beta = 2.5e-4     no photon sphere
    neutron star (1.4 Msun)    beta = 0.345      no photon sphere
    ultracompact (limit)       beta = 0.667      PHOTON SPHERE (R < 3GM/c^2)
    Buchdahl bound             beta = 0.889
    black hole                 beta = 1.0
```

A canonical neutron star (`beta ~ 0.34`) sits safely below both the Buchdahl and black-hole limits.
Between `beta = 2/3` and Buchdahl lie ULTRACOMPACT stars, compact enough (`R < 3GM/c^2`) to have a
photon sphere OUTSIDE their surface -- they would ring with light like a black hole and could mimic the
v2.229-v2.230 ringdown/shadow, the horizonless "exotic compact object" the v2.247 echo searches probe.
All 6/6 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The same compactness that the v2.270 GW170817 tidal-deformability measurement constrains is bounded here
from first principles: relativistic stellar structure forbids `2GM/Rc^2 >= 8/9`, neutron stars live at
`~0.34`, and the photon-sphere threshold at `2/3` marks the ultracompact regime that blurs the line
between a star and a black hole. This ties the relativistic-astrophysics sector to the black-hole
(v2.230 photon sphere, v2.247 echoes) and gravitational-wave (v2.270 tidal) threads.

## Honest scope

Exact analytic results (Schwarzschild interior solution, Buchdahl bound `8/9`) plus a first-principles
RK4 TOV integration verified against the analytic profile to `1e-4` -- both for the UNIFORM-DENSITY
(incompressible) star, the cleanest case. A realistic neutron star needs a microphysical equation of
state `P(rho)` (the TOV integration then gives the M-R curve and the maximum mass / TOV limit), which
this cycle does not model -- the quoted neutron-star `beta ~ 0.34` uses representative `M = 1.4 Msun`,
`R = 12 km`. The Buchdahl bound and the photon-sphere onset (`beta = 2/3`) are EOS-independent geometric
results. GW170817's tidal-deformability constraint on the EOS is referenced, not re-derived. A
classical-GR / relativistic-astrophysics result, not an engine constraint refit.
