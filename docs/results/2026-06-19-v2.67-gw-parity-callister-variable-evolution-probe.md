## Schema

The variable-evolution file is a sample-based HDF product under `/result`, not a
fixed posterior grid. Required keys:

```text
frequencies
Omg_I_model
Omg_V_model
kappa_Dc
kappa_z
R0
alpha
beta
zp
zMax
```

The parser validates:

- one-dimensional, strictly increasing positive frequency grid
- one-dimensional posterior sample arrays with a shared sample count
- `Omg_I_model` and `Omg_V_model` shapes equal to `[frequency_count, sample_count]`
- finite samples and spectra
- positive `R0`, `zp`, and `zMax`
