# v2.280 - The four classic tests of general relativity and the PPN parameters gamma, beta

## Question

Continuing the classical-GR thread (v2.278/v2.279 binary pulsars, strong field) into the solar system
(weak field). The parametrized post-Newtonian (PPN) framework reduces every weak-field test to two
Eddington parameters -- gamma and beta. Do the four classic tests confirm GR's gamma = beta = 1?

## Result

```text
  test                    PPN factor          GR value                      measurement
  light deflection        (1+gamma)/2          1.7515" (solar limb)          VLBI gamma = 0.99992 +/- 0.00023
  Shapiro time delay      (1+gamma)/2          ~250 us round-trip past Sun   Cassini gamma-1 = (2.1+/-2.3)e-5
  perihelion precession   (2+2gamma-beta)/3     42.99"/century (Mercury)      observed 42.98 +/- 0.04
  gravitational redshift  1 (equiv. principle)  2.455e-15 (Pound-Rebka)       confirmed ~1%; GPS 38 us/day
```

Light grazing the Sun is deflected by `1.7515"` (the famous `1.75 inch`), and because deflection scales
as `(1+gamma)/2`, the Newtonian `gamma = 0` prediction is exactly HALF (`0.8758"`) -- the discriminant
Eddington's 1919 eclipse used to choose Einstein over Newton. The Shapiro delay shares that
`(1+gamma)/2` factor, and Cassini measured `gamma - 1 = (2.1 +/- 2.3)e-5`, the tightest confirmation of
GR's space-curvature sector. Mercury's perihelion precesses `42.99"/century` (the anomalous `43"`),
mixing gamma and beta as `(2+2gamma-beta)/3`. The gravitational redshift `(g h/c^2 = 2.46e-15)` tests
the equivalence principle. All 8/8 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The four classic tests all reduce to gamma and beta, and GR's `gamma = beta = 1` passes every one.
Together with the v2.278/v2.279 binary pulsars (strong field), the PPN solar-system tests (weak field)
bracket general relativity across the full range of gravitational potentials -- the classical
foundation that any quantum-gravity completion must reproduce in its low-energy limit.

Exact leading-order PPN formulas with standard solar and Mercury parameters; the deflection
(`1.7515"`), Mercury precession (`~43"/century`) and Pound-Rebka shift (`~2.46e-15`) reproduce the
textbook GR values to the precision of the inputs. The measured bounds (Cassini gamma, VLBI gamma,

Point-mass, weak-field, leading PPN order; the Sun's quadrupole `J2` adds a tiny correction not
