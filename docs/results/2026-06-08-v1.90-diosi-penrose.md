## A real falsification in the foundations sector

The engine assumes gravity is **quantum** (it bootstraps a graviton EFT). The
opposite hypothesis — gravity is **classical** and itself causes wavefunction
collapse — is the Penrose (1996) / Diósi (1989) model, encoded as the engine's
`penrose_diosi` framework. v1.90 confronts it with real data.

## The experiment

Gravitational collapse forces charged particles to emit **spontaneous radiation**,
at a rate scaling as **1/R₀³** (R₀ = the model's regularization length). The
**parameter-free** Diósi–Penrose model fixes R₀ at the nucleon scale (~10⁻¹⁵ m). The
2021 underground germanium experiment found **no excess X-rays** (1–4 keV) and set

> **R₀ > 0.54 Å (5.4×10⁻¹¹ m), 95% CL** — Donadi, Piscicchia, Curceanu, Diósi,
> Laubenstein, Bassi, *Nature Physics* 17, 74 (2021).

## Result — the parameter-free model is dead

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| parameter-free R₀ (nucleon) | 10⁻¹⁵ m |
| required R₀ | > 5.4×10⁻¹¹ m |
| R₀ must exceed nucleon scale by | **~5×10⁴×** |
| predicted rate / experimental limit | **1.6×10¹⁴** |
| **exclusion** | **~14 orders of magnitude** |

The parameter-free Diósi–Penrose model is **falsified by ~14 orders of magnitude**
in the spontaneous-emission rate (even the nucleus-scale variant, R₀~10⁻¹⁴ m, is
excluded by ~11 orders). To survive, DP needs an *unmotivated macroscopic* R₀ > 0.5 Å.

## What it means — and the engine connection

Three escapes, all costly: (i) an ad-hoc macroscopic regularization length, (ii) the
collapse agent is not gravity, or (iii) **there is no gravitational collapse — gravity
is quantum.** The third is the engine's whole premise. So this real result **weakly
favors the quantum-gravity EFT** the rest of the program assumes over the
classical-gravity-collapse alternative its `penrose_diosi` framework represents.

It is also a clean example of a *foundational* test (is gravity quantum?) sitting
alongside the program's EFT-coefficient tests — a different axis of evidence.

## Honest scope

The 1/R₀³ scaling is robust; the exact prefactor and the precise "parameter-free"
choice (nucleon vs nucleus) move the exclusion between ~11 and ~14 orders — the
model is dead either way. This cycle ingests a *foundations* datum, not an
EFT-coefficient constraint, so it informs the program's premise rather than its
coefficient island.
