# v2.285 - The feasible region is not just GR: a higher-derivative theory the engine accepts

## Question

The v2.281-v2.284 sub-arc showed that none of the engine's toy frameworks (string, AS, cdt, lqg) are
strictly feasible -- only pure_gr. That raises the real question: is the engine's feasible region ONLY
general relativity, or does it admit genuine higher-derivative physics?

## Result

**A higher-derivative theory is feasible.** The point

```text
  g_4 = 0.5,  g_6 = 0.3,  g_8 = 0.3,  g_R2 = 0.1,  g_R3 = 0.05   (parities 0)
```

passes all 38 engine constraints (verified feasible) -- with nonzero Ricci-scalar (`g_R2`) and cubic
(`g_R3`) curvature couplings. So the feasible region is non-trivial. But it is tightly constrained, and
two constraints carve out a sharp curvature ceiling. Anomaly cancellation needs
`|g_4 g_6 - g_R2^2| <= 0.2`, the repulsive-force conjecture needs `g_4 g_6 >= g_R2 + g_R2^2`, and the
two windows overlap only when

```text
  g_R2 + g_R2^2 <= g_4 g_6 <= g_R2^2 + 0.2     =>     g_R2 <= 0.2   (a HARD ceiling, any completion)
```

Scanning `g_R2` with a hand-built symmetric matter completion, the demonstrated feasibility reaches
`g_R2 = 0.15` (where that completion trips another constraint), comfortably inside the analytic
`g_R2 <= 0.2` bound. The toy frameworks miss feasibility for a now-explained reason: their matter
product sits on the bare WGC line `g_4 g_6 = g_R2`, just below the repulsive-force floor
`g_R2 + g_R2^2` (string 0.20 vs 0.24, AS 0.12 vs 0.17, cdt 0.22 vs 0.27, lqg 0.27 vs 0.39). All 5/5
checks pass.

## What this establishes

The engine is not "only GR is allowed": it admits a genuine, if narrow, higher-derivative region -- a
consistent gravity in this basis can carry curvature up to a Ricci-scalar coupling of `~0.2`, with its
matter sector tuned into the anomaly-vs-repulsive window. And the toy frameworks are a prefactor-step
*under* that window, not stranded in an empty region -- closing the v2.281-v2.284 investigation
constructively: the feasible region exists, its curvature ceiling is `g_R2 <= 0.2`, and the toy
encodings sit just at its edge.

## Honest scope

A constructive feasibility result using the real `check()`/`Theory` API: the witness is the engine's
literal verdict (feasible against all 38 constraints), and the curvature ceiling is found by scanning
`g_R2` with a HAND-CONSTRUCTED matter completion (symmetric `g_4 = g_6`, `g_8` clearing the dispersion
tower, `g_R3` at `x = 0.5`) -- so the DEMONSTRATED ceiling `~0.15` is for that completion family (it
trips another constraint there); a smarter completion could push higher but PROVABLY cannot exceed the
`g_R2 <= 0.2` algebra of the anomaly-vs-repulsive windows, which is the hard ceiling. The couplings are
the engine's dimensionless toy basis with O(1) representative prefactors, so "feasible higher-derivative
theory" means a point the engine's constraint suite accepts, not a validated physical Lagrangian. A
feasible-region existence / geometry result, not a new constraint or a claim about a specific physical
theory.
