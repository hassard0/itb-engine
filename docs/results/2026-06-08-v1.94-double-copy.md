# v1.94 — The gravitational double-copy test: is the consistent graviton island the square of a gauge-theory region?

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan. Structure confirmed with Dr. M.

---

## The question

The **double copy** (Bern–Carrasco–Johansson; KLT) builds gravity from gauge × gauge:
gravity amplitudes ~ (gauge)², and the graviton Wilson coefficients are products of
gauge field-strength coefficients (the EFThedron, Arkani-Hamed–Huang–Huang, has this
structure). So: **is the engine's consistent graviton region the *square* of a gauge
positivity region?** And if not, are there "non-double-copy" gravities?

## The structure (Dr. M.-confirmed)

- A **symmetric** double copy (same gauge theory left and right — e.g. N=4 SYM ⊗ N=4
  SYM = **N=8 supergravity**) gives a CFT with **a = c**, i.e. the **a/c = 1 diagonal**
  of the Hofman–Maldacena wedge (with a = g_R2 = Euler, c = g_C = Weyl²).
- Consistent gravity **strictly contains** the double copy: the wedge is 2-D, the
  symmetric double copy is a 1-D diagonal.
- **Asymmetric** double copies (different left/right) move a/c off 1, filling only a
  *sub-region* of the wedge.

## Result — consistent gravity is strictly larger than gauge²

Projecting onto the (g_R2, g_C) = (a, c) plane:

| region | fraction of the graviton island |
|---|---|
| on the symmetric-DC diagonal (a/c ≈ 1, ±0.03) | **5.3%** |
| double-copy reachable (a/c ∈ [2/3, 3/2]) | 61.5% |
| **NON-double-copy** (a/c far from 1) | **38.5%** |

- The **symmetric double copy is a measure-zero diagonal** (~5% within a finite band) —
  consistent gravity is vastly larger.
- Even allowing asymmetric double copies (a toy a/c ∈ [2/3, 3/2] band), **~38% of the
  consistent graviton island is non-double-copy**: a continuum of consistent gravities
  with a/c near the wedge edges (toward 1/3 or 31/18) that *no* double copy reaches.
- So: **not all consistent gravity is gauge²** — the double copy is a special central
  sub-locus of a strictly larger consistent region. This is a structural statement that
  the engine makes vivid by sitting it inside the v1.71 a/c wedge.

## Where the real frameworks sit

Every framework with a curvature sector lands at **a/c ≈ 0.7–0.86** — *below* the
symmetric a/c=1 diagonal, but *within* the asymmetric double-copy band:

| framework | a/c | double-copy reachable? |
|---|---|---|
| asymptotic_safety | 0.86 | ✓ |
| string_tree_eft | 0.81 | ✓ |
| cdt | 0.79 | ✓ |
| group_field_theory | 0.72 | ✓ |
| lqg_induced | 0.70 | ✓ |

So the catalogued quantum gravities are **"almost double copies"** — asymmetric (a≠c)
but inside the reachable band — *not* symmetric double copies (none sit on a/c=1). The
engine's *own* discovered/data-driven points and the wedge edges are where the genuinely
non-double-copy gravities live.

## Honest scope

Toy gauge region (a_4 ≥ 0, a_6 ≥ a_4²), a toy symmetric double-copy map (g_R2 = g_C =
a_4², g_R3 = a_4 a_6), and a *stated* asymmetric-reach band a/c ∈ [2/3, 3/2]. The
robust content is the **structure** — symmetric DC = the a/c=1 diagonal, consistent
gravity strictly contains it, and non-double-copy points exist — not the exact 38%.
