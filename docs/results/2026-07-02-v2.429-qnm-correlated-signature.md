## What this establishes

Three facts make the candidate's three fronts a **single smoking gun**: **(1)** all three drivers are *required
nonzero* — the candidate cannot have any signal absent; **(2)** they are *positively correlated* — g_4 and g_R2
are the two matter-dominance keystones and the parity is capped by the g_R2 sector, so one structure drives all
three; **(3)** *no single front is dispensable* — a parity-conserving, non-matter-dominant, or curvature-minimal
version is each infeasible with the rest at candidate values. So the decisive test is the **conjunction**: the
candidate predicts a large inflationary matter coupling (CMB-S4 tension) **and** nonzero positive parity (CMB
birefringence) **and** a bounded dark-energy w (DESI/Euclid), all correlated. This is far more decisive than any
single front — a rival that produces one signal by accident does **not** reproduce the correlated triple, and any
single null result kills the candidate. It reframes the candidate as **one correlated make-or-break signature**
that ~2030 data can confirm or falsify *as a pattern* — the sharpest, least-evadeable test the theory admits, and
the most direct autonomous step toward the empirical solve.

## Honest scope

The fronts are linked at the level of the Wilson **couplings** (rigorously: matter dominance ties g_4–g_R2;
parity-decomposed positivity caps parity by the g_R2 sector); the map from couplings to the actual *observables*
(β in degrees, g_4's CMB-S4 imprint, w) carries O(1)-toy prefactors — so the robust content is "all three signals
required and positively correlated," not the precise observable correlation. The correlations (+0.34 to +0.44)
are moderate because the island is already tight; the stronger statement is the *conjunction* (a feasibility
fact, correlation-magnitude-independent). "Data requires parity" is contingent on the ~3.6σ birefringence hint
(v2.329); if it evaporates, the parity front detaches (the parity-conserving rival, v2.420).
