## Claim Boundary

This improves the R4 scale route by making the missing object executable, not by
claiming that object exists. The symbolic `Lambda_R4` policy and query surface
remain useful for inspection. They still do not provide the source-backed
four-dimensional frame, string/Einstein-frame map, alpha-prime/kappa conversion,
compactification policy, field-redefinition closure, covariance, and likelihood
reference needed for a numeric Wilson coefficient or framework exclusion.
