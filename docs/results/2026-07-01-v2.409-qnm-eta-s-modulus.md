## What this establishes

The candidate's transport prediction is honest and structured: it **saturates KSS at its (assumed) holographic
point**, is consistent with -- but does not require -- a **KSS-violating fluid**, and the size of any violation
is a **conformal-collider-bounded modulus**, not a fixed value. The KSS-violating region opens precisely when
`c > a` (the celebrated Gauss-Bonnet KSS violation, requiring the nonzero c−a the resolved basis permits). This
directly **refines the early v1.72 fixed-η/s claim**: that number was the a=c assumption in disguise, and the
recent recognition that a=c is unforced (v2.399) turns the sharp early value into an honest band. It is the same
lesson as v2.400 (Weyl²-sector predictions carry the c−a modulus) applied to a *new, real* observable, and it
ties the whole c−a arc to **quark-gluon-plasma transport**: if the candidate is a holographic fluid, it lies at
or above ~0.33 × KSS, saturates KSS in the Einstein limit, and can only sub-saturate through higher-derivative
(Weyl²) corrections -- exactly the regime relativistic-heavy-ion phenomenology probes.

## Honest scope

`4π(η/s) = a/c` is the Gauss-Bonnet **holographic** result (v1.72; Brigante et al. 2008, Kats-Petrov), valid for
a theory with an AdS/CFT dual. The candidate is a low-energy EFT; assuming it *has* a holographic dual is a
strong, unproven premise -- "the candidate's η/s" is conditional on holography, and if there is no dual the
transport interpretation does not apply. The a/c band [1/3, 31/18] is the engine's HM-wedge bound (v2.398); the
*real* Gauss-Bonnet causality bound (Brigante et al.: η/s ≥ 16/25 = 0.64 × KSS) is **tighter** than the HM-only
lower end (0.33), so the engine's band is looser than a full holographic causality analysis would give -- the
robust content is that η/s is a *band* (not fixed) that saturates KSS at a=c and can violate KSS only for c>a,
not the precise band edges. The early v1.72 fixed value (~0.81 × KSS) corresponds to a particular near-a=c c−a
choice. A cross-era refinement plus a new-observable mapping, no new engine datum. Robust content: assuming a
holographic dual, the candidate's η/s is a conformal-collider-bounded band that saturates KSS at its holographic
(a=c) point and enters the KSS-violating region only via nonzero c−a -- correcting the early fixed-η/s claim.
