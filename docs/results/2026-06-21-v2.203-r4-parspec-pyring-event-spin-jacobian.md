## Source Formulae

pyRing's runtime `QNM_EFT` path adds EFT shifts to `QNM_fit`, not to the
ParSpec high-spin polynomial:

```text
d(freq_EFT/freq_GR - 1)/d gamma = df_EFT_fit / F_Berti(a)
d(tau_EFT/tau_GR - 1)/d gamma = -tau_Berti_dimensionless(a) * domi_EFT_fit
```

The same pyRing `df_EFT_fit` and `domi_EFT_fit` rows are also projected through
the ParSpec high-spin `F(a)` and `tau(a)` polynomials as a comparison, not as a
claim-ready axis map.
