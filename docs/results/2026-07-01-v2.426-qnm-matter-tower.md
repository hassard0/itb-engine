A rigorous, low-risk core extension in the "make it real" direction (the ROADMAP's thrust #2). The engine had
the matter sector's leading Hankel positivity (`g_6² ≤ g_4·g_8`, dispersion_tower) but stopped at g_8, while the
curvature sector already had its next rung (`g_R3² ≤ g_R2·g_R4`, v2.375). This adds the next **matter** rung.

spectral density (Im of the forward amplitude), the Hankel matrix of moments is positive semi-definite, so the
adjacent 2×2 minor gives:

    g_8² ≤ g_6 · g_10      (source-exact Cauchy-Schwarz on a positive measure — RIGOROUS)

are tagged **`rigorous`** — the same source-exact moment-problem structure as the leading rung and the curvature
tower, zero toy input.

## Honest scope

The bound is source-exact **in form** (Hankel/Cauchy-Schwarz positivity of moments), carrying the v2.411 caveat:
the identification of the Wilson coefficients with consecutive moments μ_k is the standard dispersive-tower
convention (prefactors set to 1, as the leading rung already does), so the rigorous content is the inequality
*structure* and the resulting floor g_10 ≥ g_8²/g_6, not a claim about g_10's absolute normalization. The g_10
upper edge (0.6) is the EFT-validity/complexity ceiling, not part of the rung. Opt-in, so the default stack,
frameworks, and goldens are untouched. The "prediction" is a *lower bound* (positivity forces at least this), not
a determination of g_10's value.
