## The cumulative-phase lever arm

For ω² = k² + k⁴/M² (the α=4 LVK modified-dispersion case, A₄ = 1/M²), the
wavenumber is k ≈ ω(1 − ½(ω/M)²), so the phase over distance D acquires an anomalous
piece (Dr. M.-confirmed; Mirshekari–Yunes–Will 2012):

        δΨ ~ ½ · g_curv · E_GW³ · D / M² .

The lever arm over the speed test is the accumulated phase:

        δΨ / (δv_g/c) ~ E_GW · D ~ **10²⁰**

(ω D in natural units, 100 Hz over ~1 Gpc). That enormous factor turns the
unobservable δv_g ~ 10⁻²⁰ into an **O(0.1–1 rad)** phase — within LVK reach.

## Honest caveats (Dr. M.)

- **Ghost.** Achieving α=4 dispersion via the Weyl² (g_C) term typically introduces a
  **massive spin-2 ghost**; the A_α parametrization is bottom-up and does not
  guarantee a ghost-free UV completion. The engine's positivity bounds partially
  address healthiness, but the dispersion↔coupling map is phenomenological.
- **Degeneracy** of δΨ(f) with chirp mass / spins in template matching (needs high
  SNR / multi-band to break).
- **Mode mixing**: the LVK bound assumes the standard massless mode dominates.
- Order-of-magnitude lever arm and sensitivity; the robust content is that the
  cumulative enhancement (~E_GW·D) **closes the ~5-orders gap the speed test left**,
  bringing the dark-energy cutoff to the frontier.
