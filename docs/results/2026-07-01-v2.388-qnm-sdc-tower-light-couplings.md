# v2.388 - SWING: the SDC hierarchy bound and the string-like tower jointly fix the number of simultaneously-light couplings per sector

## Question

v2.383 (the SDC is a hierarchy bound `max/min <= 20`) and v2.375/376 (each sector is an infinite
geometrically-decaying tower `g ~ r^n`) look in *tension* -- an infinite tower's deep couplings go to zero, so
`max/min -> infinity` would violate the hierarchy bound. Are they consistent, and what do they jointly imply?

## Result (consistent, and they fix the number of light couplings)

The deep tower levels are **heavier states** -- the SDC's own exponentially-light tower at large field distance
-- so the SDC predicts exactly the tower it appears to forbid. The hierarchy bound then applies within one
"window," and the number of simultaneously-light levels is set by *both* facts:

```text
  n_max = log(R_max) / log(1/r)        (R_max = 20, the SDC bound)

  curvature tower  r = 0.466  ->  n_max = 3.93   (basis uses 3 of ~4;  saturation 0.76)
  matter    tower  r = 0.756  ->  n_max = 10.72  (basis uses 3 of ~11; saturation 0.28)
```

All 5/5 checks pass. The engine's basis fits comfortably inside both windows, and the **curvature basis nearly
saturates** its ~4-slot window while matter uses only ~3 of ~11.

## What this establishes

The SDC and the tower are two faces of one physics. Together they explain **why the gravitational EFT
truncates to a few curvature operators**: the SDC window is only ~4 deep for the curvature tower's decay rate,
so the low-energy curvature EFT is nearly as deep as the SDC allows -- the engine's finite curvature basis
(g_R2, g_R3, g_R4) is not an arbitrary truncation but the depth the SDC + tower permit. That the curvature
sector saturates its window sooner than matter is exactly the v2.376 prediction (the curvature tower is the
harder, faster-decaying one). It also predicts the **matter sector could support a much deeper tower of light
operators (~11)** than the engine carries -- room the dark matter sector (v2.381) hides.

## Honest scope

`n_max = log(R_max)/log(1/r)` is a HEURISTIC bridge, not a theorem: it counts geometric-floor tower levels
(v2.375, single-scale) before the coupling drops below `g_top/R_max`, so a faster-decaying multi-state tower
gives *fewer* levels -- `n_max` is an upper estimate. `R_max = 20` is the engine's toy SDC proxy (v2.383);
`n_max` scales as `log(R_max)`, so a different encoding shifts the windows (weakly, logarithmically). The tower
ratios (0.466, 0.756) are constructed toy-basis values, but the ORDERING (curvature window < matter window,
curvature nearer saturation) is robust -- it follows from the basis-robust v2.376 fact. "Engine basis fits the
window" is a CONSISTENCY check, not a prediction the engine independently tests; the curvature-basis-vs-window
match (3 vs ~4) is suggestive, not exact. This connects two prior toy-encoded results and adds no new datum.
Robust content: the SDC and tower are mutually consistent (deep levels = heavier states), and the curvature
sector's faster decay makes its hierarchy window shallower than matter's, so the curvature EFT truncates sooner
-- a joint-consistency explanation for the few-curvature-operator basis. Toy `R_max` and ratios, robust
ordering, heuristic count.
