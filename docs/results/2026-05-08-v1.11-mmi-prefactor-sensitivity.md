## Reading

If a framework's transition is at prefactor ≈ 1.0 (the canonical value), its status is on a knife-edge and may not survive publication-grade encoding. If the transition is far from 1.0, the failure is robust to prefactor choice.

Particular attention: LQG-induced. The transition value tells us by how much the literal BNOSSW MMI form's prefactor can vary before LQG passes.
