sphere, shadow, ringdown, ISCO, accretion efficiency -- each a geodesic/eikonal functional of the
SAME metric `f(r)`. So a single non-Kerr / higher-curvature deformation must shift ALL of them

## Result

photon sphere, v2.237 ISCO), every channel reproduces its GR baseline and responds coherently:

```text

  merger freq Omega_ISCO     GW        0.06804        +0.1110

  shadow <-> ringdown-frequency locking residual = 1.1e-11  (the Omega_c = 1/b_c identity)
```

**A single deformation imprints a coherent fingerprint across every observable.** The shadow shrinks
while the ringdown frequency rises -- EXACTLY locked (residual `1e-11`, the v2.231 `Omega_c = 1/b_c`
identity) -- and the ringdown damping, ISCO radius, accretion efficiency, and merger frequency
respond independently. The same `eps` raises the disk efficiency and the merger frequency while
shrinking the shadow and pulling the ISCO inward: the shadow (EM imaging), ringdown (GW post-merger),
disk efficiency (EM luminosity), and merger frequency (GW inspiral) are **not independent knobs but
one deformation seen six ways** -- which is exactly what makes a deviation falsifiable across both
the electromagnetic and gravitational-wave channels.
