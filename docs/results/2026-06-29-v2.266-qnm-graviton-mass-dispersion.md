# v2.266 - Graviton mass bounds from gravitational-wave dispersion (the low-energy mirror of LIV)

## Question

A fresh QG-phenomenology sector opening after the swampland arc (v2.254-v2.265). General relativity's
graviton is exactly massless; what would a nonzero graviton rest mass `m_g` do, how do gravitational
waves bound it, and how does that probe relate to the v2.251 Lorentz-violation dispersion test?

## Result

A graviton mass makes gravitational waves DISPERSIVE, `v_g/c = sqrt(1 - (m_g c^2 / E)^2)`, so the
high- and low-frequency parts of an inspiral arrive at slightly different times. Over a distance `D`
the spread between `f1 < f2` is `Delta t = (D/2c)(m_g c^2)^2 (1/E1^2 - 1/E2^2)`. At the LVC bound
`m_g < 1.2e-22 eV`:

```text
  Compton wavelength lambda_g = h c /(m_g c^2) = 1.03e13 km   (reproduces the published > 1e13 km)

  event                   D(Mpc)   dispersion delay (35-250 Hz)   delay / signal
  GW150914                  410     14.2 ms                        0.071
  GW170817 (BNS)             40      1.4 ms                        1.4e-5
  a z~1 LISA/3G source     6700    232.3 ms                        0.232

  speed deficit at E = 4e-12 eV (~kHz):  1 - v_g/c = 4.5e-22   (<< GW170817 bound |Dv/c| < 1e-15)
```

The mechanism is transparent: at the bound a graviton would smear GW150914's 35-250 Hz band by
**~14 ms over its ~0.2 s signal** -- a few-percent distortion of the chirp, right at the edge of
detectability, which is why the bound lands near `1e-22 eV`. The kHz speed deficit is only `~4.5e-22`,
far inside the GW170817 multimessenger speed bound -- so that constant-offset bound does *not*
constrain a mass this small; the in-band DISPERSIVE test is the sensitive one.

## What this establishes

The graviton-mass bound is the **low-energy mirror** of the v2.251 Lorentz-violation probe: a mass
makes the speed deficit grow as `1/E^2` (worst at low energy / long wavelength), while Planck-scale
LIV makes it grow as `E^n` (worst at high energy). Opposite energy dependence -- the two are
complementary, not redundant, and the *sign* of the energy dependence is the discriminant. A massless
graviton is exact GR; any nonzero `m_g` is a falsifiable departure, and GW dispersion is the cleanest
dynamical bound on it. (Note the float-cancellation guard: at `m/E ~ 3e-11` the exact
`sqrt(1 - (m/E)^2)` rounds to `1.0`, so the deficit is computed directly from the series `0.5 (m/E)^2`
-- the same care as the v2.247 echo tortoise coordinate.)

## Honest scope

The LVC bound `m_g < 1.2e-22 eV` is the source-backed value; the dispersion-delay estimate here
reproduces its ORDER OF MAGNITUDE via the simple two-frequency time-spread heuristic, not the full
matched-filter waveform-phase analysis LIGO actually uses (which folds the `(m_g/E)^2` term into the
inspiral phase). The Compton-wavelength inversion is exact. Static Yukawa-potential bounds
(solar-system precession, galaxy-cluster dynamics) are complementary and in places tighter
(`m_g <~ 1e-29 eV`) but more model-dependent, so the clean dynamical GW bound is quoted as primary. A
consistent nonlinear massive-gravity theory (the vDVZ discontinuity, requiring the dRGT ghost-free
construction) is a separate theoretical issue not modelled here. A QG-phenomenology probe, not an
engine constraint refit.
