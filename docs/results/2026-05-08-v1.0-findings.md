## What v1.0 actually found

### 1. The dispersion-tower bound is *real* in the engine sense

Adding `g_6² ≤ g_4·g_8` — the chained Cauchy-Schwarz between adjacent forward-dispersion coefficients that the Caron-Huot et al program uses — produced **8 cells of growth** when removed from the importance ranking, second only to the original convexity bound (14 cells). It is not redundant. The dispersion tower beyond leading order carries genuinely new information, and the engine confirmed this on a controlled toy.

### 2. Adding the tower tightened all candidate frameworks

| framework | v0.8 fragility | v1.0 fragility | nearest binding (v1.0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| string_tree_eft | 0.053 | **0.039** | dispersion_tower_g6_squared_bound |
| asymptotic_safety | 0.096 | **0.038** | dispersion_tower_g6_squared_bound |
| lqg_induced | 0.021 | 0.021 | anomaly_cancellation |

String-EFT and Asymptotic Safety became materially more fragile when the next-order dispersion bound was added — both are now closest to violating *the same constraint*, the chained Cauchy-Schwarz. This is the kind of pinching real bootstrap progress produces.

### 3. Cross-class duality collapsed

| version | IoU(A, B) | A-only cells | B-only cells |
|---|---|---|---|
| v0.8 baseline | 0.33 | 6 | 273 |
| v1.0 baseline | **0.0072** | 4 | 407 |

The amplitude-bootstrap class (A) and information-theoretic class (B) now agree on 0.7% of allowed regions. **The duality conjecture (Idea #8 from the v0.4 theorize doc) is decisively *not* exact in this regime.** A and B encode genuinely independent physics. This is one of the cleaner negative results the engine has produced — the field's tendency to treat amplitude and information bounds as substantially overlapping does not hold at our publication-grade precision.

### 4. The Weak Gravity Conjecture and LIGO bound are silent at the default scenario

Both register zero importance: removing them leaves the allowed region unchanged. This is *informative*. It tells us:

- The default scenario fixes `g_R² = 0.3`, which is well inside both bounds (LIGO bound = 0.5, WGC bound = √g_4 ≈ 0.7 for typical allowed g_4). Other constraints carve the allowed region first.
- To probe these constraints, we need scenarios that take `g_R²` larger or `g_4` smaller. The v0.9 scenario explorer machinery is the right place to add such variants.

This is the engine doing what tools are supposed to do: telling us *where in theory space each constraint actually does work*, so that experiment design and theory prioritization can target the right regions.

### 5. Pure GR remains a corner with maximal degeneracy

At v1.0 the adversarial-bootstrap analytic center is essentially the origin, with **8 simultaneously binding constraints**. Pure GR is the limit point where every linear and Cauchy-Schwarz-style positivity bound vanishes simultaneously. UV completions sit slightly inside the wedge; the engine has now made this geometrically precise.

## What this changes about the synthesis

The v0.9 honest synthesis identified these patterns:

- Anomaly cancellation does dominant constraint work (v0.8 result).
- Frameworks cluster tightly (~0.15–0.30) and are far from GR (~0.5–0.8).
- Half of constraints are redundant.
- Cross-class duality is broken.

v1.0 strengthens these in different directions:

- **Anomaly cancellation is NOT dominant once the dispersion tower is in.** The convexity + tower constraints now carry the most importance. Anomaly drops to 1-cell growth. **Updated take: each new layer of class-A constraints further marginalizes class-C anomaly arguments. The class-A bootstrap is the engine of constraint-set tightening; class C is structurally important but does less local work.**
- **Frameworks remain clustered, but at v1.0 the gap between AS and string-EFT *narrowed* (0.150 → 0.180) while their gap from GR *widened* (0.522 → 0.602 for AS, 0.671 → 0.781 for string).** Adding `g_8` predictions to each framework increased the magnitude of the framework-fingerprint diff from GR but kept the inter-framework distances similar. **This sharpens the thin-shell hypothesis (Idea B from the synthesis): the shell may be thinner than v0.9 numbers suggested.**
- **The redundancy story persists:** at v1.0, 8 of 12 constraints have zero importance at the default scenario. Half the constraint set is doing all the work; the other half awaits a scenario that probes their corner of theory space.
- **Duality is more decisively broken at v1.0** — IoU dropped from 0.33 to 0.007. Whatever theoretical hopes existed for A/B duality, this engine's encoding does not support them. The classes encode genuinely orthogonal information, which means **bootstrap programs should treat them as additive, not redundant.**
