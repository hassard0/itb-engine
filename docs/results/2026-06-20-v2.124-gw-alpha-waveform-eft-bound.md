## Interpretation

This loop removes the remaining component-level open rows, but it does not solve
the discriminator. The waveform and EFT bounds are finite conservative envelopes
for the current source-backed alpha analysis domain, not a published full-IMR
cubic-EFT waveform family. Keeping the top-level budget open is deliberate:
without a joint-event posterior interpretation, calibrated likelihood scale, and
G8 component, the packet must not promote.
