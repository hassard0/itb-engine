# v2.449 - the falsification tracker: what would kill the candidate (the actionable dual of the consilience)

v2.446 catalogued what *agrees* on the candidate (seven independent principle-areas). This cycle builds the
**dual**: a single, actionable map of what would **disprove** it — for each prediction, the observable, the
kill-threshold, and where current data sits. Falsifiability is the candidate's central scientific virtue; this
gathers it in one honest place instead of scattered across notes.

## What this makes concrete

- **The two most decisive near-term tests** are the **birefringence handedness** (β>0) and the **g_R2
  over-determination** (w>−1 *and* r≈0.004 must co-occur — one without the other kills the single-scalaron story
  even though each front alone looks fine).
- **The primordial channel is non-discriminating**: a primordial null does *not* kill the candidate (it's
  plateau-class, v2.444/v2.447), so falsification effort belongs on **late-time and joint-consistency** fronts,
  not primordial searches.
- **No prediction is currently excluded**, and several sit favorable/consistent — the candidate is alive and
  sharply falsifiable.

This is the practical companion to the near-uniqueness claim: the consilience says *many independent principles
converge here*; the tracker says *here is exactly how to prove that wrong*.

## Honest scope

A **consolidation / accounting** artifact, not a new result — it re-organizes predictions and kill-conditions
established across the program (v2.421 portfolio, v2.442 four-front, v2.430 verdict, v2.411 exclusions) and adds
explicit thresholds + current-status lines. The predictions carry their original tiers: birefringence is a
~3.6σ **hint**, not a detection; w>−1 is the refined-dS proxy and the "thawing, no phantom" sharpening is the
canonical-scalar expectation, while DESI's CPL best-fit formally implies a phantom past a canonical field can't
reach (a real tension, honestly tracked); r and n_s are plateau-*class* (a detection confirms the class, not
uniquely the candidate); the g_4 front rests on the O(1)-toy matter-dominance map; the kill-thresholds are
approximate illustrative levels, not computed exclusion contours. The over-determination kill is the genuinely
sharp one (a joint inconsistency), but it too is sign/plateau-class level. The tracker's value is organizational
and honest — it makes falsifiability concrete and calibrated, not stronger than the underlying results.
