# v2.405 - SWING (new robustness axis): the central claim is empirically robust to O(1) toy-prefactor variation -- validating the "structure robust, numbers toy" caveat

## Question

Every result carries a honest-scope caveat: *structure robust, numbers toy*. That has been asserted but never
empirically tested. Does the program's central claim (v2.322: constructed feasible AND no named framework fits
theory+data) survive O(1) variation of the engine's toy prefactors?

## Result (yes -- the failures are point shifts, not collapses)

Re-running the central claim under 0.5x-2.0x variation of five tunable prefactors (cemz_kappa, anomaly_rho,
cubic_kappa, cft_alpha, efthedron_alpha), one at a time -- 25 variations:

```text
  "no named framework fits theory+data" survives:   25/25   (never breaks)
  feasible region non-empty:                         25/25   (never collapses)
  full unique-feasibility (constructed + no fw):      88%    (22/25)
  the 3 failures are POINT SHIFTS: anomaly_rho x0.5, cft_alpha x0.5, efthedron_alpha x2.0
    -- the fixed constructed point falls outside a rescaled window, but the region stays non-empty
       (a re-centered point is feasible) and matter dominance still holds there
```

All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The qualitative result -- a nonempty consistency-carved region that excludes every named framework -- **does not
depend on the specific toy prefactors**; only the quantitative location of the constructed point does, exactly
as the honest-scope sections have claimed. This closes the robustness theme on the strongest footing: the
candidate's central structural claims have now been tested against **four independent robustness axes** --
across the feasible **family** (v2.372 etc.), under **basis** resolution (c-a, v2.398-401), **adversarially**
(matter dominance, v2.391), and now under **prefactor** variation (here) -- and survive all four. So the
pervasive "toy numbers" caveat is precise and honest: it means the specific values move with the prefactors
(verified), not that the structure is fragile (falsified -- it is not).

## Honest scope

Only the CANONICAL-sourced prefactors are varied (the ones `build_stack(prefactors=)` exposes); several
constraints hardcode their O(1) factors (AnomalyCancellation c_anom/tolerance, WGC alpha, the parity kappa's,
the birefringence kappa_beta), which are NOT varied here -- a fuller test would include them. The variations
are ONE-AT-A-TIME over 0.5x-2.0x; joint variation of all prefactors is not scanned, so "robust" means "robust
to single-prefactor O(1) moves", strong but not exhaustive. "Region non-empty" at failures is a short-walk
existence check (lower bound), not a proof the region is large. The claim tested is the central QUALITATIVE one
(nonempty region + no framework); it does NOT test that every downstream number (0.72 M_Pl, 17.6 sigma) is
prefactor-stable -- those explicitly move, which is the point. A meta-validation of the honesty caveat, no new
physical datum. Robust content: the central claim survives single-prefactor O(1) variation, with failures being
constructed-point shifts not region collapses -- "structure robust, numbers toy" is empirically confirmed for
the central claim, not merely asserted.
