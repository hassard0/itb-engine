# v1.32 — A catalog of possible quantum gravities: the frontier is parity-violating

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan (16-core). 600 random-direction optimizations, 62 s.
**Builds on:** v1.27/v1.31 generative discoveries.

---

## Context

Rather than discover novel theories one at a time, this cycle maps the **full
extent** of consistent theory space: maximize 600 random linear objectives
(random directions in 7-D Wilson space) over the feasible set of the corrected
stack. Each maximizer is an **extreme point** — a consistent theory at a corner
of what the constraints allow. Deduplicated, these are a catalog of
qualitatively distinct quantum gravities.

## Headline — the entire frontier of consistent theory space is parity-violating

600 directions → **79 distinct extreme consistent theories**. Two facts:

1. **All 79 are parity-violating** (|g_R2_parity| + |g_R3_parity| > 0.03).
2. **58 of 79 sit > 0.15 from any catalogued framework** in coefficient space.

The five catalogued frameworks (string, AS, LQG, CDT, Pure GR) are all
parity-conserving and sit in the **interior**; every extremal consistent theory
— the edge of what is allowed — turns on parity. The honest interpretation:

> The allowed region has genuine extent in the parity directions (parity
> violation is permitted up to |parity| ≈ 0.14, v1.31). A generic extreme point
> therefore carries parity. Parity *conservation* is a special, lower-extent
> slice that the catalogued frameworks happen to occupy. **Consistent quantum
> gravity generically permits parity violation; the known frameworks are the
> special parity-conserving cases.**

This reframes the new-physics frontier: the unexplored bulk of consistent
theory space is parity-violating, and the experiments that probe it are
gravitational parity-violation measurements (v1.31's parity-amplitude observable).

## Diversity of the catalog

The 79 extreme theories span distinct signatures, e.g. (12 most novel):

| novelty | signature | robustness | character |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.28 | parity-violating, **high-g8** (0.55), cubic-suppressed | **63%** | robust, large dim-8 matter |
| 0.25 | parity-violating, near CDT, g_R2≈0.39 | 9–24% | strong-curvature parity branch |
| 0.23 | parity-violating, cubic-suppressed, near AS | — | weakly-coupled parity branch |
| 0.22 | parity-violating, high-g8, cubic-suppressed | — | string-like with parity |

Notably this surfaces a **second robust novel branch** distinct from v1.27's:
a **high-g8** (g_8 ≈ 0.55), cubic-suppressed, parity-violating theory feasible in
**63%** of the prefactor box. So the robust novel branches bracket the dimension-8
coupling from both sides — v1.27's low-g8 (0.045, 76%) and this high-g8 (0.55,
63%) — both cubic-suppressed. The dimension-8 matter coupling g_8 is therefore
the most *unconstrained* direction of the allowed region (theories exist across
nearly its whole range), which is exactly why v1.27 nominated **precision g_8**
as the most discriminating invented experiment.

## What this adds to the new-physics picture

- The catalogued frameworks occupy a small, parity-conserving, mid-g8 island.
- Consistent theory space extends well beyond them, generically into parity
  violation, and is widest along the dimension-8 matter coupling g_8.
- Two robustly-feasible novel branches (low-g8 and high-g8, both cubic-suppressed)
  and a family of fragile parity-violating branches populate the frontier.

The committed `experiments/results/out_catalog.json` holds all 79 theories with
coefficients, signatures, and novelty — a machine-readable catalog of consistent
quantum gravities for follow-up.

## Honest limitations

- Extreme points maximize *linear* objectives, so they hit the boundary; a random
  direction generically has parity components, which is *why* extremal theories
  carry parity. The robust content is that the allowed region **has extent** in
  the parity directions (so parity violation is genuinely permitted), and that
  the known frameworks are non-extremal parity-conserving points — not that
  "most theories Nature could pick" are parity-violating in any measure sense.
- Toy-precision coordinates throughout.
