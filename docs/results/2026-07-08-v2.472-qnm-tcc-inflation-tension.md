# v2.472 - the candidate's swampland-consistency is non-uniform: its inflation violates the TCC

The candidate's swampland-friendliness is a selling point — it admits dS (refined-dS), selects dS/Minkowski over AdS
(distance conjecture), and its tower fits the Emergent String Conjecture. But another swampland member, the
**Trans-Planckian Censorship Conjecture** (Bedroya–Vafa 2019), caps inflationary tensors at r ≲ 10⁻³⁰ (v2.263 —
primordial GWs forever unobservable). v2.263 *predates* the candidate's inflation identification (v2.441) and its
scale-clean tensor prediction r = 3(1−n_s)² ≈ 0.0037 (v2.452), so the two were never connected. Connecting them:

```text
  candidate predicts  r ~ 0.0037   (Starobinsky, H_inf ~ 1.6e13 GeV, N ~ 55, LiteBIRD-testable)
  TCC ceiling         r <~ 1e-30   (bound N < ln(M_Pl/H) ~ 12, violated ~4.6x by N ~ 55)
  => candidate inflation is ~28 orders ABOVE the TCC ceiling -- a direct, sharp VIOLATION
```

## The swampland scorecard

```text
  refined dS conjecture        SUPPORTS  (admits dS, g_Lambda <= g_R2)
  AdS distance conjecture      SUPPORTS  (selects dS/Minkowski over AdS)
  emergent string conjecture   SUPPORTS  (tower fits heterotic XOR KK)
  trans-Planckian censorship   VIOLATES  (r ~ 0.0037 >> 1e-30; N ~ 55 > TCC bound ~12)
```

So the candidate's swampland-consistency is **non-uniform** — supported by three conjectures, in tension with a
fourth. This is an honest crack in the swampland-friendliness framing (the flagship presents the support without
this caveat). The resolution is decidable and near-term: the TCC is **conjectural and widely regarded as too
strong**, and the candidate's r ≈ 0.0037 is LiteBIRD-testable — so a B-mode detection at r ~ 10⁻³ would **disfavor
the TCC, not the candidate**. The candidate makes a concrete bet *against* the TCC, siding with observable
high-scale inflation over the most aggressive swampland conjecture, with **LiteBIRD (~2030s) as the arbiter**.

## Honest scope

A synthesis of v2.263 (TCC caps r) and v2.452 (candidate's r ≈ 0.0037), with the candidate-specific quantification
(~28 orders) and the non-uniformity framing as the new content — not a new computation. The tension is
**class-level**: *all* observable-r inflation violates the TCC, so the candidate simply inherits the generic
high-scale-inflation-vs-TCC tension once its inflation is identified. The TCC is **conjectural and contested**, so
"violates the TCC" is a tension with a conjecture, not a proof the candidate is wrong — the honest reading is that
the candidate bets the TCC is too strong, a bet LiteBIRD arbitrates. The value is honesty + completeness: it
surfaces a genuine caveat the flagship's swampland story omitted.
