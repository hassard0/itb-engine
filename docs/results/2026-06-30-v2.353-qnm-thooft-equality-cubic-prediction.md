# v2.353 - What the theory predicts for the parity-odd cubic IF anomaly matching is its physically-motivated equality

## Question

v2.352 showed the parity-odd cubic `g_R3_parity` is UNPREDICTED (every constraint reading it is even), and
flagged the single exception: the engine encodes 't Hooft anomaly matching as a conservative BOUND with
slack, but its docstring motivates an EQUALITY ("the ratio of cubic to leading parity-violating coefficients
is FIXED by the IR matter content"). What does that equality predict?

## Result (a conditional second parity-odd prediction, data-pinned)

Under the equality the cubic is locked to the (data-pinned) quadratic by a matter-fixed ratio:

```text
  g_R3_parity = r * g_R2_parity ,   r = rho_match (g_4 + g_6) = 0.5 * 0.929 = 0.4645   (magnitude)

  predicted g_R3_parity at constructed quadratic:  0.0279
  cubic window across the quadratic window:         [0.0219, 0.0364]   (naive)
                                  self-consistent:  [0.0219, 0.0304]   (equality also tightens the quadratic)
  quadratic anomaly upper edge:  0.0783 (bound)  ->  0.0654 (equality, tighter)
  predicted point feasible in the CURRENT (bound-form) engine?   Yes (no violations)
```

All 5/5 checks pass. The equality has three effects: (1) the unpredicted cubic becomes a **prediction**,
data-pinned through `g_R2_parity` to a narrow `~[0.022, 0.030]` window instead of v2.352's symmetric
`[-0.036, 0.036]` bound; (2) it **tightens the quadratic too** (the cubic eats into the anomaly budget, so
the joint anomaly+equality upper edge on `g_R2_parity` drops from 0.0783 to 0.0654); (3) the predicted cubic
sits **inside the engine's current feasible window**, so the upgrade is consistent with everything already
imposed.

## What this establishes

The theory has a **latent second parity-odd prediction** that switches on under a single, well-motivated
constraint upgrade. It would be testable the same way as the first: the cubic Pontryagin coupling drives a
chiral signal whose size is now *fixed* (not merely bounded) by the same cosmic-birefringence data that pins
the quadratic. This converts v2.352's "one parity-odd prediction, the cubic unpredicted" into "**two
parity-odd predictions, both data-pinned**" -- conditional on the matching being an equality. It is the
constructive sequel to v2.352's flagged upgrade point.

## Honest scope

Explicitly **conditional** -- this does NOT change the core engine (still the bound-form 't Hooft matching);
it computes what the EQUALITY would predict, presented as a conditional. The equality is physically motivated
(anomaly matching is an equality in field theory), but the engine's docstring is explicit that the exact
ratio "depends on which fermion content the UV theory ultimately includes", so `r = rho_match(g_4+g_6)` is a
toy proxy with an O(1) prefactor `rho_match = 0.5` -- the predicted MAGNITUDE (0.028) scales with it and is
illustrative, not sharp. The matching fixes the RATIO/magnitude, **not the sign**, so even under the equality
`g_R3_parity` is predicted only up to sign unless a further input fixes the handedness. Everything inherits
the quadratic's dependence on the cosmic-birefringence data being real (v2.329) and on the anomaly prefactor
`rho` (v2.344). Robust content: IF matching is an equality, the cubic is locked to the quadratic by a
matter-fixed ratio (structural), becomes data-pinned, and tightens the quadratic -- and the predicted value
is feasible now. Toy basis, O(1) prefactors.
