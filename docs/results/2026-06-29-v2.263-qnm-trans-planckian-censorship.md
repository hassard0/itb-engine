# v2.263 - The Trans-Planckian Censorship Conjecture: why it predicts unobservable primordial GWs

## Question

A fresh QG-consistency / swampland probe extending the inflation-tensor (v2.253) and Swampland
Distance Conjecture (v2.255) arc. The Trans-Planckian Censorship Conjecture (TCC; Bedroya-Vafa 2019)
forbids an accelerating phase from stretching any ever-sub-Planckian length scale outside the Hubble
horizon. What does that demand of inflation -- and what does it say about the one place quantum
gravity is observable, the primordial tensor (B-mode) spectrum?

## Result

The TCC condition `e^N < M_Pl / H_f` caps the inflationary Hubble rate at `H < M_Pl e^{-N}`. Fed
through the same slow-roll relations as v2.253 (`H = M_Pl pi sqrt(A_s r / 2)`, reduced
`M_Pl = 2.435e18 GeV`, `A_s = 2.1e-9`), this caps the tensor-to-scalar ratio at
`r_max(N) = 2 e^{-2N} / (pi^2 A_s)`:

```text
  N    H_max(GeV)    r_max       V^1/4_max(GeV)   orders below CMB-S4
  44   1.9e-01       5.8e-31     8.9e+08          27.2
  50   4.7e-04       3.6e-36     4.5e+07          32.4
  60   2.1e-08       7.4e-45     3.0e+05          41.1
  70   9.7e-13       1.5e-53     2.0e+03          49.8
```

For the most generous e-fold count `N ~ 44` (the CMB-pivot value Bedroya-Vafa use), `r_max ~ 6e-31`
and `V^{1/4}_max ~ 9e8 GeV` -- **reproducing the published Bedroya-Vafa headline** (`V^{1/4} <~ 6e8
GeV`, `r <~ 1e-30`) to order of magnitude. That ceiling is **~27 orders of magnitude below the
CMB-S4 / LiteBIRD reach** (`r ~ 1e-3`) and still ~24 below even a speculative `r ~ 1e-6` floor. The
round-trip `H(r_max) <-> H_max(N)` is self-consistent to 1e-9 for every `N`.

## What this establishes

The TCC predicts primordial gravitational waves are **forever unobservable**, and conversely a single
B-mode detection at `r > ~1e-3` would **falsify** the conjecture -- a sharp, decidable QG-consistency
test on the one channel where quantum gravity is observable (the quantized-graviton vacuum
fluctuations of v2.253). It is exponentially stronger than the v2.255 Swampland Distance Conjecture,
which only mildly prefers small `r` (sub-Planckian field excursion): the two swampland criteria agree
in DIRECTION (both push `r` down) but the TCC is the aggressive one. So the swampland program makes a
concrete, near-term-falsifiable bet against detectable inflationary tensors.

## Honest scope

The TCC is a conjecture, not a theorem; the bound assumes a single near-constant-`H` accelerating
phase of `N` e-folds and the leading slow-roll tensor relation (exact at that order, as in v2.253).
The ceiling is exponentially sensitive to `N` (`r_max ~ e^{-2N}`: `N=44 -> 6e-31`, `N=60 -> 7e-45`),
so the specific number is order-of-magnitude -- but the QUALITATIVE conclusion (`r` unobservably small
for any plausible `N >~ 40`) is robust across the whole range. `A_s` is measured and `M_Pl` fixed
(reduced convention). Multi-field or non-standard thermal histories can relax the e-fold bookkeeping;
this is the vanilla single-field statement. A QG-consistency / falsifiability result, not an engine
constraint refit.
