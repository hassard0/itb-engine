# v2.309 - The joint carved curvature region: intersecting all the tower bounds at once

## Question

The curvature-carving arc bounded each operator SEPARATELY -- the g_R2 four-principle bracket (v2.302),
CEMZ-causality + cubic-positivity on g_R3 (v2.303), the moment-tower floor on g_R4 (v2.292). What do they
JOINTLY imply? The intersection -- the actual region of (g_R2, g_R3, g_R4) the engine allows for a fixed
matter sector -- is a new object, and only by intersecting do we learn whether the per-operator bounds are
mutually consistent.

## Result (one bounded, ladder-ordered region; frameworks enter at the moment floor)

Intersecting the six engine curvature constraints (GSL, QFC, CEMZ, cubic positivity, Riemann^4
positivity, moment-tower mandate) yields a single joint region that is nonempty and bounded. For the
representative matter sector `g_4 = 0.5`:

```text
  coordinate   joint feasible range        bound that sets it
  g_R2         [0.00,  0.99]               QFC ceiling g_R2 <= 2 g_4 = 1.0 ; GSL floor
  g_R3         [-0.50, 0.50]               cubic positivity |g_R3| <= g_4 = 0.5 (tighter than CEMZ here)
  g_R4         [0.00,  2.00]               moment-tower floor g_R3^2/g_R2 ; EFT box ceiling
```

-- only **11.1%** of the EFT box, a tightly carved sliver. So the separately-derived bounds are MUTUALLY
CONSISTENT: they don't conflict, they nest into one bounded ladder where the matter scale `g_4` caps
`g_R2` (QFC), `g_R2` and `g_4` cap `g_R3` (CEMZ/cubic), and `g_R3` with `g_R2` floors `g_R4` (moment
tower).

The joint picture reproduces v2.292 from a new direction. The moment-tower mandate forces `g_R4 > 0`
strictly whenever `g_R3 != 0`, so every framework with its default `g_R4 = 0` sits just OUTSIDE the joint
region and enters exactly at its moment-tower floor `g_R4 = g_R3^2/g_R2`:

```text
  framework          g_R4 floor = g_R3^2/g_R2   g_R4=0 outside   at floor inside
  string_tree_eft    0.1125                     yes              yes
  asymptotic_safety  0.0667                     yes              yes
  lqg_induced        0.3000                     yes              yes
  cdt                0.1023                     yes              yes
```

Each framework's joint-feasibility was cross-checked point-by-point against the engine's own constraint
classes (the vectorized region and the engine agree to 1e-9). All 6/6 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The separately-derived tower bounds are one coherent object: a bounded, ladder-ordered curvature region
(matter -> g_R2 -> g_R3 -> g_R4) that the consistent frameworks touch from the moment-tower floor. The
carving arc is not a list of independent inequalities but a single nested region -- and it is internally
consistent (nonempty) and tight (an 11% sliver), with the frameworks sitting on its lower g_R4 boundary.
