# v2.318 - Why does consistency reward mild parity violation? Anomaly matching is the wall that wants it

## Question

v2.317 found that under the corrected `convex_hull` encoding the engine's most-robust higher-derivative
gravity carries a MILD PARITY VIOLATION (`g_R2_parity ~ 0.04`; forcing parity to zero costs ~40% of the
robustness). Why -- which consistency condition rewards parity?

## Result (anomaly matching, physically the right culprit)

The binding wall on the parity-free preferred point is t'Hooft ANOMALY MATCHING, and anomaly matching is
relieved by a parity-odd curvature coupling:

```text
  binding wall at parity-free preferred:   t_hooft_anomaly_matching  (signed distance +0.0200)
  d(t_hooft signed distance)/d(g_R2_parity) = +0.366                  -> RELIEVED by parity
  relieved by parity:    right_handed_graviton_positivity, t_hooft_anomaly_matching   (chirality family)
  penalized by parity:   swampland_distance_conjecture, generalized_anomaly_inflow, ligo_birefringence
                         (all on ample margin except in the forbidden zone below)
```

Ranking every constraint by `d(signed distance)/d(g_R2_parity)`, the constraints *relieved* by parity are
exactly the chirality-sensitive ones (anomaly matching, right-handed graviton positivity); the penalized
ones sit on ample margin, so trading a little parity raises the worst-case margin. This is physically
sensible -- anomalies are chirality/parity objects, so matching the gravitational chiral anomaly naturally
calls for a parity-odd curvature coupling. All 5/5 checks pass.

## Two honest caveats (that sharpen, not soften)

- **Distance-conjecture forbidden zone.** A *tiny* parity coupling (~0.012) makes the max/min coupling
  ratio explode and fails the distance conjecture (signed distance -21.7). So the preferred parity is not
  infinitesimal but a finite ~0.04, on the far side of the forbidden zone.
- **Joint, not isolated.** Realizing the full +0.033 robustness of v2.317 needs a coordinated shift
  (parity together with a slightly larger g_4 and trimmed g_R2): the parity-preferring optimum binds
  `bnossw_monogamy` at +0.033 vs the parity-free +0.020. Parity at the fixed point gains only marginally;
  it is a relaxation *led* by the relieved anomaly wall, not an isolated knob.

## What this establishes

The corrected new-theory headline becomes mechanistic: the most-robustly-consistent gravity is mildly
parity-violating BECAUSE anomaly matching -- the binding universality wall (v2.314/v2.315) -- is relieved
by a parity-odd curvature coupling. The same universality family that excludes lqg and bounds the
preferred framework also, through its anomaly member, mildly prefers CP violation in the curvature sector.
