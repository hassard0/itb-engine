## Result (a concrete, refutable roadmap)

The constructed theory's single parity coupling (`g_R2_parity = 0.06`) drives three correlated probes, all
predicted to be exactly zero by the parity-even frameworks:

```text

```

The constructed framework predicts `beta = 0.20 deg` (the theory+data window spans 0.16-0.27 deg),
consistent with the measured `0.34 +/- 0.09 deg` at ~1.5 sigma but at the LOW edge -- the central value
mildly prefers more parity (toward lqg's larger coupling). The chiral primordial GW is the cleanest future
test: a right-handed `Pi = +0.24` (nonzero TB/EB) versus exactly zero for parity-even gravity. All 5/5
checks pass.

## What this establishes

The constructed theory is genuinely refutable through a clean discriminator: parity-even gravity predicts
exactly zero on all three parity probes (a symmetry statement), while the constructed theory predicts a
definite right-handed signal on each, and the three are correlated (one coupling). The decisive next test
is the CMB TB/EB chirality (LiteBIRD / CMB-S4); the whole parity-violating story stands or falls on whether
the cosmic-birefringence signal confirms -- a confirmed `beta = 0` would end it. The matter sector is the
loosely-pinned region (v2.327) future positivity/dispersion data would tighten, but it does not
discriminate on parity. This turns the program's predictions into an actionable testability map.
