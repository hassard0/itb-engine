# v1.29 — Experiment-priority ranking on the corrected stack: the metric saturates

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan.
**Builds on:** corrected v1.27 stack; v1.19 priority ranking.

---

## Context

The engine's flagship real-world output is a ranked list of experiments by how
much currently-allowed theory space each would carve out. The published v1.19
ranking used the pre-audit stack. This recomputes it on the corrected stack
(convex-hull RFC, geometric BNOSSW, forward positivity, matter s³) and adds the
two experiments v1.27 invented (precision g_8; cubic gravitational parity
g_R3_parity).

## Result — the ranking saturates and is not informative as currently defined

On both swept planes, **six to seven experiments tie at 100% exclusion**:

| plane | baseline allowed cells | experiments at 100% |
|---|---|---|
| g_R2 × g_R2_parity | 33 | LIGO-O5-graviton-mass, Eöt-Wash, CMB-S4-tight, Bouwmeester-collapse, Bouwmeester-g_R3, **INVENTED-precision-g8** |
| g_R3 × g_R3_parity | 7 | LIGO-O5-graviton-mass, Eöt-Wash, CMB-S4-tight, Bouwmeester-collapse, **INVENTED-precision-g8** |

The two invented experiments rank in the top tier (g_8 at 100%), but the tie
makes the ordering meaningless.

## Diagnosis — the metric rewards forecast central value, not precision

Each experiment is modelled as a forecast with a *central value* (here null, =0)
and a precision. The allowed region requires **nonzero** couplings (g_4≈0.5,
g_R2≈0.2, …). A forecast centred at 0 with any reasonable precision therefore
excludes the *entire* allowed region — so every experiment targeting an
in-region coefficient scores ~100%, regardless of its precision or which
theories it actually discriminates. The ranking is dominated by the arbitrary
choice of forecast central value, not by informativeness.

This is the same class of finding as the rest of the realism program: a headline
engine output (here the experiment ranking) does not survive scrutiny in its
current form. The v1.19 "CMB-S4 / Bouwmeester over LIGO" ranking should not be
trusted — its ordering is an artifact of the forecast central values and the
specific 2-D plane, not a robust statement of experimental priority.

## What a meaningful metric would require

- Forecast central values placed at each framework's *predicted* coefficient
  value (so "if the world is string-EFT, what does this experiment exclude?"),
  not a uniform null.
- Exclusion measured against a realistic prior over the **feasible region**
  (importance-sampled, since it is a thin sliver — v1.28), not a 2-D plane with
  the other five coefficients pinned.
- Reporting *discrimination between surviving frameworks* (string/AS/CDT, which
  v1.26 showed cannot yet be ranked), not raw allowed-cell counts.

That is a genuine subproject (a Fisher-information / expected-discrimination
metric on the feasible region) and is logged as the v1.30 target. Until then,
the engine can honestly say *which observables differ most between candidate
theories* (the v1.27 "invented experiment" = max-separation coordinate: g_8 for
the novel branch, g_R3_parity for the parity branch) — but **not** a robust
numerical priority ordering.
