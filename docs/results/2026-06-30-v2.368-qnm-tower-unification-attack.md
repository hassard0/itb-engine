# v2.368 - ATTACK on the v2.367 conjecture: a spin-2-vs-matter form factor breaks the SHARP value but not the BAND

## Question (attacking my own swing)

The retrospective's rule: attack the conjecture, don't admire it. v2.367 conjectured that a shared matter+
graviton Regge tower forces `r_curv = r_matter`, pinning the ringdown quartic to `g_R4 = 0.0555`. The obvious
attack: the spin-2 graviton and the matter probe couple to the tower states through DIFFERENT form factors, so
even with shared states the spectral weights differ and the two dispersion ratios need not be equal. Does the
prediction survive?

## Result (strong form dies, weak form survives)

A toy two-state Regge tower (masses (1, 3), matter weights (1, 0.5)) reproduces `r_matter = 0.758 ~ 0.756`.
The curvature sector sees the SAME states with a spin form factor `mu^s` (s=0 = identical form factors = the
conjecture's exact case). Scanning s:

```text
  toy tower reproduces r_matter = 0.758   (target 0.756)
  sharp s=0 prediction:  g_R4 = 0.0554
  plausible band s in [-1, 1]:  r_curv in [0.751, 0.835],  g_R4 in [0.0503, 0.0559]   (~10% spread)
  STRONG form (sharp 0.0555) fragile:  True
  WEAK form (multi-state, above floor 0.042) robust:  True
```

All 5/5 checks pass. **The strong form is refuted:** the sharp value `g_R4 = 0.0555` is the `s = 0` special
case; once the curvature sector weights the shared states by a spin form factor, `r_curv` drifts and `g_R4`
drifts over a ~10% band, not a single value. The form-factor objection is real. **But the weak form
survives:** across the entire plausible band (and out to `s = 2`, a large difference), the curvature sector
stays MULTI-STATE (`r_curv < 1`) and `g_R4` stays STRICTLY ABOVE the moment floor 0.042, in a narrow band
`~[0.050, 0.056]` -- i.e. ~1.2-1.3x the floor, well below the causality cap 0.339.

## What this establishes

The conjecture is **downgraded from a point to a robust band**, which is its correct, defensible strength. The
shared-tower hypothesis still makes a genuine, falsifiable ringdown claim: **IF matter and the graviton share
the tower, the ringdown quartic is ~1.2-1.3x its moment-tower minimum (`g_R4 ~ 0.050-0.056`), robustly above
the floor** -- and a ringdown measurement finding `g_R4` AT the floor (single-state curvature) would still
refute it. This is a swing that died honestly in its strong form and survived in its weak form -- exactly the
report the mandate asks for. Net: the ringdown channel gets a *band* prediction from a UV-structure argument,
which is more than the "loose [0.05, 0.60]" it had and less than the over-claimed point of v2.367.

## Honest scope

This is a TOY two-state tower -- its job is to probe the STRUCTURAL sensitivity of the ratio to a form-factor
difference, not to compute the real `g_R4`. The form-factor difference is modeled as a single power `mu^s` (a
moment-index shift); real spin-2 vs scalar form factors are more complex (possibly non-monotonic, with full
residue structure), so the specific `[0.050, 0.056]` band is illustrative of ROBUSTNESS, not a derived
interval. The matter ratio 0.756 is the engine's toy encoding (v2.343). The plausible band `s in [-1, 1]` is a
judgement about how different spin-2 and scalar form factors realistically are; a pathological form factor
(very large `|s|` or a sign-structured residue) could push `r_curv` to 1 and break even the weak form -- so
the weak form is robust to MODERATE, not arbitrary, differences. The whole thing is layered on the v2.367
shared-tower hypothesis, itself a conjecture. Robust content: the sharp value is form-factor-fragile (strong
form refuted); "curvature multi-state, `g_R4 ~1.2-1.3x` floor" is robust to moderate form-factor differences.
Toy model + toy basis.
