# v2.383 - SWING (landscape topology): the space of consistent QG EFTs is NON-convex, and the Swampland Distance Conjecture is the sole cause

## Question (a fresh structural conjecture)

Is the space of consistent quantum-gravity EFTs **convex** -- a single connected convex family, so any two
consistent theories interpolate through consistent ones -- or not?

## Result (non-convex; the SDC alone breaks it)

Testing directly -- sample feasible theories, take straight-line convex combinations, check the interpolants
against the real feasibility oracle:

```text
  interpolants infeasible (full stack):   7.8%      -> NON-convex
  SDC share of all midpoint violations:   99.9%
  interpolants infeasible WITHOUT the SDC: 0.01%     -> convexity restored
  violations by constraint: {swampland_distance_conjecture: 781, anomaly_cancellation: 1}
```

All 5/5 checks pass. ~8% of interpolations between two consistent theories pass through an *inconsistent* one
(oracle-checked, not sampling), and **99.9% of the violations are the Swampland Distance Conjecture**. Dropping
the SDC restores convexity to 99.99%.

## What this establishes

The amplitude-level consistency conditions -- positivity, causality, dispersion, anomaly matching, the
EFT-hedron -- are all **convex** (intersections of PSD cones and half-spaces, as expected for forward-limit and
unitarity bounds). The **one genuinely quantum-gravitational condition, the SDC, is the one that breaks
convexity**. The SDC is encoded as an aspect-ratio / hierarchy bound (`max|g|/min|g_nonzero| <= 20`):
interpolating a coupling from a symmetry-protected zero up through tiny-but-nonzero values crosses the
hierarchy bound -- a large-field-distance region where the conjecture's light tower would appear. So consistent
QG EFTs form a **connected but non-convex family** (the random walk stayed in one component throughout), and
the non-convexity is a genuine **swampland signature**: invisible to the low-energy amplitude bounds, showing
up only in the field-space-geometry constraint. Two consistent theories can be separated by a "swampland
valley," and bridging it requires routing *around* the tiny-coupling region.

## Honest scope

The non-convexity is measured with the real oracle on the midpoints, so it is a genuine property of the encoded
region (endpoints verified feasible, midpoint verified infeasible) -- not a sampling artifact. The SDC
attribution and drop-SDC control are direct. CAVEATS: the SDC is the engine's TOY encoding of Ooguri-Vafa -- an
aspect-ratio bound "much looser than the literal exponential of moduli distance" (its own docstring). A
strictly-positive aspect-ratio bound is itself convex (pairwise half-spaces `g_i <= 20 g_j`); the non-convexity
enters specifically through the treatment of **near-zero couplings** -- a coupling exactly zero
(symmetry-protected) is excluded from the ratio and allowed, but a tiny nonzero value is included and can blow
the ratio past 20, so the boundary is non-convex around the coupling-approaches-zero locus. That is a real
feature of the encoding (and arguably of the physics: exact zero vs small explicit breaking differ), but it is
encoding-specific -- a different SDC proxy would move the exact non-convex locus. The convexity of the
amplitude constraints is robust (they are literally PSD/half-space conditions). "Connected" is about the
sampled component, not a global proof. Robust content: the amplitude sector is convex and the swampland (SDC)
constraint is what makes the consistent-EFT region non-convex. Toy SDC proxy, real oracle-checked
non-convexity.
