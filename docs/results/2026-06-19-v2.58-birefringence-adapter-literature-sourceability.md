# v2.58 - Birefringence adapter sourceability: CMB beta is not a direct gravity-parity map

## Question

v2.56 and v2.57 showed that the engine needs a source-backed map from CMB
polarization rotation `beta` to the gravitational parity coefficients
`g_R2_parity` / `g_R3_parity`. This audit checks the literature split: does CMB
cosmic birefringence directly source that map, or does it live in a separate
electromagnetic/axion channel?

## Interpretation

The literature supports CMB beta as an electromagnetic axion-photon
birefringence observable. It also supports gravitational Chern-Simons parity
channels, usually through gravitational-wave observables. It does not provide a
universal, engine-normalized bridge from CMB beta to
`g_R2_parity`/`g_R3_parity`.

The direct beta-to-gravity-parity route must therefore remain blocked or be
split into:

- an electromagnetic/axion route for CMB beta; and
- a gravitational-wave birefringence route for engine gravitational parity.
