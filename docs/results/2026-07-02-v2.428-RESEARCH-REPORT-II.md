## Abstract

Intersecting the known consistency conditions on a low-energy effective field theory of gravity + matter —
amplitude positivity, causality, the conformal-collider bootstrap, and the swampland conjectures — over a
space of Wilson coefficients leaves a **single, tiny, connected region**: one candidate low-energy quantum-gravity
EFT, not a landscape. This report presents that candidate, and — the central methodological contribution — a
**first-class rigor classification** separating what the engine establishes with *zero toy input* (source-exact
amplitude/causality bounds) from what rests on conjectural or O(1)-normalized proxies. The result: the candidate's
**entire matter-gravity content is rigorous** (LQG-induced gravity excluded; matter dominance's ceiling and
extremal-black-hole decay forced; the leading curvature coupling forced by the matter × cubic-curvature pair),
with the toy-dependence chased down to a single parity-magnitude coefficient that is itself rigorously bounded and
data-pinned. Rigor determines a *family*; data (cosmic birefringence) selects the *point*. The candidate is a **multi-state (stringy) EFT** whose leading, best-motivated UV completion is a **heterotic string compactification + its model-independent axion** (its one data-selected coupling) — though CDT and asymptotic safety remain competitive, and discriminating them needs UV data. The candidate is
falsifiable on four near-term experiments resting on three keystone couplings (two over-determined), and it has a
cosmological history structured by the R² operator — a heavy scalaron drives Starobinsky inflation and *bounds*
a separate quintessence dark energy — the parity axion itself, which also sources cosmic birefringence, so β and w are correlated and f_a ~ M_Pl is triply required (corrected v2.457, resolved v2.458–461). Underlying the near-uniqueness is a **consilience** -- the candidate is the single point where seven independent theoretical-consistency areas (analyticity, causality, holography, swampland, black-hole thermodynamics, anomalies, EFT-validity) and six measurements all agree, with an actionable falsification tracker giving each prediction's kill-threshold (none currently excluded). Its sharpest, most scale-clean content is a **scale-independent core** (v2.451–455): the cosmic-birefringence size β ~ α_EM (a dimensionless UV near-prediction of the measured 0.34°), the inflation consistency line r = 3(1−n_s)², and the dark-energy thawing line wa ≈ −1.5(1+w0) — predictions of real data needing no string scale or toy coefficient. Every magnitude is O(1)-toy; the robust content throughout is signs, orderings, structure, and which experiment constrains what.

## 1. Method: swampland-complete carving + rigor tiering

The engine ([`itb.engine.check`](../../src/itb/engine.py)) evaluates a **Theory** (a vector of Wilson
coefficients — matter g_4, g_6, g_8, g_10; curvature g_R2, g_R3, g_R4; parity g_R2_parity, g_R3_parity; Weyl² g_C;
vacuum energy g_Lambda) against a stack of **42 base constraints** (plus opt-in extensions). The constraints are
now **rigor-tagged** (`experiments/stack.py`, v2.411):

- **19 rigorous** — source-exact amplitude positivity / causality / bootstrap bounds (Adams–Nicolis–Rattazzi 2006;
  Caron-Huot et al 2021/2024; Camanho–Edelstein–Maldacena–Zhiboedov 2014; Hofman–Maldacena 2008;
  Arkani-Hamed–Huang–Huang EFThedron). "Rigorous" = the inequality *structure* is the published result; some
  carry a simplified O(1) prefactor, shown not to drive the headlines ([v2.427]).
- **17 sourced_proxy** — real conjectures/theorems (WGC, swampland distance/species, anomaly matching, GSL/QFC,
  complexity) via toy O(1) proxy forms.
- **6 data** — real measurements via O(1) observable maps.

The **method itself is the original claim**: the falsifiable output is the *geometry of the feasible region* — a
family carved by the rigorous rules, collapsed to a point by data.

## 3. What is rigorous (the de-toying arc, v2.411–427)

The central result of this report. Running the analysis on the **rigorous core alone** (zero toy input):

- **LQG-induced gravity is excluded** — its couplings violate graviton forward positivity, the cross-sector
  EFThedron, and the CFT flat-space bootstrap ([v2.411]). This exclusion is **prefactor-independent** ([v2.427]).
- **Matter dominance's ceiling** (g_R2 ≤ √g_4, the WGC) and **extremal-BH decay** (ΔS_ext ≥ 0) are *implied by*
  the rigorous core over the feasible region ([v2.412]).
- **The leading curvature coupling** is forced positive by the **matter × cubic-curvature** pair (matter alone
  doesn't; g_R3 alone is infeasible; the full feasibility lattice, [v2.417], corrects the earlier "matter sources

  candidate ([v2.419]).

The verified per-claim rigor ledger is [v2.415]. The **single residual toy** is the parity-magnitude coefficient
— itself rigorously capped (parity-decomposed positivity) and data-pinned (birefringence), with the toy playing
only a minor upper-edge role ([v2.418]).

## 4. The dark-energy sector: a quintessence axion (v2.422–425, corrected v2.457, resolved v2.458–461)

A new opt-in sector adds a vacuum-energy coupling g_Lambda and swampland constraints (all `sourced_proxy`):
- **admits** a positive cosmological constant, capped at g_Lambda ≤ g_R2 (refined dS conjecture — the keystone
  curvature coupling also bounds the dark energy);
- **selects de Sitter / Minkowski over anti-de Sitter** (the bounded tower forbids a small-|Λ| AdS vacuum via the
  AdS distance conjecture);
- **predicts w ≳ −1** (flat Starobinsky plateau → true-CC-to-mild-quintessence; DESI/Euclid-testable).

Here g_R2 both **drives inflation** (a heavy scalaron) and **bounds** the dark energy (g_Lambda ≤ g_R2) — a structural link between two *separate* fields ~25 orders apart, not one dynamical scalaron (corrected v2.457). This addresses the *structure* of the vacuum energy, **not** the CC magnitude problem (g_Lambda is dimensionless; the engine has no absolute scale).

**The dark-energy field is the parity axion (v2.458–461).** With the heavy R² scalaron removed from the dark-energy role, the natural occupant is the model-independent **axion** itself — nearly massless (f_a ~ M_Pl), already the birefringence source. So *one rolling axion* sources **both** the dark energy (thawing, w > −1) and cosmic birefringence (β ~ α_EM), from a single O(1) roll — a **genuine same-field over-determination**: β ≠ 0 requires dynamical w0 > −1, and both are currently hinted (v2.459). Its decay constant is fixed by a **triple convergence** — swampland quintessence (refined-dS + slow-roll), the scale-clean β ~ α_EM, and the negligible birefringence anisotropy *all* require f_a ~ M_Pl (v2.461), the model-independent axion's value. The axion sits at the marginal swampland edge, predicting **detectable** dynamical dark energy (1+w0 ~ O(0.1), not a pure CC), with the honest tension that matching DESI's exact w0 nudges f_a slightly super-Planckian (the generic quintessence-swampland difficulty). This is one instance of a broader **pattern**: the candidate satisfies the *structural* swampland conjectures (refined-dS, distance, ESC) but violates the two *aggressive* ones — the TCC (on its r ~ 0.0037) and the axion-WGC (on its ultralight DE axion) — and those two violations are exactly what would forbid its two *observable* predictions (inflationary r, dynamical w). So the candidate's observable quantum-gravity predictions live at the aggressive-swampland edge and *arbitrate* those contested conjectures: a LiteBIRD r-detection or a DESI dynamical-w detection disfavors the TCC / axion-WGC, not the candidate (v2.472–473). Consequence: the dark energy is an *ultralight* axion (no sub-mm fifth force — retiring the old scalaron-DE prediction, v2.459).

## 5. The UV completion (v2.433–466): a leading candidate, an honest degeneracy, and two layers

The deepest question — what UV theory does the candidate embed in? — has a concrete, honestly-bounded answer.

- **The candidate is a small parity deformation of string tree-level EFT.** The engine's string tree-EFT point
  is feasible in the rigorous cage, is the parity-conserving rival (rejected only by cosmic birefringence), and
  sits within 0.067 (parity-even) of the candidate — differing *only* in the parity coupling (v2.433).
- **Specifically heterotic, not type II** (v2.434), on two independent arguments: (1) given the candidate's
  cubic-curvature and parity couplings, a nonzero R² (Gauss–Bonnet) term is *rigorously required* (setting
  g_R2=0 violates graviton forward positivity + CEMZ causality + cross-sector EFThedron) — which tree-level type
  II (R⁴-only) lacks but heterotic has at α′; (2) the candidate's one data-selected coupling, the parity, is a
  gravitational-Chern–Simons `R∧R̃` term — exactly the heterotic **model-independent axion**'s Green–Schwarz
  coupling.
- **The parity may be *determined*, not free** (v2.435): the model-independent axion's coupling is *universal*
  (fixed by the gravitational anomaly, the same in every heterotic compactification). So — contingent on that
  identification — the candidate's last free coupling is fixed, heterotic *predicts* cosmic birefringence rather
  than accommodating it, and the candidate approaches a (near-)parameter-free heterotic prediction.

**Everything converges on one object — the parity coupling:** the thing the rigorous cage cannot force (§3), the
empirical verdict tests (§6), and the UV embedding sources — and, under heterotic universality, the thing that
becomes fixed. *This is a structural identification from textbook string facts + the engine, not a
compactification computation; the concrete remaining work is a heterotic vacuum whose R²:R³:R⁴ and axion coupling
land in the cage. Type II is disfavored, not excluded (the clean statement is tree-level); the β magnitude needs
the string scale.*

**But heterotic is the *leading* completion, not the unique one (v2.436–438).** A full UV tournament finds
**CDT (a near-tie) and asymptotic safety (competitive)** also sit in the rigorous cage and are reachable by the
same parity deformation — only LQG is excluded (v2.436). The low-energy EFT *cannot* discriminate string vs CDT vs
asymptotic safety: even the species scale fails (all give N≈1.5–1.7 towers, v2.437), because these are low-energy
quantities the completions share. What the low-energy amplitude *can* say is that the UV is a **multi-state tower**
(the candidate is interior to the positivity bound, not saturating — v2.438), ruling out a single-resonance
completion; but the tower's **type** (Regge / Kaluza–Klein / fixed-point) — the actual discriminator — is a
**UV/high-energy observable** (the cutoff-scale spectrum), outside the low-energy program. So: heterotic/string is
the best-motivated leading completion, but the honest statement is *"a multi-state (stringy) EFT whose specific UV
completion the low-energy data cannot yet resolve."*

**The Emergent String Conjecture sharpens the frontier (v2.440).** The swampland ESC — every infinite tower is a
KK or an emergent-string tower — collapses the degeneracy into a clean *geometric* dichotomy: the candidate's
confirmed multi-state tower is a **heterotic-string tower XOR a Planckian-KK decompactification**. On the KK
branch the extra dimensions are ~Planckian (~10⁻³⁴ m), submm-invisible — so the candidate is *not* the
large-extra-dimensions scenario (consistent with its satisfied sub-mm constraint). A conjecture-tier sharpening
that presupposes the string landscape (it refines the string branch, doesn't exclude CDT/asymptotic safety).

**A scale-clean handle after all (v2.464–466).** The degeneracy above says the *low-energy* data cannot pick the UV completion — but a scale-independent handle *does* exist. In a string α′ expansion the couplings scale as g_k ~ (α′M_Pl²)^k, so every moment-tower consecutive-rung **double-ratio** (g_{k−1}g_{k+1})/g_k² has the string scale **cancel** (α′-powers sum to zero): it is a pure number, a scale-clean UV-embedding test that evades the 'blocked on the string scale' wall (v2.464), and the set of them is the candidate's **scale-clean fingerprint** (v2.465). Executing it with a toy flat-residue Regge tower — whose moments are Riemann ζ-values — gives ζ(2)ζ(4)/ζ(3)² = 1.23 and ζ(3)ζ(5)/ζ(4)² = 1.06, and the candidate's fingerprint (1.32, 1.00) is **string-like** in *shape*: > 1 (log-convex/multi-state) and decreasing toward 1 with rung, the low ratio matching to ~7% (v2.466). So the matter sector carries scale-clean, low-energy evidence that the candidate embeds in a *Regge (string) tower* — a currently-*passing* check at the toy-Regge level. Honest: a proof of concept (flat residues, not the exact Virasoro–Shapiro; the shape is robust, the exact value is not). The same fingerprint **discriminates the ESC dichotomy**: a Regge (string) tower and a KK tower give *different* scale-clean double-ratios (1.23 vs 1.05, since m²~n vs m~n), and the candidate's 1.32 favors the **heterotic-string branch** — the low-energy handle on tower *type* that the degeneracy above (v2.438) said was missing, giving the heterotic identification independent low-energy support (v2.474).

**Two layers of the solve (v2.439).** Underneath all of the above is a premise the engine does not carve: every
coupling it uses is *higher-derivative* (R² and up); the Einstein-Hilbert term — the graviton itself — is
presupposed. So "solving QG" splits into **Layer 1** (*is* gravity quantum — an empirical, table-top question:
classical collapse is excluded ~14 orders by Diósi–Penrose, and BMV gravitationally-induced entanglement is the
decisive ~2030s test) and **Layer 2** (*which* higher-derivative EFT — the entire program). The two are
observationally separate — BMV probes the leading Newtonian sector, the candidate's correlated signature probes
the higher-derivative couplings — so the complete solve needs **both** experiments: BMV (is it quantum) and the
correlated cosmological signature (which EFT).

## 6. Falsifiability — four experiments, three keystones, two over-determined (v2.442)

Atop the rigorous backbone (which already excludes LQG and the extreme archetypes), the candidate is falsifiable
on **four** near-term experiments resting on **three** keystone couplings:
1. **Parity** (g_R2_parity) — CMB cosmic birefringence (LiteBIRD/CMB-S4) now, GW birefringence (LISA/ET) later.
   The candidate's single live rival is a parity-conserving EFT, separated by *exactly* the birefringence datum
   ([v2.420]).
2. **Matter** (g_4) — CMB-S4: matter dominance forces a large g_4 (rigorous), but its CMB-S4 signature rests on
   a **toy O(1) map** (g_4 ↔ inflaton self-coupling; the engine's own `cmb_s4.py` says so), so the ">10σ
   tension" is the *weakest*, toy-map-tier front — not co-equal with the scale-clean parity/inflation ones (v2.460).
3. **Dark energy** (g_R2) — DESI/Euclid w(z): w > −1 mild-quintessence plateau.
4. **Primordial tensors** (g_R2) — LiteBIRD: R² Starobinsky inflation, n_s ≈ 0.964, r ≈ 0.004, *swampland-safe*
   (the inflation survives its own tower, v2.441).

The new structure (v2.442–443): **two keystones are over-determined**. g_R2 drives *both* the dark-energy plateau
(DESI w) *and* inflation (LiteBIRD r) — so w > −1 and r ≈ 0.004 must co-occur, or the single-scalaron story fails
— but at the OPERATOR level, not one dynamical field: the heavy inflaton (M ~ 3e13 GeV) is negligible today and g_R2 *bounds* (does not *be*) the separate quintessence dark energy, so this is a same-operator link, not a same-field lockstep (v2.457). The parity keystone is likewise
over-determined (birefringence + primordial chiral GWs, locked handedness), though the chiral-GW amplitude is
Planckian-suppressed and unobservable (v2.444), so parity's observable probe stays the late-time birefringence.
So the candidate is not hostage to any single measurement — it is a multi-front signature with two built-in
consistency cross-checks, and the inflation observables are plateau-*class* (they confirm the class, not uniquely
the candidate).

Two structural notes complete the cosmology. The candidate's late universe is a **two-field system** (v2.448): R²
(parity-even) propagates a **scalaron** — the inflaton *and* dark-energy field — while R∧R̃ (parity-odd) couples a
distinct **axion** for birefringence; opposite parity means they cannot be the same field (the scalaron dominates
ρ_DE, the axion is a subdominant birefringence source). And the **primordial channel is closed as a discriminator**
(v2.447): the chiral GW is Planckian-suppressed, the (n_s, r) are plateau-class, and no state sits in the
cosmological-collider window (the tower is ~10³–10⁵ × H, the axion ≪ H) — so the candidate's inflation is
observationally generic Starobinsky and *all* candidate-specific discrimination is late-time.

## 7. The consilience and its dual (v2.445–449)

Two capstone reframings. First, a **thermodynamic pillar** (v2.445): causality (the Hofman–Maldacena wedge,
forcing the Weyl² coupling g_C ≥ 0, independent of the a=c assumption) and the black-hole second law
(Reall–Santos / Cheung–Liu–Remmen entropy positivity, ΔS ~ +g_C) *agree* on the candidate's g_C sign — a positive
neutral-Schwarzschild entropy shift.

This exposes the deeper structure (v2.446): grouping the 42 constraints by independent physical principle, the
candidate is the single point where **seven distinct theoretical-consistency areas** — analyticity/unitarity,
causality, holography, swampland, black-hole thermodynamics, anomalies, EFT-validity — **plus six measurements**
all agree. These areas are genuinely independent (positivity does not imply causality, the swampland does not imply
BH-entropy positivity, …), so the near-uniqueness is a **consilience**: robustness from the *convergence of
independent lines*, not any single rule. Even the rigorous core alone spans three of the seven areas.

The **dual** of the consilience is the **falsification tracker** (v2.449): for each prediction, the kill-threshold
and current status. Ten predictions, nine near-term falsifiers, **none currently excluded**; the two most decisive
tests are the **birefringence handedness** (β > 0) and the **g_R2 over-determination** (w > −1 *and* r ≈ 0.004 —
a same-operator consistency link, tempered v2.457). The consilience says *many independent principles converge here*; the tracker says *here is exactly how
to prove that wrong*.

**The scale-independent core (v2.451–455).** The program's most scale-clean, directly-testable content: the candidate touches real data through *dimensionless* relations that need neither the string scale nor any O(1)-toy coefficient. The heterotic axion's *universal* anomaly coupling gives cosmic birefringence β = (c_γ α_EM/4π)·Δθ ~ α_EM — scale-independent, matching the measured 0.34° in order of magnitude (v2.451), with the anisotropy suppressed by H_inf/(2π f_a) ~ 10⁻⁶ as an f_a probe (v2.455). The R² plateau gives the inflation consistency line r = 3(1−n_s)² (pinning r ≈ 0.0037, LiteBIRD-testable; n_t, α_s follow — v2.452–453). The dark-energy thawing line wa ≈ −1.5(1+w0) with w ≥ −1 is the *most vulnerable* front: it matches DESI's w0 > −1 sign but predicts milder evolution than DESI's steep central wa / CPL phantom-past hint (a trend-tension, falsifiable by a robust w < −1, v2.454). All are plateau-/class-level (they validate the class, not uniquely this candidate), but they are the program's cleanest falsifiable predictions.

## 8. Honest limitations

- **All magnitudes are O(1)-toy.** The robust content is structure (signs, orderings, which-experiment), not
  coordinates. This does not address the CC magnitude problem or produce dimensionful predictions.
- **Three constructed-point values are assumptions, not predictions**: a=c, g_6=g_8, g_R3_parity=0.
- **A ~3D dark hidden sector** no observable probes.
- **The data-pinning hangs on one ~3.6σ hint** (cosmic birefringence); the CC sector is entirely conjectural
  (swampland conjectures).
- **Blocked frontiers**: the source-backed qNM→R4 ringdown map does not exist in the public literature
  ([v2.209]); real prefactor normalization would require inputs the engine does not have.
