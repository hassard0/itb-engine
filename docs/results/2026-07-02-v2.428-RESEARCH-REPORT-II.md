## Abstract

Intersecting the known consistency conditions on a low-energy effective field theory of gravity + matter —
amplitude positivity, causality, the conformal-collider bootstrap, and the swampland conjectures — over a
space of Wilson coefficients leaves a **single, tiny, connected region**: one candidate low-energy quantum-gravity
EFT, not a landscape. This report presents that candidate, and — the central methodological contribution — a
**first-class rigor classification** separating what the engine establishes with *zero toy input* (source-exact
amplitude/causality bounds) from what rests on conjectural or O(1)-normalized proxies. The result: the candidate's
**entire matter-gravity content is rigorous** (LQG-induced gravity excluded; matter dominance's ceiling and
extremal-black-hole decay forced; the leading curvature coupling forced by the matter × cubic-curvature pair),
with the toy-dependence chased down to a single parity-magnitude coefficient that is itself rigorously bounded and
data-pinned. Rigor determines a *family*; data (cosmic birefringence) selects the *point*. The candidate is a **multi-state (stringy) EFT** whose leading, best-motivated UV completion is a **heterotic string compactification + its model-independent axion** (its one data-selected coupling) — though CDT and asymptotic safety remain competitive, and discriminating them needs UV data. The candidate is
falsifiable on three independent observational fronts, and a newly-added dark-energy sector gives it a coherent —
if conjectural — cosmological-constant story. Every magnitude is O(1)-toy; the robust content throughout is signs,
orderings, structure, and which experiment constrains what.

## 1. Method: swampland-complete carving + rigor tiering

The engine ([`itb.engine.check`](../../src/itb/engine.py)) evaluates a **Theory** (a vector of Wilson
coefficients — matter g_4, g_6, g_8, g_10; curvature g_R2, g_R3, g_R4; parity g_R2_parity, g_R3_parity; Weyl² g_C;
vacuum energy g_Lambda) against a stack of **42 base constraints** (plus opt-in extensions). The constraints are
now **rigor-tagged** (`experiments/stack.py`, v2.411):

- **19 rigorous** — source-exact amplitude positivity / causality / bootstrap bounds (Adams–Nicolis–Rattazzi 2006;
  Caron-Huot et al 2021/2024; Camanho–Edelstein–Maldacena–Zhiboedov 2014; Hofman–Maldacena 2008;
  Arkani-Hamed–Huang–Huang EFThedron). "Rigorous" = the inequality *structure* is the published result; some
  carry a simplified O(1) prefactor, shown not to drive the headlines ([v2.427]).
- **17 sourced_proxy** — real conjectures/theorems (WGC, swampland distance/species, anomaly matching, GSL/QFC,
  complexity) via toy O(1) proxy forms.
- **6 data** — real measurements via O(1) observable maps.

The **method itself is the original claim**: the falsifiable output is the *geometry of the feasible region* — a
family carved by the rigorous rules, collapsed to a point by data.

## 2. The candidate

Deepest-interior (Chebyshev-center) point of the feasible region: `g_4≈0.53, g_6≈g_8≈0.40, g_R2≈0.19, g_R3≈0.09,
g_R2_parity≈0.06`. In one sentence: a **matter-dominant, near-Planckian, ghost-safe, string-like, globally-unique**
consistent EFT (live-verified profile → [v2.402]). Its structural facts:

- **Matter–gravity locking.** Matter forces the leading curvature coupling and caps gravity at ≤40% of its own
  strength; g_4 and g_R2 are the two keystones driving ~⅔ of all constraints.
- **Ghost-safe & near-Planckian.** The Weyl² Ostrogradsky ghost sits above a Dvali species-scale cutoff ~0.72 M_Pl.
- **String-like.** Matter and curvature couplings form infinite log-convex moment towers (extended one rigorous
  rung in [v2.426]: g_8² ≤ g_6·g_10 forces g_10 ≥ 0.4).
- **Extremal black holes decay** (WGC automatic); **parity is a chirality structure** (order-2 L/R graviton
  asymmetry locked to the CMB handedness).
- **Uniqueness.** A multi-start search funnels to a single global island; the swampland-complete carving has
  converged (~10⁻⁵ of the a-priori box).

## 3. What is rigorous (the de-toying arc, v2.411–427)

The central result of this report. Running the analysis on the **rigorous core alone** (zero toy input):

- **LQG-induced gravity is excluded** — its couplings violate graviton forward positivity, the cross-sector
  EFThedron, and the CFT flat-space bootstrap ([v2.411]). This exclusion is **prefactor-independent** ([v2.427]).
- **Matter dominance's ceiling** (g_R2 ≤ √g_4, the WGC) and **extremal-BH decay** (ΔS_ext ≥ 0) are *implied by*
  the rigorous core over the feasible region ([v2.412]).
- **The leading curvature coupling** is forced positive by the **matter × cubic-curvature** pair (matter alone
  doesn't; g_R3 alone is infeasible; the full feasibility lattice, [v2.417], corrects the earlier "matter sources
  gravity" attribution [v2.416]).
- **Rigor determines a family, data the point.** The rigorous-core feasible set is ~5.6× looser than the
  data-informed island and permits g_R2→0 and parity→0; the cosmic-birefringence datum collapses it to the
  candidate ([v2.419]).

The verified per-claim rigor ledger is [v2.415]. The **single residual toy** is the parity-magnitude coefficient
— itself rigorously capped (parity-decomposed positivity) and data-pinned (birefringence), with the toy playing
only a minor upper-edge role ([v2.418]).

## 4. The cosmological-constant / dark-energy sector (v2.422–425, opt-in, conjectural)

A new opt-in sector adds a vacuum-energy coupling g_Lambda and swampland constraints (all `sourced_proxy`):
- **admits** a positive cosmological constant, capped at g_Lambda ≤ g_R2 (refined dS conjecture — the keystone
  curvature coupling also bounds the dark energy);
- **selects de Sitter / Minkowski over anti-de Sitter** (the bounded tower forbids a small-|Λ| AdS vacuum via the
  AdS distance conjecture);
- **predicts w ≳ −1** (flat Starobinsky plateau → true-CC-to-mild-quintessence; DESI/Euclid-testable).

An **inflation-to-dark-energy story on the single keystone scalaron g_R2**. This addresses the *structure* of the
vacuum energy, **not** the CC magnitude problem (g_Lambda is dimensionless; the engine has no absolute scale).

## 5. The UV completion (v2.433–439): a leading candidate, an honest degeneracy, and two layers

The deepest question — what UV theory does the candidate embed in? — has a concrete, honestly-bounded answer.

- **The candidate is a small parity deformation of string tree-level EFT.** The engine's string tree-EFT point
  is feasible in the rigorous cage, is the parity-conserving rival (rejected only by cosmic birefringence), and
  sits within 0.067 (parity-even) of the candidate — differing *only* in the parity coupling (v2.433).
- **Specifically heterotic, not type II** (v2.434), on two independent arguments: (1) given the candidate's
  cubic-curvature and parity couplings, a nonzero R² (Gauss–Bonnet) term is *rigorously required* (setting
  g_R2=0 violates graviton forward positivity + CEMZ causality + cross-sector EFThedron) — which tree-level type
  II (R⁴-only) lacks but heterotic has at α′; (2) the candidate's one data-selected coupling, the parity, is a
  gravitational-Chern–Simons `R∧R̃` term — exactly the heterotic **model-independent axion**'s Green–Schwarz
  coupling.
- **The parity may be *determined*, not free** (v2.435): the model-independent axion's coupling is *universal*
  (fixed by the gravitational anomaly, the same in every heterotic compactification). So — contingent on that
  identification — the candidate's last free coupling is fixed, heterotic *predicts* cosmic birefringence rather
  than accommodating it, and the candidate approaches a (near-)parameter-free heterotic prediction.

**Everything converges on one object — the parity coupling:** the thing the rigorous cage cannot force (§3), the
empirical verdict tests (§6), and the UV embedding sources — and, under heterotic universality, the thing that
becomes fixed. *This is a structural identification from textbook string facts + the engine, not a
compactification computation; the concrete remaining work is a heterotic vacuum whose R²:R³:R⁴ and axion coupling
land in the cage. Type II is disfavored, not excluded (the clean statement is tree-level); the β magnitude needs
the string scale.*

**But heterotic is the *leading* completion, not the unique one (v2.436–438).** A full UV tournament finds
**CDT (a near-tie) and asymptotic safety (competitive)** also sit in the rigorous cage and are reachable by the
same parity deformation — only LQG is excluded (v2.436). The low-energy EFT *cannot* discriminate string vs CDT vs
asymptotic safety: even the species scale fails (all give N≈1.5–1.7 towers, v2.437), because these are low-energy
quantities the completions share. What the low-energy amplitude *can* say is that the UV is a **multi-state tower**
(the candidate is interior to the positivity bound, not saturating — v2.438), ruling out a single-resonance
completion; but the tower's **type** (Regge / Kaluza–Klein / fixed-point) — the actual discriminator — is a
**UV/high-energy observable** (the cutoff-scale spectrum), outside the low-energy program. So: heterotic/string is
the best-motivated leading completion, but the honest statement is *"a multi-state (stringy) EFT whose specific UV
completion the low-energy data cannot yet resolve."*

**Two layers of the solve (v2.439).** Underneath all of the above is a premise the engine does not carve: every
coupling it uses is *higher-derivative* (R² and up); the Einstein-Hilbert term — the graviton itself — is
presupposed. So "solving QG" splits into **Layer 1** (*is* gravity quantum — an empirical, table-top question:
classical collapse is excluded ~14 orders by Diósi–Penrose, and BMV gravitationally-induced entanglement is the
decisive ~2030s test) and **Layer 2** (*which* higher-derivative EFT — the entire program). The two are
observationally separate — BMV probes the leading Newtonian sector, the candidate's correlated signature probes
the higher-derivative couplings — so the complete solve needs **both** experiments: BMV (is it quantum) and the
correlated cosmological signature (which EFT).

## 6. Falsifiability — three independent fronts

Atop the rigorous backbone (which already excludes LQG and the extreme archetypes), the candidate is falsifiable
on three near-term observational fronts ([v2.421] portfolio):
1. **Parity** — CMB cosmic birefringence (LiteBIRD/CMB-S4) now, GW birefringence (LISA/ET) later. The
   candidate's single live rival is a parity-conserving EFT, separated by *exactly* the birefringence datum
   ([v2.420]).
2. **Matter** — CMB-S4 inflation: matter dominance forces a large g_4, in >10σ tension with single-field
   slow-roll.
3. **Dark energy** — DESI/Euclid w(z).

So the candidate is not hostage to a single measurement; the parity question has redundant probes and the matter
and dark-energy fronts are orthogonal.

## 7. Honest limitations

- **All magnitudes are O(1)-toy.** The robust content is structure (signs, orderings, which-experiment), not
  coordinates. This does not address the CC magnitude problem or produce dimensionful predictions.
- **Three constructed-point values are assumptions, not predictions**: a=c, g_6=g_8, g_R3_parity=0.
- **A ~3D dark hidden sector** no observable probes.
- **The data-pinning hangs on one ~3.6σ hint** (cosmic birefringence); the CC sector is entirely conjectural
  (swampland conjectures).
- **Blocked frontiers**: the source-backed qNM→R4 ringdown map does not exist in the public literature
  ([v2.209]); real prefactor normalization would require inputs the engine does not have.
