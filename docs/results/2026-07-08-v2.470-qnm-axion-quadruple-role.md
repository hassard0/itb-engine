## Honest scope

A **structural unification** (one field carries four roles), *not* four solved problems. Each role keeps its own
caveats: dark energy doesn't solve the CC magnitude problem and is only neutral-to-mildly-helpful on the tensions
(v2.467/v2.469); birefringence is β ~ α_EM at *order of magnitude* with a model-dependent c_γ (v2.468); the
**baryon-asymmetry magnitude η_B is not computed** — gravitational leptogenesis is a real mechanism but its η_B is
scale-dependent, so v2.324 claims only existence/discriminator/sign/unification, not the observed ~6×10⁻¹⁰; the
chiral GW is a future prediction. So this adds *no new physics* beyond v2.324 + v2.458 — it's the synthesis into the
single-field-quadruple-role headline plus the doc fix. It also doesn't uniquely pick *this* candidate (any parity
axion with these couplings shares it), consistent with the class-level status of the cosmological predictions.
