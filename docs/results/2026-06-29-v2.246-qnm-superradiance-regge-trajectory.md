# v2.246 - The superradiance spin-down endpoint: the Regge trajectory observed spins test

## Question

v2.243-v2.245 gave the superradiance condition, the growth-time exclusion, and the cloud's direct GW
signal. What is the SPIN-DOWN ENDPOINT -- the physical mechanism behind the bound, and the line that
observed black-hole spins are actually compared against?

## Result

Superradiance transfers the hole's angular momentum to the boson cloud until the instability
saturates, i.e. until the horizon angular velocity drops to the boson frequency:
`Omega_H(a*_f) = mu/m`. For coupling `alpha = M mu` and the dominant `m=1` level, `Omega_H(a*_f) =
alpha`, so `a*_f(alpha)` is the maximum spin a hole can retain:

```text
  alpha    a*_final (max retained spin)
  0.05     0.198
  0.10     0.385
  0.15     0.551
  0.20     0.690
  0.30     0.882
  0.40     0.976
```

(Consistency: `Omega_H(a*_f(0.1)) = 0.1`, exact.) A boson `alpha=0.1` spins a near-extremal
(`a*=0.99`) hole down to `a*=0.385` (extracting `Delta a* = 0.61`); `alpha=0.3` only to `a*=0.882`. A
measured high spin therefore EXCLUDES every boson with `alpha < Omega_H(a*_obs)`:

```text
  observed system               a*       excludes bosons with alpha <
  Cygnus X-1                     0.95     0.362
  GRS 1915+105                   0.98     0.409
  M87* (EHT-favoured)            0.90     0.313
```

**The Regge trajectory `a*_f(alpha)` is the line observed spins test.** Black holes measured ABOVE
it could not have kept their spin in the presence of the corresponding boson, so the well-measured
high spins of Cygnus X-1 and GRS 1915+105 (and the EHT-favoured spin of M87*) carve gaps in
ultralight-boson parameter space. This is the spin-extraction endpoint that completes the
v2.243-v2.245 thread: condition -> exclusion -> GW signal -> spin gaps.

## What this establishes

The physical mechanism (adiabatic spin-down to the saturation spin) behind the superradiance bound,
and the observable (the Regge trajectory / spin gaps) that real spin measurements are compared
against -- closing the superradiance probe with its data-comparison side, fully verifiable (the
saturation condition is checked exactly).

## Honest scope

Exact-Kerr saturation condition `Omega_H(a*_f) = alpha` (verified), the EXACT endpoint of an
idealized adiabatic spin-down. The real Regge boundary also requires the growth to complete within
the BH age (the v2.244 timescale) and folds in the measured spin POSTERIORS and their systematics
(X-ray continuum/iron-line vs EHT methods differ); the cloud also re-deposits some angular momentum

reconstruction of a real BSM probe, not a published exclusion. Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark
(v2.209).
