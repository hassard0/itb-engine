## Source

Primary source:

- Anthony Ashmore and Fabian Ruehle, "Moduli-dependent KK towers and the swampland
  distance conjecture on the quintic Calabi-Yau manifold", `arXiv:2103.07472`

The paper computes moduli-dependent Calabi-Yau metrics, geodesic distances, and a
scalar Laplacian KK subtower for the one-parameter quintic. It reports a fit for a
Laplacian eigenvalue:

```text
lambda_3 ~= 56.4 * exp(-(0.906 +/- 0.034) d(2,rho))
```

The paper states that the quoted error is a 95% confidence interval. Since
`m_KK ~ sqrt(lambda)`, this audit maps the fit to:

```json

  "phi_tower_mean_for_Delta_1": 0.453,
  "phi_tower_sigma_for_Delta_1": 0.008673469387755101
}
```
