# v2.251 - Lorentz-violation / modified dispersion: testing Planck-scale spacetime structure

## Question

A fresh thread, pivoting off black holes to the flagship quantum-gravity-PHENOMENOLOGY probe: is
spacetime smooth at the Planck length, or does a discrete/quantum structure reveal itself as an
energy-dependent propagation speed? And how do photon and gravitational-wave messengers compare?

## Result

A generic Lorentz-violating dispersion `E^2 = p^2 c^2 [1 - s (E/E_QG)^n]` gives
`v(E)/c ~ 1 - s(n+1)/2 (E/E_QG)^n`, so two messengers emitted together from light-travel distance
`D` arrive separated by `Delta t ~ (D/c)[(E_hi/E_QG)^n - (E_lo/E_QG)^n]`, bounding (linear `n=1`)
`E_QG > (D/c) Delta E / Delta t_obs`:

```text
  messenger                   E_QG lower bound (eV)    / E_Planck
  Fermi GRB 090510 (30 GeV)   6.6e27                   0.54
  LIGO/Virgo GW dispersion    1.6e6                    1.4e-22
```

plus the multi-messenger **GW170817 GW-vs-photon speed bound** `|Delta v/c| < 1e-15` (the strongest
on the `n=0` constant-offset / graviton-mass sector).

**A single 30 GeV GRB photon reaches `E_QG ~ 0.5 E_Planck`** for linear LIV -- the headline
quantum-gravity-phenomenology result, right at the Planck scale. The same analysis on LIGO gravitons
gives a bound **22 orders of magnitude weaker**, because the bound scales with the MESSENGER ENERGY
(verified: the bound ratio equals the energy ratio exactly at fixed `D, Delta t`) and gravitons
(`~1e-12 eV`) are `~1e40` times lower-energy than GeV gamma-rays. So gravitational waves are a POOR
probe of energy-dependent (`n>=1`) LIV dispersion -- but GW170817's GW-vs-photon speed comparison is
the STRONGEST bound on the constant-offset sector. **The channels are complementary:** high-energy
photons probe Planck-suppressed dispersion, GW170817 probes the constant speed offset -- together
testing whether spacetime is smooth at the Planck length.
