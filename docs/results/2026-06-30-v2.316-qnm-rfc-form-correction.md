# v2.316 - Correction: the "all frameworks infeasible" verdict was a deprecated-RFC-form artifact

## Question

v2.315 found every community framework infeasible, all bound by `repulsive_force_conjecture`, and built a
picture on it ("only pure GR feasible", "universality decisive", "the engine prefers a unique constructed
framework over all community ones", v2.312-v2.315). On scrutiny -- is that verdict robust to the encoding
of its decisive constraint?

## Result (it was an artifact; the engine documents it)

No. `build_stack()` defaults the repulsive-force conjecture to its `matter_product` form
(`g_4*g_6 - g_R2 - gamma*g_R2^2 >= 0`). The engine's own `RepulsiveForceConjecture` docstring states this
form "excludes EVERY candidate framework for any gamma>0 -- a 100% universal exclusion that is an artifact
of the spurious product (the encoded frameworks all have g_4*g_6 ~ g_R2 by construction)", and recommends
the physically-corrected `convex_hull` form (`g_4 - g_R2 - gamma*g_R2^2 >= 0`) for new analyses. Re-scoring
under the recommended form changes the picture decisively:

```text
  framework           matter_product (default)   convex_hull (recommended)
  string_tree_eft     -0.0400  infeasible         +0.0060  FEASIBLE
  asymptotic_safety   -0.0525  infeasible         +0.0036  FEASIBLE
  cdt                 -0.0484  infeasible         +0.0073  FEASIBLE
  lqg_induced         -0.1200  infeasible         -0.0750  infeasible (binding: cft_flat_space_bound)
```

Three of the four community frameworks become feasible; only lqg remains infeasible, now bound by a
cubic-curvature / CFT condition -- exactly the "real discriminating physics" the docstring points to, and
the outlier-`g_R3` mechanism v2.311 already identified. All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this corrects, and what survives

**Corrected (artifact of the deprecated default form):** the "universal exclusion" / "only pure GR
feasible" framing, and the v2.312/v2.315 headlines that the engine prefers a unique constructed framework
*more robust than every community one* and that "universality is decisive". Under the engine's recommended
form, the community higher-derivative frameworks (except lqg) ARE in the consistent region, so they are not
all ruled out and the constructed framework is not uniquely feasible.

**Robust (unaffected or independently confirmed):**
- lqg is infeasible under BOTH forms -- the genuine boundary framework -- and its infeasibility traces to
  its outlier cubic curvature `g_R3` (v2.311's PRIMARY finding), not the repulsive-force box (v2.311's
  secondary, matter_product-specific finding).
- The carving / convexity / moment-tower / finite-cutoff / cosmology results (v2.292, v2.302-v2.309
  structural, v2.304-v2.306, v2.307-v2.308) do not invoke the repulsive-force conjecture and are
  unaffected.

Future cycles should pass `form="convex_hull"`.

## Honest scope

This is a self-correction, and the correction itself is the engine's literal verdict: the form-dependence
is direct `check()` output, and the artifact is documented in the engine's own `RepulsiveForceConjecture`
docstring (citing a prior 2026-06-08 realism study). Every recent cycle that called `build_stack()` with no
`rfc_form` argument inherited the deprecated form, so the affected results are the FEASIBILITY verdicts that
depended on the repulsive-force constraint. Results not involving it are unaffected. The exact convex_hull
margins still carry the O(1) prefactor caveat. Toy basis, O(1) prefactors.
