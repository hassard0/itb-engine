## From "minimum set" to a timeline

v1.88 found that **6 measurements** pin all 8 Wilson coefficients. v1.92 turns that
static answer into a **timeline**: each measurement gets its realistic future
milestone year and projected precision, and we accumulate the Fisher information
year by year (2025–2042) to forecast *when* the consistent EFT is pinned to
uniqueness.

## Result — the timeline stalls at rank 6/8

| year | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2030 | 2032 … 2042 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fisher rank | 1 | 2 | 4 | 4 | **6** | **6 (flat)** |

The Fisher rank climbs to **6/8 by ~2030 and then plateaus forever.** The consistent
QG EFT is **never fully pinned on the funded roadmap** — two coefficient directions,
**g_8 (the s⁴ matter moment) and g_R3 (cubic curvature), have no funded experiment.**
The constrained 6-dimensional subspace tightens nicely; the other two stay unbounded.

**If** the two blind-spot probes were built (a high-scattering-moment / higher-partial-
wave measurement and a cubic-graviton / GW-nonlinearity measurement), the rank reaches
8 — the EFT *would* be pinned. They are the missing legs of the program.

## The honest headline

> **The timeline to solving quantum gravity has a gap.** Every funded experiment on
> the horizon (sub-mm 2028, LiteBIRD 2030, SKA 2032, ET 2035) constrains the matter
> low-moments, g_R2, and the parity sector — pinning 6 of 8 directions by ~2030. But
> **g_8 and g_R3 have no funded probe**, so the program stalls at rank 6/8. Closing it
> requires *inventing two experiments*: a high-energy/high-partial-wave scattering
> measurement (g_8) and a cubic-curvature / graviton-nonlinearity measurement (g_R3).

This is the actionable output of the whole experimental-design line (v1.88 → v1.92):
not just *which* measurements, but *when* — and the concrete, fundable gap that
otherwise blocks a unique answer.

## Honest scope

Projected σ's and years are approximate roadmap estimates (LiteBIRD ~2032 launch,
CMB-S4 / Simons ~2030s, ET/CE ~2035, SKA ~2030s); BH-entropy and η/s are
theoretical/indirect inputs counted as "available." The robust content is the **gap**
— 2 of 8 directions have no funded probe — not the exact stall year.
