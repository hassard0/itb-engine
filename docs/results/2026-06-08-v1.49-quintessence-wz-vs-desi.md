## What this does

v1.47 used the schematic w₀ = −1 + c²/3. Here we solve the **actual** quintessence
axion equation of motion in an expanding universe,

    φ̈ + 3H φ̇ + V′(φ) = 0,   V = m²f²[1 − cos(φ/f)],
    H² = (1/3)(ρ_m0/a³ + ½φ̇² + V)   (units 8πG = 1, H₀ = 1),

with the axion **fixed by the birefringence picture** (f = 1.58 M_Pl, v1.47),
shooting the initial misalignment so Ω_φ(a=1) = 0.69 (flat, Ω_m = 0.31), and
reading off w₀ = w_φ(today) and the CPL slope wₐ. Then we confront DESI 2024
(w₀ = −0.83 ± 0.06, wₐ = −0.7 ± 0.3).

## Two honest tensions (this is the point of a real test)

1. **wₐ is the right sign but too shallow.** The solved wₐ ≈ −0.28 (at the best w₀)
   follows the **thawing track** wₐ ≈ −1.5(1+w₀) = −0.27 — exactly as expected for
   a minimal axion. DESI's central wₐ = −0.7 is *steeper*; the axion is within
   ~1.4σ but does not reach DESI's preferred steepness. This is the known,
   active tension between **minimal thawing quintessence and DESI's w₀wₐ contour**
   — DESI's steep wₐ is hard for any minimal single-field model, and is itself
   still under scrutiny (data systematics / model space). Our calculation
   reproduces that tension honestly rather than papering over it.

2. **The field excursion is small → the birefringence needs a largish coupling.**
   The cosmology wants Δφ/f ≈ 0.2 (the field is mostly frozen, thawing only
   recently), *not* the O(1) roll v1.46 loosely assumed. Since β ∝ c_γ·(Δφ/f),
   reproducing β = 0.34° then needs **c_γ ≈ 25** (not ≈ 5) — a larger
   electromagnetic anomaly coefficient. Still possible (O(10) anomaly
   coefficients occur), but it is a genuine quantitative cost, now visible only
   because we solved the dynamics instead of estimating.

## Verdict

The unified dark-energy-axion picture **survives the quantitative test on its
strongest leg (w₀ = −0.82 vs DESI −0.83) and on the qualitative shape (thawing,
wₐ < 0)**, while exposing two real tensions: (i) it sits on the thawing track,
shallower than DESI's central wₐ (a tension shared by *all* minimal quintessence,
not specific to this model); (ii) the small dynamical field excursion pushes the
birefringence anomaly coefficient to ~25. Neither kills the picture; both are
sharp, falsifiable handles:

- a future w(z) measurement that **confirms DESI's steep wₐ** would disfavour
  minimal thawing axions (this one included) and point to non-minimal dynamics;
- one that **relaxes toward the thawing track** (|wₐ| ≲ 0.3) would fit this axion
  cleanly.

Either way the engine's unique prediction — the correlated **GW birefringence at
|g_R2_parity| ~ 0.09** (v1.46, LIGO O5) — is untouched by these cosmological
tensions and remains the cleanest discriminator.

## Honest limitations

Minimal cosine potential; CPL fit of wₐ over a ∈ [0.5,1]; radiation neglected (late
times); DESI numbers are the DR1 BAO+CMB+SNe combination (DR2 and alternative
combinations shift the contour). The robust content: w₀ matches at m ~ H₀,
f ~ M_Pl; the model is on the thawing track; and the dynamics make the
birefringence coupling requirement explicit.
