# v2.245 - The boson cloud as a continuous-GW source: the direct-detection side of superradiance

## Question

v2.243/v2.244 used superradiance as an INDIRECT probe (a spun-down hole excludes a boson). But the
superradiant cloud is also a DIRECT gravitational-wave source: the bosons annihilate in pairs to
gravitons, radiating a nearly monochromatic, long-lived ("continuous-wave") signal. At what
frequencies, and in which detector bands, do the superradiance windows show up directly?

## Result

The boson-annihilation GW frequency (two bosons of rest energy `~mu` -> one graviton of energy
`~2 mu`) is `f_GW = mu c^2 / (pi hbar)`, with only a tiny secular drift as the cloud depletes.
Mapping the v2.244 Regge-excluded boson-mass windows:

```text
  system                                  f_GW (Hz)            band
  stellar BH (10 Msun, a*=0.9)            52 - 2020            LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA (10-2000 Hz)
  GW150914-like remnant (62 Msun)         8 - 212              LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA
  SMBH (1e6 Msun)                         1.6e-3 - 2.0e-2      LISA (0.1-100 mHz)
  M87* (6.5e9 Msun)                       7.7e-7 - 3.1e-6      PTA / nHz
```

**The superradiance boson-mass windows map directly onto the gravitational-wave detector bands.** A
measured high black-hole spin says "a boson cloud should be there" and predicts a nearly-monochromatic
GW line at a definite frequency that a continuous-wave search can directly target. So superradiance is
a TWO-SIDED probe: the indirect spin-exclusion (v2.243/v2.244) plus the direct continuous-wave signal,
with the detector band set simply by the black-hole mass -- stellar holes -> LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA,
supermassive -> LISA, the largest -> PTA. The same physics that makes black-hole spin an
ultralight-boson detector also makes the boson cloud a gravitational-wave beacon.

## What this establishes

Completes the superradiance thread (v2.243-v2.245): the condition, the actual Regge exclusion, and now
the direct continuous-wave signature, with the boson-mass windows mapped onto LIGO/LISA/PTA bands. A
self-contained, verifiable reconstruction of both sides of one of the leading ultralight-boson search
strategies -- the repo's strong-field machinery delivering a complete BSM probe.

## Honest scope

The annihilation FREQUENCY `f = mu c^2/(pi hbar)` is exact at leading order (small `alpha^2` binding
corrections and the tiny secular spin-up as the cloud depletes are neglected -- the signal is
monochromatic to high precision, the basis of continuous-wave searches). The detector-band mapping is
robust; the STRAIN amplitude / detectability (which sets whether each source is actually seen) depends
on the cloud mass (~a few % of `M`), distance, and search coherence -- not computed here. The
boson-mass windows are the v2.244 representative ones (their caveats carry). This is the
frequency/band reconstruction of the direct-detection channel, not a detectability forecast.
Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
