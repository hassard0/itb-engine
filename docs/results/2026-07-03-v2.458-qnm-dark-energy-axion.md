# v2.458 - the dark-energy field *is* the parity axion: unifying dark energy and birefringence into one rolling field

v2.457 removed the heavy R² scalaron from the dark-energy role and left the question: what *is* the candidate's
dark energy? The clean answer — which also sharpens the picture — is the **parity axion itself**. The
model-independent axion is nearly massless (f_a ~ M_Pl → a natural quintessence field) and is already the
birefringence source (v2.434), so **one rolling axion does both**:

- **Dark energy**: its potential energy is ρ_DE; as it thaws and rolls by Δθ ~ O(1) (in units of f_a) it gives
  w > −1 on the thawing line wa ≈ −1.5(1+w0), w ≥ −1 (canonical).
- **Birefringence**: the *same* roll Δθ rotates the CMB polarization, β = (c_γ α_EM/4π)·Δθ.

## The consistency + the genuine correlation

The measured β = 0.34° needs c_γ·Δθ ~ 10 — an **O(1) roll × an O(1–10) anomaly coefficient** — and an O(1) roll
*is* the thawing-quintessence regime (the field rolls ~ f_a). So the same roll that makes the axion a viable
thawing dark energy produces the observed birefringence. Because **both β and (1+w0) increase with Δθ, they are
positively correlated** — a larger cosmic birefringence comes with a dark energy further from w = −1. Measuring β
and w0 (DESI) *together* over-tests the single-axion picture. 5/5 checks pass.

This is a **genuine same-field over-determination** — unlike the g_R2 one that v2.457 tempered to a mere
same-*operator* link. It yields the clean late-time two-field picture:

```text
  scalaron phi (parity-even, R^2):  INFLATION early (M ~ 3e13 GeV), then decays -> negligible late
  axion theta (parity-odd):         DARK ENERGY (quintessence) + BIREFRINGENCE late -- one rolling field, beta<->w correlated
```

So the correction chain v2.457 → v2.458 turns a *spurious operator-level* unification into a *genuine field-level*
one. (This revises v2.448, where the axion was taken as a *subdominant* late field under the now-corrected
assumption that the scalaron was the dark energy; post-v2.457 the axion is the **dominant** late-time field — the
dark energy itself. The two-fields-of-opposite-parity structure of v2.448 stands; only the DE role moves from
scalaron to axion.)

## Honest scope

Well-motivated (the model-independent axion is naturally ultralight, f_a ~ M_Pl — the v1.46–47 dark-energy-axion
idea tied to the candidate's parity), **but** it requires the axion potential to sit at the dark-energy scale
V ~ (meV)⁴ and mass m ~ H₀ — *not* explained (the coincidence / CC-magnitude problem remains; this identifies the
*field*, it doesn't solve the DE magnitude). The β↔w correlation *direction* (both grow with Δθ) is robust, but
the exact quantitative β–w relation is potential-shape-dependent and not computed here — so the prediction is
"larger β correlates with larger (1+w0)", a directional over-determination, not a computed curve. β carries the
v2.451 caveats (O(1–10) inputs; β a ~3.6σ hint).
