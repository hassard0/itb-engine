## Interpretation

Current string/swampland sources provide valuable tower candidates, positive
controls, and framework-adjacent theory context. They still do not satisfy the
native pass condition:

- source-owned `TowerSpectrum`
- source-owned `TowerEvidence`
- asymptotic range
- registered framework endpoint ownership
- registered framework displacement ownership
- two-sigma lower tower exponent exceeding the threshold

The common blocker is ownership, not lack of interesting tower physics. A future
claim needs a registered framework adapter whose source owns the endpoint and
displacement being tested.
