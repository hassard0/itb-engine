# v2.300 - It from qubit: entanglement monogamy and positivity are complementary curvature bounds

## Question

A fresh swing (milestone v2.300) in a sector the new-theory arc had not engaged -- the
information-theoretic / entanglement constraints. The engine encodes an entanglement-monogamy bound that
constrains the gravitational curvature coupling. Is that "it from qubit" structure redundant with
amplitude positivity, or does entanglement give genuinely new information about the geometry?

## Result (independent, complementary bounds with a derivable crossover)

The engine's BNOSSW 4-region monogamy bound is `g_4 g_6 >= (1/3) g_R2 (g_4 + g_6)`, i.e.

```text
  entanglement:  g_R2 <= 3 g_4 g_6 / (g_4 + g_6)   = (3/2) x HARMONIC-mean(g_4, g_6)
  positivity:    g_R2 <= sqrt(g_4 g_6)             =        GEOMETRIC-mean(g_4, g_6)
```

Harmonic and geometric means cross, so neither bound implies the other. For nearly-equal matter
couplings positivity is tighter; past a derivable asymmetry `g_4/g_6 ~ 6.85` (the larger root of
`9 r = (r+1)^2`) the entanglement bound wins:

```text
  framework          g_4/g_6   monogamy   positivity   binding
  string_tree_eft    1.25      0.667      0.447        positivity
  asymptotic_safety  1.33      0.514      0.346        positivity
  cdt                1.38      0.695      0.469        positivity
  lqg_induced        1.33      0.771      0.520        positivity

  asymmetric UV point (g_4 = 5, g_6 = 0.5, asymmetry 10):
     monogamy 1.36  <  positivity 1.58   ->  ENTANGLEMENT bites (gap 0.22)
```

The engine's frameworks all sit in the mild-asymmetry, positivity-dominated regime, so for them
entanglement monogamy is currently slack -- but it is NOT redundant: an asymmetric UV completion has its
curvature coupling bounded by entanglement far below the positivity bound. The entanglement constraint
carries information about the geometry that no amplitude positivity bound does, in exactly the asymmetric
regime the "it from qubit" program predicts matters most. All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

The engine's information-theoretic and amplitude-bootstrap sectors are genuinely complementary, not
redundant: entanglement monogamy and positivity bound the same curvature coupling `g_R2` by the harmonic
and geometric means of the matter couplings respectively, meeting at a sharp, derivable crossover.
Entanglement structure literally constrains the gravitational sector beyond what unitarity and causality
(positivity) alone require. This is a concrete, quantitative "it from qubit" statement -- a fresh
new-theory result outside the curvature-moment vein the rest of the arc mined.

## Honest scope

The monogamy bound (prefactor `1/3`) and the positivity bound (`kappa = 1`) are the engine's
representative O(1) encodings, so the crossover asymmetry `~6.85` shifts with the prefactors. But the
QUALITATIVE result is prefactor-ROBUST: the harmonic mean is always `<=` the geometric mean (equality
only at `g_4 = g_6`), so entanglement is ALWAYS the tighter bound for sufficiently asymmetric couplings
regardless of the O(1) constants -- only the crossover location is prefactor-dependent. That the engine's
frameworks are positivity-bound reflects their specific (mildly asymmetric) encodings. The "it from
qubit" reading is the standard interpretation of monogamy-as-geometric-constraint, here made quantitative
against positivity. Toy basis, O(1) prefactors. A fresh-sector new-theory result.
