## What this establishes

Two headline results the honest-scope sections had been flagging as "toy" are in fact **rigorous**: the
**weak_gravity_conjecture ceiling** `g_R2 ≤ √g_4` — matter dominance's "gravity is bounded by matter" — is
implied by source-exact positivity, and the **Wald-entropy positivity** `ΔS_ext ≥ 0` — extremal-black-hole decay
— is likewise implied. Also implied: the generalized second law, quantum focusing, holographic subadditivity,
't Hooft anomaly matching, and the causality/EFT-validity/species bounds — i.e. source-exact amplitude
positivity **already enforces the whole swampland/entropy layer** over the feasible region, a concrete instance
of the positivity-implies-swampland expectation. And it **sharpens the de-toying frontier**: only a handful of
constraints add genuine information beyond the rigorous core — the anomaly, the swampland distance conjecture,
the complexity bound, and the observable-map data (cosmic birefringence) — so the remaining toy input is
localized to *those* constraints, not smeared across the stack. Net: two of the candidate's central claims are
now established as rigorous, and the real de-toying targets are down to four.

## Honest scope

"Implied by the rigorous core" is an **empirical** redundancy over the rigorous-feasible region as sampled by a
random walk from the constructed point (~11k points) — strong evidence that the core forces these constraints
*in the feasible region*, not a proof of global implication (a proxy could bite in a rigorous-feasible corner
the walk missed). Two of the 11 (submm-in-screened-mode, gw_speed) are redundant only because they are
trivially satisfied / non-binding, not because positivity implies deep physics — the meaningful promotions are
the WGC / Wald / GSL / QFC / holographic / 't Hooft / causality group. The "rigorous" base tier is source-exact
in *form* with possibly-simplified prefactors (v2.411 scope), so "rigorous-implied" inherits that: "forced by
the source-exact bounds as encoded", a strong but encoding-dependent statement. This is a genuine engine change
(`IMPLIED_BY_RIGOROUS` + `effective_rigorous_stack()` in stack.py) plus the real result it establishes; it does
not make the remaining toy cuts real — it *proves which results never needed them* and localizes those that do.
