## Method

The harness loads the v2.119 packet and the v2.113 H1/L1 complex LALSuite
IMR alpha projections. It replaces the detector-calibration proxy with a
conservative corner propagation of the published GW170608 strain calibration
envelope.

Source:
`https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05578`

The GW170608 event paper reports maximum 1-sigma calibration uncertainties of
5% in strain amplitude and 3 degrees in phase over the analysis band. v2.120
propagates a 2-sigma conservative envelope:

```text
amplitude_fraction = 0.10
phase_degrees = 6.0
detectors = H1, L1
corner_count = 16
```
