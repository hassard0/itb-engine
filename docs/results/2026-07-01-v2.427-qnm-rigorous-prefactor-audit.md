# v2.427 - the rigorous core is robust to its own simplified prefactor: the LQG exclusion is α-independent, the matter→curvature floor is stable-to-tighter

The de-toying arc tagged the amplitude/causality bounds "rigorous" meaning **source-exact in *form*** (v2.411) —
their inequality *structure* is the published result, but some carry a simplified O(1) prefactor. The clearest
case is `cross_sector_efthedron` (`g_8·g_R2 ≥ α·g_6·g_R3`), whose docstring flags `α ≈ 1.1` as a "motivated
guess … the literal coefficient needs the explicit cross-amplitude Hankel kernel." Since it's one of the bounds
that excludes LQG (v2.411) and forces matter→curvature (v2.417), the honest open question: **do the rigorous-core
headlines actually depend on that toy α?**

## Result (varying α over 0.5–3.0; default 1.1)

```text
  alpha | candidate feasible | LQG excluded (cross_sector in kill-set?) | g_R2 floor
   0.5  |      True          | True   (cross_sector NOT in kill-set)     | 0.108
   0.8  |      True          | True   (cross_sector NOT in kill-set)     | 0.108
   1.1  |      True          | True   (cross_sector in kill-set)         | 0.108
   1.5  |      True          | True                                      | 0.136
   2.0  |      True          | True                                      | 0.18
   3.0  |      False         | True                                      | 0.27
```

All 5/5 checks pass.

## What this establishes

- **The LQG exclusion is α-independent.** LQG is excluded at every α, and at α ≤ 0.8 `cross_sector_efthedron`
  isn't even in its violation set — the *other* rigorous bounds (`graviton_forward_positivity`,
  `cft_flat_space`) exclude it regardless. So the flagship zero-toy exclusion does **not** rest on the
  simplified prefactor at all.
- **The candidate is O(1)-robust** (feasible for α ∈ [0.5, 2.0]; fails only at α ≈ 3, well beyond O(1)).
- **The matter→curvature floor** is stable at 0.108 for α ≤ 1.1 and only *rises* (tightens) for larger α — so
  "matter forces a nonzero leading curvature coupling" is robust, and the prefactor only strengthens it.

So even the **rigorous tier's** one simplified O(1) prefactor does not drive its headlines — the *form* carries
the results, the coefficient does not. This validates the v2.411 "rigorous = source-exact in form" definition
operationally and **closes the last honest gap in the de-toying story**: not only is the toy confined to the
proxy/data tiers (v2.412–419), but the rigorous tier's own prefactor simplifications are shown not to drive the
conclusions either.

## Honest scope

This audits **one** simplified prefactor (cross_sector_efthedron's α) — the clearest and most load-bearing
"source-exact in form, prefactor simplified" case — not every prefactor in every rigorous bound; strong evidence
via the most load-bearing example, not an exhaustive proof. "α-independent LQG exclusion" is demonstrated by
cross_sector dropping out of the kill-set at low α while LQG stays excluded (redundancy with the other
source-exact-in-form bounds). The candidate failing at α ≈ 3 is expected (far outside the O(1) range the guess
spans).
