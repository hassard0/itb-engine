# v2.241 - The eikonal Kerr ringdown damping from the photon-orbit Lyapunov exponent

## Question

v2.240 gave the eikonal Kerr ringdown FREQUENCY from the equatorial photon orbit. Complete the
eikonal Kerr QNM: the DAMPING (imaginary part) is set by the Lyapunov instability rate of the same
orbit. How does the damping -- and hence the ringdown quality factor -- depend on spin?

## Result

Equatorial Kerr null geodesics (M=1, E=1): `rdot^2 = 1 - V`, `V = (b^2-a^2)/r^2 - 2(b-a)^2/r^3`. The
photon orbit sits at the maximum of `V` (`V'=0`, `V=1`), giving `b_c = a(r_ph+3)/(3-r_ph)` (-> +/-
`3 sqrt3` as `a -> 0`). The Lyapunov rate in coordinate time is `lambda = sqrt(-V''(r_ph)/2)/tdot`:

```text
  a        lambda (pro/retro)     quality factor Q ~ Omega_ph/(2 lambda), prograde
  0.000     0.19245 / 0.19245       0.500
  0.300     0.19067 / 0.19146       0.574
  0.500     0.18592 / 0.19009       0.657
  0.700     0.17389 / 0.18841       0.808
  0.900     0.13608 / 0.18655       1.292
  0.998     0.02926 / 0.18560       8.095
```

Validated against Schwarzschild (`a=0 -> lambda = Omega_c = 1/(3 sqrt3)`, the v2.229 identity).

**With spin the prograde damping FALLS monotonically** -- from 0.192 toward the extremal zero-damping
limit (`lambda -> 0`) -- while retrograde stays ~0.186. So the prograde ringdown **quality factor
rises sharply**, from 0.50 (Schwarzschild) to 8.1 at `a=0.998`: near-extremal black holes ring for
many more cycles, the long-lived "zero-damping modes" that make near-extremal remnants the prime
targets for ringdown spectroscopy. Combined with v2.240 (frequency) this gives the full eikonal Kerr
QNM `omega_lmn(a) = (l+1/2) Omega_ph - i(n+1/2) lambda` from the photon orbit alone -- without the
deferred Teukolsky/Leaver solver.
