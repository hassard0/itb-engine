# v1.66 — Survival on the full 35-constraint stack: the new bounds shrink, don't reshape

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan (200k draws/framework).
**Compares to:** v1.26 (31-constraint joint survival).

---

## The rerun

Joint coefficient × prefactor survival, now on the full **35-constraint** stack
(adding the v1.61 CEMZ causality + cross-sector EFThedron) and with GFT added.
Out-of-scope frameworks (Hořava–Lifshitz, Causal Set, Emergent) are skipped.

| framework | v1.66 (35 constraints) | v1.26 (31) | shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| asymptotic safety | 45% | 44% | +1% |
| CDT | 19% | 19% | ~0% |
| string tree-EFT | 19% | 24% | **−5%** |
| group_field_theory | 4% | (new) | — |
| LQG-induced | 2% | 2% | ~0% |
| discovered branches | 0–3% | (new, see note) | — |

## Reading — for the named frameworks (the fair comparison)

Adding the two new constraints **modestly tightens** survival and **preserves the
ordering and the LQG-disfavoured verdict**. String drops the most (−5%) because
the prefactor sampling includes the EFThedron α over [0.8, 1.5], and string's
α_crit is 1.33 (v1.64), so ~half the α-draws exclude it. AS, CDT, LQG are
essentially unchanged. GFT enters at 4% — low, with LQG, as a spin-foam framework
(g_R3 ≈ g_R2). **The new bounds make the allowed region smaller, not different:**
the v1.26 conclusions are robust to the expanded stack.

## Honest caveat — the discovered-theory numbers are a DIFFERENT quantity

The discovered branches read 0–3% here, which looks like a collapse from their
v1.27 robustness (76% / 63% / 18%). It is **not** a disfavoring — it is the wrong
comparison. The discovered theories are **boundary-optimized points** (found at
worst-case margin ~0.005–0.016, on the feasibility frontier). This survival run
perturbs their coefficients by ±30%, which knocks a frontier point out of the
thin feasible sliver almost immediately. Their v1.27 number (prefactor-only
robustness of the *fixed* point) is the apples-to-apples figure; the v1.66 number
is "how feasible is a ±30%-jittered version," and for a boundary-optimized point
that is naturally tiny.

This is itself an honest finding: **the discovered theories are robust as
specific points across prefactor uncertainty, but they sit on the feasibility
boundary, so they are coefficient-fragile** — unlike the named frameworks, which
are representative interior-ish points. A discovered theory is a *target* (look
here), not a *basin* (a wide region of safety). The named frameworks and the
discovered points should not be ranked on the same survival axis.

## Net

- **Named frameworks:** v1.26 verdicts robust to the 35-constraint stack; the new
  constraints shrink the region modestly (string −5%, others ~0); LQG and GFT at
  the bottom (2%, 4%).
- **Discovered theories:** boundary points — robust to prefactors (v1.27), fragile
  to coefficient jitter (here); a different, complementary measure.
- **The LQG/spin-foam-disfavoured conclusion stands** on the larger stack.
