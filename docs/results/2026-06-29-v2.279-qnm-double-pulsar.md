# v2.279 - The double pulsar PSR J0737-3039: general relativity's most stringent test

## Question

The natural sequel to the v2.278 Hulse-Taylor pulsar. In the double pulsar BOTH neutron stars are
visible, so the system over-determines its two masses with five post-Keplerian (PK) parameters. Does
general relativity pass that over-determined consistency test?

## Result

Periastron advance alone fixes the total mass; the directly-measured mass ratio `R = m_A/m_B` (unique
to the double pulsar, since both orbits are seen) splits it; then every other PK parameter is PREDICTED
and checked:

```text
  omega_dot -> M = 2.5871 M_sun   (measured 2.58708);  R = 1.0714 -> m_A = 1.3381, m_B = 1.2490

  parameter        predicted       measured        frac diff
  total mass       +2.58708 Msun   +2.58708 Msun    0.00%
  m_A              +1.33813 Msun   +1.33810 Msun    0.00%
  m_B              +1.24895 Msun   +1.24890 Msun    0.00%
  gamma (Einstein) +0.38403 ms     +0.38560 ms      0.41%
  Pdot_b (GW)      -1.24783e-12    -1.25200e-12     0.33%
  Shapiro r        +6.1517 us      +6.2100 us       0.94%
```

With the masses fixed by just two inputs (`omega_dot` and `R`), the Einstein delay, the GW orbital
decay and the Shapiro range are all predicted and match to `~1%` -- all 6/6 checks pass. The five PK
curves intersect at a single point in the mass plane, which they need not have: that is the test, and
GR passes. The Shapiro-delay shape `s = sin i` sharpens it to `~0.05%` (observed/GR-predicted
`= 0.99987 +/- 0.00050`, Kramer et al. 2006).

## What this establishes

The double pulsar is GR's most stringent binary test: with both masses fixed by two measurements, every
remaining strong-field observable is a falsifiable prediction, and they all agree. Together with the
v2.278 Hulse-Taylor decay, the binary pulsars confirm both that GR's quadrupole GW emission is real AND
that the full strong-field two-body dynamics is Einsteinian -- the classical bedrock under the
v2.266-v2.272 GW program.

## Honest scope

Exact leading-order Damour-Deruelle PK formulas with the source-backed Kramer et al. 2006 values; the
predicted-vs-measured agreements reproduce the published consistency to `~1%` (the actual analysis
reaches far higher precision with full timing and the Kramer 2021 update -- this is the structural
demonstration, not the full timing fit). The Shapiro shape `0.05%` figure is the published headline,
cited not re-derived (`s` needs the inclination from the timing solution, independent of the masses).
`omega_dot` is taken as pure GR (a tiny spin-orbit / higher-PN contribution exists). Point-mass,
leading PN PK order. A classical-GR / strong-field-test result, not an engine constraint refit.
