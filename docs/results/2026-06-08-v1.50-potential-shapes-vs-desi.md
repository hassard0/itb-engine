## The test

v1.49 found the minimal cosine axion sits on the shallow thawing track. Does a
different potential shape steepen wₐ to DESI's central −0.7 while keeping
w₀ ≈ −0.83 and Ω_φ = 0.69? Same EOM, four potential families, each shot for
Ω_φ = 0.69.

## Result — corrected for shoot convergence

| potential | w₀ | wₐ | Ω_φ (target 0.69) | valid? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cosine | −0.820 | **−0.283** | 0.690 | ✅ converged |
| exp (λ=1.5) | −0.862 | **−0.206** | 0.690 | ✅ converged |
| inverse-power | −0.877 | **−0.176** | 0.690 | ✅ converged |

**Every potential that actually converged to Ω_φ = 0.69 gives wₐ ∈ [−0.28, −0.18]
— the shallow thawing track — at w₀ ≈ −0.82 to −0.88.** None reaches DESI's
central wₐ = −0.7. The one run with a steep wₐ (exp λ=3, wₐ = −0.67) did *not*
give the dark-energy density (Ω_φ = 0.51) and had the wrong w₀ (−0.48) — it is in
a scaling regime, not a viable dark-energy solution.

## Honest verdict

**The v1.49 tension is robust to the potential shape.** Minimal single-field
quintessence — cosine, exponential, or inverse-power — generically predicts the
shallow thawing track (|wₐ| ≲ 0.3) at w₀ ≈ −0.83. The models sit inside DESI's 2σ
only because DESI's wₐ uncertainty is large (±0.3); they systematically populate
the *shallow* edge and cannot reach the steep central value.

This is the well-established result that **DESI's preferred steep (w₀, wₐ) is hard
for any minimal single-field quintessence**, and it is informative either way:

- If future data (DESI DR2+, Euclid, Roman) **confirms** a steep wₐ ≈ −0.7, then
  the dark-energy axion in its minimal form is disfavoured, pointing to
  **non-minimal dynamics** — coupled quintessence, multi-field, or early dark
  energy — or to residual systematics in the w₀wₐ fit.
- If the contour **relaxes toward |wₐ| ≲ 0.3** (as some reanalyses suggest), the
  minimal axion fits cleanly and the unified picture stands.

## What this does and does not touch

It does *not* touch the engine's distinctive prediction. The cosmological
model-building (potential shape, wₐ steepness) is a question about the dark-energy
sector; the engine's falsifiable output — the correlated **GW birefringence at
|g_R2_parity| ~ 0.09** (v1.46, LIGO O5) and the **CMB EB spectrum** (v1.48) — is
independent of how wₐ ultimately resolves. The unified picture's cosmology leg
has a known, quantified tension; its quantum-gravity-parity leg is untouched and
remains the clean discriminator.

## Honest limitations

Single-field, minimally-coupled, standard kinetic term throughout; CPL fit of wₐ;
DESI DR1 contour. The robust, model-independent content: across three converging
potential shapes the axion lands on the shallow thawing track — changing V(φ)
does not rescue DESI's steep wₐ.
