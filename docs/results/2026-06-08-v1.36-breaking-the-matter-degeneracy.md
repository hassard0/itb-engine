# v1.36 — Breaking the matter degeneracy: spin-4 partial wave isolates g_8

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Compute:** Vulcan.
**Builds on / resolves:** v1.35 (matter sector observationally degenerate).

---

## Context

v1.35 found the matter couplings g_4/g_6/g_8 observationally degenerate from a
single forward amplitude (the monomials s², s³, s⁴ are collinear), and on that
basis *downgraded* the "precision g_8" invented experiment. But standard EFT says
the fix is **angular information**: a 2k-derivative operator first contributes to
the spin-2k partial wave, so higher partial waves isolate higher-derivative
couplings. This cycle tests whether that breaks the degeneracy.

## Result — angular data resolves g_8; energy reach does it only artefactually

Matter-sector Fisher condition number and the marginal resolution of g_8 under
three observable designs (σ = 0.02 throughout):

| design | condition # | resolution g_4 / g_6 / g_8 | g_8 status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. forward, s∈[0.2,1.0] (v1.35) | 11 956 | 0.27 / 0.71 / 0.45 | degenerate |
| B. forward, wide s∈[0.2,3.0] | 53 422 | 0.03 / 0.03 / 0.006 | "resolved" — but artefact |
| **C. partial waves spin-0/2/4** | **14.6** | **0.018 / 0.020 / 0.015** | **cleanly resolved** |

- **Design C (partial waves) is the real fix.** The condition number collapses
  from ~12 000 to **14.6** (≈800× better conditioned) and g_8 resolution improves
  30× (0.45 → 0.015 ≪ its constraint-freedom range 0.471). The reason is
  structural: the **spin-4 partial wave is sourced only by the dimension-8
  coupling g_8**, the spin-2 wave by g_6 (and g_8), the spin-0 wave by all three —
  so the angular decomposition is upper-triangular in derivative order and
  inverts cleanly. Angular information, not energy, breaks the degeneracy.

- **Design B (wide energy) "resolves" g_8 only artefactually.** Extending s to 3.0
  makes s⁴ ≈ 81, so g_8's signal dwarfs the fixed σ — but that assumes
  measurements at energies where the EFT expansion is itself breaking down
  (s → cutoff). Its condition number actually *worsens* (53 000). So energy reach
  is not a clean fix; angular data is.

## Consequence — the "measure g_8" experiment is revived, correctly specified

v1.27/v1.34 proposed "precision g_8"; v1.35 downgraded it (forward-amplitude
degeneracy); v1.36 **revives it with the right observable**:

> **Invented experiment (corrected): measure the spin-4 partial wave of 2→2
> matter scattering.** It is sourced only by the dimension-8 coupling g_8, so it
> isolates the loosest, most new-physics-rich direction (v1.33) that the forward
> amplitude alone cannot. Combined with the spin-2 wave (g_6) and spin-0 (g_4),
> the full angular decomposition resolves the entire matter sector.

So the new-physics freedom that v1.35 showed hides in the matter direction is
**not fundamentally hidden** — it is accessible to an angular (partial-wave)
measurement, just not to a forward-amplitude one. The two robust novel branches
(low-g8 vs high-g8, v1.27/v1.32) are therefore experimentally distinguishable
after all, via the spin-4 channel.

## Updated experiment ranking (principled, post-v1.35/36)

   (v1.35); top testable signal.

   cycle); distinguishes the novel branches.
3. Forward matter amplitude alone — degenerate; insufficient by itself (v1.35).

## Honest limitations

- The spin map (g_4→ℓ0; g_6→ℓ0,2; g_8→ℓ0,2,4) is a physically-motivated model of
  how the toy coefficients populate partial waves; the exact partial-wave content
  depends on the real operators. The robust, model-independent content is the
  standard EFT fact that higher-derivative operators reach higher spin, so an
  angular decomposition is upper-triangular and breaks the forward degeneracy.
- Linear-observable, toy-precision; σ assumed uniform.
