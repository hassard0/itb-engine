# v2.228 - End-to-end R4 detectability forecast (and a Leaver high-accuracy solver foundation)

## Question

The v2.217-v2.227 ringdown program built each piece of the R4 detectability chain separately.
Integrate them into one forecast: given a per-event ringdown SNR `rho` and a network of `N`
detected events, what is the reach on the ParSpec R4 coupling `gamma`? And lay the foundation for
the high-accuracy Leaver solver needed to close the recurring cross-multipole gap.

## Integrated forecast

Chain (all source-backed / first-principles from prior cycles):
- **v2.217**: the `n=1` overtone DAMPING channel is the dominant R4 lever (`dtq_1 = 171.35`).
- **v2.227**: the covariance-corrected (realistic two-mode) no-hair damping critical SNR is
  `rho_crit(1 sigma) = 0.133 / gamma`, so the 1-sigma detectable coupling is `gamma_min = 0.133 / rho`.
- **v2.223**: `N` independent events combine the Gaussian-in-`gamma` constraint as `1/sqrt(N)`, and
  the length scale `ell ~ gamma^{1/p}` (`p = 6`) tightens only as `N^{-1/12}`.

Bottom line: `gamma_reach(5 sigma) = 0.665 / (rho sqrt(N))`.

```text
  scenario                rho     N     gamma_reach(5σ)   ell (relative)
  current single event     10     1       0.0667           0.681
  loud single event        30     1       0.0222           0.567
  O4-era network           10    50       0.0094           0.492
  3G/LISA loud event      100     1       0.0067           0.464
  3G network               30  1000       0.0007           0.319
```

The reach is dominated by the overtone damping channel (v2.217-v2.227), and limited on the
length-scale axis by the steep `p=6` power (`ell ~ (rho sqrt N)^{-1/6}`), so loud events dominate
quiet populations (v2.223). Note the current single-event reach (`gamma ~ 0.067`) sits at the edge
of perturbative validity (`gamma << 6e-3`, v2.217), quantifying why a confident single-event R4
ringdown bound is hard today and improves to `gamma ~ 0.001-0.007` only with 3G/LISA-era SNR.

## Leaver high-accuracy solver: foundation laid (on Vulcan)

To close the recurring v2.212 cross-multipole WKB-sensitivity gap (which blocks the 330/440
no-hair violation and reliable absolute operator sensitivities), a high-accuracy Leaver
continued-fraction solver was scoped this cycle on Vulcan (sympy 1.14 / mpmath 1.3). The exact
Regge-Wheeler recurrence polynomials were DERIVED symbolically (2M=1, ansatz
`psi = e^{iwr}(r-1)^{-iw} r^{2iw} sum a_n ((r-1)/r)^n`), yielding
`A = x^2(1-x)^4`, `B`, `C` of degree 6/5/4. The derivation reveals a **5-term recurrence**
(coupling `a_k ... a_{k-4}`), not the textbook 3-term, so a validated solver requires a matrix
continued fraction / Gaussian-elimination-to-3-term step (Leaver 1990). **Foundation laid; the
validated solver is deferred to a dedicated cycle rather than shipped unvalidated** -- the
symbolic recurrence is the reusable artifact for that next effort.

## What this establishes

A single integrated R4 reach number for any `(rho, N)`, tying the 12-cycle ringdown program
together, plus the verified symbolic foundation for the high-accuracy solver. It is a synthesis of
validated pieces, not a new measurement -- its value is the end-to-end picture and the honest
placement of current vs future detectability against the perturbative-validity boundary.

## Honest scope

Integrates prior-cycle results, each with its caveats: the covariance-corrected single-event
number (v2.227, equal-amplitude white-noise two-mode Fisher), the population `1/sqrt(N)` (v2.223,
independent half-normal posteriors), and the source-backed overtone qEFT coefficient (v2.217,
`dtq_1=171.35`, perturbatively valid for `gamma << 6e-3`). `gamma` is the ParSpec coupling;
converting to an absolute `ell` in km needs the v2.215 normalization (not claimed -- `ell` is a
relative scaling). Parity-odd `g_R4_c3` stays dark (v2.209).
