# v2.232 - Inverting a two-parameter photon-sphere deviation from shadow + ringdown

## Question

v2.231 established that a non-Kerr photon-sphere deformation has exactly TWO independent geodesic
observables: the shadow radius `b_c` (= the eikonal ringdown frequency, locked via `Omega_c = 1/b_c`)
and the ringdown damping (Lyapunov rate `lambda`). Can a joint shadow + ringdown measurement
DETERMINE a two-parameter non-Kerr deformation?

## Result

Deform the lapse with two bumps `f = 1 - 2/r + e1/r^3 + e2/r^4` (M=1) and compute the 2x2 Jacobian
`J = d(b_c, lambda) / d(e1, e2)`:

```text
det J = 1.37e-3      condition number = 67.6      invertible = True
linear recovery of (e1,e2) = (0.01, -0.02)  ->  (0.01, -0.02001)   (error 1.5e-5)
```

**The two independent geodesic observables invert the two-parameter deformation.** The Jacobian is
non-singular, so a joint shadow + ringdown-damping measurement recovers BOTH parameters. The
ringdown FREQUENCY (`= 1/b_c` by the v2.231 locking identity) is a redundant third measurement, so
the joint observation is **over-determined** (3 measurements, 2 parameters) -- the redundancy is a
built-in GW/EM consistency check. The moderate condition number (67.6) reflects that the damping
responds similarly to the two radial profiles (`1/r^3`, `1/r^4`), so the shadow/frequency carries
most of the discriminating power between them.

## What this establishes

Completes the geodesic sub-arc (v2.229-v2.232): the photon sphere underlies ringdown and the
shadow (v2.229-v2.230), a deformation locks frequency<->shadow while the damping is independent
(v2.231), and now the two independent observables provably invert a two-parameter deformation with
a redundant consistency check. A clean, self-contained falsifiable test structure for non-Kerr /
quantum-corrected compact objects combining gravitational-wave and electromagnetic data.

## Honest scope

Eikonal (large-`l`) and static-spherical only (v2.229-v2.231 caveats): the inversion is exact for
the geodesic observables in the eikonal limit; finite-`l` `O(1/l)` corrections and Kerr rotation

demonstrates the INVERTIBILITY structure (2 observables -> 2 parameters, +1 redundant check); a real
